# A5XEF Typed Failure-State Contract

- Failed N5-SYNTH conformance returns `IMPLEMENTATION_FAILURE`, prohibits release and emits no classification.
- Failed N5-RUN or malformed/incomplete post-authorization evidence returns `INVALID_EXPERIMENT` and emits no classification.
- Well-formed negative null, dominance or P1–P5 evidence remains `VALID_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT` and follows the frozen mutually exclusive classifier.

Invalidity precedes P1–P5 and classification. No producer-supplied favorable field can preserve a partially derived label.
