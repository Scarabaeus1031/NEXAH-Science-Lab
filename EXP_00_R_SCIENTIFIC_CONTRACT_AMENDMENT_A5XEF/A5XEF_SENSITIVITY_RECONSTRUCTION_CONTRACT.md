# A5XEF Sensitivity Reconstruction Contract

The accepted twelve-member registry is exact and ordered. Each record binds ordinal, ID, changed path, primary/alternate value, full patched configuration, canonical full hash, unchanged-leaf hash, seed registry, rows-per-seed, required outputs and row-level variant representation evidence.

The derivation repeats score→rank, support, population, coherence and model reconstruction for each variant. Supplied coherence/coefficient/gain/pass summaries are not inputs. Every variant must return both-carrier coefficient/gain, support and provenance; only the two amplitudes enter P5 and the other ten remain mandatory report-only.

Wrong identity, path/value, unrelated changed leaf, hash, population, required output or copied variant evidence invalidates the experiment.
