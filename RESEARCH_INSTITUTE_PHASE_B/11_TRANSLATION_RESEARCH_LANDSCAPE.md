# Translation Research Landscape

Status: `DISTRIBUTED RESEARCH LANDSCAPE — NO PROGRAM ADOPTION`

Operational effect: `NONE`

Authority change: `NONE`

Program status: `NOT ADOPTED AS ONE UNIFIED PROGRAM`

## 1. Scope

This document is a pointer and landscape record for existing translation-related
work. It makes distributed artifacts discoverable while preserving their local
scientific objects, evidence status, owners and governance boundaries.

It does not create an architecture, re-evaluate a scientific claim, or replace
an owning Program, Lab, protocol, audit or report. The starting disposition is:

> Translation is an identifiable but distributed research direction.

The repository explicitly identifies `Orientation Translation` as applied
research, maintains a Mathematical Research Direction for representation maps
and identifiability, and contains bounded projection, reconstruction, artifact
and interoperability questions. It does not document those materials as one
adopted `Translation Science` program.

## 2. Landscape working definition

For this index only:

> Translation research examines declared relations between source and target
> representations, including preservation, loss, artifacts, unknowns,
> equivalence, defect, reconstruction and provenance.

This is a landscape working definition distilled from the existing
[Mathematical Core](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM_B_MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATIONS/RESEARCH_DIRECTION/01_MATHEMATICAL_CORE.md),
[Translation Audit](../NEXAH_TRANSLATION_AUDIT_REPORT_01/README.md),
[Track-B I-L-A-U audit](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM_A_MOVING_MASK_FEASIBILITY/TRACE_TO_MOTION_0_1_PROTOCOL_DRAFT/TRACK_B_REVERSAL_RELATION_AND_ILAU_AUDIT_DE.md)
and [Reflection specification](../MISSION_01_SCIENTIFIC_BACKBONE/11_REFLECTION_INVOLUTION_SPECIFICATION.md).
It is not a canonical definition, OLS primitive, operator contract, theorem or
program-admission statement.

## 6. Existing study families

### A. Applied Orientation Translation

- The [Research Program Discovery](03_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_DISCOVERY.md) identifies
  Orientation Translation as an explicit applied research program.
- The [Lab Catalogue](../RESEARCH_INSTITUTE_AUDIT/03_EXISTING_AND_CANDIDATE_LAB_CATALOGUE.md)
  records Photosynthesis Pilot 01, Cellular Respiration Pilot 02 and Mitosis
  Pilot 03 as existing methodological Labs.
- The [Claim Inventory](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM_C_VALIDATION_LANDSCAPE/01_CLAIM_INVENTORY.md)
  classifies recurring editorial operations and source/evidence-boundary
  practice as partially supported, rejects promotion to a formal cross-domain
  grammar, and leaves reader benefit unvalidated.

### B. Mathematical representation research

- Representation and observation maps, representation changes, compatibility,
  comparison defect, identifiability, invariance and failure sets are defined in
  the Mathematical Core.
- Program B retains map preservation and multiple-view reconstruction as
  `WORKING` questions.
- Round-trip inconsistency as a loss diagnostic remains `SPECULATIVE`; the
  [cross-program traceability](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM_F_CROSS_PROGRAM_SYNTHESIS/01_PROGRAM_A_TO_E_TRACEABILITY.md)
  records no direct result connecting cycle error to information loss.
- Registration is a bounded subproblem under established terminology, not a new
  direction or package.

### C. Finite projection and reconstruction

- Lab 0.4 implements four finite projections, stable source identity, projection
  collapse, post-projection masking and explicit `UNKNOWN` behavior.
- Program G freezes a finite displayed-record discrepancy and partition rule; it
  does not establish statistical, physical or continuous equivalence.
- Track B implements a technical candidate/back-projection path and records
  retained graph structure, lost order, introduced technical choices and
  unknown source direction.
- Common support and back-projection are local contracts, not one general
  translation method.

### D. Scientific artifact translation

- Translation Audit 01 freezes a Lab report, associated poster and HTML
  instrument.
- Its relation register records preservation, compression, added interaction,
  absent derivation history, evidence status and non-implications.
- It rejects a complete five-stage chain through Experience and ORION because
  the corresponding endpoints are absent.
- The independent two-pass Human reconstruction test remains unexecuted.

### E. Interoperability and implementation

- The repository maintains an open question about exchange of governed records
  between independent implementations.
- The application assessment requires machine syntax/schema, validators,
  conformance cases, standards mapping and two implementations.
- No two-system exchange has occurred. The general OLS interoperability claim is
  `UNVALIDATED`, and the application remains `BLOCKED`.

## 7. Distinction from existing programs

| Dimension | `TRACE_TO_MOTION` | `FIELD_LAYER / NAVIGATION` | distributed Translation landscape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| central question | which declared geometric and temporal relations survive movement-to-trace representation and masking? | how do declared field states and C3-to-C2 transitions behave under free flow and controllers? | what declared relations, distinctions and limits persist across representations or artifacts? |
| scientific objects | source trajectory, final trace, mask, schedule, record, reconstruction and direction-free packet | state point, field, regime label, integrator, controller, transition and endpoint record | source/target representation, map, correspondence, equivalence, defect, loss/unknown and provenance chain |
| transformations | calibration, normalization, rendering, masking, reconstruction and technical projection | field integration, pulse, capture and lock | projection, representation change, registration, reconstruction, comparison and artifact implementation |
| metrics | availability, reconstruction error, common support, geometric diagnostics and statuses | success, transition time, control cost, relapse and endpoint spread | local equivalence discrepancy, defect, reconstruction/round-trip discrepancy, preserved/lost fields and reviewer score |
| failure modes | `UNKNOWN`, `INVALID`, `BLOCKED`, leakage and absent common support | timeout, non-finite state, domain exit and unsuccessful residence | undefined map, non-identifiability, missing correspondence/provenance, unsupported inference and absent endpoint |
| evidence status | Track A technical conformance; Track B scientific identifiability blocked; Track C not built | controlled-model package under `FIELD_NOTES`; no automatic Research/Evidence promotion | mixture of definitions, synthetic tests, partially supported applied method, proposed diagnostics and pending audit |
| governance | explicit Program A design with track-specific gates | current C2/C3 package remains governed as Field Notes | several distinct owners and homes; Orientation Translation explicit, broader landscape not adopted as one program |
| repository homes | Program A and `TRACE_TO_MOTION_0_1_PROTOCOL_DRAFT/` | `FIELD_NOTES/C3_PROFILE_2026-08-06/` | Orientation Translation references, Program B, Program G, Rödelheim, Mission 01, Programs C/D and Translation Audit |
| claim boundary | representation/reconstruction does not recover source cause or meaning | controlled capture does not establish a free basin, universal edge or transfer | preservation is not truth; invariance is not causality; resemblance is not derivation; provenance is not identifiability |

`TRACE_TO_MOTION` is a bounded representation/reconstruction case, not the
entire Translation landscape. The current
[C3-to-C2 transition experiment](../FIELD_NOTES/C3_PROFILE_2026-08-06/C3_TO_C2_TRANSITION_2026-08-06/README.md)
compares controller conditions inside one field model and is not a Translation
experiment. Translation is more than shared infrastructure because several
artifacts make maps, preservation, equivalence, loss or artifact relations the
research object. Those artifacts still do not constitute one unified third
program.

## 8. Program-candidate gaps

A possible future broad Translation program would still require:

1. an adopted central question and scope boundary;
2. one explicit owner;
3. one declared Research home;
4. a non-duplicating boundary against Orientation Translation, Orientation
   among representations, finite identifiability, Program B, Program G and
   `TRACE_TO_MOTION`;
5. a shared source/target/map contract;
6. a common or explicitly compatible metric family;
7. falsifiable program-level hypotheses;
8. several independently executed studies under that same question;
9. a review and evidence structure;
10. a governance decision.

This gap list is descriptive. It is not a roadmap authorization, owner request,
execution permission or recommendation to adopt a program.

```text

```

an adopted repository architecture. The three areas do not have equal maturity,
evidence or governance. `TFT` is therefore only a non-authorized working
abbreviation in this boundary statement and must not be used as a Program,
operator, architecture or authority label.

No numerical, symmetrical or aesthetic property supports the grouping.
