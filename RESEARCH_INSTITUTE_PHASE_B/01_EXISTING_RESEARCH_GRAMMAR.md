# Existing Research Grammar

## Finding

`SUPPORTED WITH ONE CORRECTION`

NEXAH already has an implicit scientific grammar and an explicit governed
lifecycle. They overlap but are not identical.

## Grammar A — exploratory emergence

Historical and current work commonly begins as:

```text
Human observation or source
→ visual or conceptual pattern
→ working language
→ bounded question
```

Evidence:

- Library Works and whiteboards preserve recurring motifs before formal review;
- Field Notes explicitly preserve unreviewed observations;
- Observer Geometry and early Research files begin from visual or dynamical
  recurrences;
- Programs A and B reduce those recurrences to ordinary scientific questions.

This grammar is useful for discovery. It creates no evidence, definition or
Research authority automatically. 【LIB; EXP; PA; PB】

## Grammar B — bounded scientific work

Recent, stronger packages follow:

```text
Question
→ Scope and non-claims
→ Declared scientific object
→ Definitions
→ Protocol or method
→ Experiment, study or derivation
→ Result
→ Evidence and provenance
→ Review
→ Disposition or STOP
```

This form is visible in:

- Rödelheim Labs 0.3 and 0.4;
- prime-modular and wheel/product validation packages;
- IEEE Geometry V1;
- Orientation Translation pilots, comparisons and meta reviews;
- Programs F and G;
- Phase A evidence consolidation and Phase B source-freeze preparation.

Negative, inconclusive and invalid outcomes remain admissible endpoints.
【RO; EVID; APP; PF; PG】

## Grammar C — governed ecosystem continuation

The adopted Control Desk lifecycle explicitly records:

```text
QUESTION
→ RESEARCH
→ EVIDENCE
→ REVIEW
→ ADOPTION
→ IMPLEMENTATION
→ PUBLICATION
→ MAINTENANCE
→ RETIREMENT
```

This is a permission and responsibility grammar, not a compulsory scientific
sequence. Research may terminate after Review. Adoption may conclude `NO
CHANGE`. Publication may be unnecessary. Maintenance may open a new Question.
【CD】

## Natural artifact grammar

| Stage | Existing artifact forms | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| source/observation | source record, Field Note, fixed public source, dataset | source owner / Human / Research as declared |
| question | Lab question, research question, protocol question | Research owner |
| object | source family, state system, graph, projection family, benchmark | scientific owner |
| method | specification, protocol, study method, frozen manifest | method/protocol owner |
| experiment | script, deterministic run, comparison, reader study | Lab or application owner |
| result | JSON/CSV output, result table, negative finding | evidence owner |
| report | Lab note, `RESULTS.md`, pilot/study report, replay review | report owner |
| disposition | supported, negative, inconclusive, STOP, frozen, candidate | competent reviewer / Human owner |
| continuation | new question, adoption route, publication candidate, archive | destination authority |

## Corrections to the proposed hierarchy

1. `Research Program` and `Research Area` do not follow a report. They group
   questions and Labs across all stages.
2. `Application` may contain Research; it is not necessarily downstream from
   publication.
3. `Library` owns Work and Edition identity, not scientific maturity.
4. `Website` presents and routes; it does not complete a scientific chain.
5. `Publication` is optional and cannot upgrade evidence.

## Minimum grammar

The smallest evidence-supported description is:

> NEXAH turns observations into bounded questions, investigates declared
> objects through traceable methods, preserves results as evidence, and
> requires review before any change of authority or public status.
