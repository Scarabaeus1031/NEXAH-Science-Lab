## 4. Repository principle

The documented Backbone structure is treated as consolidated at this baseline.
Further Backbone expansion is appropriate only when new evidence or new
Governance requirements justify reconsideration.

New work should primarily:

- extend existing Programs within their declared boundaries;
- add bounded experiments;
- generate evidence;

rather than repeatedly restructure the Backbone.

This principle records the baseline disposition. It does not prohibit ordinary
repository maintenance, scientific criticism, negative results or an authorized
review under the triggers below.

## 5. Review triggers
