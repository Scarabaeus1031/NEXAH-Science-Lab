# Critical Self Review

## Possible overreach

### Recovered Areas

The four Area clusters compress real recurrence, but only the transition and
translation programs possess strong explicit identities. Treating the Area
names as canonical would exceed the evidence.

### Program reconstruction

The 0.2–0.4/Programs B–G chain is coherent by dependency, but its materials
have different owners and repository roles. Coherence does not authorize one
Program identity.

### One-document recommendation

One navigator is smaller than three documents, but it may become too wide if
hundreds of historical experiments are admitted. The pilot scope must remain
small and owner-selected.

## Evidence limitations

- File inventories are complete, but generated images and historical scripts
  were interpreted through their owning documentation rather than individually.
- Some research questions exist in long logs without stable identity; this
  review consolidated only questions supported by owning context.
- No experiment was replayed.
- No external literature was consulted.
- Control Desk and canonical NEXAH states are independent worktrees; the review
  did not treat them as one atomic release.
- Publication and Website state were read through repository and Control Desk
  records, not independently reverified online.

## Alternative explanations

1. The missing navigation may reflect intentionally distributed authority, not
   a documentation defect.
2. The adopted Research Architecture plus Evidence Atlas may be sufficient for
   expert readers; the proposed navigator mainly serves repeated startup.
3. Historical programs may not need current question identities.
4. A Catalog could eventually supply the relation, but current Catalog
   governance prohibits population and automation.

## Confidence assessment

| every historical Lab should be indexed | LOW |

## What would falsify this review?

- an existing maintained document already joins bounded questions, Labs,
  reports and evidence dispositions across all owning areas;
- owners reject the recovered relation between Labs 0.2–0.4 and the
  Mathematical Research Direction;
- a three-Lab manual pilot cannot fit one pointer record without copying
  scientific content;
- report-method differences require separate governance before navigation is
  possible;
- maintaining the navigator duplicates Evidence Atlas or Control Desk state.

## Conclusion

The audit survives, but only after reduction. The repository supports one
manual navigation pilot, not a Research Institute implementation.
