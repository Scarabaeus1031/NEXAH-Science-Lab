# Research Area Discovery

## Method

A Research Area is admitted in this review only when it groups at least two
program-level bodies by shared scientific objects or established disciplines.
Visual vocabulary alone is insufficient.

## Strongest recovered areas

### 1. Representations, observation and identifiability

Shared objects:

- source spaces and identities;
- observation and representation maps;
- projection, masks and missing records;
- observational equivalence;
- identifiability, reconstruction and stability.

Programs and Labs:

- Mathematical Research Direction;
- Rödelheim 0.2–0.4 chain;
- Moving Mask candidate;
- representation-loss and registration questions.

Status: `RECOVERED HIGH CONFIDENCE`. This is ordinary inverse-problems,
observability and registration territory, not a new geometry. 【PB; PE; PG】

### 2. Dynamical structure and transition analysis

Shared objects:

- trajectories and reconstructed fields;
- density, coherence and local diagnostics;
- declared transitions and phase/mismatch;
- transport corridors, basins and paths.

Programs:

- canonical transition-geometry Research;
- Observer Geometry Lab;
- dynamical-system cases;
- parts of Power Systems.

Status: `EXPLICIT RESEARCH AREA IN PRACTICE`, though its internal definitions
remain heterogeneous. 【RES; EXP; APP】

### 3. Graph structure and relational navigation

Shared objects:

- typed graphs and relations;
- reachability and components;
- bottlenecks, reductions and preserved properties;
- relation-derived observables.

Programs:

- Network Orientation;
- power-system transport graph work;
- FP/RR/OE candidate;
- Mathematical Research RQ-04/RQ-05.

Status: `RECOVERED MEDIUM CONFIDENCE`. Application claims remain local. 【APP; PB】

### 4. Evidence-bounded Human and editorial orientation

Shared objects:

- fixed public representations;
- source and evidence records;
- reader paths, neighborhoods and comparisons;
- analyst decisions, STOP rules and reader outcomes.

Programs:

- Orientation Translation;
- Library/editorial review instruments;
- open reader-effect work.

Status: `RECOVERED MEDIUM CONFIDENCE`. The repository demonstrates
methodological artifacts, not yet Human outcome effects. 【APP; LIB; EVID】

## Areas not supported as independent identities

| Candidate | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Projection Geometry | subarea/question family within representations and identifiability |
| Observer Geometry | Lab environment spanning areas 1 and 2 |
| Network Orientation | application/program within area 3 |
| Human Orientation | ecosystem purpose broader than a scientific Area |
| Mathematical Foundations | layer and method role, not one scientific discipline |
| Orientation Science | Library Work title and historical language; not sufficient area authority |

## Area topology

```text
Representations / Observation / Identifiability
            ↕
Dynamical Structure / Transition Analysis
            ↕
Graph Structure / Relational Navigation

Evidence-bounded Human and Editorial Orientation
connects through representations and evaluation,
not through a shared physical mechanism.
```

## Boundary

These four areas are a descriptive compression of existing work. They must not
be added to governance, directories or registries without an owner decision.
