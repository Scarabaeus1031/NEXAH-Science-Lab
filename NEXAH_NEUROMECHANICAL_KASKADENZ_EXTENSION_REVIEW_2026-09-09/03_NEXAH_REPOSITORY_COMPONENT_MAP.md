THE EYE and NEXAHEDRON may later present records but may not substitute display projection for measured state. The Rödelheim four-view pattern supports projection separation only. Representation Ledger V3 can document provenance, but its failed stable-claim-identity gate prevents assigning stronger operational lineage authority.
