# Edge-Boundary Model

Every record declares `source_boundary`, `operator_boundary`, and `target_boundary`. Each carries a resolution state and judgment basis.

An operator is:

- `ATOMIC` when one justified callable/definition maps the declared source contract to target contract;
- `COMPOSITE` when ordered scientifically material components are declared;
- `BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED` when evidence does not support a unique decomposition.

Composite components require order, name, definition reference, input/output contract labels, and judgment. A boundary justification states why the chosen grouping is the unit under review.

If any boundary is unresolved, `claim_support_status` cannot be `SUPPORTED` and `implementation_status` cannot be `LOCATED`; this does not erase a legitimately reproduced enclosing pipeline. Micro-operations need not become separate ledger edges unless their assumptions/loss are material to the claim.
