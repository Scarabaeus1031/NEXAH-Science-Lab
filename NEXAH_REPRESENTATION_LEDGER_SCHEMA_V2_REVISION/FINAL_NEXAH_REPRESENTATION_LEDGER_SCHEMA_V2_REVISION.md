`SCHEMA_V2_READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REREVIEW`

Four actual YAML instances retain the difficult evidence rather than selecting cleaner examples. A and B expose partial historical provenance. The revision does not prove that these scientific judgments are true. Schema validity checks form and internal constraints only. A second independent review must reconstruct the cases and test whether V2 materially reduces disagreement. Validator implementation remains unauthorized until that review passes.
