# Human Judgment and Validation Boundary

Material decisions use `DETERMINISTIC`, `RULE_GUIDED`, `EXPERT_JUDGMENT`, or `UNKNOWN`, with assessor and rationale. This applies to boundaries, criteria/object selection, provenance sufficiency, epistemic status, and claim support.

Evidence uses eligibility:

- `TECHNICAL_SUPPORT` may support claims;
- `EXPLANATORY_ONLY` and `NONTECHNICAL_SYMBOLIC` must have no supported claim IDs.

A schema-valid record can still contain a false technical assertion. Syntax checks only presence, allowed values, conditional requirements, and internal form. Scientific checking still requires source inspection, execution, proof, or expert review. Fabricated quantitative uncertainty is not made true by a numeric field; quantified/bounded states require a method and evidence reference.
