# Primary Chain Selection

## Selection matrix

Scores use `1–5`; higher is favorable except risk, where lower is favorable.

| Criterion | Candidate 1 | Candidate 2 | Candidate 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Small unresolved question | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| Existing evidence | 5 | 1 | 5 |
| Standard terminology | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Least new machinery | 5 | 2 | 5 |
| Finite case or benchmark | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| Independent reproducibility potential | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Negative-result value | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Bounded external audience | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Freedom from speculative NEXAH objects | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Overclaiming risk | 2 | 4 | 2 |

## Decision

`PRIMARY CHAIN: Finite identifiability classification under four orthographic projections.`

Candidate 1 is selected because it is the smallest unresolved formalization attached to the strongest directly relevant evidence. It needs no new operator, estimator, dataset, domain claim, or external benchmark run.

Candidate 2 remains the next protocol-design candidate after separate authority. It is not first because it has no result, more parameters, and critical confounding controls.

Candidate 3 is already a frozen negative result. External review may inspect it, but reopening it would violate `STOP_AND_RETAIN` and would not answer the current need for a first new protocol specification.

## Selection boundary

The primary chain combines Program B FQ-01 and RQ-01 only within the Lab 0.4 finite case. It does not promote a general theory of representation, information loss, observability, or multi-view reconstruction.

## Status

`PRIMARY CHAIN READY FOR PROTOCOL DESIGN`

The status means the source object, question, evidence, methods, and review field are sufficient to design a frozen protocol. It does not mean the protocol already exists or that any run, contact, or publication is authorized.
