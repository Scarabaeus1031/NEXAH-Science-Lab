# Constitution Compatibility Review

## Verdict

`COMPATIBLE ONLY AS A THIN RESEARCH-OWNED ORIENTATION LAYER`

The proposed institute workflow is compatible with the Constitution when it
indexes existing Research authority and leaves every artifact with its current
canonical carrier. It becomes incompatible if it is treated as a new House,
central authority, migration program or automatic promotion mechanism.

## Compatibility matrix

| Principle | Compatible form | Violation |
|---|---|---|
| Human authority | owner approves question status, Lab admission and closeout | ORION or index assigns truth, priority or promotion |
| one canonical home | index points to the owning source | copy becomes a second source master |
| no duplicate authority | Research owns scientific question records; OLS and Library retain their objects | institute redefines OLS operators or Library identities |
| Research authority | questions, methods, evidence, negative results and uncertainty remain Research objects | architecture or publication status upgrades evidence |
| Archives remain historical | records preserve predecessor and successor links | archived claim returns as current authority through indexing |
| provenance | every Lab record cites source, protocol, result and review | summary loses source revision or boundary |
| uncertainty is information | open, blocked, rejected and inconclusive states remain visible | only successful Labs are indexed |
| no silent promotion | owner gate required for every status transition | folder creation creates a Lab or Research candidate |
| Operations boundary | Control Desk may point to current work | Control Desk becomes scientific object owner |

## Would the institute simplify existing work?

Yes, if limited to stable question and Lab references. It would reduce repeated
searches across Research, Applications, validation, Experimental, Control Desk
and Science Lab.

## Would it introduce duplication?

Yes, if it copies definitions, literature, results or reports. The layer must
store identity, status, authority, links and one-line scope only.

## Maintenance burden

Low for two registries and one template, provided updates occur only at owner
gates. High if every experiment, image, script or question mark becomes a
central record.

## Additive character

The proposal is additive when:

- current paths remain unchanged;
- historical Lab names remain unchanged;
- existing local reports remain endpoints;
- the institute layer has no execution or publication effect;
- only owner-accepted records enter the indexes.

## Constitution STOP conditions

STOP if the layer would:

- create a new constitutional House;
- transfer scientific ownership from Research or an application owner;
- redefine OLS, ORION, Library or Control Desk authority;
- infer canonicality from placement;
- merge Lab identities;
- overwrite historical status;
- activate an experiment or publication.
