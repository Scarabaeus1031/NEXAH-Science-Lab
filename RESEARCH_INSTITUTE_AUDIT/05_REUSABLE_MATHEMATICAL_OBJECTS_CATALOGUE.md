# Reusable Mathematical Objects Catalogue

## Canonicality rule

Reuse does not imply universal validity. OLS owns normative semantic objects;
Research owns scientific objects and hypotheses; Library Operator Concepts own
editorial classification only. No registry may silently combine these classes.

## Duplicated object families

- `boundary`, `gate`, `aperture`, `cut` and `mask` are related but not
  interchangeable;
- `operator` denotes OLS semantics, Library editorial concepts and research
  computations;
- `state`, `phase`, `regime`, `basin` and `territory` sometimes refer to
  different levels of representation;
- `validation`, `verification`, `conformance`, `replay` and `approval` have
  distinct authorities but are occasionally compressed in historical text.

## Canonical recommendation boundary

The Mathematical Core is the strongest candidate canonical home for
representation maps, equivalence, identifiability, invariance, stability and
failure distinctions after owner adoption. OLS remains canonical for released
semantic operators. The Library Registry remains canonical for editorial
Operator Concepts. Research measures remain namespaced and local. No new
operator registry is justified. 【N-OLS; N-LIB; M01; PB】
