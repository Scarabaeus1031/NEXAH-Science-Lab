## Historical relationship

RP-02 through RP-06 form the original dynamical-systems research lineage.
RP-01 and RP-07 are the narrower current mathematical formalization. RP-11,
RP-12 and RP-16 test methodological and Human-facing consequences. They should
remain related without being described as one theorem or mechanism.
