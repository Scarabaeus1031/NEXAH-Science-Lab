# Existing and Candidate Lab Catalogue

## Admission rule

An `existing Lab` has a bounded question, declared object or input, a method or
protocol, a result record and an explicit boundary. A `candidate Lab` has a
bounded question but lacks at least one required freeze or report component.
The labels below are audit descriptions only.
