# Research Question Catalogue

## Catalogue rule

The repository contains hundreds of question marks, including reader prompts,
report headings and operational checks. This catalogue admits a question only
when an owning research or application source treats it as an unresolved or
tested scientific, mathematical or methodological question. Equivalent
wordings are consolidated; source-specific subquestions remain attached to the
parent question.

Status terms are descriptive and do not change the owning source.

## Domain-local question groups

The biology, planetary chemistry and source-specific pilot documents contain
many legitimate domain questions. They remain local because their answers
require domain authority and are not questions about NEXAH mathematics. Their
current role is source-bounded orientation and method evaluation, not a central
NEXAH research program.
