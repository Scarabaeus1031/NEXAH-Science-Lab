# Critical Self Review

## Where the proposal may be over-engineered

- Three documents may still be too many if `QUESTIONS.md` and `LABS.md` can be
  one small table during the pilot.
- A universal Lab Report template may not fit theorem work, numerical studies
  and Human-subject reader studies equally well.
- Mapping historical states into a common lifecycle can create false
  equivalence.

## Where the proposal is under-specified

- It does not define who owns the central Research indexes.
- It does not define whether application-local scientific questions may be
  admitted without the central Research owner.
- It does not define an ID migration rule for already named Labs and studies.
- It does not define how a question changes without losing its history.
- It does not define the minimum evidence for `ACCEPTED` because that must
  remain method- and domain-specific.

## Weak assumptions

1. That the question is always the correct unit. Some infrastructure and
   editorial work is better governed by a contract or decision.
2. That one Lab can always have one report. Long programs may need several
   bounded Labs and one synthesis report.
3. That all implicit Labs should be surfaced. Historical work may be better
   left in place rather than normalized retroactively.
4. That current source paths will remain stable enough for a manual index.

## Contradicting repository evidence

- The adopted Research Architecture intentionally describes a nonlinear,
  cross-repository program rather than a linear institute pipeline.
- The Library and Orientation Translation demonstrate valuable exploratory
  work that is not reducible to mathematical Labs.
- Power-system research grew through serial experiment logs; forcing every
  historical run into a Lab may destroy its development context.
- OLS shows that mature artifacts sometimes require a release suite rather
  than one report.

## Confidence

| Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|
| distributed Labs already exist | HIGH |
| missing question-to-report navigation is real | HIGH |
| no new repository is needed | HIGH |
| no new operator registry is needed | HIGH |
| Labs 0.2–0.4 and finite validations are exemplars | HIGH |
| three documents are the exact minimum | MEDIUM |
| one lifecycle vocabulary can cover all research | LOW |
| historical experiment families can be normalized safely | LOW |

## Remaining unknowns

- owner intent for the relation between canonical NEXAH Research and the
  Science Lab Programs A–G;
- canonical home and owner for a future question registry;
- whether Phase B adoption already implies a general Lab-report convention;
- which power-system experiment groups are still active versus historical;
- whether the current Research README's transition-geometry narrative should
  remain the primary research entry after a question registry exists;
- whether Field Notes should be visible in Research navigation without being
  admitted into Research.

## Self-review conclusion

The audit supports a pilot index, not an institute implementation. The
proposal should be tested on no more than three existing Labs before any
repository-wide adoption.
