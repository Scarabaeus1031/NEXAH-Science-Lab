Temporal Z Cylinder

Post-close view adapter · implemented internal view adapter · non-evidentiary. Reused Study identity: NEXAH_LIFE_BOUNDARY_CYLINDER_LIFE08_2026_10_11.

Coordinate contract

For an addressed event (x,y,g) on a declared cylinder-x or cylinder-y carrier, the adapter renders:

theta = 2*pi*wrapped_address/N
radius = R + lane_depth*other_address/(N-1)
z = scaled(g-first_generation)

No source coordinate is discarded: the wrapped coordinate becomes angle, the other spatial coordinate becomes a radial lane, and discrete generation becomes world Z. Birth, survival and death retain the existing event alphabet and persistent event IDs.

Animation and export boundary

The moving Z sweep is visual interpolation. It is not a Life state, does not alter B3/S23 and is excluded from scientific interpretation. The GLB export stores axis semantics, address sources, generation interval, boundary, projection_only=true and rule_unchanged=true as extras.
