# A5 P4 Prospective Choice Justification

## Candidate interpretations considered before outcomes

1. **Classification-critical standalone prediction.** Require positive incremental prediction from each representation alone. This would add a new comparator/design because V1 does not define a scalar single-view coherence or an exact standalone nested-model contrast.
2. **Mandatory report-only diagnostics.** Execute the fixed single-view outcome predictors, but restrict P4 to the two symmetric coherence carrier analyses. Missing diagnostics remain validity failures.
3. **Invent leave-one/pseudo-coherence.** Treat a single ranking as coherence or compare it with an oracle/reference. This changes the estimand and information contract and is rejected.
4. **Carrier-policy performance attribution.** Make standalone policy success a P4 threshold. V1 supplies no frozen performance threshold and explicitly separates agreement-as-information from fusion/control advantage; rejected.

## Prospective selection

Option 2 is selected and labeled **NEW PROSPECTIVE SCIENTIFIC CHOICE**. The selection is outcome-blind. It preserves the typed coherence estimand, the two-carrier symmetry, and mandatory attribution reporting without inventing a new null, threshold, comparator, oracle, or single-view coherence. It is not a claim that single-view diagnostics are scientifically unimportant.

Classification consequence: their values cannot pass, fail, rescue, or veto P4; their absence or invalid provenance makes the entire experiment invalid. This is the same substantive scope A4 attempted, but A5 legitimately installs it prospectively rather than presenting it as an already frozen fact.
