## 2. Anchor and orientation

- `Q°` is the relational controller/reference origin of a declared representation.
- `Q°` is not a fifth quaternion component, world center or hidden physical object.
- An anchor alone does not recover direction, scale or handedness. Anchor plus frame can.
- Orientation is in the relations among source, frame, observer, cut and return—not merely in a point.

Operational form:

```text
maps -> translation -> binding -> Q° -> alignment -> motion
```

## 3. Observer cut and representation

A source state and its record are different objects:

```text
S(t) -> Q°_n(t) -> R_Q°(t) -> Phi -> M
S(t) != R_Q°(t)
```

Two maps may refer to the same source while retaining different information, losing distinctions, introducing artifacts or leaving regions non-reconstructable. Objectivity is therefore operationalized as declared conditions, reproducibility and valid transforms between views—not a view from nowhere.

## 8. Ring, seam, plates, tiles and antipode

- A dome is a continuous angular field.
- Rings/plates are declared slices of that field.
- An ordered plate sequence can encode memory as retained records.
- A seam is introduced by opening or flattening a closed representation; it belongs to the map, not automatically to the source field.
- A joint is the rule that connects while retaining distinction.
- Rasterization/voxelization creates tiles/cubes. Rotation alone preserves form and does not turn continents into lines or cubes.

For angular point `(A,h)`, the antipode is:

```text
(A,h) -> ((A + 180°) mod 360°, -h)
```

The violin forms in the supplied Test 08C visual are distribution-density shapes. Their resemblance to an amphora or water tower is morphological, not evidence of identity.
