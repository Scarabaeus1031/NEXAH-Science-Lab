## What this thread brought together

The thread joined five previously adjacent strands into one bounded working
grammar:

1. **Orientation and measurement** — groma, sightline, horizon, panorama,
   cadastral map and observer heading are treated as declared reference systems.
2. **Representation** — field, observer cut, record, map, plate, tile and visual
   projection are separated instead of treated as one object.
3. **Memory and experience** — memory is an ordered retained record; X-Perience
   is a conceptual update grammar relating retained state, observation and
   transformation.
4. **Joint, seam and hinge** — an interface may connect two states while
   preserving their difference. A seam introduced by measurement or
   discretization is not silently assigned to the source.
5. **Return and comparison** — every interpretation returns to its source,
   declared transform, residual and claim ceiling.

The resulting canonical working chain is:

```text
SOURCE
  -> REFERENCE SPACE
  -> OBSERVER CUT
  -> TRANSFORM
  -> RECORD
  -> ORDERED MEMORY
  -> JOINT / SEAM
  -> ORIENTATION
  -> MOTION
  -> RETURN / COMPARISON
```

The later closeout refined this into the compact method object:

```text
NEXAH = {SOURCE, CUTS / MAPS, TRANSITIONS, OVERLAPS, RESIDUAL, RETURN}
```

The residual is retained with provenance and support boundaries. It is neither
discarded to force closure nor promoted automatically to hidden structure.

The closeout also records self-knowledge as a recursive observer-dependent cut:
a situated record is not exhaustive identity with the changing source.
Specialist architectures retain their existing definitions and gates; they are
not added to the compact public method vocabulary.

## What was genuinely extended

### 1. Representation discipline

The Source Court method now distinguishes continuous field from declared
slices, plate stacks from flattened maps, and rotation from discretization.
Lines, tiles or cubes may be created by projection and sampling; they are not
therefore intrinsic source structures.

### 2. Observer-aware memory grammar

The thread added a compact working grammar:

```text
M_(t+1) = Update(M_t, R_t)
X_(t+1) = T_t(X_t, M_t, O_t)
```

This is a design and reasoning scaffold, not an admitted empirical law.

### 3. Topology-versus-identity test

The cadastral comparison yielded one portable methodological distinction:

```text
continuous visible edge chain
!= one parcel identity
!= one legal relation
!= one functional or physical network
```

A node-edge rendering is therefore useful for examining adjacency and turns,
but cannot by itself establish ownership, function, causation or a hidden band.
The local source material that motivated this distinction is now parked and is
not an active evidence object.

### 4. Visual morphology boundary

Amphora, tower, dome, groove, stream, ring, moon plate and sun plate remain
productive visual analogies. Their contribution is compositional and
explanatory. Morphological resemblance is not identity, provenance or mechanism.

### 5. Privacy and source-governance boundary

`HOMEBASE` is now an explicit source class:

```text
HOMEBASE = PRIVATE_ORIGIN_CONTEXT
HOMEBASE_ACTIVE_RESEARCH_OBJECT = NO
HOMEBASE_MEASUREMENT_GATE = NONE
HOMEBASE_PUBLICATION_OR_PROMOTION = NO
```

Private home, cadastral, building, address, coordinate and viewpoint material
may remain local provenance. It is excluded from new gates, scoring functions,
public examples and scientific promotion unless the Human Owner separately and
explicitly reopens a bounded use.

### 6. Method stewardship and outward path

The disclosure rule is now:

> Open in benefit. Testable in claims. Protected in the special recipe.
> Explicit in provenance.

Public claims must disclose enough method, input and evaluation detail to be
tested. A sealed reference implementation may be made available under
controlled review. Homebase and implementation know-how not needed to test a
public claim remain private. The full agreement and bounded release sequence
are recorded in
[`09_NEXAH_METHOD_STEWARDSHIP_AND_RELEASE_PATH.md`](09_NEXAH_METHOD_STEWARDSHIP_AND_RELEASE_PATH.md).
