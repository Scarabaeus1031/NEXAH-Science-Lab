## Evidence-bearing completed lines

Fidelity sequence:

1. a minimal controlled translation study;
2. a preregistered eight-family/four-configuration replication;
3. an independent-decoder fidelity study;
4. a cross-study synthesis and two literature/novelty audits.

All three study result files have recorded byte-identical local replays. In
this audit, 61 manifest-listed files and the two historical canonical source
objects were independently SHA-256 checked with zero mismatch. This establishes
artifact integrity and deterministic local replay history, not external
validity.

The broad invariance/discrimination relationship is established prior art.
The exact hierarchy did not replicate. The remaining candidate contribution is
a collision-aware, dual-axis evaluation package and its transparent negative
results.

valid held-out runs contained zero events and zero detections. It cannot support
an early-warning claim.
