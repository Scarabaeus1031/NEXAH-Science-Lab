# Candidate C — Adversarial research methodology

## Proposed object

A self-audit discipline combining strongest comparators, information/resource
parity, externally owned task definitions, preregistration, stop rules,
negative-result retention and claim reduction.

## Audit result

`NOT_READY_AS_A_METHODS_PAPER`

The audit sequence demonstrates sincere use of the rules: T02 was frozen,
ORION was left unconfirmed, Ledger claims were reduced, and the case-study
result was not promoted. This is credible process evidence. It is not evidence
that the combined method improves error detection, calibration or decisions
relative to established scientific practice.

Preregistration and Registered Reports already separate design from result and
reduce outcome-dependent publication incentives. Transparent data, code and
protocol availability are established reproducibility practices. Negative
results and fail-closed interpretation are scientifically valuable but not new
merely because NEXAH applies them consistently.
