# Candidate B — Representation-transition case study

## Object

Study-3 baseline versus one-step-delay decoding on 20 frozen synthetic
four-state transition systems.

## Evidence

- mapping accuracy: `1.0000 -> 0.7896`;
- dominant collision pairs: `0 -> 20`;
- edge recall: `1.0000 -> 0.6081`;
- C1-C6 exact preservation on delay comparisons: `0/20`.

The delay feature package retains the current observations and is invertible
relative to that source package. Normalization and subsequent deterministic
k-medoids alter decoder-relevant geometry; clustering then loses state and
transition identity. Thus source recoverability and downstream operational
fidelity are different scoped questions.

## Audit result

`SUPPLEMENT_ONLY`

The result, operator map, limitations and frozen artifacts are clear and
NEXAH-removable. The numerical finding is reproducible and useful as a worked
example. It does not establish a new principle: delay-embedding theorems do not
guarantee performance of a finite downstream decoder, and task/decoder
dependence is established. Alone, twenty designed systems under one decoder and
oracle alignment are too narrow for a substantive external research claim.
