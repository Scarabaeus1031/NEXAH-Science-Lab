## Three different operations that must remain distinct

### Two directed cuts

`R1` and `R2` are two bounded readings of one carrier. Neither duplicates the
carrier and neither is automatically the truth of the whole carrier.

### Antipode

An antipode is an involution only after an ambient carrier is declared. For a
geographic sphere it is the geodetic opposite. For an even `N`-clock:

```text
A_N(k) = k + N/2 (mod N),  A_N^2 = id.
```

For the 20-clock this gives `0 <-> 10` and `13 <-> 3`. A 90-degree
quarter-turn remains a different operator from a 180-degree antipode.

### Frame change

A frame change reprojects or recentres the same carrier. The visible map may
change while a persistent record address remains fixed. Return of the operator
does not imply that every one-step observable is invariant.
