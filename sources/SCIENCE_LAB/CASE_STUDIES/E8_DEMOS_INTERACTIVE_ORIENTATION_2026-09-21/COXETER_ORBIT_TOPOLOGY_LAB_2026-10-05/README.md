## Exact mathematical core

### Reflection operator

For a unit normal `alpha_i`, the mirror generator is

```text
r_i(v) = v - 2 <v, alpha_i> alpha_i.
```

It is an involution:

```text
r_i^2 = I.
```

Only the component in the normal direction changes sign. The component in the
mirror plane is retained. This is the exact source of the visible flip and the
shortest return word.

### Coxeter relations

For generators `r_i` and `r_j`, the product relation is

```text
(r_i r_j)^m_ij = I.
```

The two mirror hyperplanes meet at angle `pi / m_ij`; the product of the two
reflections is a rotation through `2 pi / m_ij`.

The current H3 system has Coxeter diagram `[5,3]`:

```text
(r_1 r_2)^5 = I
(r_2 r_3)^3 = I
(r_1 r_3)^2 = I
```

Therefore the paired motions return after rotations of `72 degrees`,
`120 degrees` and `180 degrees`, respectively.

### Generator words

A sequence such as `2-1-3` is a word in the generators. With the Lab's
left-to-right interaction convention it means: apply `r_2`, then `r_1`, then
`r_3`. Repeating, reducing and comparing such words produces paths, returns
and equal endpoints in the orbit graph.

The executable sequencer distinguishes two clocks:

```text

macro clock = completed word power and orbit phase
```

For the registered E8 word `C=mu_1...mu_8`, eight microsteps make one
macrostep and thirty macrosteps return. The eight 30-state rings are the
