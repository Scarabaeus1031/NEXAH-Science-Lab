## What the repeated circular pattern means

The common visual grammar is:

```text
one carrier
  + several labelled cyclic or curved families
  + a selected local operation or coordinate layer
  + a declared return criterion
  + a projection that makes all families jointly visible
```

This is stronger than a superficial resemblance between circles, but weaker than identity of the underlying mechanisms.

## The key structural distinction

The E8 rings and the Root-Zeta wheels are **orbit/phase decompositions**. The astrolabe image is primarily a **co-registration of coordinate foliations**: altitude curves, azimuth arcs, horizons, tropics and hour lines share a projection surface. Their crossings do not automatically denote state transitions.

Thus the visual analogy becomes operational only after every layer supplies:

```text
(carrier, operator, orbit or leaf, projection, return criterion, residual)
```

This six-part typed record is the reusable NEXAH result.

## What E8 teaches the wider architecture

E8 supplies a positive control in which all levels are known:

1. the carrier has exactly 240 roots;
2. local reflections generate a finite family;
3. a chosen global Coxeter composition has order 30;
4. the carrier decomposes into eight 30-cycles;
5. the Coxeter plane displays those cycles as rings;
6. metric and reflection invariants survive the representation change.

The circular image is therefore not the proof. It is the final readout of an already typed operator chain.

## What the other visuals contribute

- **Root-Zeta** contributes synchronization, drift and least-common-multiple return between independent cyclic counters.
- **Root-432** separates multiplicative scale return from angular modulo return. It is a constructed scale map, not a universal law.
- **Astrolabe** contributes the idea of one plate carrying several coordinate systems without collapsing them into one system.
- **AXIS08** contributes quotient/residual discipline: a visible reduced coordinate requires the omitted component to be recorded if reconstruction matters.

## New NEXAH formulation

The strongest synthesis is:

> A NEXAH circular field is not defined by its rings. It is defined by which operator moves which carrier, which cycles or coordinate leaves result, how they are projected onto one plate, and what counts as return while the residual remains recorded.

## Consequence for the standalone HTML

The next justified extension is not another decorative ring. It is a `Return Type` layer that lets the same instrument distinguish:

1. local involution (`mu_i^2`),
2. word/Coxeter periodicity (`C^h`),
3. multi-wheel synchronization (`gcd/lcm`),
4. angular residue (`mod 360 degrees`),
5. coordinate projection/co-registration (astrolabe),
6. quotient plus residual (AXIS08).

Each mode must keep its own carrier and return test visible. A shared circular display may compare the modes, but must not imply that they are one physical mechanism.
