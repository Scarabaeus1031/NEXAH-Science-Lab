Status: `LOCKED_BEFORE_EXECUTION`

CA-IDENT-04 is a corrective fusion audit, not another search over visual
representations. It retains the CA-IDENT-03 carrier, target, features and
deterministic 3-nearest-neighbour classifier. It replaces the ineffective
pre-standardization group scaling with explicit post-standardization view
balancing.

The training set expands to deterministic seeds `R00-R19`. The untouched
holdout is `R20-R24`; its results may not select weights or features.

Two questions are kept separate:

1. Does a balanced Shadow Memory + radial Fourier core improve over its best
   single view by at least `0.02` macro-F1 without reducing `transient` recall?
2. Does Cube magnitude then add at least another `0.02` macro-F1 without
   reducing `transient` recall?

The legacy 110-coordinate concatenation is retained only as a failure-control.
No resonance, biological, quantum or universal-law claim is in scope.
