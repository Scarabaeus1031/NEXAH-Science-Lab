## Result in one sentence

NEXAH has matured from a collection of resonant visual and numerical motifs
into an executable method for recording what a transformation preserves,
changes, merges, loses and can reconstruct across multiple typed carriers and
views.

## What is established

- A canonical inspection grammar:
  `Carrier -> State -> Operator -> View -> Fiber -> Return -> Residual -> Evidence`.
- Exact finite transformations, affine involutions, recurrences, rotations,
  quotient counts and full-data returns within declared domains.
- A bounded distinction between a structure and its representation.
- A multi-grid architecture rather than one universal grid.
- Exact square cardinalities including `7^2=49`, `8^2=64`, `9^2=81`,
  `11^2=121` and `111^2=12321`.
- The exact fold of `{0,...,100}` under `J(k)=100-k`:
  `51 = 49 interior pairs + boundary pair + fixed hinge`.
- A typed node/edge ledger in which every cross-grid relation declares what it
  preserves, loses and needs for return.
- Negative findings remain first-class results rather than being removed from
  the story.

## The strongest architectural synthesis

The reviewed corpus organizes a sequence from field to manifestation:

```text
FIELD -> GRID -> NUMBER -> PROJECTION -> TRANSITION -> PASCAL
      -> AXES -> COLOR -> OPERATORS -> APPLICATIONS
```

Grids are carriers or coordinate views inside this process. Relations between
grids are the scientific object. A repeated number, common center, nested frame
or visual resemblance is a candidate relation until an operator and return are
registered.

## What was rejected or bounded

- Life/E8 specificity and antipodal-successor claims failed their registered
  controls.
- Stable Prime recurrence advantage was not independently reproduced.
- `0.04 -> 0.02` remains post-hoc numerical concordance, not an identified law.
- `4.84 / 0.04 = 121` is exact arithmetic without a recovered selection rule.
- Rath, Euler, Ramanujan, PG88 and Ghostgrid names retain provenance value but
  do not transfer theorem, mechanism or physical meaning automatically.
- No universal carrier, operator, decoder or new physics is claimed.

## What the project can now do

NEXAH can compare heterogeneous instruments without erasing their differences.
It can expose projection collisions, quotient fibers, missing side information,
representation limits and exact or conditional returns. Navigator and Mission
Control provide orientation and custody; the source instruments remain the
result authorities.

## Remaining frontier

The next legitimate question is no longer whether the numbers look connected.
It is whether a declared path through several grids produces a stable,
out-of-sample and return-capable relation that outperforms alternative
mappings. That work is listed, but not opened, in the controlling backlog.
