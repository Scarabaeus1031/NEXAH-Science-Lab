## Canonical formal core

For a declared carrier `X`, state `x`, transform `T` and observation `O`:

`x ∈ X`, `x' = T(x)`, `y = O(x')`.

The observation fiber is

`E(y) = {x ∈ X : O(T(x)) = y}`, with `μ(y) = |E(y)|`.

- `μ=1`: identified within the declared model and available information;
- `μ>1`: equivalence only; independent information is required;
- undefined or unmeasured: no identity statement is licensed.

A return has the typed form `x_hat = R(y,c)`, where `c` records any extra
reference, phase, address or provenance needed by the return. The residual must
remain explicit.
