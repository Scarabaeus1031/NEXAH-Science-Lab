## Root Space: the green bridge

The clean mathematical carrier is a binary sign-state graph:

```text
Q3 = sign choices for sqrt(2), sqrt(3), sqrt(5)       -> 8 states
Q4 = Q3 plus the independent sqrt(7) sign coordinate -> 16 states
```

The three-coordinate carrier has 8 vertices and 12 edges. The four-coordinate
carrier has 16 vertices and 32 edges. In the displayed two-layer projection,
the eight green edges pair corresponding Q3 states across the fourth binary
coordinate.

Green can therefore be used as the release mnemonic for the **bridge or frame
binding channel**. It is not the complete frame by itself. A replayable frame
also declares the origin, ordered basis, scale, orientation, projection or
section operator and aperture.

At zero visible Gamma split, the two projected layers occupy the same eight
screen positions while the abstract carrier still contains sixteen states.
Changing the slider changes display separation, not the underlying state
count.
