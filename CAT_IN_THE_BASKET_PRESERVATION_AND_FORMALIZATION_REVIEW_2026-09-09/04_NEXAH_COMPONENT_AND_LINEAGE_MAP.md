## Lineage without promotion

The strongest reusable chain is:

    Human target/tolerances
      -> OLS record and selection basis
      -> feasible/reachable trajectory family
      -> observer projection plus loss/residual
      -> State / Relation / Return comparison
      -> Pareto trade-offs
      -> Human selection or STOP

This is a new composite specification over existing parts. It neither expands OLS nor changes ORION, THE EYE, NEXAHEDRON, a Science-Lab result or either predecessor.
