# Candidate Biological Operators

## Disposition

No biological primitive operator is admitted.

The three pilots are source-bounded orientation constructions over tertiary sources. They explicitly prohibit universal biological models, operator promotion, and mechanism claims. Their strongest contribution is a test set for existing semantic operators and missing record types.

## Testable hypotheses retained

These are research questions, not operator definitions:

1. Separating phase, state, event, structure, condition, and outcome reduces category errors in mitosis questions.
2. Separating sequence and spatial organization improves reconstruction of cellular-respiration relations.
3. Explicit scale and source boundaries reduce false equivalence in photosynthesis/respiration comparison.
4. Reader paths preserve more correct non-implications than a baseline summary.
5. Independent analysts reconstruct sufficiently similar typed relations from the same frozen source.

## Minimum experiments

### B1 — Independent reconstruction

- Freeze one primary or high-quality review source.
- Use at least three analysts who do not share the existing templates.
- Predefine state, transition, relation, boundary, and evidence fields.
- Measure agreement and preserve disagreements.
- Falsification: low agreement after type definitions indicates the proposed record model is not stable.

### B2 — Reader boundary test

- Randomize baseline versus orientation artifact.
- Ask readers to identify source scope, scale, causal non-implications, unresolved questions, and valid continuation paths.
- Report each outcome separately.
- Falsification: no material improvement or new systematic errors.

### B3 — Domain-mechanism firewall

- Ask domain experts to label which relations are observation, representation, functional analogy, mechanism, or causal claim.
- Compare against current artifacts.
- Falsification: operator language systematically promotes analogies into mechanisms.

## Admission result

Biology currently supplies validation cases, not new primitives. Revisit only if a domain-specific transformation or measurement has a distinct contract, independent evidence, and cannot be expressed as a specialization of OBSERVE, REPRESENT, COMPARE, TRANSFORM, VALIDATE, or RECORD.
