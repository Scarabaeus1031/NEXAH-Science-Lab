## OP-OBSERVE — OBSERVE

- **Definition:** Admit identifiable source material as observations while preserving source and epistemic status.
- **Purpose:** Establish what was noticed or captured before representation or inference.
- **Observable behaviour:** Produces source-linked observations; does not promote them to evidence, truth, causality, or outcome.
- **Input:** Declared source material with identifiable provenance and applicable context, time, evidence-class, and uncertainty declarations.
- **Output:** One or more observations linked to source, provenance, status, and applicable declarations.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Measurement or source-admission boundary.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Construction of an indexed observation record; not an estimator or truth function by itself.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Admit microscopy, assay, sequence, phenotype, or pathway-source observations without declaring mechanism.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Admit spectra, composition, pressure, temperature, phase, or reaction observations without declaring a reaction law.
- **Related visuals:** `APPLICATIONS/orientation_translation/pilots/photosynthesis_v1/OBSERVATION_RECORD.md`; `ARCHITECTURE/visuals/current/what-is-nexah-2026-07-16.png` as a dated observation snapshot.
- **Related books:** THE VISITOR'S GUIDE; GEOMETRIA NOVA; THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK; THE OPERATOR LIBRARY; THE ATLAS OF ATLASES.
- **Related experiments:** Photosynthesis Pilot 01; Cellular Respiration Pilot 02; Mitosis Pilot 03.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-REPRESENT — REPRESENT

- **Definition:** Construct a structured, analyzable representation from declared observations or data while preserving provenance, status, and the representation/reality distinction.
- **Purpose:** Make material inspectable without treating the constructed form as reality.
- **Observable behaviour:** Produces a typed representation with declared context and perspective; records loss and limitations where relevant.
- **Input:** Observations or data with source, provenance, and status.
- **Output:** A declared representation linked to inputs, type, perspective, provenance, and applicable status.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Model, map, graph, field reconstruction, table, or state-space encoding.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** A typed construction or partial map into a representation-specific carrier.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Phase map, compartment map, pathway representation, neighborhood graph, or multi-lens account.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Composition table, reaction network, phase diagram, atmospheric comparison surface, or spectral representation.
- **Related visuals:** `APPLICATIONS/orientation_translation/studies/morphologies_of_transition_01/EDITORIAL_WHITEBOARD.png`; IEEE geometry and state-space figures.
- **Related books:** GEOMETRIA NOVA; THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK; THE LANGUAGE ATLAS; THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY; LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related experiments:** All three Orientation Translation pilots; IEEE Geometry v1; Atlas reconstruction experiments.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-COMPARE — COMPARE

- **Definition:** Identify differences, agreements, or mismatches among declared compatible items relative to a declared basis.
- **Purpose:** Make non-equivalence inspectable without creating causality, preference, validation, or identity.
- **Observable behaviour:** Preserves item identity, comparison basis, asymmetry, incompatible cases, evidence, and uncertainty.
- **Input:** Two or more declared items plus a declared comparison basis and compatibility conditions.
- **Output:** Comparison findings linked to items, basis, evidence, provenance, and uncertainty.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Controlled contrast, ablation comparison, cross-case evaluation, or model/data mismatch analysis.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Evaluation of a declared relation, distance, divergence, predicate, or invariant on compatible inputs.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Compare phases, pathways, organisms, compartments, or source-bounded process representations without asserting homology or mechanism.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Compare compositions, phases, reaction conditions, planets, spectra, or process roles without composition-to-function shortcuts.
- **Related visuals:** `APPLICATIONS/orientation_translation/visualizations/solar_lunar_carbon_orientation_cartography_01/EVIDENCE_MAP.png`; `APPLICATIONS/orientation_translation/pilots/photosynthesis_v1/COMPARISON_MATRIX.md`.
- **Related books:** THE LANGUAGE BOOK; THE LANGUAGE ATLAS; THE ATLAS OF ATLASES; LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related experiments:** Photosynthesis–Cellular Respiration Comparison 01; Planetary Chemistry Atlas 01; EXP-44B Atlas–Koopman cross-validation.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-ORIENT — ORIENT

- **Definition:** Produce situated understanding from declared observations, representation, context, perspective, position or focus, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and limitations.
- **Purpose:** State what is supported, unsupported, blocked, uncertain, or navigable before recommendation or action.
- **Observable behaviour:** Returns a bounded orientation report and preserves disagreements and non-implications.
- **Input:** Declared observations, representation, comparisons, context, perspective, focus, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and limits.
- **Output:** Supported findings, unsupported conclusions, disagreements, uncertainty, limitations, and applicable declarations.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Contextual evaluation of a represented system and a specific question.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** A partial evaluation `O(N,q) -> Report`, not a universal coordinate or optimizer.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Situate a reader or analyst among mechanisms, scales, stages, variants, and unknowns.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Situate composition and process claims within world, pressure, temperature, scale, and evidence boundaries.
- **Related visuals:** `ARCHITECTURE/visuals/current/what-is-nexah-2026-07-16.png`; the three pilot Orientation Maps.
- **Related books:** All ten canonical pilot Works; especially THE VISITOR'S GUIDE, THE LANGUAGE BOOK, THE ATLAS OF ATLASES, and LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related experiments:** Network Orientation; IEEE Geometry orientation briefs; Orientation Translation pilots.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-EXPLAIN — EXPLAIN

- **Definition:** Communicate structured findings while preserving evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, unsupported conclusions, and limitations.
- **Purpose:** Produce a readable account without raising epistemic or authority status.
- **Observable behaviour:** Changes presentation, not scientific status; known uncertainty and negative findings remain visible.
- **Input:** Findings or orientation plus evidence, provenance, uncertainty, disagreements, unsupported conclusions, limits, and applicable declarations.
- **Output:** An explanation retaining the status and boundaries of its source findings.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Reporting layer for reproducible, inspectable claims.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Status-preserving serialization or view over an orientation result.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Reader-facing explanation of a pathway or process that retains organism, scale, and source limits.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Explanation of compositional and process differences that retains retrieval, model, and measurement uncertainty.
- **Related visuals:** Pilot Reader Mode and Explain Mode artifacts; evidence-boundary figures under IEEE Geometry.
- **Related books:** THE VISITOR'S GUIDE; THE LANGUAGE BOOK; THE OPERATOR LIBRARY; THE ATLAS OF ATLASES.
- **Related experiments:** Photosynthesis Reader Mode evaluation proposal; Orientation Operations 01.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-SELECT — SELECT

- **Definition:** Choose one or more declared alternatives under a declared basis or constraint without implying recommendation, authority, execution, or optimality.
- **Purpose:** Make a bounded path or alternative selection inspectable.
- **Observable behaviour:** Reports selected and relevant unselected alternatives, basis, constraints, evidence, and uncertainty.
- **Input:** Declared alternatives, selection basis or constraint, and the representation containing them.
- **Output:** Selected alternative set with basis, constraints, declarations, provenance, and uncertainty.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Explicit sampling, route choice, feature selection, or scenario selection under a recorded rule.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Set-valued selection function over declared alternatives; not necessarily optimization.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** An analyst selects a comparison path; a biological branch is not automatically a SELECT invocation.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** An analyst selects species, worlds, conditions, or pathways for comparison; reaction selectivity is a different domain claim.
- **Related visuals:** Reading-path and alternative-route diagrams in the Library and Orientation Translation pilots.
- **Related books:** THE VISITOR'S GUIDE; THE LANGUAGE ATLAS; THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY; THE ATLAS OF ATLASES; LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related experiments:** Library reading-path queries; Network Orientation route enumeration. The three biology pilots intentionally preserve alternatives without invoking OP-SELECT.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-TRANSFORM — TRANSFORM

- **Definition:** Change a declared form or state into another declared form or state while preserving input/output identity, order, constraints, provenance, and status.
- **Purpose:** Represent an intentional or declared transformation without implying improvement, validation, execution success, or admitted outcome.
- **Observable behaviour:** Produces a distinct resulting form or state linked to its input and transformation description.
- **Input:** Identified form or state, transformation description, identity rule, time/order semantics, and applicable constraint.
- **Output:** Resulting form or state with input link, declarations, provenance, evidence, uncertainty, and constraints.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** A scoped data, model, state, or representation transformation.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Typed map, possibly partial or lossy, with declared domain and preserved metadata.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** State or form change such as a phase transition, conformational change, or pathway step only when the source and result are explicitly represented.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Reaction, phase change, photochemical conversion, condensation, or transport transformation under declared conditions.
- **Related visuals:** Fold, Projection, Transition, and form-change motifs in THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK and THE LANGUAGE ATLAS.
- **Related books:** GEOMETRIA NOVA; THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK; THE LANGUAGE BOOK; THE LANGUAGE ATLAS; FIELD ATLAS I — WATER; THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY; THE OPERATOR LIBRARY; THE ATLAS OF ATLASES.
- **Related experiments:** Representation maps in the Mathematical Framework Note; reconstruction and projection steps in IEEE Geometry.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-VALIDATE — VALIDATE

- **Definition:** Test a declared subject against declared criteria and evidence and assign a scoped validation status.
- **Purpose:** Separate testing from observation, comparison, approval, and outcome admission.
- **Observable behaviour:** Returns status with subject, criteria, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and scope; can return insufficiency or failure.
- **Input:** Claim, model, change, or candidate outcome; criteria; evidence with provenance and status.
- **Output:** Scoped validation status linked to subject, criteria, evidence, uncertainty, and declarations.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Reproducibility, holdout, ablation, benchmark, falsification, or conformance test.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Predicate or graded test evaluated on a declared domain with explicit insufficiency behavior.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Source-fidelity, assay, model-fit, or reader test under predeclared criteria; not the biological checkpoint mechanism itself.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Test a compositional, kinetic, phase, or retrieval claim against measurements and criteria.
- **Related visuals:** `APPLICATIONS/power_systems/ieee_geometry_v1/showcase/figures/04-evidence-boundary.png`; validation dashboards.
- **Related books:** THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY; LIBRARYBOOK. No Library Work defines the OLS contract.
- **Related experiments:** Demonstrator null baseline; IEEE Geometry v1; memory holdout tests; power-system validation campaigns.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-RECORD — RECORD

- **Definition:** Store declared observations or admitted outcomes with identity, time, provenance, evidence, admission, and uncertainty status, without upgrading them.
- **Purpose:** Preserve reconstructable history and status.
- **Observable behaviour:** Creates an immutable or append-only reference while retaining source and epistemic boundaries.
- **Input:** Observation or admitted outcome with identity, time, provenance, evidence, uncertainty, and admission status.
- **Output:** Record reference or recorded state preserving the input material and all relevant status.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Provenance-preserving research record or outcome episode.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Append-only event insertion into an indexed history; not learning by itself.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Store assay, image, sequence, phenotype, or intervention outcomes without inferring biological memory.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Store run conditions, spectra, products, yields, and uncertainty without treating persistence as validation.
- **Related visuals:** Memory and trace motifs in THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK, THE LANGUAGE ATLAS, and LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related books:** THE OPERATOR'S HANDBOOK; THE LANGUAGE ATLAS; LIBRARYBOOK.
- **Related experiments:** State–Report–Outcome Episode store; memory-generalization v1/v2; validation manifests and replay records.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## OP-APPROVE — APPROVE

- **Definition:** Assign an approved governed status to a declared proposal or change within a declared authority scope.
- **Purpose:** Make human or institutional governance explicit and separate from scientific truth or validation.
- **Observable behaviour:** Changes governed status only when actor/role, target, basis, prior status, scope, and time are valid.
- **Input:** Proposal or change, current status, approval basis, authority record, target, and scope.
- **Output:** Approved governed status linked to authority, scope, target, basis, provenance, and declarations.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Approval of a protocol, artifact, release, or Registry change; never empirical confirmation.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Guarded state transition in a governance state machine.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Protocol, dataset, or interpretation approval; not biological fitness or selection.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Procedure, data release, or safety-review approval; not chemical validity.
- **Related visuals:** Editorial governance and Library workflow diagrams.
- **Related books:** No visual Work owns this semantic contract; Library publication state is a governed application.
- **Related experiments:** Editorial Writer dry-run/apply gates; Library Registry review; OLS release verification.
- **Current maturity:** FOUNDATION.

## RESEARCH-GATE-INSTABILITY — Gate Instability Measure

- **Definition:** A normalized local instability score `G(x) = (1-rho_hat)(1-C_hat)(1-R_hat)` combining low occupancy density, low flow–density alignment, and low rotational structure.
- **Purpose:** Identify candidate regions of weak local structural support in a reconstructed flow field.
- **Observable behaviour:** High values mark local-instability regions. High values do not guarantee transitions, and transitions can occur without strong peaks.
- **Input:** Reconstructed flow field, trajectory-density estimate, coherence measure, rotation measure, normalization choices, and smoothing parameters.
- **Output:** A scalar instability field over the declared representation.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Detector for local structural instability; not a transition detector.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Composite scalar functional over estimated local field quantities. KDE and normalization make it representation- and parameter-dependent.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Candidate detector on sufficiently sampled biological trajectories; currently unvalidated in biological systems.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Candidate detector on reaction, phase, or transport trajectories; currently unvalidated in chemical systems.
- **Related visuals:** `PROTO_CORE/NEXAH_DEMONSTRATOR/visuals/unified/nexah_unified_gate_operator_v25.png`; transition-geometry kernel mask.
- **Related books:** FIELD ATLAS I — WATER; THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY; LIBRARYBOOK as visual context only.
- **Related experiments:** Lorenz, Rössler, and Kuramoto Demonstrator runs; Gate ablations; power-system gate experiments.
- **Current maturity:** WORKING.

## RESEARCH-JANUS-DCO — Janus Directional Coherence Measure

- **Definition:** Compare forward and backward local differences around a centered ordered trajectory position to measure directional alignment or mismatch.
- **Purpose:** Expose local directional coherence and asymmetry without assuming reversibility or inverse transitions.
- **Observable behaviour:** Produces an alignment or mismatch value from at least three ordered samples; low coherence is hypothesized to occur near some transition regions.
- **Input:** Ordered local samples or forward/backward field estimates, representation, parameter semantics, neighborhood rule, and overlap metric.
- **Output:** Local directional-coherence or mismatch field with provenance and insufficiency behavior.
- **Possible scientific interpretation:** Local-flow diagnostic for directional organization.
- **Possible mathematical interpretation:** Cosine, dot-product, projection, or kernel comparison of adjacent displacement estimates. It is not the inverse of an edge.
- **Possible biological interpretation:** Candidate measure for ordered cell-state, population, or transport trajectories; no biological validation exists.
- **Possible chemical interpretation:** Candidate measure for reaction-coordinate, phase, or transport trajectories; no chemical validation exists.
- **Related visuals:** JANUS research outputs and directional-coherence diagrams; do not substitute the visual Janus motif.
- **Related books:** THE CARTOGRAPHY LABORATORY and LIBRARYBOOK as provenance; the Registry Janus concept is not this measure.
- **Related experiments:** JANUS Operator experimental series; local IEEE geometry direction-change and curvature measurements.
- **Current maturity:** SPECULATIVE.

## Exclusions

The following remain important but are not retained as primitive operators:

- observer, relation, state, transition, difference, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, orientation, context, perspective, position, scale, field, and boundary are concepts, declarations, records, or carriers;
- Circle, Aperture, Needle, Bridge, Projection, Fold, Gradient, Navigation, and Janus are controlled visual concepts or specializations;
- the Atlas chain Q–S–P–J–H–N is a conceptual pipeline;
- the six IEEE Geometry measurements are a frozen application measurement suite;
- Work-level Moves remain descriptive, non-canonical vocabulary.
