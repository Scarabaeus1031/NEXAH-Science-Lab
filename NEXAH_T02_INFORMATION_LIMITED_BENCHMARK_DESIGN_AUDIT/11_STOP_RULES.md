# Stop Rules

Stop the T02 line before preregistration if any occurs:

1. no independent external authority supplies the decision threshold and costs;
2. B8 can compute truth directly from public inputs rather than estimate it;
3. information/resource parity cannot be audited exactly;
4. NEXAH requires privileged correspondence, task semantics or preprocessing;
5. the process-family distribution is chosen after evaluating methods;
6. no meaningful finite-budget loss difference is statistically identifiable;
7. NEXAH is only a deterministic report-format transformation of B8;
8. `UNKNOWN`, equivalence or baseline victory cannot be represented;
9. scorer truth is not independently identifiable;
10. the intended claim exceeds finite-sample benchmark performance.

Passing this design audit does not authorize implementation. The next permitted
step is only an owner-independent cost/task contract and a separate prospective
v3 preregistration design review.
