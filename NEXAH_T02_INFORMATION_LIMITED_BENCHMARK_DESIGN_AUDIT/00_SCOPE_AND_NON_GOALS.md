# Scope and Non-goals

This is a design audit only. It neither implements nor runs an experiment and it
does not freeze or preregister a protocol. No `results/` directory or hash
manifest belongs here.

The audit asks whether a fair information-limited comparison remains after exact
T02-v2 collapsed to a direct plug-in estimator. It does not try to rescue a NEXAH
claim. `NO_ADVANTAGE`, equivalence, baseline dominance and `UNKNOWN` are valid.

Excluded claims: new mathematics/information theory, a universal translation
law, new invariant, physical or power-grid validity, prediction, early warning,
risk, causality, control, ORION validity or general NEXAH superiority.

The original T02, its adversarial review, the rejected T02-v2 draft and canonical
NEXAH are immutable authorities. The prior literature audit already classifies
task-relative sufficiency, rate–distortion, Markov aggregation, graph expressivity
and bisimulation as established frameworks; this audit assumes no novelty from
renaming them.
