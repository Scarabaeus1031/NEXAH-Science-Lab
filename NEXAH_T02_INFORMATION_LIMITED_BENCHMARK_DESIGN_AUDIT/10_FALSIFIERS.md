# Falsifiers

A future v3 must include at least:

- F1 `DIRECT_ESTIMATOR_DOMINATES`: B8 lower/equal loss within the frozen practical
  margin → no incremental value.
- F2 `NEXAH_REEXPRESSION`: decisions reconstructible from B8 outputs → redundant.
- F3 `ABLATION_EQUAL`: any simpler NEXAH ablation matches complete method → claimed
  component adds nothing.
- F4 `ASYMMETRIC_INFORMATION`: any unequal task/correspondence/label access →
  protocol invalid.
- F5 `COST_SENSITIVITY`: conclusion changes across externally plausible frozen
  cost scenarios without a primary owner-selected contract → inconclusive.
- F6 `UNKNOWN_DOMINATES`: improvement comes only from excessive abstention when
  charged common cost → no advantage.
- F7 `RARE_STRATUM_OVERFIT`: training collision strata do not generalize to
  held-out process families → no support.
- F8 `RESOURCE_ACCOUNTING_FAILURE`: unequal samples, queries, compute or tuning →
  invalid.
- F9 `INFINITE_DATA_CLAIM`: finite-budget result generalized as new information or
  asymptotic dominance → prohibited interpretation.
- F10 `NO_POWER`: design precision cannot distinguish the minimum meaningful loss
  difference → do not run.
- F11 `UNIDENTIFIABLE_TRUTH`: scorer cannot establish population risk/action under
  the sealed law → invalid.
- F12 `STANDARD_THEORY_SUFFICIENT`: established task-risk, lumpability,
  bisimulation or information estimator yields same/lower loss and complexity →
  operational synthesis unsupported.

Null/equivalence is primary evidence. No falsifier may be overridden by a visually
interesting collision ledger.
