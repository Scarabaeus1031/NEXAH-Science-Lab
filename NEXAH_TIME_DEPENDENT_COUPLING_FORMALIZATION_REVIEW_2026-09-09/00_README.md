## Result

The candidate is not a new physical law or quaternion operation. Its mechanical content is a conventional, falsifiable control hypothesis over a constrained multibody system with time-varying compliant joints:

> At fixed model, boundary conditions, initial state and accounted input work, do coupling and proximal-braking schedules alter distal peak speed?

No existing repository operator or test covers that question end to end. Existing semantic, transformation, counter-rotation, release-mode, provenance and validation components can discipline the experiment, but they do not supply its multibody state, torques, constraints, energy ledger or comparison. A small specification is therefore justified; implementation is not authorized.
