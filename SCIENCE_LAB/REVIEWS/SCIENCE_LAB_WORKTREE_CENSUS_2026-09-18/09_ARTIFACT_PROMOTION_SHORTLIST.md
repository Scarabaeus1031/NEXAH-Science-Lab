# HTML / GLB Promotion Shortlist

Status: `CURATION SHORTLIST / NOT ADOPTION`

## What the artifacts are

The 14 HTML files and one GLB are factual repository artifacts in the narrow
sense that they are byte-identifiable, hashable, inspectable and executable or
renderable. They preserve concrete information in code, embedded data,
geometry, labels, state transitions and animations.

They are not automatically scientific facts. Their claim authority remains
that of the package, manifest and gate that surrounds them. Rendering is not
validation, and a richer variant is not automatically the canonical one.

## Missing promotion requirements

Before copying any Tier-A artifact into NEXAHEDRON or an outreach surface:

1. retain the exact Science Lab SHA-256 and source path;
2. add title, role, package, date, claim ceiling and dependency metadata;
3. record whether external CDN/font access is required;
4. test interaction or animation in the intended viewer;
5. label embedded or declared data separately from computed data;
6. keep the Science Lab copy immutable;
7. never make the curated copy the evidence authority.

## GLB contents recorded

The GLB is glTF 2.0 and contains 12 nodes, 9 meshes, 9 materials, 11
animations and 7 embedded PNG images. Named components include local,
relation and return layers, a neon backplane, a hinge, a residual bound and a
TQR root. Its animation set covers front/back motion, grid rotation,
local-to-antipode motion, layer explode/collapse and solos, backplane reveal,
return trace and an ILAU cycle.

The generator metadata names `NEXAH TQR Pancake GLB Builder 1.0`, but the
builder source is absent. The GLB is therefore a valuable sealed demonstrator
asset, not the canonical model implementation.
