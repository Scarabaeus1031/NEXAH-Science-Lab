# Invariant / Difference / Residual / Ambiguity Ledger

| Term | Required basis | Meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| invariant assessment | compatible objects, declared transform/comparison rule, property | property preserved under the stated rule | universal invariance |
| difference | typed comparator and compatible representation/normalization | observed or computed non-equality | error, residual or importance |
| residual | model/comparator, target, fit/reconstruction and remainder rule | remainder after a declared account | unexplained physical cause |
| ambiguity | alternatives or non-identifiability plus cause/status | more than one allowed interpretation/result | missing data only |
| abstention | decision rule and insufficient/forbidden basis | no authorized selection | nonexistence or failure |
| reconstruction | evidence, rule, assumptions and output | newly produced representation/result | original state/event/history |
