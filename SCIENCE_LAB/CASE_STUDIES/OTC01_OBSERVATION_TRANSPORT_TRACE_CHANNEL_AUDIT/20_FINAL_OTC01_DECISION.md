# Final OTC-01 Decision

## Judgment

The frozen architecture can distinguish what generated a signal, what the signal
traversed, what the medium and boundaries changed, what the instrument and sensor
did, what was integrated or clipped, what became readout, and what may be inferred
back about the source. It does so only when those stages are composed explicitly
and their provenance, configuration, uncertainty and losses are retained.

The candle, Schlieren, temporal/dynamic, astronomy and ambiguity controls all
survive. A named observation-channel record and loss ledger improve reviewability,
but add no missing primitive. The correct class is therefore C, not A: the
existing pieces suffice, while the complete channel is not already one canonical
record. No RID gap or activation follows.
