# Boundary and Nonclaims

OTC-01 establishes only a documentary typing result under standard physical
controls.

It does **not** claim:

- a new theory of light, observation, transport or information;
- a universal physical mechanism across candle, Schlieren and astronomy;
- that any trace uniquely reconstructs a source or path;
- that a Schlieren image is a direct density field;
- that an observability window is a truth window;
- that black is absence or white is maximum information;
- a new OLS primitive, RID schema, operator or ontology;
- validation of NEXAH as science or technology;
- ORION capability, Rust implementation or prototype readiness;
- historical, phonetic, glyph or etymological evidence.

No predecessor was edited or reopened. No external experiment was run. Sources
were consulted only for the specified controls. All historical expressions remain
quarantined.
