| 9 | Schlieren gradient-to-contrast conversion | Medium/gradient/deflection/intensity | PASS |

All controls are documentary/analytical. No new experiment was run. “PASS” means
the existing type composition can represent the distinction, not that a new
physical result or NEXAH validation was obtained.
