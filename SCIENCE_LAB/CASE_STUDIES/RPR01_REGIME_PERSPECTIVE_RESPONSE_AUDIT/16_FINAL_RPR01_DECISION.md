# Final RPR-01 Decision

## Result

The five phenomena require separate typing:

```text
VIEW_CHANGE
≠ FRAME_CHANGE
≠ REGIME_CHANGE
≠ STATE_RESPONSE
≠ REALIZED_FORM_OR_BEHAVIOR_CHANGE
```

The separation survives the camera, embedding, candle, biology, ferrofluid and clock controls. The domains share a useful record structure but not a common physical mechanism.

OLS already supplies context, perspective, position, state, transition, relation, identity/time declarations and provenance. RID supplies entity/revision references, frames, constraints, states, events, comparisons, history and tagged views. Together they can represent a regime as an explicit composition and a response as a state transition/comparison under history.

A dedicated contextual-role or regime record would improve interface clarity, but it adds no missing primitive and is not required to represent the controls. Accordingly no RID schema gap is found.

Historical P/R/T/Q°, TAO/THETA, CON-DAO, IOTA/YUGO and Lippe/Klippe labels contribute no evidence to this result. The neutral distinction stands without them.
