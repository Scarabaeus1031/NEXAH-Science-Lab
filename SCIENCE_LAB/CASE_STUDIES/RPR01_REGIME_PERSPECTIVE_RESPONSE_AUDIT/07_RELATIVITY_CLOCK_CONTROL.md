# Relativity / Clock Control

NIST compared highly similar optical clocks after changing height or ion motion and measured the relativistic frequency differences predicted for different gravitational potentials and speeds.

Typed distinction:

- a coordinate/reference-frame change can re-express one physical history;
- a camera/view change can change only its presentation;
- different motion or gravitational conditions define different physical worldlines/conditions;
- clocks following those conditions can accumulate different proper time;
- the later readout compares physical clock outcomes, not a perspective illusion.

`REFERENCE_FRAME ≠ CAMERA_VIEW` and `FRAME_CHANGE ≠ PHYSICAL_TRAJECTORY_OR_REGIME_CHANGE`. Observer grammar is unnecessary to establish this standard physics distinction.
