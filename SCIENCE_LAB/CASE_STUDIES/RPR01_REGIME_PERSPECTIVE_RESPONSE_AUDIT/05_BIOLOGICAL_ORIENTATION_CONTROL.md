# Biological Orientation Control

## Fish

Spaceflight/parabolic-flight studies distinguish vestibular input, visual input, behavior and adaptation. Goldfish rolling/looping changed during flight and after return; medaka responses varied by strain and visual conditions. These results do not support “orientationless fish,” a universal species response, or necessary permanent morphology change.

## Plants

ISS plant studies show that removing the dominant gravity vector can reveal or alter responses to light, moisture and chemical cues. Arabidopsis roots retained directional responses to light, while other work separated developmental auxin patterns from gravity response. This is cue weighting and growth response, not conscious orientation.
