# Ferrofluid Control

A bounded Rosensweig-instability example has a ferrofluid free surface under a magnetic field normal to the surface. Magnetic forcing destabilizes the flat interface; gravity and interfacial tension stabilize it. Above a documented threshold, a peak pattern can appear. Fluid magnetization, density contrast, surface tension, field strength/gradient, layer depth, container geometry and gravity belong in the regime/constraint record.

```text
same material system
+ changed magnetic/boundary conditions
→ possibly negligible deformation
→ possibly continuous deformation
→ possibly thresholded qualitative morphology change
```

The threshold belongs to this specified stability problem. It does not justify treating every ferrofluid response, every regime change, or every cross-domain morphology change as a bifurcation.
