# Boundary and Nonclaims

RPR-01 does not claim:

- one cross-domain physical mechanism or law;
- that gravity alone determines flame, behavior, morphology or time;
- that microgravity means zero gravity;
- that all regime changes are thresholds or bifurcations;
- that frame changes are physical changes;
- that organisms are orientationless in microgravity or after return;
- that adaptation implies morphology;
- that any historical symbol names a recovered type;
- a new OLS primitive, RID schema change, operator, ontology or NEXAH architecture;
- ORION, Rust, implementation, research, experiment or successor activation.

External controls are used only to test the type distinctions. No result is generalized beyond its declared source and domain.
