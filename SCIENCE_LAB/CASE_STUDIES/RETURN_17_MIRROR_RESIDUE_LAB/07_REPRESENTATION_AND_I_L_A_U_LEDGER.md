# Representation and I–L–A–U Ledger

## Representation audit

| Representation | Retained | Lost | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number line | signed difference and magnitude | modular equivalence unless stated | origin and positive direction convention |
| Modular circle | residue class and chosen orientation | completed cycle count (`-33` and `+1` coincide mod 17) | modulus, seam, clockwise/counter-clockwise convention |
| Ordered sequence | item order and declared traversal | arithmetic distance unless labels/operators exist | start point and temporal indexing convention |
| Unordered set | membership | subtraction order, sign, traversal, mirror direction | no natural return operation |
| Directed graph | source/target and directed relation | numeric difference unless edge weight exists | edge semantics and chosen orientation |
| Undirected graph | adjacency | `+1` versus `-1`, source/target distinction | symmetric relation not present in subtraction |
| Image-space markers | displayed labels, pixels, local positions | calculation and generation process without source code | camera, projection, annotation, sequence filenames |

Arithmetic exactness is preserved on the modular circle only after modulus and orientation are introduced. “Return,” “mirror,” and “crown” are not representation-invariant meanings.

## I–L–A–U

- **I — RETAINED:** exact integer identities; declared subtraction order; frozen modulus/signed-residue convention; visually verified archive occurrences; complete neighbor/modulus enumerations; deterministic controls and replay.
- **L — LOST:** historical generation process for most PNGs; author intention; continuous temporal context; code behind historical figures; whether output numbers were semantically selected or merely export indices.
- **A — INTRODUCED:** selection of 41 and 74 as operands; modulus range 2–40; neighbor window; P2 archive-number corpus; random generator and seed; signed half-cycle tie; modular-circle/cycle/graph representations.
- **U — UNRESOLVED:** any independent historical reason to pair 41 and 74; historical meaning of return; repeated-origin versus external-anchor +1; relation to historical crown imagery.

## Authority result

The ledger preserves provenance and uncertainty. It does not convert a filename sequence, an annotated visual number, or a retrospective hypothesis into an executable historical operator.
