# 16 — MAM / JJA / SON / DJF Partition Control

H4 documents one climate-analysis convention:

```text
MAM = March, April, May
JJA = June, July, August
SON = September, October, November
DJF = December, January, February
```

This is four groups of three months: `12 = 4*3`. It is a calendar grouping with domain-specific seasonal meaning, not a consequence of factorization alone.

Alternative arithmetic factorizations exist:

```text
12 = 3*4 = 2*6 = 6*2
```

They do not automatically define equivalent seasons. Choosing a different start month rotates group membership; DJF also crosses the Gregorian year boundary. Labels and seasonal interpretations depend on convention, hemisphere, application and chosen cut.
