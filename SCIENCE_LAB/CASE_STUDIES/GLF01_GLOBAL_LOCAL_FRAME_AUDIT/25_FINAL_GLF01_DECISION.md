# 25 — Final GLF-01 Decision

## Primary result

`B_STANDARD_MATHEMATICS_SUPPORTS_PARTIAL_GLOBAL_LOCAL_TRANSFER_WITH_EXPLICIT_CUT_FRAME_DEPENDENCE`

Stable mathematical content survives only under specified transformations. A point is not its coordinate; a circle is not an ordinary interval; a seam is not necessarily a removed point; and a local tangent/frame is not the global curve. Regular-polygon counts, parity, angles and dihedral symmetry survive rotations and relabelings, while first-vertex and clock-position patterns do not. Calendar name/ordinal pairings are historically contingent, and seasonal groupings depend on the selected cut and partition.

The object/representation schema is useful as a ledger but too weak to replace domain-specific topology, differential geometry, Euclidean geometry, group theory or calendar history. No new mathematical operator or NEXAH structure is warranted.
