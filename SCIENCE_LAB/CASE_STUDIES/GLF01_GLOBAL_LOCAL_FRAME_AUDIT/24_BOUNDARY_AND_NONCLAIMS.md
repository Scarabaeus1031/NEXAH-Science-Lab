## Established within GLF-01

- a bare circle has no intrinsic start;
- puncture, seam and endpoint quotient are different constructions;
- differentiability gives local first-order linearization, not global identity;
- regular-polygon counts, angles, parity and symmetry have standard invariant/covariant behavior;
- fixed-circumradius polygons converge to the circle in specified quantities while remaining non-circular at finite `n`;
- month-name/ordinal mismatches are historical traces, not hidden mathematics;
- three-month seasonal groups are convention- and cut-dependent.
