# 01 — Source and Claim Ledger

| ID | Source | Bounded claim used | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | [MIT — Topics in Topology](https://math.mit.edu/~pieloch/teaching/shp-notes/shp-topics-in-topology-fall-2019-Dec-14.pdf) | The quotient of `[0,1]` obtained by identifying `0` and `1` is homeomorphic to the circle. | Does not identify the ordinary interval itself with `S^1`. |
| M2 | [MIT — Lurie, Lecture 1](https://math.mit.edu/~lurie/937notes/937Lecture1.pdf) | `S^1` may be represented as the quotient `R/Z`; a base point is additional structure used by base-point-preserving maps. | Quotient representation is not intrinsic coordinate zero. |
| M3 | [Stanford — Linear Approximation](https://math.stanford.edu/~jmadnick/T19.pdf) | Differentiability at a point gives the tangent-line linearization `f(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h+o(h)`. | Local approximation does not establish global equality. |
| M4 | [MIT — Moving Frames](https://math.mit.edu/~mckernan/Teaching/10-11/Autumn/18.022/l_16.pdf) | A regular smooth curve admits tangent/normal/binormal data under the stated hypotheses; Frenet equations describe their change. | A Frenet frame is local curve data in Euclidean space, not world structure. |
| M5 | [University of Chicago — dihedral groups](https://math.uchicago.edu/~margalit/repthy/Baker%2C%20Representations%20of%20Finite%20Groups.pdf) | The symmetry group of a regular `n`-gon is dihedral, generated by an order-`n` rotation and a reflection. | Group notation conventions vary; this audit records `n` rotations plus `n` reflections. |
| M6 | [Wolfram MathWorld — Regular Polygon](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/RegularPolygon.html) | Standard side, inradius, circumradius, area and angle formulas; fixed-radius polygons approach the disk in selected quantities. | Used as a reference compilation, not for pattern search. |
| H1 | [Académie française — Des Mois](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/des-mois) | September–December derive from Latin seven–ten while now denoting months 9–12; this is a historical trace of Roman calendar history. | Etymology is not present mathematical structure. |
| H2 | [The National Archives (UK) — Dating Latin Documents](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/latin/reference/dating-latin-documents/) | Source-bound Latin month forms for September, October, November and December. | Supports names, not symbolic number relations. |
| H3 | [Metropolitan Museum of Art — January / Janus](https://blog.metmuseum.org/cloistersgardens/2010/01/15/the-january-feast/top-detail_250/) | Documents the cultural association of January with Janus and beginnings/endings in the described object context. | Cultural iconography is not a property of cyclic mathematics. |
| H4 | [NOAA — Regional Climate Trends and Scenarios](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/56802/noaa_56802_DS1.pdf) | Uses DJF, MAM, JJA and SON as four named three-month seasons in a specific climate-analysis convention. | Grouping is hemisphere/domain/convention dependent, not universal astronomy. |

## Evidence classes

- `STANDARD_MATHEMATICS`: M1–M6 and explicit derivations from their standard definitions.
- `HISTORICAL_FACT`: H1–H3.
- `CALENDAR/CLIMATE_CONVENTION`: H4.
- `HUMAN_ANNOTATION`: quarantined expressions only.
- `NEXAH_EXPRESSION`: evaluated only after the mathematics, with no evidential force.
