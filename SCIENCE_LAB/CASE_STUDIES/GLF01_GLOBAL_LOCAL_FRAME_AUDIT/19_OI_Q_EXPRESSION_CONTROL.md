# 19 — O|I / Q° Expression Control

The standard mathematics was completed without these glyphs.

## `O|I`

If `O` is explicitly declared to depict a cycle and `|` or `I` a chosen seam/opened interval representation, `O|I` can visually remind a reader of “cyclic object plus chosen cut.” It does not specify whether a point is removed, a seam is chosen, or endpoints are identified, so it cannot replace the typed construction.

`OI_STATUS=B_PARTIAL_VISUAL_ANALOGY`

## `Q°`

No frozen definition was supplied. Letter shape and degree sign provide no mathematical semantics.
