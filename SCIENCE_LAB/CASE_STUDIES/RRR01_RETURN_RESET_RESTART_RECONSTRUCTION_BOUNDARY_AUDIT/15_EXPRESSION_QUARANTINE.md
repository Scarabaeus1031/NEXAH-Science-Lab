# Expression Quarantine

Human-supplied strings are retained only as expression/provenance annotations:

`RNJ_STATUS=EXPRESSION_ONLY`

`NRJ_STATUS=EXPRESSION_ONLY`

`NRG_STATUS=EXPRESSION_ONLY`

No phonetic, letter-number, etymological or physical interpretation is admitted.

`NRG_EQUALS_ENERGY_FORMALLY=NO`

`RETURN_EQUALS_ENERGY=NO`

`RESET_EQUALS_ENERGY=NO`

`RESTART_EQUALS_ENERGY=NO`

The ordinary operation words RESET, RESTART and NEUSTART acquire only the explicit definitions in this package. They do not carry physics, entropy, cosmology or conservation semantics.
