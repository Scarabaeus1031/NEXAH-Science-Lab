# Boundary and Nonclaims

RRR-01 is a formal state/execution/provenance audit only. It does not establish:

- a new return operator, ontology or mathematical theory;
- energy, entropy, thermodynamics or conservation claims;
- physical time reversal or history erasure;
- cyclic-universe, quantum, Möbius or cosmological meaning;
- semantics for RNJ, NRJ or NRG;
- numerical, prime, root, `pi`, `phi` or symbolic relations;
- a NEXAH architecture change or ORION capability;
- Rust interface changes or implementation authority;
- any actual reset, restart, reconstruction, measurement or execution.

Documentary history accumulation is explicitly limited to the registered append-only example and is not a physical law.

All predecessor packages remain closed and unmodified. No successor or commit is created.
