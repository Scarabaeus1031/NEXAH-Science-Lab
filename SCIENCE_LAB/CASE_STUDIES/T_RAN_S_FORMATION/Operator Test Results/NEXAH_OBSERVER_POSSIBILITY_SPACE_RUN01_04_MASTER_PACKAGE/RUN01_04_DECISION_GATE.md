## 5. Reopening Condition

RUN 05 may be reconsidered only when exactly one proposition is supplied with independently specified inputs and explicit falsification criteria. A valid reopening package must take one of these forms:

1. **Application-specific:** declared carrier, required global domain, one \(\tau\), one \(\varepsilon_\star\), sensor/cost class, and pass/fail criterion.
2. **Historical-formal:** explicit FF/SMA/BN-TL/PG objects and operators defined without numerical or visual inference, plus a proposition that can fail.
3. **External empirical:** dataset with measured traces, ground-truth states, declared operators, comparator, and falsifiable reconstruction metric.

Symbolic resemblance, a new number relation, a new diagram, or another restatement of possibility-space contraction is insufficient.
