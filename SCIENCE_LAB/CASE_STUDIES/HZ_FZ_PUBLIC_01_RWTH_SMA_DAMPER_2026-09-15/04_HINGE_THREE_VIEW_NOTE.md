# HZ_FZ_PUBLIC_01 — Hinge Three-View

The same sample neighborhood is shown in three coordinate views:

1. measured force through one active time cycle;
2. force against displacement in physical units;
3. force difference `Cut B − Cut A`, including the difference left after each
   cut's fitted fundamental is removed.

The yellow location is selected by a frozen descriptive rule: within cycle
fractions 0.4–0.6, take the maximum combined curvature of the two smoothed
non-fundamental force traces. It is a candidate for closer inspection, not a
validated switching event.

The loop area uses the identity `1 kN mm = 1 J`. It can be interpreted as
mechanical work per closed cycle only when force and displacement are the
conjugate, synchronized channels under the dataset's sign convention. The
figure therefore reports signed and absolute areas but makes no electrical
power, AC/DC, wiring, or causal claim.

## Observed result

- At 0.5 Hz the candidate lies at cycle fraction `0.45996`. Cut A changes its
  local force/displacement tangent by `+0.261 kN/mm`; Cut B changes by only
  `+0.010 kN/mm`. The non-fundamental A/B difference is `0.539 kN`. The feature
  is therefore predominantly a cut-specific shape difference in this cycle.
- At 1.0 Hz the candidate lies at cycle fraction `0.54297`. Both cuts change
  tangent (`+0.693` and `+0.913 kN/mm`), while their non-fundamental difference
  at the candidate is only `-0.039 kN`. The feature is predominantly common to
  both cuts rather than an A/B-only event.
- Absolute loop area changes from `66.3` to `61.5 J` at 0.5 Hz (`-7.3%`) and
  from `212.7` to `217.0 J` at 1.0 Hz (`+2.0%`) for actuator 1, cycle 8.
