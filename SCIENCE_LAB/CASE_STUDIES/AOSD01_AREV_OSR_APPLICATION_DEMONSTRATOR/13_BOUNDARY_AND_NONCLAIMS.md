# Boundary and Nonclaims

AOSD-01 demonstrates application consistency only. It does not establish:

- a new primitive, operator, ontology or architecture;
- new mathematics, graph theory, mechanics or physics;
- product or production readiness;
- intrinsic meaning for ratios, numbers, month positions or dashboard glyphs;
- hidden numerical, prime, calendar, Q, planetary, biological or historical significance;
- that a mnemonic is a calendar state;
- that a display or view is its source;
- historical semantics for Q°, Iota, Yugo, Nautilus, Leviathan, Archy, prime rhythm, pinkies or `77`;
- an ORION capability;
- implementation authority.

Exactly four application cases were used. The short historical mapping note is a quarantine/classification note, not a fifth case.

`NEW_OPERATOR_INVENTED=NO`

`NEW_ONTOLOGY_CREATED=NO`

`NEW_MATHEMATICAL_DISCOVERY=NO`

`NEW_PHYSICAL_DISCOVERY=NO`

`NEXAH_ARCHITECTURE_CHANGED=NO`

`IMPLEMENTATION_ACTIVATION=NO`

`NEW_RESEARCH_ACTIVATION=NO`
