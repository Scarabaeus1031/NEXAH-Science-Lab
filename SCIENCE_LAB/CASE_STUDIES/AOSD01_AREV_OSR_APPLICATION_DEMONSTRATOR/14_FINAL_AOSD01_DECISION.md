# Final AOSD-01 Decision

The existing AREV-01 and OSR-01 grammar is sufficient and materially useful across all four authorized cases. Its value is not that the cases look alike, but that the same compact provenance rules preserve their differences: graph relations remain separate from embeddings; observables from measurement events; values from readouts; and views from sources.

No case requires a new primitive or a geometry/measurement collapse. A source-typed `View` can combine both pipelines without merging them. Different states can produce equal readouts, and one state can produce multiple readouts. One graph can produce multiple embeddings, and one embedding multiple views. A ratio does not identify its generating selection, and a mnemonic does not identify the calendar source it helps recall.

The future-facing interface judgment is positive only at the conceptual level: provenance-bearing `Geometry`, `Measurement` and `Composite` view sources can prevent invalid combinations. No Rust implementation or activation follows.
