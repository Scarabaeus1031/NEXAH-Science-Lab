## Primary result

`C_COMPLEX_SLICE_OR_COORDINATE_VIEW_SUFFICIENT`

The burden of proof for a quaternion interpretation was not met. Historical quaternion terminology and Q/stick/multi-view representations are physically present, but no recoverable historical structure declares a four-real-component `H` state, a map from Q labels into `1,i,j,k`, source operations closed under that map, or the decisive noncommutative relations `ij=k` and `ji=-k`. Those relations pass only as standard positive controls.

The complex example `c=-0.750+0.100i` is one parameter in one complex plane. Re-embedding its coordinate pair into `C_j` gives an isomorphic complex system but a distinct fixed-basis quaternion element. If the quadratic state and parameter remain within one slice, the system is an isomorphic copy of ordinary complex quadratic dynamics; full quaternion algebra is unnecessary.

The supplied boards are class-D representations. Their axes, sticks, rotations, mirrors, planes, and multiple views admit ordinary `R²/R³`, complex, projection, and coordinate-frame descriptions. Neutral relabeling removes the quaternion suggestion while preserving those structures.
