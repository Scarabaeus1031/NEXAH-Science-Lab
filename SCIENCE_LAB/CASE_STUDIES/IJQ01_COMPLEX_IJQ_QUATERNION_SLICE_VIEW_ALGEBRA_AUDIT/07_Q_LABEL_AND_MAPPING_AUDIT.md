# 07 — Q Label and Mapping Audit

## Distinct objects

- lowercase `q`: conventional variable that may denote a quaternion only when typed `q∈H`;
- uppercase `Q`: a glyph/name with no intrinsic algebra;
- `Q1...Qn`: indexed diagram labels;
- `Q°`: local historical orientation/observer notation in readable NEXAH ledgers;
- four or six visual nodes/ropes: finite diagram structures, not dimensions by count.

No inspected source supplies a map `m:{Q1,Q2,Q3,Q4}→{1,i,j,k}` together with binary operations on the source labels that preserve quaternion multiplication. Without source operations, all `4!=24` bijective label assignments are algebraically ungrounded and none can be preferred. Assigning labels after viewing a picture would be cherry-picking and still would not define multiplication.

Q1–Q6 cannot define six quaternion dimensions: `H` is four-dimensional over `R`, while indexed labels may be arbitrarily numerous.

```text
q_EQUALS_Q=NO_UNLESS_FORMALLY_DEFINED
Q_GLYPH_EQUALS_QUATERNION=NO

```

Historical quaternion terminology is preserved as provenance. It is not a recoverable algebra.
