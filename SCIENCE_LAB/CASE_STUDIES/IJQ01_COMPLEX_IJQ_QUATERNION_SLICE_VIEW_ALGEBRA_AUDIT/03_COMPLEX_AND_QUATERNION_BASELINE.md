# 03 — Complex and Quaternion Baseline

## Complex numbers

`C={a+bi : a,b∈R, i²=-1}` is a two-dimensional real commutative algebra. A value `c=a+bi` is one number with two real coordinates in the declared basis; `i` is a basis unit, not a second parameter.

## Quaternions

`H={a+bi+cj+dk : a,b,c,d∈R}` is a four-dimensional real associative division algebra with basis `1,i,j,k` and

```text
i²=j²=k²=-1
ij=k,  jk=i,  ki=j
ji=-k, kj=-i, ik=-j
```

The order reversal `ij≠ji` is decisive. Four labels, points, colors, or directions do not supply these operations.

## Complex slices

For any pure unit quaternion `u` with `u²=-1`,

`C_u={a+bu : a,b∈R}`

is a real subalgebra isomorphic to `C` and closed under addition and multiplication. It does not require the other quaternion units for calculations that remain inside the slice.

Under the fixed standard basis, `C_i`, `C_j`, and `C_k` are distinct subsets of `H` (intersecting on `R`) but are isomorphic as complex algebras.

## Positive discriminator

Standard baseline:

`i*j=k` while `j*i=-k`; also `j*k=i` while `k*j=-i`.

A historical mapping is quaternionic only if comparable operations and roles are actually declared and preserved—not merely drawable.
