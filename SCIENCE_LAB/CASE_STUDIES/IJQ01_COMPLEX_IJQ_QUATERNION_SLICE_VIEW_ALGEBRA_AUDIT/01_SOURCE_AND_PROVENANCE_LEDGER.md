# 01 — Source and Provenance Ledger

## Authoritative mathematical baseline

- [Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Quaternion](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/index.php?title=Quaternion): `ℍ` is a four-dimensional real algebra with basis `1,i,j,k`; its multiplication table includes `ij=k` and `ji=-k`, and it is noncommutative.
- [Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Complex number](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Complex_number): complex numbers are represented by two real components; quaternions are a noncommutative extension.
- [Encyclopedia of Mathematics — Julia set](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Julia_set): for `z²+c`, the Mandelbrot set is a parameter-space object associated with connected Julia sets.
- [Altavilla et al., Mathematische Zeitschrift — Slice conformality](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-022-03079-4): every quaternionic imaginary unit `I` with `I²=-1` generates a real-algebra copy `C_I` of the complex plane.

These sources define the discriminator; they do not validate NEXAH terminology.
