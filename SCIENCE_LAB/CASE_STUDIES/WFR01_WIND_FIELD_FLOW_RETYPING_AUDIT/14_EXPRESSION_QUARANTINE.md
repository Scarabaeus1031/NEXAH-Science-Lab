# Expression quarantine

The following remain expression or historical provenance unless a separately adopted source supplies formal typing:

- `FIELD / WIND / HIMMLISCHES KIND`;
- Regenbogen-Wind, wind wheels, and wind-state illustrations;
- Kepler/orbit imagery; Cubit Elevator Axis; Observer Node; Dual Fiber;
- Newton Bucket / Einstein Elevator;
- NTO/NOT/TON/TNO permutations and THOTH/THOOTH associations;
- Q° without a declared frame;
- unlabeled `PULL → STRUCTURE → FLOW → STILLPOINT` arrows;
- visual certificates, proof labels, and “verified” wording without authority.

Deleting all quarantined phrases leaves the formal result unchanged: existing types cover bounded view, structure, input, state, flow, frame, history, and provenance; eleven is not recovered; 77 remains arithmetic.

Expression may be preserved creatively or historically. Preservation does not make it implemented, measured, or scientific evidence.
