# Physical wind boundary

A bounded atmospheric record may distinguish:

| Role | Documentary type |
|---|---|
| Pressure-gradient contribution | Declared forcing/input |
| Coriolis contribution | Frame-dependent dynamical contribution |
| Friction / boundary layer | Boundary/process contribution |
| Buoyancy / thermal contribution | Declared forcing/input |
| Terrain / geometry | Environment, boundary, or structural constraint |
| Rotation / vorticity | Derived or state quantity when defined |
| Velocity field | Vector field over a domain |
| Transport | Evolution of a carried quantity |
| Observed trajectory | Measurement/readout history |

These are typing distinctions, not a new atmospheric model. They require domain, units, frame, model, measurement provenance, and uncertainty before any physical claim.

A named wind may involve several roles and overlap other classifications. A storm, current, regional name, intensity term, and symbolic visual are not automatic peers. The corpus is not physically homogeneous; no universal mechanism or WIND primitive is introduced.
