# NOT-FIBER and NTO control

`A != B` records non-identity; a relation may require map/rule, parameter/time, frame, loss, provenance, criterion, and input. `NOT-FIBER` is only `USEFUL_EXPRESSION_LABEL`: **not identical—relation must be typed**. It claims no mathematical fiber or bundle.

The NTO /11357 package documents NTO as an exploratory representation-family name with original/inverse and cutaway/motion transformations. It does not establish spelling-derived equivalence with NOT.

- `NTO_STATUS=HISTORICAL_EXPLORATORY_REPRESENTATION_LABEL_SEMANTICS_UNDERDEFINED`
- `NTO_EQUALS_NOT=NO`
- NOT may mean “not identical” in this audit.
- NTO/NOT/TON/TNO permutations are expression provenance.
- THOTH/THOOTH derivations are unsupported.

No ancient, Egyptian, linguistic, or universal derivation is inferred.
