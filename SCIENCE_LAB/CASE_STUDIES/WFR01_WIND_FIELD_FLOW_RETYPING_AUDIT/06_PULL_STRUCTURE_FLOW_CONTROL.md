# PULL / STRUCTURE / FLOW control

PULL remains underdefined; context may represent it as declared input, influence, parameter, selection, constraint, or gradient-derived tendency. It is not an operator. STRUCTURE is declared relations/constraints/rules and may constrain dynamics without being dynamics. FLOW must be qualified as physical transport, state evolution, vector field, graph transition, visual pattern, or metaphor. STILLPOINT may be state, region, criterion, figure, or expression; without criterion it is underdefined.

The historical `PULL → STRUCTURE → FLOW → STILLPOINT` is not retained as unlabeled causation. Its arrows lack verified rule, parameter, time, loss, and provenance and remain `EXPRESSION_ONLY`.

Neutral bookkeeping:

`STATE --[declared dynamics; structure; input/pull; wind/forcing; conditions; time; provenance]--> STATE_NEXT`

This is existing-role documentation, not a NEXAH law. `PULL + STRUCTURE + WIND = FLOW` is rejected.
