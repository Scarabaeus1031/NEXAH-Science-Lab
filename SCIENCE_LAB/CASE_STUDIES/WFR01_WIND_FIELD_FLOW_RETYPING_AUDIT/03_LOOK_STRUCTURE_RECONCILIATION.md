# LOOK / STRUCTURE reconciliation

`LOOK != STRUCTURE` survives. STRUCTURE is a declared relation, constraint, pattern, or rule structure. LOOK is not a precise frozen type; in context it can refer to PROJECTION, FIGURE / VIEW, GESTALT, or INTERPRETATION.

No universal `LOOK=FIGURE`, `LOOK=GESTALT`, or `LOOK=INTERPRETATION` is adopted.

`LOOK_FORMAL_STATUS=UNDERDEFINED_COMPOSITE_OF_PROJECTION_FIGURE_GESTALT_INTERPRETATION`

This adds no type. It tells a record to use an existing type only when context supports it; otherwise retain `UNDERDEFINED`. A changed view may change figure or gestalt without changing source structure. A structural change may or may not appear in the selected view.
