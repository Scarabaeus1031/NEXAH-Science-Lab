# Boundary and nonclaims

WFR-01 does not claim a universal FIELD/WIND/FLOW/PULL type; PULL as operator; `PULL + STRUCTURE + WIND = FLOW`; a dynamical or atmospheric law; a mathematical fiber bundle; NTO=NOT; THOTH/ancient derivation; Q° as source/center/origin; visual measurement/proof; exactly eleven wind classes; 77 as architecture, state space, transitions, symmetry, measurement, or science; a new OLS primitive, RID gap, operator, ontology, physical theory, or number theory; or ORION, Rust, prototype, implementation, or research activation.

Its positive scope is documentary retyping: LOOK remains distinct from STRUCTURE; bounded field, wind, pull, and flow occurrences can often use existing types; non-identity and orientation metadata can be preserved; unresolved occurrences remain unresolved.

All predecessor packages remain read-only.
