# 04 — R / 2R Radius–Diameter Control

For a circle of registered radius `R>0`, the diameter is

`D=2R`.

A diameter can be represented as two opposite radius vectors along one line:

`-R ← 0 → +R`.

Those two directed halves are one geometric decomposition, not two independent mechanisms or operators.

```text
R_EQUALS_RADIUS=YES_IN_REGISTERED_CIRCLE_GEOMETRY
TWO_R_EQUALS_DIAMETER=YES_FOR_CIRCLE
RADIUS_EQUALS_DIAMETER=NO
RADIUS_EQUALS_RETURN=NO
RADIUS_EQUALS_ROTATION=NO
DIAMETER_EQUALS_TWO_DIRECTIONAL_OPERATORS=NO
```

Human annotation `2R = two-sided axis` is admissible only as `GEOMETRIC_SHORTHAND` when the intended diameter/reference line is explicit. It carries no mechanical or symbolic semantics by itself.
