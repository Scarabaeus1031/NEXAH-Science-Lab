# 11 — Vehicle / Ground Frame Control

Human statement: “Nothing moves; only the wheels move.”

In a vehicle-fixed frame during steady travel, the chassis and many components have approximately fixed coordinates while wheels and shafts rotate. In the road/ground frame, the chassis and wheel centers translate. A material point on a rolling wheel has a time-dependent velocity in both descriptions; the contact point is instantaneously at rest relative to the ground only under ideal no-slip contact.

Thus the sentence is neither globally true nor paradoxical; it omits the reference frame and component.
