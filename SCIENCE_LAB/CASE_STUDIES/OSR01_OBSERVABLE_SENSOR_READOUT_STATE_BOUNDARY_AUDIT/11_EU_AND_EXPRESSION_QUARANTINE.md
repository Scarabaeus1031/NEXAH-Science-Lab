# EU and Expression Quarantine

`EU` is semantically empty in this audit. No meaning is derived from its letters, pronunciation, geography, product associations or earlier symbolic use.

The missing functional distinction, where needed, is already expressed by neutral types:

- `MeasurementView` for a view sourced from measurement records;
- `CompositeView` for a view sourced from both embedding and measurements;
- `Readout` for a display transformation of measurement values.

These names state dependencies without creating semantic reliance on a mnemonic.

`EU_ROLE_STATUS=EXPRESSION_ONLY`

`EU_CANONICAL_TYPE_CREATED=NO`

`WORDPLAY_EVIDENTIAL_WEIGHT=ZERO`

Neutral relabeling of every identifier leaves the complete pipeline intact.
