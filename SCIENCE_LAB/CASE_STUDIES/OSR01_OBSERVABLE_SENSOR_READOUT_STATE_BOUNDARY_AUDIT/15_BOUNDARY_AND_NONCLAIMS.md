# Boundary and Nonclaims

OSR-01 adds no NEXAH-specific operator or theory. It consolidates standard measurement and representation types for safer future interface design.

It does not claim:

- state reconstruction from a single observable in general;
- that a sensor, observable, event, value, readout or view is truth;
- a physical instrument validation;
- universal observer behavior;
- new vehicle mechanics;
- that gear is dimension or clutch is gate;
- a new ontology, mathematics or physics;
- meaning for `EU` or any wordplay;
- Rust implementation or production readiness;
- a change to AREV-01, GARC-01, QAW-01, PRR-01, ASZ-01, EMP-02, EMP-03 or MJTR-01.
