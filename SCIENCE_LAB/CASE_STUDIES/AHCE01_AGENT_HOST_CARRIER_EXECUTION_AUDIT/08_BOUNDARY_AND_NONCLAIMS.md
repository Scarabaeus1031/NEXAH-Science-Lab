# AHCE-01 — Boundary and Nonclaims

The supported object is a **common interaction-record shape**, not a common mechanism.

## Necessary boundaries

- Bacteria, viruses, cancer cells, fungi, and software/AI are not identified.
- Infection, colonization, pathogenicity, symbiosis, invasion, metastasis, execution, copying, and output are separate predicates/events.
- Cancer is an internal altered cell lineage in the ordinary registered case, not an external infectious agent.
- Viral genomes carry information but host-cell machinery executes many replication/expression functions.
- Stored software/model information is not execution; model output is not Human authority.
- A fungal radiation-response observation does not establish photosynthesis, radiosynthesis as a universal mechanism, or autonomous carbon fixation.
- Observation does not establish a complete mechanism.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `FALSE`, and `UNKNOWN` remain different values.
