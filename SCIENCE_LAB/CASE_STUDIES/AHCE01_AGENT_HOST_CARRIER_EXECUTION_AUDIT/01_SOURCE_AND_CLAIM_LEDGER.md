# AHCE-01 — Source and Claim Ledger

| ID | Source | Claim used | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | [NCBI Bookshelf — Introduction to Bacteriology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK8120/?report=reader) | Bacteria are cellular microorganisms with organism-specific growth/reproduction conditions. | Does not imply pathogenicity, infection, or host dependence for every bacterium. |
| S2 | [CDC — Colonization and infection definitions](https://www.cdc.gov/antimicrobial-resistance/media/pdfs/session-1-john-jernigan-508.pdf) | Colonization can occur without disease/symptoms and can precede infection. | Colonization, infection, transmission, and pathogenicity remain distinct. |
| S3 | [NCBI Bookshelf — Structure and Classification of Viruses](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK8174/) | Viruses carry DNA or RNA and depend on host-cell metabolic/biosynthetic machinery for propagation. | Viral information is not host execution; mechanisms vary by virus. |
| S4 | [NCI — What Is Cancer?](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/understanding/what-is-cancer) | Cancer arises when changes disrupt the normal process by which an organism's cells grow and divide. | Does not classify ordinary cancer as an external infectious agent. |
| S5 | [NCI — Metastatic Cancer](https://www.cancer.gov/types/metastatic-cancer) | Metastatic cells retain features/name of the primary cancer; invasion and transport are distinct steps. | Proliferation, local invasion, dissemination, and metastatic growth are not collapsed. |
| S6 | [CDC — One Health and Fungal Diseases](https://www.cdc.gov/fungal/about/one-health.html) | Fungal exposures/infections may arise from environmental, animal, or human routes depending on the fungus. | Environmental growth, colonization, infection, symbiosis, and transmission are not equivalent. |
| S7 | [Dadachova et al., PLOS ONE 2007](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0000457) | Ionizing radiation changed measured electronic properties of melanin and enhanced growth of melanized fungi under the study conditions. | No photosynthesis, autonomous carbon fixation, universal radiosynthesis, or general fungal mechanism is inferred. |
| S8 | [NIST software glossary](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/software) | Software is a technical artifact/instructional information category. | Stored artifact is not an execution event. |
| S9 | [Python execution model](https://docs.python.org/3/reference/executionmodel.html) | Execution has an explicit language/runtime model and evaluation context. | Python is an illustrative technical control, not a universal model of all software. |
| S10 | [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/nist.ai.100-1.pdf) | AI risk governance assigns human/organizational accountability and oversight roles. | Model output is not Human authority or automatic authorization. |

## Evidence rule

The ledger supports only distinctions needed by the schema. It does not establish a biological mechanism, technical-biological equivalence, or historical NEXAH validation.
