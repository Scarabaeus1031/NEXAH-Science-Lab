## Capability retained

fixed equal-information contract without reopening or merging the source
studies. It can separate three questions that had often been conflated:

1. can the source be reconstructed exactly;
2. is the mapping canonical or carrier-specific;
3. does the record use fewer fixed-width bits than the source baseline.

Exact Return transfers across all three tested carriers. Positive bit economy
is demonstrated only for the typed SCC-003 seam under its declared contract.
