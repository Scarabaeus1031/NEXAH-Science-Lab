# Iapetus Observability Control

[NASA's Iapetus record](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/iapetus/) reports that the same entity appeared brighter/fainter over its orbit and that images resolved very different leading/trailing reflectivities. It preserves uncertainty: alternatives are discussed while thermal segregation is described as probably the main process.

    IAPETUS identity
    + surface/albedo state
    + orbital orientation
    + illumination and observation geometry
    -> different signal/readout

NOT_OBSERVED != NOT_PRESENT
BLACK_READOUT != ABSENT_ENTITY
SIGNAL_LOSS != OBJECT_LOSS
VIEW_CHANGE != ENTITY_CHANGE
OBSERVABILITY != EXISTENCE

IAPETUS_OBSERVABILITY_CONTROL_SUPPORTED=YES
NOT_OBSERVED_DISTINCT_FROM_NOT_PRESENT=YES

"The Other Side of the Moon" remains expression only.
