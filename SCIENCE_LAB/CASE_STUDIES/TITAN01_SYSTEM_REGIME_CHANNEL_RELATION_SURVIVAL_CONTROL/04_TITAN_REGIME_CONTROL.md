# Titan Regime Control

[NASA's Titan facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/facts/) support a dense predominantly nitrogen atmosphere and a cycle involving hydrocarbon clouds, rain, rivers, lakes, seas and evaporation. The regime differs strongly from Earth: water ice behaves as rock, while methane/ethane sculpt channels and fill lakes.

| Role | Earth reference | Titan control |
|---|---|---|
| condensable liquid | water | methane/ethane hydrocarbons |
| precipitation/transport | water cycle | hydrocarbon cycle |
| substrate | context-dependent rock/soil/ice | water ice in a rock role |
| thermal regime | terrestrial | about 94 K |
| forms | rivers/channels/lakes | same broad form roles under different materials |

The recoverable level is a process-role relation. Material, rates, rheology, detailed erosion law, atmosphere, gravity and history are not identical.
