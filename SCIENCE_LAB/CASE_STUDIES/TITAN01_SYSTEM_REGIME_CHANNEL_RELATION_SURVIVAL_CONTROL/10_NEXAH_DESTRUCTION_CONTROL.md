# NEXAH Destruction Control

All NEXAH, ORION, NEXUS, NTO and expression-specific names are removed.

Surviving ordinary-language machine:

> Register a system, its members, context, state and provenance. Declare an interaction or transformation and its admissible inputs. Record the channel, medium, boundary conditions and losses. Distinguish observation, signal, trace, readout and interpretation. Compare two regimes only under explicit criteria and classify each relation as preserved, transformed, partial, unresolved, undefined or not comparable. Retain identity, history, uncertainty and provenance. Refuse unsupported conclusions. Leave normative decisions to an identified human authority.
