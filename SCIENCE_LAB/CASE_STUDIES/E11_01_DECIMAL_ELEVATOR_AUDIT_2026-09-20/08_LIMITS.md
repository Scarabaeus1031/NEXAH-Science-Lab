# E11-01 — Limits and claim boundary

1. The hypothesis was formed after inspecting phase values. This is a
   post-hoc exploratory audit, even though the new statistic was frozen before
   execution.
2. Decimal blocks depend on unit, origin, quantum and rounding rule. They are
   representations of the phase, not the phase itself.
3. The 36 Kappa carriers are derived from the 72 absolute carriers and are not
   an independent replication corpus.
4. Cycles within a run can be serially dependent. Step-spectrum comparisons
   provide specificity controls but do not create independent runs.
5. A two-decimal `11` step covers a raw interval around 0.11 degree. The exact
   three-decimal changes show that the label is quantization-dependent.
6. KAPPA-02 local windows overlap strongly. Their repeated 4.88-like values
   cannot be counted as independent confirmations.
7. Frequency and displacement amplitude are confounded in the current two
   conditions.

Allowed claim: the data contain several declared 11-family codes, including
one directed `+11` Kappa transition, but the full corpus shows no specific
11-grid or magnitude-11 enrichment.

Not allowed: 11 causes the phase evolution, decimal notation is physically
privileged, or an octave, synchronization or morphogenetic mechanism has been
demonstrated.
