# 10 — Cuneiform Determinative Cross-check

CUN-01 established that a determinative can provide semantic-class information without ordinary syllabic pronunciation. GRB-01 asks only the shared documentary question: where is information carried?

The analogy is partial:

- both cases require separation of visible representation from linguistic/grammatical function;
- both require language and context;
- a determinative is an overt cuneiform sign/function, not a modern article and not zero marking;
- definiteness and cuneiform semantic classification are different domain categories.

```text
DETERMINATIVE_EQUALS_ARTICLE=NO

CUNEIFORM_EQUALS_MODERN_GRAMMAR=NO
```
