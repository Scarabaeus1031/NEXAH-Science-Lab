`BOUNDED_CROSS_LANGUAGE_GRAMMATICAL_REPRESENTATION_AUDIT / CLOSED`

GRB-01 tests two bounded distinctions: several visible characters may participate in one conventional orthographic unit, and a grammatical feature may be interpreted without one particular overt marker.

## Result

`C_STANDARD_LINGUISTIC_TERMINOLOGY_SUFFICIENT_NO_NEXAH_SPECIFIC_GAIN`

The distinctions are real, but standard terms—digraph, trigraph, composite symbol, orthographic unit, article, definiteness, agreement, context and non-overt/zero expression—are sufficient. `BINDER` is retained only as a Human-supplied meta-term and is not needed as a formal term or operator.

The audit uses English, German, Croatian, French and one bounded Dutch `ij` control. It creates no grammar, linguistic discovery, NEXAH operator, architecture change, implementation, research activation or ORION capability.

Frozen and unmodified: IOTB-01, AHCE-01, QRO-01, GIJ-01, VOR-01 and CUN-01. Final action: `STOP`.
