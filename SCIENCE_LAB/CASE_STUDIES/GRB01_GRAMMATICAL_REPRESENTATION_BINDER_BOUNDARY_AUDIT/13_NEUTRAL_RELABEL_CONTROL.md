# 13 — Neutral Relabel Control

```text
MARK=A
BOUND_UNIT=B
LANGUAGE=C
PRONUNCIATION=D
GRAMMATICAL_MARKER=E
GRAMMATICAL_FEATURE=F
CONTEXT=G
ZERO_MARKING=H
```

After removing all specified strings and `NEXAH`:

```text
A != B
B != D
E != F
C selects valid mappings
G constrains interpretation
absence of E does not automatically imply absence of F
```

The result does not depend on `TH`, `SCH`, `IJ`, article forms, personal names or the word `BINDER`.
