# 15 — Boundary and Nonclaims

## Bounded findings

- `CHARACTER_COUNT != FUNCTIONAL_ORTHOGRAPHIC_UNIT_COUNT` in the documented controls.
- Orthographic-unit status is language- and task-specific.
- `GRAMMATICAL_MARKER != GRAMMATICAL_FEATURE`.
- Definiteness is not identical to English `the` or any one surface form.
- Absence of an article does not by itself establish absence of definiteness information.
- Information may be distributed across visible segment, unit, morphology, word form, position, agreement, lexical entry, context and discourse.

## Nonclaims

GRB-01 does not establish:

- a universal grammar, new grammatical model or linguistic discovery;
- a new category or operator named `BINDER`;
- that all digraphs, trigraphs, alphabet letters or `ij` are structurally identical;
- that all languages encode definiteness in the same way;
- one universal zero-marker mechanism;
- that cuneiform determinatives are articles or zero markers;
- etymological, symbolic, personal-name or ancient continuity claims;
- a NEXAH-specific scientific gain;
- architecture, ORION, implementation or research activation.

```text
REPRESENTATION != FUNCTION
COMPONENTS != COMPOSITE_UNIT
MARKER != FEATURE
ABSENCE_OF_MARKER != ABSENCE_OF_INFORMATION
LANGUAGE_AND_CONTEXT_SELECT_VALID_INTERPRETATION
```
