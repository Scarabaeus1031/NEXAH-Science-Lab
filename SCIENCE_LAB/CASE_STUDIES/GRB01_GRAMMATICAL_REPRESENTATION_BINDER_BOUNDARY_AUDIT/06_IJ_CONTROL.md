# 06 — Dutch IJ Control

The Nederlandse Taalunie technical manual and Taaladvies.net support a deliberately non-binary classification:

- `ij` consists of the letter combination `i+j` and is alphabetized under `i`;
- it behaves as a unit in initial capitalization (`IJ...`) and often in crossword/layout practice;
- the official manual therefore says it sometimes behaves as though it were one letter.

```text
IJ_STATUS=TWO_LETTER_SEQUENCE_WITH_CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_LETTER_LIKE_BEHAVIOR
IJ_EQUALS_TH=NO
IJ_TH_RELATION=COMMON_REPRESENTATION_PROBLEM_BUT_STRUCTURALLY_DISTINCT
```

No single classification is forced across alphabetization, capitalization, typography and pronunciation.
