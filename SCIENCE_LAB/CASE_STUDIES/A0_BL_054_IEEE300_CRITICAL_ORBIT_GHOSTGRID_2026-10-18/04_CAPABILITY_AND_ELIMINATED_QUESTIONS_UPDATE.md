# A0-BL-054 — Capability and eliminated-questions update

## Bounded capability retained

The frozen adapter is deterministic, byte-replayable, bus-label invariant,
non-leaky and responsive to every declared state control. At 16 and 32 bins,
all registered view stresses preserve the frozen ordering threshold. The
primary 16-bin ordered view records `rho = 0.958546`.

This is a useful case-local engineering representation boundary. It adds no
canonical physical orbit, preferred geometry, prediction method or public
capability.

## Eliminated or qualified

- universal stability across the registered view family is refuted: 21 of 81
  configurations fail, all at the coarse 8-bin resolution;
- label invariance and state sensitivity do not identify a physical Critical
  Orbit, causal mechanism or early-warning capability;
- the registered Ghostgrid representation has no demonstrated advantage over
  the random direct-summary comparator, whose q95 ordering correlation is
  `1.0`;
- a stable 16-bin primary view does not license a resolution-independent or
  operational grid-utility claim.

## Still open, but not activated by closure

- a separately admitted coarse-view redesign with a new preregistration;
- an independent prediction or early-warning task with held-out outcomes;
- physical interpretation, operational utility and external replication;
- publication and public release.
