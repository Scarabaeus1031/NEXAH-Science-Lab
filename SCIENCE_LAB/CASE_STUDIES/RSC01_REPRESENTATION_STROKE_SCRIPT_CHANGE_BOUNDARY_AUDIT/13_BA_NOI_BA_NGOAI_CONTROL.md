# 13 — `Bà Nội` / `Bà Ngoại` Control

## Verified correction

The Human recollection is supported in its core distinction:

| Form | Morphological record used here | Kin relation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bà nội` | `bà` + paternal-side modifier `nội` | paternal grandmother | K2/K3 |
| `bà ngoại` | `bà` + maternal-side modifier `ngoại` | maternal grandmother | K3 |

K3 explicitly says the modifiers identify the paternal versus maternal side. RSC-01 does not adopt literal “inside/outside” glosses as explanations and makes no value judgment from them.

## Address/reference behavior

K2 shows `bà nội` functioning as first-person self-reference when a paternal grandmother speaks to a grandchild. The same study notes that `ông`/`bà` kin terms may occur without proper names, and that speakers can omit the paternal/maternal modifier in vernacular interaction when context supplies the relation.

Thus:

```text
FORM_ALONE_UNIQUELY_IDENTIFIES_DISCOURSE_PERSON=NO
GENEALOGICAL_SIDE_DISTINCTION=SUPPORTED
DISCOURSE_ROLE_REQUIRED=YES
CONTEXTUAL_OMISSION_POSSIBLE=YES_SOURCE_BOUND
```

No symbolic interpretation of `bà`, `nội`, `ngoại`, spelling or sound is made.
