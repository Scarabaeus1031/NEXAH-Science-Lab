# Final GARC-01 Decision

The candidate boundary is correct in standard form: conserving an abstract graph conserves adjacency, valence, paths and cycles, but does not conserve Euclidean angles or lengths across arbitrary embeddings. Angles are properties of embedded directions under a metric and frame. Translation, rotation, reflection and uniform scaling preserve unsigned angle magnitude; a general affine shear need not. Observation adds a further layer and cannot be identified with either graph or embedding.

The same three-leaf star was explicitly embedded with angle sets `90°/90°/180°` and `45°/90°/135°`, while all graph-theoretic structure remained fixed. A separate shear changed a right angle to `45°`. Neutral relabeling did not affect either result.

No uniquely identified source image was physically supplied in the current GARC-01 package, so no claim about that image was made. The formal audit is nevertheless complete because its result does not depend on image interpretation.
