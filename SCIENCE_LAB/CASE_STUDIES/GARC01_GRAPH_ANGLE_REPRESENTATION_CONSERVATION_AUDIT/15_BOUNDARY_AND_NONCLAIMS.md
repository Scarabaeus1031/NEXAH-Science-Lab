# Boundary and Nonclaims

GARC-01 establishes only a standard typing boundary:

```text
abstract graph structure
!= embedded Euclidean geometry
!= observed representation
```

It does not establish:

- a new graph invariant;
- a new geometry or angle law;
- a source-image angle or relation;
- a special NEXAH graph;
- a numerical, prime or factorization geometry;
- a physical mechanism;
- a quaternion or higher-dimensional requirement;
- a universal representation law;
- an operator;
- an ontology;
- an ORION capability;
- an implementation requirement.

Predecessors remain frozen. `J_R` is neither used nor modified. VST-01's source gap is not filled. NLO-01 documentary morphology is not promoted to formal structure.
