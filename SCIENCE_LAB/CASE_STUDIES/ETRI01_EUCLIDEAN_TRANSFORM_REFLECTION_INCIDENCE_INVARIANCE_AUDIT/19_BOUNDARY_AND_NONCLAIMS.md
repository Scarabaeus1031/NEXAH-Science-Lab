# Boundary and Nonclaims

ETRI-01 establishes only standard Euclidean, affine and graph distinctions. It does not establish:

- a new geometry, transform or invariant;
- screenshot-derived exact angles;
- symbolic meaning from color, letter, layout or wordplay;
- a circle, sphere or dimension from reflected copies;
- quaternion structure;
- physical mechanics, chemistry, astronomy or planetary relations;
- number, prime or factorization significance;
- a new NEXAH operator, ontology or architecture;
- an ORION capability;
- Rust implementation or activation.

The formal construction is independent of the unavailable motivating image. All predecessor decisions remain closed and unchanged.
