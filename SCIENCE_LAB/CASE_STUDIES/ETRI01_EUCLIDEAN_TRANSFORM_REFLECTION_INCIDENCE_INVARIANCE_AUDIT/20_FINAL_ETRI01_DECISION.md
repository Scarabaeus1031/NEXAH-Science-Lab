# Final ETRI-01 Decision

The neutral construction validates the intended relation using standard Euclidean geometry: two parallel lines, a triangle between them and the straight-angle/alternate-interior-angle relations give `a+y+b=180°`. No screenshot measurement or symbolic interpretation is needed.

For a fixed graph, Euclidean reflection changes coordinates and handedness while preserving incidence, parallelism, perpendicularity, distance and unsigned angle magnitude. Horizontal, vertical and diagonal reflections share this invariant class but are distinct transforms. Two reflections across intersecting axes compose to a rotation, and transform history must remain separate from the final map or view.

Adding the perpendicular `PH` is not reflection or re-embedding. It introduces point `H`, subdivides edge `AB`, adds incidence and changes graph counts, degrees and cycle structure while partitioning `y=y_L+y_R`. A general affine shear completes the destruction control: parallelism survives while a right angle can change.

AREV-01 and OSR-01 already type every required object and boundary. No interface extension is necessary.
