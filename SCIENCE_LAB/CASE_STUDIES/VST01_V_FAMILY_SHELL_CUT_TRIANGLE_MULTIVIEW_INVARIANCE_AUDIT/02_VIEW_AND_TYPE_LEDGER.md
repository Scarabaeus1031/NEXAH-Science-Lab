# View and Type Ledger

| Object/type | Bounded role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `V_BRANCH_PAIR` | two registered rays/branches with a common source relation or identifiable vertex | not a triangle or Y |
| `SHELL` | circle `r=R` in a declared polar model | not a physical horizon |
| `INTERSECTION_POINT` | meeting of a branch with the shell | requires actual branch and shell |
| `CHORD` | segment joining two shell points | additional admitted relation |
| `TRIANGLE` | three vertices and three sides | not identical to two branches |
| `VIEW` | coordinate/plot representation | not the underlying relation |
| `RELATION` | source-defined or exact geometric relation | not inferred from similar appearance |

Declared view types:

- EXP20-B: intended `POLAR_REPRESENTATION`, unverified physically;
- EXP09: intended `NESTED_MODULAR_GRID_REPRESENTATION`, unverified physically;
- EXP11: intended `RESIDUE_PAIR_COORDINATE_REPRESENTATION`, unverified physically;
- EXP_AK-AO: intended `POLAR_DIRECTIONAL_REPRESENTATION`, unverified physically.

`SAME_RELATION_EQUALS_SAME_SHAPE=NO_IN_GENERAL`
