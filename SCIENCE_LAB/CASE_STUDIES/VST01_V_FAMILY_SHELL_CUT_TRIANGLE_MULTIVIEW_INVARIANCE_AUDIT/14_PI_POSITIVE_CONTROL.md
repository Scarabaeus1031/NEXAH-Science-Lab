# Pi Positive Control

For a Euclidean circle of radius `R>0`:

- circumference `C=2*pi*R`;
- one full revolution is `2*pi` radians;
- `2*pi rad = 360 degrees`.

This is standard circle/radian geometry. Pi receives no symbolic or cross-domain interpretation.
