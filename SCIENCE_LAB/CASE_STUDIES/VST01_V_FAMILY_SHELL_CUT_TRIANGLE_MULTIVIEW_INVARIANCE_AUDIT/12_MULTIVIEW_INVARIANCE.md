# Multi-View Invariance

The central question cannot be answered affirmatively or negatively for the historical EXP relation because the required objects and transformation chain are unavailable.

Standard representation theory allows a relation to persist while its plotted shape, orientation, angle, or label changes under a coordinate map. Conversely, similar shapes can arise from different relations. Therefore the following fields remain distinct:

- `RELATION_SURVIVES`;
- `SHAPE_SURVIVES`;
- `ORIENTATION_SURVIVES`;
- `ANGLE_SURVIVES`;
- `LABEL_SURVIVES`.

For the frozen corpus:

- relation survival: `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- shape survival: `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- orientation survival: `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- angle survival: `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- label survival: irrelevant to formal invariance.
