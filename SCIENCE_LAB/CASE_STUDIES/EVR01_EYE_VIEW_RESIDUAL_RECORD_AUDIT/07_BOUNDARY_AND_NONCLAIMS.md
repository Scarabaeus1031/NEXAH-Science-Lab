## Expression quarantine

The following may be retained as expression but are not formalized here:

`REST`, `ROST`, `RADIATION`, `ROTATION`, `TRANSPORT`, `TRANSFUSION`, `TRANSITION`, `TRANSLATION`, `TRANSFORMATION`, `TORSION`, `TEMPERATURE`, `PRESSURE`, `Pa`.

```text
WORD_RESONANCE_NE_FORMAL_IDENTITY
REST_NE_RUST
ROTATION_NE_RADIATION
TEMPERATURE_NE_TRANSPORT
PRESSURE_NE_TRANSITION
```

No etymology, syllable mapping, glyph mapping, operator family or physical/biological bridge is inferred from these words.
