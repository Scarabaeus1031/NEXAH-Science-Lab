### B. Record, projection and view pipeline

The Five-H record, `Pi_Q` projection, fixed mask and Format Elevator form one
verified observation pipeline:

```text
source phase record
  -> keyed reconstruction
  -> Pi_Q projection
  -> mask classification
  -> normalized FIT view
  -> comparison / receipt.
```

The pipeline preserves identities, normalized geometry and declared classes.
Raw pixel coordinates and digit splits are view-dependent. `RETURN` is a
comparison/routing operation here; no next-state mutation was found.

### C. SNCE typed carrier profile

The tested `101*2^k` family admits a reversible parity/factor-four coding and
roundtrips exactly. The profile is a lossless representation grammar, not a
predictive process. `101` is an exact factor of `F_50`, but `F_50` supplies no
unique intrinsic selector: `151` is an equal rank-50 peer and decimal
palindromy is non-unique. Base 101 therefore remains an external typed choice
unless an independent selector contract is supplied.

### D. Coordinate and basis views

The Descartes substitution, finite Fourier coordinates and Cayley map each
passed within their declared scope:

- `y=x+6n` produces the exact `(-6)^0...(-6)^5` coefficient tail and is a
  coordinate change only;
- the complete eleven-component DFT reconstructs the eLine samples exactly;
  the first harmonic carries `83.32598611%` of non-DC energy but is not the
  full signal;
- the upper-half-plane and disk records preserve all 55 tested hyperbolic
  pair distances to numerical precision;
- the sampled eLine curve is not a Poincare geodesic.

These are reversible or exact views of a declared carrier. They do not supply
a unique continuous curve, physical spectrum or shared causal geometry.

### E. Reference and calibration layer

E8/H4, established prime facts and standard Poincare/Fourier mathematics stay
reference controls. They calibrate the instruments and vocabularies; they do
not become identities of Root7, Ghostgrid, SNCE or Five-H.
