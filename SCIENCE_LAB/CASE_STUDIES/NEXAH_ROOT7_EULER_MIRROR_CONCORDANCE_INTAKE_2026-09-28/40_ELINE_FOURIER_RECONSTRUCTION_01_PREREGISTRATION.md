## Frozen Fourier convention

For `N=11`:

```text
F_k = sum_(t=0..N-1) s_t exp(-2*pi*i*k*t/N)
s_t = (1/N) sum_(k=0..N-1) F_k exp(+2*pi*i*k*t/N).
```

## Mandatory checks

1. all frozen source hashes match;
2. source dimensions and extracted y positions match the frozen values;
3. normalized samples have minimum `-1` and maximum `+1`;
4. full DFT/IDFT reconstruction has maximum absolute error `<=1e-12`;
5. Parseval equality holds to relative error `<=1e-12`;
6. conjugate symmetry holds to `<=1e-12`;
7. the DC plus first harmonic pair contains between `0.82` and `0.85` of
   total spectral energy;
8. the one-harmonic reconstruction RMSE lies between `0.25` and `0.30`, so it
   is dominant but not exact;
9. retaining harmonics through `k=2` contains more than `0.94` of energy;
10. retain the conclusion as finite representation equivalence only, not a
    physical frequency law or proof that the drawn curve was generated by a
    Fourier process.
