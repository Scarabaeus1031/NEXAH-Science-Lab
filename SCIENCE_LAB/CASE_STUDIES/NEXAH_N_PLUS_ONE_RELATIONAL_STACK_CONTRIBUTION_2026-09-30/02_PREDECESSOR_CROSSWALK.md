## Present synthesis

The candidate has three deliberately separate layers:

1. **Number layer** — optional residue or prime-index channels.
2. **Coordinate layer** — local records/meshes/frames remain bounded and
   independently addressable.
3. **Relation layer** — the Binder stores mappings, provenance, I-L-A-U,
   tolerances, redundant paths and return residuals.

The synthesis is not an identity claim between these layers. A residue vector
is not a coordinate mesh, and a coordinate transform is not automatically an
information-preserving map.

## QR analogy boundary

The QR analogy supplies an orientation design principle:

- multiple recognizable anchors make reading direction and placement
  recoverable;
- asymmetry matters;
- redundancy can support detection or bounded correction.

The candidate is not a QR Code variant. QR finder patterns do not constitute
three autonomous coordinate systems, and QR error correction does not prove
that arbitrary semantic relations are recoverable.

## Prior-art boundary

The following are established and therefore excluded from novelty claims:

- local scene coordinates and transform hierarchies in glTF and OpenUSD;
- graph and frame transformations;
- cycle consistency and map/transform synchronization;
- the Chinese remainder theorem;
- redundant residue number systems and error correction;
- QR position detection and Reed-Solomon error correction.

## Candidate remainder after comparison

The potentially distinctive remainder is the combined architecture and its
human-facing purpose:

> A scalable stack of bounded representations whose mappings, losses,
> additions, unresolved parts, provenance and return consistency are stored as
> first-class inspectable data without collapsing the records into one
> authoritative master representation.

Whether this remainder is scientifically novel or practically superior is
unknown until the minimum comparison is executed and a targeted literature
and patent search is performed against a frozen claim.
