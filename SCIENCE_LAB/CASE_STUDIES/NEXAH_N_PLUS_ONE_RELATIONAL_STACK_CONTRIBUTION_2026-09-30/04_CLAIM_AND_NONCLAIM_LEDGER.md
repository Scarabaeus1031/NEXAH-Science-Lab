## Terminology boundary

- **Record**: one bounded representation with identity and provenance.
- **Frame**: the coordinate/reference context in which a record is expressed.
- **Mesh**: optional geometric carrier; not every record must be a mesh.
- **Relation**: a typed map with declared domain, codomain and information
  boundary.
- **Binder**: the contract that stores and tests relations; not an authority
  that merges records.
- **Return**: a declared comparison after one or more transitions; not proof
  of global identity.
- **Residual**: discrepancy under the frozen comparator; not automatically an
  error or causal diagnosis.
- **Parity**: redundant check information only when redundancy is actually
  encoded.
