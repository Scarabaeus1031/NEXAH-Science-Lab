## Novelty ceiling

The possible novelty is a disciplined visual and typed orientation method for
keeping observer-relative records, transforms, invariants, losses and return
boundaries together. No mathematical, algorithmic, educational-utility or
physical novelty is established by this case.

## Follow-on boundary

A later usefulness study would require an external reader task and a strong
standard teaching baseline. This package does not activate that study.
