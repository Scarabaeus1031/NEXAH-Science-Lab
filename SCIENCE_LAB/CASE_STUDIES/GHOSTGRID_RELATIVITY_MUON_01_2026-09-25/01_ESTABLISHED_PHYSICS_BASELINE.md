# Established physics baseline

Status: `ESTABLISHED_PHYSICS / SOURCE_BOUND`

## Source facts

The Particle Data Group 2025 listing gives the muon mean life at rest as

\[
\tau_0=(2.1969811\pm0.0000022)\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{s}.
\]

Source: [Particle Data Group — muon listing](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/listings/rpp2025-list-muon.pdf).

CERN describes charged-pion decay in the atmosphere as a source of muons and
records that atmospheric muons reach Earth's surface and penetrate below
ground. Source: [CERN — Cosmic rays](https://home.cern/science/physics/cosmic-rays-particles-outer-space/).

The 1977 CERN muon-storage-ring experiment measured relativistic muon
lifetimes at \(\gamma=29.33\) and found agreement with special-relativistic time
dilation. Source: [Bailey et al., Nature 268 (1977), CERN record](https://cds.cern.ch/record/878581).

## Standard relations

For speed \(v=\beta c\),

\[
\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\beta^2}}.
\]

For two events on the muon's worldline:

\[
\Delta t_{\mathrm{Earth}}=\gamma\,\Delta\tau,
\qquad
L_{\mathrm{muon}}=\frac{L_{\mathrm{Earth}}}{\gamma}.
\]

The one-dimensional Lorentz transformation is

\[
x'=\gamma(x-vt),
\qquad
t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right).
\]

Its inverse is obtained by changing \(v\) to \(-v\). The invariant interval is

\[
c^2\Delta t^2-\Delta x^2=c^2\Delta t'^2-\Delta x'^2=c^2\Delta\tau^2
\]

for the timelike muon trajectory.

For a fixed path length \(L\), constant \(\beta\), and exponential decay, the
idealized survival probability is

\[
P_{\mathrm{survive}}(L)=
\exp\left(-\frac{L}{\beta\gamma c\tau_0}\right).
\]

This is a simplified teaching fixture. Real atmospheric muons have a
distribution of production altitudes, energies, paths and energy losses.

## Causal-order boundary

Creation and later detection or decay on a muon's timelike worldline retain
their causal order in every inertial frame. An inverse Lorentz transformation
changes coordinates back; it does not make the muon undecay and does not run
physical time backward.
