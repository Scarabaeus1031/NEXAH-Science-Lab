# Scope and authority

## Exact question

Can the existing Ghostgrid / Fugennetz / return-with-trace vocabulary represent
the standard cosmic-muon relativity example without changing the physics,
confusing inverse coordinate transformation with physical time reversal, or
turning the illustrative factor near 22 into a privileged NEXAH number?

## Inputs

- the established Lorentz transformation and invariant interval;
- Particle Data Group 2025 muon mean-life listing;
- CERN material on cosmic-ray muons and the 1977 storage-ring time-dilation
  measurement;
- existing NEXAH observer, projection, seam, trace, return and residual
  definitions;
- the Owner-provided video transcript and images as prompts for reconstruction,
  not as physics authority.

## Authority order

1. established physics and cited particle-data / experiment sources;
2. deterministic calculation in this package;
3. NEXAH mapping as a representation model;
4. visual or poetic interpretation.

No lower layer may redefine a higher layer.

## Fixed scope

- one-dimensional relative motion at constant velocity;
- two events: atmospheric creation and a selected ground-detection event;
- Earth-frame and muon-rest-frame descriptions;
- full-record Lorentz round trip;
- one deliberately lossy time-only projection;
- explicit separation of inverse, return, trace and time reversal.

Entropy, the thermodynamic arrow of time, Olbers' paradox, gravitation,
accelerated frames and general relativity are outside this first case. They may
be separate later cases; they are not silently inferred here.

## Stop rule and completion

The case stops when the standard equations, one deterministic numerical
fixture, one exact round trip, one lossy-return boundary and one claim ledger
are present and verified. Those conditions are met. No active research cycle
remains.
