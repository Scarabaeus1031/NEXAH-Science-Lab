# Grip, gap and boundary-coupling annotation

## Bounded vocabulary

`GRIP` names a declared retention or coupling constraint that keeps distinguishable parts in an operational relation.

`GAP` names a preserved separation, clearance or unresolved interval between parts. A gap does not by itself imply absence, failure or disconnection.

`BOUNDARY` names the declared interface across which contact, passage, comparison, projection or return is described.

`HARNESS` names a representational arrangement in which several distinct channels are held, routed and compared without being merged into one identity.

These are NEXAH operational metaphors and visual mnemonics. They are not standard mathematical operators or physical laws.

## Preserved distinctions

```text
GRIP != MERGER
GAP != NONEXISTENCE
GAP != DISCONNECTION
CONTACT != IDENTITY
COUPLING != CAUSATION
CLOSURE != IDENTITY
BOUNDARY != ONTOLOGICAL SUBSTANCE
VISUAL LOCK != VERIFIED PHYSICAL LOCK
HARNESS != AUTONOMOUS MACHINE AUTHORITY
```

The shortest bounded reading is:

```text
THE GRIP HOLDS A DECLARED RELATION.
THE GAP PRESERVES THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE PARTS.
THE BOUNDARY DECLARES WHERE THE RELATION IS TESTED OR REPRESENTED.
```

## Three visual lock families

The visual `THREE LOCKS · ONE RELATION` places three distinct mechanisms side by side:

- `CONTACT LOCK`: direct contact or seam relation.
- `BARYCENTER LOCK`: a relational reference around a declared center. It becomes mathematical only when positions and weights or masses are defined.
- `LINK LOCK`: articulated connection with pins, clasp and explicit gaps.

They share a documentary theme of retained relation. They are not the same mechanism and do not establish operator equivalence.

## Grip/gate/counterhold harness

The visual `MAKE IT TIGHT` supplies a mechanical mnemonic:

- a pin locates or hinges a connection;
- a gate controls admissible passage;
- counterhold preserves opposing support;
- a gap keeps channels or parts distinguishable;
- a seal marks a declared closure condition;
- a return path can close a cycle without proving identity.

The colored channels remain separate representations. Their common harness does not prove a shared ontology, force, resonance or causal mechanism.

## Relation to LQE-01

The annotation can clarify LQE-01's existing source/body/receiver/view separation: projection depends on declared geometry while the parts remain distinct. It does not change the optical demonstrator, add a new state, define a numerical residual, or expand the closed LQE-01 result.
