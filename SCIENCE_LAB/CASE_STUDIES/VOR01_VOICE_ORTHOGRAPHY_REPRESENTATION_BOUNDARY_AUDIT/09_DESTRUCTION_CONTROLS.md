# 09 — Destruction Controls

| Control | Forced collapse | Observed failure / requirement | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `LETTER = SOUND` | Written units and physical/phonetic events become indistinguishable; multilingual and contextual mappings cannot be represented. | Boundary fails as expected. |
| 2 | `SAME_STRING = SAME_MEANING` | English `content` readings collapse despite documented pronunciation, category and meaning differences. | Boundary fails as expected. |
| 3 | `SIMILAR_STRING = SHARED_ETYMOLOGY` | `content` and `contempt` would be assigned a relation contradicted by their documented histories. | Boundary fails as expected. |
| 4 | Remove `LANGUAGE` | `oi`, `ou`, `ei`, `e`, `i` and `y` no longer select a unique language-specific mapping. | Language required. |
| 5 | Remove orthographic/lexical context | English `e/i/y` and German alternatives cannot be recovered uniquely. | Context required. |
| 6 | Treat `CON|TIN|ENT` as authoritative | The supplied segmentation conflicts with the independently attested lexical history and lacks documented units. | Segmentation rejected as evidence. |
| 7 | Replace words with neutral IDs | The distinctions among form, pronunciation, lexical item, meaning, language and history remain. | Control survives. |

`DESTRUCTION_CONTROLS=7_OF_7_BOUNDARY_FAILURES_OR_REQUIRED_CONTEXT_CONFIRMED`
