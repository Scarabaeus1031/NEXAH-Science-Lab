# 04 — VOWEL / VOKAL Control

| Relation | Finding |
|---|---|
| String identity | No: `VOWEL != VOKAL`. |
| Pronunciation identity | No claim; the cited English and German entries document different language-specific forms. |
| Semantic relation | Both dictionary entries denote the corresponding linguistic category of vowel sound/letter. |
| Translation relation | `TRANSLATION_EQUIVALENT` within that bounded category. |
| Etymological relation | `HISTORICALLY_RELATED_VIA_LATIN`, documented independently by Merriam-Webster and Duden. |

The historical conclusion is source-led, not inferred from visible similarity. `R_EN(X)=VOWEL` and `R_DE(X)=VOKAL` preserve only the selected lexical category `X`; they do not preserve string or pronunciation.

`VOWL`, `OWL`, `AWE`, `OWE`, `WOLF`, `WULF` and `WAU` play no role and remain quarantined.
