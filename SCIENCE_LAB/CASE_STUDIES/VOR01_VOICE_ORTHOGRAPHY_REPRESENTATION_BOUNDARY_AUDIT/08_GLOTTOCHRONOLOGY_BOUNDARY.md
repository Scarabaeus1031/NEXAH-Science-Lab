# 08 — Glottochronology Boundary

Merriam-Webster defines glottochronology as a linguistic method that uses a presumed vocabulary-replacement rate to estimate when genetically related languages diverged. Larousse likewise describes the Swadesh-associated basic lexical stock/rate premise.

VOR-01:

- estimates no divergence time;
- assumes no lexical replacement rate;
- measures no basic-vocabulary retention;
- performs no lexicostatistics.

Therefore:

```text
GLOTTOCHRONOLOGY_DIRECT_TEST=NO
GLOTTOCHRONOLOGY_RELATION=METHODOLOGICALLY_RELEVANT_BOUNDARY_ONLY
```

Careful separation of spelling, pronunciation, lexical identity and attested history is relevant to any later historical-linguistic work, but that relevance is not a glottochronological result or authorization.
