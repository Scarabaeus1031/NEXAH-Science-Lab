# 12 — Boundary and Nonclaims

VOR-01 supports only a documentary boundary result:

- orthographic form, phonological form, phonetic realization, lexical item, meaning, language and historical relation must remain typed separately;
- cross-language translation can preserve a bounded category without preserving string or pronunciation;
- language and local orthographic/lexical context are required for the selected mappings;
- etymological claims require authoritative historical evidence.

VOR-01 does **not** establish:

- a new linguistic discovery, etymology or language-origin theory;
- a universal model of writing, speech or meaning;
- that every letter maps to one phoneme, or every execution of speech produces one invariant sound;
- that similar form or sound proves identity, common ancestry or causal order;
- that `CON|TIN|ENT`, `LM` or an ocean boundary has linguistic standing;
- a glottochronological measurement or future research program;
- a NEXAH-specific linguistic gain beyond disciplined typing;
- a new operator, NEXAH architecture change, ORION capability or implementation.

Frozen controls remain:

```text
SIMILARITY != IDENTITY
REPRESENTATION != OBJECT
RECONSTRUCTABLE != ATTESTED
LEXICAL_ORDER != CAUSAL_ORDER
SUGGESTIVE_LABEL != FORMAL_EVIDENCE
PHONETICALLY_PLAUSIBLE != HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED
```
