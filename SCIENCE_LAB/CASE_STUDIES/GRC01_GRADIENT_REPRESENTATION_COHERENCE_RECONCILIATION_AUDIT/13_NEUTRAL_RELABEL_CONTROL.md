# 13 — Neutral Relabel Control

Remove all suggestive labels:

`sampled ordered states -> estimated scalar field -> local derivative -> comparison with another vector quantity -> local scalar relation -> thresholded candidate subset`

The schema remains mathematically intelligible without `NEXAH`, `ORION`, `LENS`, `JANUS`, `APERTURE`, `GATE`, or `COHERENCE`. Its surviving content is ordinary dynamical-systems, statistical-estimation, differential, and numerical-analysis structure.

What does not survive neutral relabeling is the historical interpretive load: “stability,” “navigation,” “gate,” and cross-system meaning. Those require separate evidence and are not created by labels.
