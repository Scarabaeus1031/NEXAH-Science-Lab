# 13 — Boundary and Nonclaims

MJTR-01 is a post-hoc type reconciliation only.

It does not claim:

- a new fact about Mandelbrot or Julia sets;
- exact Julia-set construction in MJPR-01;
- global parameter identifiability from finite observations;
- Julia-set identity from registered array equality;
- nontrivial complementarity from reconstruction-set contraction;
- equivalence of parameter and dynamical planes;
- equivalence of a rule, its execution, its result, or its trace;
- that repeated symbols introduce extra parameters or intrinsic roles;
- a transformation, quotient, transport relation, new operator, or new ontology;
- any quaternion, `i/j/k`, Q1–Q6, slice-dynamics, or visual-axis interpretation;
- any validation from GRC-01 or historical gradient/coherence/navigation visuals.

The type ledger uses standard mathematical and documentary terms. It preserves MJPR-01’s finite-resolution limitations and closed numerical findings without rerunning them.
