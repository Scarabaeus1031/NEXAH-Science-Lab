# 06 — Observation / Reconstruction Boundary

## Typed structure

For a declared observation rule and source representation:

`y=P(E_c)`

and, on the frozen candidate family,

`Θ_P(y)={c'∈C_441 : P(E_c')=y}`.

The conceptual exact-object notation `y=P(J_c)` is admissible only when `P` is actually defined on exact Julia sets. MJPR-01 instead registered finite observations of `E_c`; MJTR-01 preserves that operational domain.

## Closed findings retained

- P1 and P5 were singleton for all 441 tested parameter values at the registered resolution.
- P2, P3, and P4 produced ambiguous reconstruction sets, with maxima 13, 243, and 5 respectively.
- intersecting reduced-view classes with P5 yielded singletons, but an unrelated fixed 16-bit feature gave the same aggregate contraction for P2.
- therefore contraction was ordinary information addition, not a special geometric complementarity result.

## Boundaries

`OBSERVATION_EQUALS_OBJECT=NO`  
`OBSERVATION_EQUALS_PARAMETER=NO`  
`RECONSTRUCTION_SET_EQUALS_TRUE_PARAMETER=NO`  
`NUMERICAL_EQUALITY_EQUALS_JULIA_SET_IDENTITY=NO`  
`VISUAL_SIMILARITY_EQUALS_PARAMETER_IDENTITY=NO`

When `|Θ_y|>1`, the registered observation is ambiguous on the frozen candidate family. When a complementary observation reduces `Θ_y`, this is reconstruction improvement/information addition—not transformation of `c`.
