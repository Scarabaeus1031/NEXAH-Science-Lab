# 06 — Neutral Relabelling Control

| Original | Neutral form | What survives | What disappears |
|---|---|---|---|
| `{2,3,5}` | `{A,B,C}` | three distinguishable labels | prime ordering and all arithmetic facts |
| `11,22,33` as family values | `X1,X2,X3` plus registered generator | translation/scale only if numeric carrier and rule are retained | repeated decimal digits and “stream” language |
| `6,9` glyph masks | `G1,G2` plus `ROTATE_180` | rigid rotation match | numeral identity and arithmetic meaning |
| Delta inputs | `{X1,X2,X3}→Y` | three-to-one convergence | delta name and geographical imagery |

The local operators survive neutral labels only when their typed rule remains.
No cross-representation recurrence appears after relabelling. Claims based on
`stream`, `mirror`, `triangle`, `MAIN`, `MAAS` or `flow` disappear because they
were not operator definitions.
