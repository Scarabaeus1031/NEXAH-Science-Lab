# OVR-01 — Four-Question Vocabulary Coverage

| Question | Minimum vocabulary | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| WHAT IS IT? | OBJECT, ID, STATE | COMPLETE |
| HOW IS IT ORIENTED? | ORIENTATION, REFLECT/ROTATE/TRANSLATE where executed | COMPLETE |
| WHAT HAPPENED? | OPERATOR, EXECUTION, TRACE, RESULT/OBSERVATION | COMPLETE |
| WHERE DID IT COME FROM? | PROVENANCE, DERIVATION, GENERATION | COMPLETE |

The four questions remain expressible after neutral relabelling because their distinctions depend on types and relations, not evocative names.
