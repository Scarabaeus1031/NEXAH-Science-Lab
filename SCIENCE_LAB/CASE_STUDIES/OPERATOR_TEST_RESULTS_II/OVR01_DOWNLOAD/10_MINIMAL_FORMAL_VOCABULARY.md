# OVR-01 — Minimal Formal Vocabulary

## Layer 1 — Types / things

`OBJECT`, `STATE`, `ORIENTATION`, `PROVENANCE`, `GENERATION`, `EXECUTION`, `TRACE`

## Layer 2 — Operations

`REFLECT`, `ROTATE`, `BRANCH`, `TRANSLATE`, `REPEAT`, `SCALE`, `CONVERGE`, `BIND`, `TRANSFORM`, `COPY`, `PROJECT`, `NORMALIZE`, `CORRECT`, `OBSERVE`

## Layer 3 — Relations, conditions, and results

`CONSTRAINT`, `PROJECTION`, `EQUIVALENCE`, `DERIVATION`, `RETURN_RELATION`, `INVERSE_RELATION`, `OBSERVATION`

This is a reconciliation subset, not a new ontology or language. Generic `TRANSFORM` remains an operator schema; specialized operators are retained because they encode materially different actions.
