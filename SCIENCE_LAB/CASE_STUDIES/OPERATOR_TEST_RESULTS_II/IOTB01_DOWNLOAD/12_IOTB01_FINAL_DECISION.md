# 12 — IOTB-01 Final Decision

## Primary result

**A_THREE_CLOSED_AUDITS_FORM_COHERENT_NONCOLLAPSING_BINDER**

The five-field object record and the separate operator/execution/derivation
types represent all imported distinctions without identifying them. The binder
is minimal for these three audits and generic: it is neither a new ontology nor
a universal NEXAH architecture.

Its strongest surviving statement is:

> An endpoint can tell us what is observable now; orientation tells us how that
> record is posed; identity tells us which object record is tracked; provenance
> and generation tell us where it came from; only an attested trace tells us
> what execution history was recorded.
