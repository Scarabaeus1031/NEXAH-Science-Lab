# 08 — Zero / One Identity-Element Check

For every number x in the respective algebraic domains:

```text
x+0=x
x*1=x
```

Therefore 0 is the additive identity and 1 is the multiplicative identity.
These are algebraic action properties. They provide no derivation edge, source
record, manufacturing history or generation relation.
