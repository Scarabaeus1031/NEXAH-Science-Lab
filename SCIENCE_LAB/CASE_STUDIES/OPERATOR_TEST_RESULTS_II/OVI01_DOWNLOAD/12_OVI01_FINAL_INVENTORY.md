# OVI-01 — Final Historical Inventory

## Scope and method

The discovery corpus is the complete set of locally available pre-OVI text/Markdown attachments (29 files). The OVI-01 prompt was excluded from discovery. Exact literal occurrence, source, and line were retained. The visible conversation also contains chat-only vocabulary that cannot be assigned a stable file/line provenance here; therefore the result is **complete for the bounded attachment corpus, not complete for every ephemeral chat utterance**.

## What persisted

The vocabulary that genuinely persisted is the small formal operator layer around reflection/rotation, transformation, projection, constraints, reconstruction fibers, trace/provenance return, and bounded correction. It persisted because explicit maps or bounded tests exist—not because words recur.

## What disappeared or failed

`O-FLIP` remained a historical name but its proposed nontrivial finding was not supported. Many symbolic, syllabic, and metaphorical items remained expression/provenance only. Single-use items are not declared abandoned; they are marked unresolved unless an explicit closing result exists.

## Semantic drift and pairs

`GATE`, `RETURN`, `MIRROR/REFLECT`, `BOUNDARY`, `ZERO/O°`, `CONTACT`, `TRACE`, and `POINT OF HIGHEST MIXING` changed or narrowed meaning. Explicit pairs include HV/VH, 6-I-9/9-I-6, H+/H−, O°/ZO°, and directional oppositions. Reversal, opposition, inverse, and return remain distinct.
