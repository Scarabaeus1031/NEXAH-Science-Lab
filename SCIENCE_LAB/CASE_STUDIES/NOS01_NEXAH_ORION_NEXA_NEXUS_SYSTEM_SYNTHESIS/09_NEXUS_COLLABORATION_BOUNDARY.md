# NEXUS Collaboration Boundary

NEXUS-01 supports a derived record linking states, traces, evidence, interpretations, knowledge status, criteria and human decisions. It does not make collaboration a truth engine.

Required non-equivalences:

- more observers `!=` more truth;
- more representations `!=` more objects;
- more agreement `!=` validation;
- more data `!=` more information;
- more interpretations `!=` more knowledge.

Collaboration can legitimately improve coverage, falsification pressure, edge-case discovery, provenance, independent replication, alternative representations, declared criteria and error detection—provided observer identity, inputs, methods, disagreements and decision authority are recorded.

Human Authority is not collapsed into NEXUS. Knowledge-status records can organize what people know or dispute; they cannot decide goals, validate claims by consensus, or continue work without authorization.
