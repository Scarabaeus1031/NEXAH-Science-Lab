Outcomes distinguish preservation, transformation, difference, ambiguity, unresolved and undefined states, abstention and error. Results, traces, history and provenance remain separate but linked. Reconstruction does not recreate the original event; return does not erase path history. Interpretations and knowledge-status records remain downstream.
