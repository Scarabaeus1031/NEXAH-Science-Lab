# Final NOS-01 Decision

## Decision

`B_COHERENT_ARCHITECTURE_RECOVERED_WITH_INTERFACE_GAPS`

The frozen corpus supports a coherent, non-collapsing architecture centered on registered identity/state, typed representation and change, declared comparison, explicit loss and uncertainty, result/trace/history/provenance separation, and a preserved Human Authority boundary. WNI-01 supplies a bounded positive example: winding can survive representation change even when coordinates do not, provided order, closure, reference and capacity remain adequate.

This is not class A. ORION V1 is narrow; RID-01 defines a schema but no validator, serializer, adapter or integrated runtime; end-to-end conformance and human-readable RID binding remain open. The proposed NEXA layer is underdefined in authoritative predecessors and cannot be promoted to a canonical center. NEXUS is useful as a derived epistemic/collaboration record, not as truth or decision authority.

The stripped machine survives removal of historical names, glyphs, numerology and analogy. The red thread is supported. No new primitive or activation is required.
