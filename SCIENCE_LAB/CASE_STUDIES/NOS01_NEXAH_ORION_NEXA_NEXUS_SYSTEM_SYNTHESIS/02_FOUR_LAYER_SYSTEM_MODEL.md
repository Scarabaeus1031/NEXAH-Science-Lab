# Four-Layer System Model

| Term | Supplied role | Classification | Documentary finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEXAH | orientation discipline; distinction, claim and representation governance | `SUPPORTED` | OLS, TITAN-00 and the closed audits consistently govern typed representations, claims, losses, provenance and stopping. |
| ORION | deterministic relation/transformation/comparison/orientation machinery | `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` | Certified ORION V1 supplies a narrow deterministic structural pipeline and frozen membrane. The broader machinery is heterogeneous and not one integrated general engine. |
| NEXA | declared object/state/relational configuration under examination | `UNDERDEFINED` | No canonical NEXA type or definition occurs in the inspected authoritative predecessors. The phrase may be a local documentary alias only; it is not a recovered system layer. |
| NEXUS | human/collaborative interpretation, knowledge-status, criterion, decision and continuation | `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` | NEXUS-01 supports a derived epistemic record and a human-authority boundary. NEXUS itself is not human authority, truth or an implemented collaboration system. |

The proposed four-layer picture is therefore useful as a **qualified documentary map**, not as a new canonical ontology. NEXAH, ORION and Human Authority remain distinct. `NEXA` cannot be placed at the canonical center of the architecture on current evidence.

Explicit rejections: `NEXA != TRUTH`, `NEXA != ESSENCE`, `NEXA != REALITY_ITSELF`, `NEXA != UNIVERSAL_FIELD`.
