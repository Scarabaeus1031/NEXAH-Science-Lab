# Red-Thread Test

Result: `SUPPORTED`.

The frozen corpus does not demand identical representations. It requires typed representation changes, declared comparison criteria, explicit preservation/loss, and recoverable history/provenance. This is supported by OLS, SWM/RID, the ORION membrane, OTC, RPR, NEXUS and the WNI controls.

| Clause | Concrete architectural counterpart |
|---|---|
| SEE THE RELATION. | registered graph/relation, source/state identity and declared comparison scope |
| KEEP THE DIFFERENCE. | difference is retained separately from equality, residual and loss |
| TYPE THE CHANGE. | representation/view/transformation/event types with frame and criterion |
| PRESERVE THE HISTORY. | revision, trace, history and provenance remain distinct and linked |
| FAIL CLOSED. | invalid, undefined, ambiguous or unsupported transitions do not fabricate results |

Boundaries: recoverability does not imply identity; a preserved invariant is not a complete description; declared criteria do not make a claim true.
