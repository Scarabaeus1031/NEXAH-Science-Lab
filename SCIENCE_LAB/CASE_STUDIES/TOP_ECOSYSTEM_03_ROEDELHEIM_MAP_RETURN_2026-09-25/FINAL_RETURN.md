## What the test establishes

TOP remains useful when the material is historically ambiguous rather than
mathematically preregistered. It keeps the source, the selected visual, the
historical reading and the currentness receipt distinct. Visible river bends
and the bounded island context survive the return; the exact split, return and
cross-map location do not.

The transfer result is therefore methodological, not historical novelty:

> TOP can carry unequal evidence states across a historical map lineage without
> forcing them into one global pass or one global failure.

## What the test does not establish

- no verified date or complete archival chain for every selected file;
- no georeferenced equality between the Flurkarte and another map;
- no exact former channel beneath a named modern street;
- no complete five-stage hydrological reconstruction;
- no hidden survey geometry, military code or causal landscape mechanism;
- no validation of the stronger separate historical closing report;
- no general proof that TOP works in every discipline;
- no research, product, publication or outreach activation.

## Generality finding

AXIS08 tested differentiated return where numerical tolerance was available.
Rödelheim tests the same discipline where measurement is absent and historical
provenance is incomplete. The method still produces a useful bounded result,
but the statuses change: numerical return becomes not applicable, while
provenance, selection and interpretation residuals become central.

That difference is the evidence that TOP is functioning as a perspective and
audit instrument rather than copying the logic of one mathematical case.
