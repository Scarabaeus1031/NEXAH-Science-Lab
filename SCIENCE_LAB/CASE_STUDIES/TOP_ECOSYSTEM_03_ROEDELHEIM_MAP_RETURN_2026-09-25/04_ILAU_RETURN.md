# 04 - I-L-A-U and Return

## I - retained

- the primary map bytes, title field and visible Flur/water content;
- the distinction between three map scales;
- visible curved water morphology near Rödelheim;
- the bounded local island reading;
- explicit separation of source marks, derived annotations and interpretation;
- the report's negative claim boundaries;
- Science Lab evidence custody and Human interpretive authority.

## L - lost

- full archival catalogue metadata and verified map date are absent from the
  selected image bytes;
- cropping and layout remove parts of the original map context;
- the comparison visual does not retain a coordinate-level trace from every
  mark back to the source image;
- scale detail is lost in the two smaller regional representations;
- the Mission Control receipt summarizes rather than embeds the maps.

## A - introduced

- crops, circles, station numbers, labels and the five-step ordering;
- comparison across three unequal scales;
- the concepts `Zufluss`, `Teilung`, `Schleife`, `Insel` and `Rückführung` as
  an analytic sequence;
- report prose, evidence classes and current claim ceiling;
- TOP edge and return status labels.

## U - unresolved

- exact dating and complete archive provenance of the primary reproduction;
- reproducible crop coordinates and transform recipe;
- the exact branching and reconnection points of the water system;
- pointwise persistence across the Flurkarte and regional maps;
- modern location identity without controlled georeferencing;
- generalization to the remaining Rödelheim corpus.
