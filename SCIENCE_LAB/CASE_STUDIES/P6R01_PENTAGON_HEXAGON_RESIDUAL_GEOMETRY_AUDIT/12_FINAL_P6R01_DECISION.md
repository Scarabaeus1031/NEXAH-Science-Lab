# Final P6R-01 Decision

## Primary decision

`E_SOURCE_GEOMETRY_UNDERDEFINED`

The regular pentagon is reproducible, but the proposed sixfold inner object is not. The current record does not identify six circle radii, the radius of their center orbit, the initial phase, the set operation that forms `H`, or the presence and role of a central circle. Locally available Pentagon–Hexagon demos define attractor points rather than circle regions. The known hexagon visual defines a graph rather than a six-circle set.

Consequently, intersection, residual, union, boundary, topology, metric, arc, tangency, and containment questions have no unique answer. Choosing one candidate construction would invent the source geometry. The audit therefore closes at the admission gate.

The only unconditional mathematics retained is ordinary angular arithmetic and group separation: 72 degrees for fivefold steps, 60 degrees for sixfold steps, a 12-degree common sampling increment, `D5 != D6`, and no implication of a common `D30` symmetry.
