# Group Symmetry Control

For the defined regular pentagon:

`Sym(P)=D5`.

For a genuinely sixfold centered construction made from one six-element orbit of congruent circles:

`D6` would be the candidate symmetry group, subject to the set-construction rule preserving those symmetries.

`D5_DISTINCT_FROM_D6=YES`

The rotational subgroups `C5` and `C6` have only the identity in common. The common 12-degree sampling grid comes from arithmetic refinement of angular steps; it is not a symmetry group of the combined configuration.

`COMMON_GRID_EQUALS_COMMON_SYMMETRY_GROUP=NO`

No shared `D30` symmetry was inferred. Because `H` is underdefined, no actual combined reflection subgroup was classified.
