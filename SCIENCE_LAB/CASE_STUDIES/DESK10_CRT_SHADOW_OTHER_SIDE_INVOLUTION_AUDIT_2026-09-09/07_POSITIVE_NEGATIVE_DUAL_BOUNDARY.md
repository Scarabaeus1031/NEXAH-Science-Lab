# Positive / negative / dual boundary

- `POSITIVE REPRESENTATIVE`: the canonical integer in `[0,30999]`.
- `MODULAR NEGATIVE`: `S_N(x)=(-x) mod31000`, represented canonically as a nonnegative integer.
- `DUAL DISPLAY`: showing `x` and `S_N(x)` as paired coordinates or values.
- `RETURN`: applying the involution twice to recover `x`, or CRT-reconstructing a residue tuple to its class.

This is mathematical orientation language only. It does not imply negative physical light speed, reverse causation, energy transport, or a physical shadow mechanism.

Permitted metaphor ceilings:

- `LIGHT <-> SHADOW`: boundary-conditioned reception analogy.
- `CRY <-> ECHO`: delayed or filtered return analogy.
- `EVENT <-> RECORD`: source-to-observation analogy.

None is mathematical evidence or physical validation.
