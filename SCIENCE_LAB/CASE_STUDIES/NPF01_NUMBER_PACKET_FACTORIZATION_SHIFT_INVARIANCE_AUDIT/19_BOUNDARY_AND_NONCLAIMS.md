# Boundary and Nonclaims

NPF-01 preserves these closed predecessor boundaries without reopening or modifying GLF-01, SFM-01, PAT-01, ATS-01, CROA-01, or PRR-01:

- `OBJECT != REPRESENTATION`
- `CHOSEN_CUT_IS_NOT_INTRINSIC`
- `RELABEL_DEPENDENT_PATTERNS_EXIST`
- `PARTITION_DEPENDENT_PATTERNS_EXIST`
- `PRIME_GAPS_REQUIRE_EXPLICIT_ARITHMETIC_DEFINITION`
- `VISUAL_SIMILARITY != STRUCTURAL_IDENTITY`
- `SAME_STRING / SAME_DIGITS != SAME_NUMBER_OBJECT` when segmentation differs.

NPF-01 does not claim:

- that packet selection reveals a hidden code;
- that decimal segmentation has intrinsic arithmetic meaning;
- that one local packet is statistically rare or significant;
- that approximate decimal resemblance is explanatory;
- that E/F, G/H, or another packet is a natural mathematical family;
- that graph layout creates arithmetic relations;
- that prime indices have lexical, physical, or symbolic meaning;
- that the supplied HTML validates its own claims;
- that NEXAH discovered a new prime, operator, ontology, theorem, architecture, or scientific field.

The package establishes only exact bounded arithmetic and typing boundaries between integer, digit representation, segmentation, packet, relation, and display.
