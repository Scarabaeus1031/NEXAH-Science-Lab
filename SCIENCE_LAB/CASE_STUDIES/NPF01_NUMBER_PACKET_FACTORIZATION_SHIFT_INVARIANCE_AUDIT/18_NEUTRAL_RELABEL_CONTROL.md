# Neutral Relabel Control

Packet labels A–K were replaced conceptually with `P01` through `P11`; relation-edge labels with `R01` and `R02`; and graph node styling with uniform neutral identifiers.

The ledger values, exact equations, ratio reciprocity, and fixed null comparison remain unchanged. Interpretations that depend on a letter, color, spatial layout, or memorable expression do not survive.

`NEUTRAL_RELABEL_SURVIVES=YES`

This result applies to the typed arithmetic structure, not to mnemonic or semantic readings.
