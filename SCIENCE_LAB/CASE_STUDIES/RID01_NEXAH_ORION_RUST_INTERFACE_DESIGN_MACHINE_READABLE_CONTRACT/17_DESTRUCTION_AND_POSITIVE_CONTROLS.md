# Destruction and Positive Controls

## Destruction controls

The schema differentiates or rejects all 19 attempted collapses:

`Source/View`, `Graph/Embedding`, `Observable/MeasurementEvent`, `MeasurementEvent/MeasurementValue`, `MeasurementValue/Readout`, `Rule/Event`, `Event/Result`, `Result/Provenance`, `Transform/Augment`, `Augment/Partition`, `Difference/Residual`, `Reconstruct/Decide`, `Ambiguity/Abstain`, `Return/Reset`, `Return/Restart`, `StateEquality/HistoryEquality`, `Zero/Null`, `Unknown/Absent`, `SameResult/SameProvenance`.

`DESTRUCTION_CONTROLS=19_OF_19_DIFFERENTIATED_OR_REJECTED`

## Positive controls

The contract represents all 12 required positives: one graph/multiple embeddings; one embedding/multiple views; one state/multiple observations; one observable/multiple events; equal result/different provenance; returned state/different history; explicit uncertainty; calibration; frame dependence; composite source; unresolved provenance; and abstention without a decision.

`POSITIVE_CONTROLS=12_OF_12_REPRESENTABLE`
