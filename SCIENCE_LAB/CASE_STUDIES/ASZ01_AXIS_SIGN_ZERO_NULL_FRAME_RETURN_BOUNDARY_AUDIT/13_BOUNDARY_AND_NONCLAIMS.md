# Boundary and Nonclaims

ASZ-01 establishes no new symbolic language, universal duality, operator, physics, mechanics, number theory, ontology, architecture, implementation, research program, or ORION capability.

It does not claim:

- that zero, null, origin, reference, missing value, empty set, and zero vector are interchangeable;
- that negative/positive intrinsically mean left/right, down/up, inward/outward, backward/forward, bad/good, bowl/cap, or any semantic pair;
- that an origin or reference is intrinsic to a general object;
- that every sign reversal is a return;
- that a PRR state return restores path or history;
- that concavity words determine coordinate sign or curvature sign without conventions;
- that a gear is an axis, direction, dimension, space, state, or operator;
- that a changed transmission ratio creates a new geometry;
- that coordinate labels change the underlying object;
- that PRR-01, GLF-01, SFM-01, or RSC-01 was reopened or modified.

The recoverable content is standard mathematics/mechanics plus the already registered PRR involution.
