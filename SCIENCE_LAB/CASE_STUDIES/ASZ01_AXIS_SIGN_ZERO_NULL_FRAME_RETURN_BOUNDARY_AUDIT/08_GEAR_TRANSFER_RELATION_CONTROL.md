# Gear Transfer-Relation Control

The phrase “another gear” is tested only as a standard mechanical candidate: the same modeled mechanism uses a different transmission ratio or selected engagement state.

For an ideal external gear pair with tooth counts `N_in,N_out`, a signed convention may write:

`omega_out/omega_in = -N_in/N_out`.

The magnitude is the speed-transfer ratio; the minus sign records opposite rotation for an external pair under the chosen convention. Other gear arrangements require their own topology and sign convention.

Changing tooth ratio or engaged pair changes the transfer relation among angular speed and torque. It does not create a coordinate dimension, axis, direction, space, or new state ontology.

Results:

- `GEAR_EQUALS_DIMENSION=NO`
- `GEAR_EQUALS_AXIS=NO`
- `GEAR_EQUALS_DIRECTION=NO`
- `GEAR_CHANGE_EQUALS_NEW_SPACE=NO`
- `GEAR_CHANGE_EQUALS_CHANGED_TRANSFER_RELATION=YES_IN_STANDARD_MECHANICAL_MODEL`

Rotation, translation, coupling, rolling contact, and the PRR return relation remain distinct typed roles.
