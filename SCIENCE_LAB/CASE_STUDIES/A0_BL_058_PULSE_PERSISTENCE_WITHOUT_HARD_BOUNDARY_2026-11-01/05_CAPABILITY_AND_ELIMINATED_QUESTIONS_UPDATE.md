# A0-BL-058 — capability and eliminated-questions update

## Capability retained

The Lab can now distinguish, inside one locked deterministic signal family:

- hard-cutoff sensitivity;
- smooth-boundary locking;
- carrier-time persistence;
- sampling instability;
- Fourier reconstruction instability; and
- phase-order sensitivity at fixed harmonic amplitudes.

## Eliminated or bounded questions

- Smoothing the boundary does **not** make the strongest event intrinsic to one
  carrier time in the frozen model.
- Stable Fourier reconstruction does **not** imply a carrier-intrinsic event.
- A magnitude spectrum alone does **not** determine the event: the frozen
  phase control materially changes it.
- Exact reciprocal Alpha/Beta display channels do **not** establish pulse
  origin or carrier Return.

## Still open

Whether an independently measured physical system contains a comparable event
is wholly open and was not approached. No physical successor is implied.
