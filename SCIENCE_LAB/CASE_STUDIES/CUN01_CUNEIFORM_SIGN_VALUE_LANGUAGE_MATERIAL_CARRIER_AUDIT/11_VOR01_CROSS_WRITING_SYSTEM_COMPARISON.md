# 11 — VOR-01 Cross-Writing-System Comparison

## Shared boundary discipline

| VOR-01 layer | CUN-01 counterpart | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| grapheme / orthographic unit | sign identity and sign form | Related type role, not identical category. |
| phoneme / pronunciation | syllabic value and reading | Language-specific relation remains necessary. |
| orthographic string | inscription/sign sequence | Written sequence does not uniquely determine reading without context. |
| lexical item | lexical reading/lemma | Separate from visible sign form. |
| meaning | translation/interpretation | Separate from sign value and lexical identity. |
| language | Sumerian/Akkadian field | Mandatory in both audits. |
| etymological/historical evidence | value/period/corpus attestation | Historical claims remain source-constrained. |

## Cuneiform-specific extensions

- material carrier and impressed mark;
- sign identity versus historically variable sign form;
- syllabic, logographic and determinative functions;
- period/corpus and inscription context;
- lexical-list evidence and palaeographic attestation.

Modern alphabetic `LETTER`, `GRAPHEME` and `PHONEME` do not transfer as a complete cuneiform model. The anti-collapse principles survive, while the domain types require extension.
