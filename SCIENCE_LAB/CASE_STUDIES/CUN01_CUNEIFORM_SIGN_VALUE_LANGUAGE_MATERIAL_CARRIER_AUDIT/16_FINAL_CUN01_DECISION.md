# CUN-01 — Final Decision

## Primary result

`B_PARTIAL_TYPING_CUNEIFORM_SPECIFIC_EXTENSIONS_REQUIRED`

The VOR-01 anti-collapse boundaries remain necessary, and the neutral model survives removal of all suggestive names. They are not sufficient as a complete cuneiform description. Authoritative sources require additional domain fields for material carrier, impressed mark, sign identity/form, attested value type, language, period/corpus, determinative function and inscription context.

Polyvalence, multilingual use, syllabic values, determinatives and lexical-list meta-representation are supported. One bounded first-millennium hermeneutic syllable/wordplay practice is also documented; it is not generalized. Cone geometry, cosmic wedge semantics, ancient NEXAH and a Babylonian operator language are unsupported.
