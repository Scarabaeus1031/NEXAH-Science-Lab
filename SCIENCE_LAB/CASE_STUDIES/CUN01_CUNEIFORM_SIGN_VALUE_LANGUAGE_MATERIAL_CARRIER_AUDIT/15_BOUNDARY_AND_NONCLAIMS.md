# 15 — Boundary and Nonclaims

CUN-01 supports only the following bounded result:

- one sign form can have multiple attested, context-dependent functions/values;
- sign form, sign value, lexical reading and meaning must remain distinct;
- language, period/corpus and inscription context constrain readings;
- determinatives may add semantic classification without ordinary syllabic pronunciation;
- carrier, physical mark, inscription, sign and reading are different records;
- lexical lists document historical meta-representation practices.

CUN-01 does **not** establish:

- ancient NEXAH, a Babylonian operator language or a hidden world model;
- new Assyriology, decipherment, etymology or language history;
- a universal theory of cuneiform signs or all periods/corpora;
- cosmic meanings for wedge orientation;
- semantic content from cone, tablet, pot or other material geometry;
- that documented hermeneutic wordplay describes all ancient writing or thought;
- a unified ontology for alphabetic and cuneiform writing;
- a new operator, architecture change, ORION capability, implementation or research program.

```text
SOURCE != MODEL
SIGN_FORM != SIGN_VALUE
SIGN_VALUE != MEANING
SAME_SCRIPT != SAME_LANGUAGE
MATERIAL_FORM != INSCRIPTION_CONTENT
VISUALLY_PLAUSIBLE != HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED
PHONETICALLY_PLAUSIBLE != HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED
MODERN_ANALOGY != ANCIENT_CONCEPTUAL_IDENTITY
```
