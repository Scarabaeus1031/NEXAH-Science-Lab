# 10 — Wedge Orientation Control

Penn's account of scribal exercises distinguishes vertical, horizontal and corner impressions and combinations of such wedges. It also distinguishes exercises in making wedges from combinations that form meaningful signs. The Met and British Museum document the wedge-shaped stylus/impression technology.

Thus orientation and combination are structurally relevant to the physical realization of sign forms:

```text
WEDGE_ORIENTATION_AFFECTS_SIGN_FORM=YES
```

No cited source maps horizontal, vertical or diagonal wedges to world axes, cosmic axes, transitions or a universal orientation system:

```text
WEDGE_ORIENTATION_IMPLIES_COSMIC_SEMANTICS=NO
```

Structural relevance to graphic composition is not semantic symbolism.
