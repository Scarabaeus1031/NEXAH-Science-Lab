# 09 — Cone / Foundation Object Control

The Met records an inscribed clay foundation cone of Enmetena of Lagash (ca. 2500–2350 BCE). It also records a later Lipit-Eshtar votive cone and relates that object's form to earlier foundation pegs associated with building ground plans, while noting the widened inscription-bearing body.

This establishes that inscribed cones/foundation objects existed and had documented architectural/depositional contexts. It does not establish that their geometry encoded the linguistic content of their inscriptions.
