# 05 — Language / Script Control

DCCLT states that cuneiform was adapted from Sumerian to Akkadian and that the two languages use the same sign inventory differently. Its sign lists distinguish Sumerian lemmas/readings from Akkadian translations. The British Museum likewise identifies Sumerian and Akkadian as the two principal languages written in cuneiform.

Therefore:

```text
SAME_SCRIPT_IMPLIES_SAME_LANGUAGE=NO
MULTILINGUAL_CUNEIFORM_SUPPORTED=YES
SAME_OR_SIMILAR_SIGN_FORM_IMPLIES_SAME_LANGUAGE_VALUE=NO
```

The audit uses only Sumerian and Akkadian. It does not propose a universal cuneiform pronunciation, and it does not transfer one language's reading into the other without a source.
