# PHX-01 — Residuals and Limits

## Evidence limits

- Only one source run exists per cut and operating condition. Cycles are
  repeated within-run observations, not independent experimental replicates.
- Cut B follows an earthquake campaign. The observed phase differences are not
  a causal earthquake-effect estimate.
- The selected 0.5 Hz records use 20 mm and the selected 1.0 Hz records use
  40 mm. Frequency and amplitude effects cannot be separated.
- The files have independent relative time axes. Sample-wise absolute phase
  across files is unavailable.
- Measured electrical drive voltage, a zero-drive null, calibration uncertainty
  and an independent same-condition replay are absent. Local `HZ_FZ_01`
  admission remains fail-closed.
- The ensemble coherence estimate treats active cycles as spectral segments;
  it does not establish broadband or population-level coherence.
- The 90% activity threshold and boundary exclusion were frozen from the prior
  source contract. Other reasonable segmentation choices were not promoted to
  primary analyses.

## Unresolved NEXAH linkage

The claimed prior `O-FLIP` / `ORDER_SENSITIVITY` result and its canonical
source were not found in the authorized Science Lab search root. PHX-01 does
not retrofit its phase result as an explanation for that claim.

## Required next evidence for stronger claims

To separate frequency, amplitude and condition effects, acquire or bind a
factorial design with multiple independent runs per cell, shared acquisition
hardware, explicit calibration uncertainty and the same amplitude grid at
each frequency. For local admission, complete the existing `NULL`,
`REFERENCE_A` and `REPLAY_B` contract with measured drive voltage.

No prime-number or morphogenesis test is justified by the present result.
