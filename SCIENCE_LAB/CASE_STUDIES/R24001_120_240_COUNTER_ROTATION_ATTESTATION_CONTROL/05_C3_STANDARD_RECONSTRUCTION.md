# C3 Standard Reconstruction

## Standard mathematics

Let `r=R(120°)` on a plane. Then `r^2=R(240°)`, `r^3=I`, and `r^-1=r^2`. This is the ordinary cyclic group action of order three.

## Historical boundary

S1 implements copies of one group at rotations 0°, 120° and 240°. That is compatible with, and locally realizes, a standard C3 orbit on SVG elements. Nothing non-standard is recovered.

S2 merely positions three meridians. S3 uses three phase offsets. S4–S6 use angles in still other roles. These artifacts do not state that they are instances of one common C3 action.
