# Boundary and Nonclaims

- Angle recurrence is not a shared operator.
- A triangle or three positions are not automatically a C3 action.
- Standard inverse rotations do not prove historical inverse intent.
- SVG rotation does not establish physical rotation.
- Electrical phase offsets are not the same object as geometric SVG copies.
- The unrecovered Outerborn formula is not attested by conditional arithmetic.
- No date, timeline position, symbolic label or Observer layout was converted into an angle action.
- No new mathematical or physical theory was created.
- No new ontology, operator, OLS primitive or RID schema gap was introduced.
- CRIP-01 and all other predecessors remain closed and unchanged.
- ORION v1 was not modified; implementation and research were not activated.
