# Boundary and Nonclaims

- Object is not representation; representation is not view; view is not interpretation.
- Same color, position, label, count, endpoint, or appearance is insufficient for identity or equivalence.
- Grid cell is not graph node until a mapping is declared.
- Graph is not its embedding.
- Matrix index is not spatial coordinate.
- Polar angle is not physical phase.
- Height and color encode magnitude only under a declared rule.
- A cone-like graphic is not a field or admissible region.
- A tensor label is not a tensor; a dimension label is not mathematical dimension.
- A point is not a trajectory; a trajectory is not its history.
- Trace is not source; reconstruction is not attestation; compatible is not derived.
- Preserved means preserved under the declared contract, not world-true.
