# Yellow-Point / Purple-Region Control

## Source gate

The exact artifact behind the phrase “the yellow point lies outside the purple cone” was not recovered with source coordinates, common frame, region definition, or construction code.

Therefore the following are absent:

- a registered point `p`;
- a defined set or region `K`;
- proof that both use the same coordinate system;
- a membership predicate;
- a transformation to another representation.

Color and apparent shape remain styling. A cone-like drawing is not a mathematical cone or field.

## Formal requirement

Only after defining `p`, `K`, and their common carrier can one evaluate `p∈K` or `p∉K`. If a projection removes the coordinate needed by the membership rule, recoverability may be lost even though the underlying relation remains fixed. Visual overlap can also change under projection without changing membership in the original space.

## Result

`YELLOW_POINT_PURPLE_REGION_STATUS=OUTSIDE_RELATION_UNDEFINED`

No solar, infinity, escape, portal, field, or excluded-state semantics are assigned.
