# Boundary and Nonclaims

GAVP-01 establishes standard operation typing and provenance only. It does not establish:

- a new operational calculus, primitive, graph theory or geometry;
- a NEXAH-specific operator or ontology;
- canonical semantics for `G°`, `D`, ROOT, M, SM, REST or MASK;
- a mathematical residual from exploratory language;
- commutativity without explicit compatibility conditions;
- meaning from historical visuals, glyphs, colors or symbols;
- Möbius/projective geometry, topology, knot theory or quaternions;
- number, root, prime, harmonic, calendar, astronomy, Giza, Rosetta, Leonardo or prism claims;
- physics, quantum terminology or universal geometry;
- NEXAH validation, architecture change or ORION capability;
- a Rust interface change or implementation activation.

All predecessor packages remain closed and unmodified. No commit is authorized or created.
