# Mission Control Assessment

## A. Urteil

`External Test 01` is a meaningful first practical external comparison. It
partly supports the method hypothesis that NEXAH can add value by reconciling
answers rather than generating another answer. It does not yet validate a
product or market.

The stable cross-platform consensus was sensible but generic: preserve
liquidity, prepare refinancing early and object by object, keep a reserve asset
optional, resolve securities/tax/free-cash evidence before irreversible acts,
and treat high rates combined with lower operating surplus as the critical
stress regime.

The deeper finding was model divergence. Platforms used different or
insufficiently transparent interpretations of debt at the refinancing horizon,
the refinanced share and the remaining old-rate debt. The supplied NEXAH model
advanced the debt by four repayment years from normalized `53` to `45`, treated
`34.2` as refinanced and `10.8` as remaining at an implied old rate, then
reported cash-flow, break-even and loan-to-value boundaries. Those figures are
reported model outputs, not independently verified facts.

## B. Durable system return

### Into the Compass / strategy

- Broad multi-answer comparison is commodity-level functionality.
- The candidate wedge is **advisory-report Decision Assurance**.
- Candidate value lies in common-model reconciliation, assumption attribution,
  boundary search, audit trail, calibrated abstention and Human Return.
- The test strengthens the existing NEXAH Compare direction but does not select
  or activate the wedge.

### Into the method / Assurance specification

- one normalized input register;
- one declared time axis and calculation model;
- fact/calculation/assumption/recommendation separation;
- output-to-assumption trace;
- reproducible formulas and counter-calculation;
- explicit missing-evidence and abstention fields;
- boundary and sensitivity search;
- final Human-readable reconciliation with unresolved differences retained.

### Into the result register

- one qualified exploratory external method finding;
- one delivery/transparency observation for a non-returning comparison service;
- no accuracy winner and no financial conclusion.

### Into competition observation

- Perplexity: strong presentation and source orientation; timeline/debt logic
  remained insufficiently resolved in the supplied comparison.
- Duck.ai: structured and action-oriented; the supplied review identified a
  more pessimistic result produced by applying the new rate to a larger or
  insufficiently advanced debt base.
- DeepAI: strongest restraint around missing evidence; less complete scenario
  and boundary calculation in this case.
- CrossChecked: relevant overlap through its multi-model-review promise; two
  delivery attempts produced no usable full report, making result access and
  transparency themselves part of the product comparison.
- NEXAH/Codex: strongest explicit time and boundary model in the supplied
  comparison; still dependent on an unverified allocation assumption.

These are observations from one supplied case, not stable vendor scores.

### Do not carry forward

- identifying or object-specific financial details;
- unverified real-world recommendations;
- exact vendor ranking;
- the claim that NEXAH is correct because it is more explicit;
- duplicated source conversations or private documents.

## C. Compass placement

The existing strategy is sufficient; no new architecture is required.

The test touches the current NEXAH Compare sequence as follows:

| Compass horizon | External Test 01 finding |
|---|---|
| Select the wedge | advisory-report comparison is now a concrete candidate, not selected |
| Assemble minimum instrument | a manual workflow was demonstrated; no integrated instrument exists |
| Prove/reject usefulness | not reached; no ground truth or blinded Human outcome |
| External legibility | partly reached through comparison with live offerings; not a customer test |

Compass statement:

> NEXAH should not compete as another answer aggregator. Its viable hypothesis
> is a Decision-Assurance layer that reconciles consequential model differences
> and preserves the evidence boundary and audit trail.

Unresolved: whether the workflow improves correctness, defect detection,
calibration, time, trust or decision quality enough for an external person to
adopt or pay for it.

## D. Differentiation test

Demonstrated once:

- a common comparison frame can expose model disagreement hidden by prose
  consensus;
- a difference can be traced to debt-horizon and refinancing assumptions;
- missing evidence and unverified assumptions can be kept visible;
- a bounded reconciliation can avoid forcing a winner.

Still asserted rather than demonstrated:

- reliable automated normalization across arbitrary reports;
- superior error detection under blind ground truth;
- reproducible operation by a new user without private guidance;
- lower review cost or better Human decisions;
- market demand and payment.
