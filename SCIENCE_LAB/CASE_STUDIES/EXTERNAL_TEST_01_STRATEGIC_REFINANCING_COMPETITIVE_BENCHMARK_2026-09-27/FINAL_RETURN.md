# Final Return

Decision: `QUALIFIED_FIRST_EXTERNAL_TEST_ACCEPTED`

`External Test 01` is retained as the first meaningful exploratory NEXAH
external comparison and competitive benchmark. It partially supports a narrow
Decision-Assurance hypothesis: the useful distinction is not collecting more
answers but reconciling their data, time axes, assumptions, calculations and
remaining uncertainty.

It does not establish calculation truth, financial advice, product-market fit,
external Human utility, demand or willingness to pay. No wedge, experiment,
build, outreach or customer action is activated by this return.

The one admissible successor is a separately authorized, preregistered
ground-truth benchmark with a strong equal-information baseline and blinded
external evaluation.
