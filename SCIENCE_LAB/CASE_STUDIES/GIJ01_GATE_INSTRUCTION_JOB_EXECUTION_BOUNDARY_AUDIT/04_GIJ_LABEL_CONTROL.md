# GIJ-01 - GIJ Label Control

## Initial state

`GIJ_SEMANTIC_STATUS=EMPTY`

No meaning is derived from names, initials, military associations, alphabet positions, typography, glyphs or wordplay.

## Independent formal result

The formal model requires more than three labels:

```text
instruction + environment + satisfied rule/authority
        -> dispatch or attempt
        -> separately attested execution event
        -> outcome
        -> optional result and trace
```

Only after this derivation can the mnemonic be considered:

- `G` may remind a reader of environment or ground.
- `I` may remind a reader of instruction.
- `J` may remind a reader of job or event.

The mnemonic omits rule satisfaction, authority, trigger/attempt, outcome and attestation. It therefore adds no evidence and is not a sufficient model.

```text
GIJ_MAPPING_STATUS=B
GIJ_MAPPING_MEANING=USEFUL_BUT_INCOMPLETE_MNEMONIC_AFTER_FORMAL_DERIVATION
LEXICAL_ORDER_IMPLIES_EXECUTION_ORDER=NO
```

Permuting `GIJ` changes only the string. Type dependency, causal order, temporal order and execution order come from the declared relations.
