# Boundary and Nonclaims

QAW-01 establishes only three bounded image-plane angle estimates for one repeated straight-sided documentary motif.

It does not claim:

- an object-space angle;
- a cross-view angular invariant;
- a registered axis-cut-view transformation;
- a VST-01 source-derived angle;
- a common-radius shell in the historical images;
- that V, triangle, wedge, pyramid, splinter, axis, cut, or operator are interchangeable;
- that Q° is an angle, quaternion, zero angle, or observer angle;
- that a familiar nearby angle is exact or meaningful;
- that similar image motifs are the same object;
- that projection, rotation, view change, and state change are equivalent;
- a new geometry, physics, operator, ontology, NEXAH architecture, or ORION capability.

VST-01, NLO-01, IJQ-01, PRR-01, and ASZ-01 remain frozen and unchanged.
