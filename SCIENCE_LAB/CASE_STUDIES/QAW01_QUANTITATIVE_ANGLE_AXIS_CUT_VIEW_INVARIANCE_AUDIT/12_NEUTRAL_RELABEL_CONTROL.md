# Neutral Relabel Control

All historical terms, animals, locations, mythological/planetary labels, Q°, colors, and symbolic prose were removed.

The admitted record reduces to:

- one repeated straight-sided motif `OBJ_A`;
- one apex `VTX_A` per rendering;
- sides `RAY_A`, `RAY_B`;
- image frames `VIEW_1`, `VIEW_2`, `VIEW_3`;
- measured interior angles and uncertainty.

The numerical image-plane measurements remain unchanged. The historical meanings disappear, and no operator or invariant appears.

`NEUTRAL_RELABEL_SURVIVES=YES_FOR_IMAGE_PLANE_MEASUREMENTS_ONLY`
