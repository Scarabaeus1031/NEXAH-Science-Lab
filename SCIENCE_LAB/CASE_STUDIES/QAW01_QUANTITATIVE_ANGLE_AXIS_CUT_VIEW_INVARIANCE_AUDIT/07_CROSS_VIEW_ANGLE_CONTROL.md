# Cross-View Angle Control

The repeated TERRA layout provides a defensible image-motif correspondence among three files, but not a registered object-space/view transformation.

| Comparison | Angles | Combined uncertainty | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| VIEW_1 vs VIEW_2 | 59.9° vs 58.0° | ±4.0° | `EQUAL_WITHIN_UNCERTAINTY` |
| VIEW_1 vs VIEW_3 | 59.9° vs 53.1° | ±2.3° | `NOT_EQUAL` |
| VIEW_2 vs VIEW_3 | 58.0° vs 53.1° | ±3.3° | `NOT_EQUAL` |

One equal pair is not an invariant. The portrait/landscape renderings have different aspect/layout behavior, and no source transformation establishes what should be preserved.

`ANGLE_RELATION_TYPE=PROJECTION_OR_NONUNIFORM_RENDERING_DEPENDENT`

`ANGLE_RELATION_SURVIVES_VIEW_CHANGE=NO_STABLE_RELATION`

`ANGLE_INVARIANT_FOUND=NO`
