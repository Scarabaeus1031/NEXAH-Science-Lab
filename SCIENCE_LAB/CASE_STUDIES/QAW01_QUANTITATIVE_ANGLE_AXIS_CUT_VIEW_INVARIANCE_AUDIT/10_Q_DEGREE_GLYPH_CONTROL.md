# Q° Glyph Control

The degree-like glyph following Q is materially visible in IMG_01 and IMG_02.

Its local prose roles include observer/measure and zero-node language. No angular variable, ray pair, reference axis, numerical degree value, measurement procedure, or transformation is attached to the glyph.

- `Q_DEGREE_GLYPH_ATTESTED=YES`
- `Q_DEGREE_EQUALS_MEASURED_ANGLE=NO_UNLESS_INDEPENDENTLY_ESTABLISHED`
- `DEGREE_GLYPH_EQUALS_ANGULAR_QUANTITY=NO`
- `Q_EQUALS_QUATERNION=NO`
- `Q_DEGREE_EQUALS_ZERO_ANGLE=NO`
- `Q_DEGREE_EQUALS_OBSERVER_ANGLE=NO`
