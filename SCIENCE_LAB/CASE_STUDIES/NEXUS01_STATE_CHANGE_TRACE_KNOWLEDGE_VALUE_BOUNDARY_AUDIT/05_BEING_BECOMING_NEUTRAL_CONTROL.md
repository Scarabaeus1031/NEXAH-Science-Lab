# Being / Becoming Neutral Control

The formal questions require no philosophical primitive:

| Question | Neutral typed answer |
|---|---|
| What is registered now? | State/state reference at a declared time or revision |
| What changed? | Difference, transition, derivative or event under a named rule |
| What remained? | Equality, equivalence or invariant under a named relation |
| Where did it go? | Ordered state/point sequence under a representation |
| What can be inferred? | Evidence-constrained claim, ambiguity or abstention |

```text
STATE_AT_t ≠ TRANSITION ≠ DERIVATIVE ≠ TRAJECTORY ≠ EVENT_HISTORY
```

“Being” and “Becoming” may summarize state/change concerns after the typing is
complete. They do not create operators, types or historical provenance.
