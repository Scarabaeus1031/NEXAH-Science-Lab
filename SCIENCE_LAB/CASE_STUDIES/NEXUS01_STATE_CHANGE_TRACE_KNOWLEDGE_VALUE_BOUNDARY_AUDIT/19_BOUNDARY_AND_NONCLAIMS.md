# Boundary and Nonclaims

NEXUS-01 establishes only a bounded type/provenance result.

It does **not** claim:

- that a plotted point is the system or complete state;
- that a point establishes a path, cause or history;
- that a trace is truth, evidence in every context or unique reconstruction;
- absolute philosophical knowledge;
- that measurement values carry intrinsic normative value;
- that the machine invents policy, goals or human authority;
- that Nexus is a center, origin, zero, observer, consciousness or singularity;
- a new OLS primitive, RID schema, operator or ontology;
- a new mathematical, physical, historical or philosophical result;
- ORION capability, Rust implementation or activation.

No predecessor was edited or reopened. No external research or experiment was
performed. Philosophy and historical labels remain expression/provenance only.
