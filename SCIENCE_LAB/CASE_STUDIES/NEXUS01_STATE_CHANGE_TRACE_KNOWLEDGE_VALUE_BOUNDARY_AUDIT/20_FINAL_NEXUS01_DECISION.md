# Final NEXUS-01 Decision

## Judgment

The frozen architecture can distinguish a registered entity from its state, the
state from coordinates and views, a point from trajectory and history, a trace
from evidence and interpretation, and measurement from normative value. It can
also preserve ambiguity, abstain when evidence is underdetermined, and require a
declared criterion and authority before a decision.

`NexusRecord`, trajectory/evidence bundles and knowledge-status records improve
reviewability but compose existing references. They neither create physical
meaning nor expose a mandatory schema gap. Decision class C is therefore the
most accurate: useful named records, no new primitive.
