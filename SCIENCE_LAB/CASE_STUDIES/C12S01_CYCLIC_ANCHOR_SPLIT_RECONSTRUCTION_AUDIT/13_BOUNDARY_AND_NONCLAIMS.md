# Boundary and Nonclaims

## Evidence boundaries

- Historical occurrence is not independent mathematical validation.
- A compatible reconstruction is not an attested authorial derivation.
- A picture is not a graph, metric space, group action, or physical model unless those objects are defined.
- Shared numbers or labels do not establish a shared operator.
- A rounded known constant does not become generated by a nearby motif.
- Cardinality does not determine symmetry.

## Explicit nonclaims

C12S-01 does not claim:

- that `I` is the integer `1`;
- that `6+I+6` equals 13 objects;
- that a clock produces the double pyramid;
- that 11, 12, and 13 encode negative, zero, and positive physical domains;
- that `12.x` or `13.x` decimals encode ordered pairs;
- that mirror pairs share a prime operator;
- that the golden angle is derived from the clock split;
- that the historical visuals establish a universal geometry;
- that P6R-01 is repaired;
- that any new OLS primitive, RID field, ORION capability, mathematical theory, number theory, or physical theory is required.

## Governance boundary

The result is a closed audit record. It authorizes no implementation, new research, visual reinterpretation, schema migration, or architecture change.
