# Six/Seven Control

## Counting statement

If a model explicitly declares six distinct peripheral objects and one distinct center, its object count is seven:

`6 + 1 = 7`.

This is a conditional arithmetic statement. The recovered `6+I+6` source does not establish that `I` is one counted center or that either `6` is a six-node peripheral set.

## Symmetry destruction test

Even in a separately defined six-around-one diagram, seven counted objects do not imply cyclic group `C7`:

- the six peripheral locations may have `C6` or `D6` symmetry;
- the center can be fixed by every symmetry;
- a seven-cycle would have to permute all seven objects transitively, which the center/periphery distinction prevents;
- count alone never determines adjacency, action, or symmetry group.
