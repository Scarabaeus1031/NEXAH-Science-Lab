# C12S-01 Specification

## Scope

C12S-01 is a bounded historical-source reconstruction and formal-typing audit. It asks whether a cyclic twelve-position model, its adjacent residues, and the historical expression `6 + I + 6` can be related without converting labels, imagery, or retrospective language into mathematics.

## Frozen boundary

- P6R-01 remains closed with primary result `E_UNDERDEFINED`.
- No predecessor record is edited or superseded.
- No physical, number-theoretic, OLS, RID, or ORION claim is activated.
- Historical attestation, formal reconstruction, interpretation, and expression are separate evidence classes.

## Tested objects

1. A neutral cyclic clock model on `Z/12Z`.
2. The predecessor/anchor/successor split around residue zero.
3. The historical strings and depictions `6 + I + 6`, `11–12–13`, `12.6`, `12.7`, `13.6`, `13.7`, `137.5°`, and selected mirror-pair language.
4. The survival of a formal object after expressive language is removed.

## Decision vocabulary

- `A_HISTORICALLY_ATTESTED_FORMAL_RECONSTRUCTION`
- `B_USEFUL_RECONSTRUCTION_OF_STANDARD_MATHEMATICS`
- `C_PARTIAL_RECONSTRUCTION_WITH_SOURCE_GAPS`
- `D_EXPRESSION_ONLY_NO_STABLE_FORMAL_OBJECT`
- `E_UNDERDEFINED`

## Stop rule

The audit ends after source typing, destruction controls, the closed decision, and the machine-readable result. It does not authorize a successor audit or implementation.
