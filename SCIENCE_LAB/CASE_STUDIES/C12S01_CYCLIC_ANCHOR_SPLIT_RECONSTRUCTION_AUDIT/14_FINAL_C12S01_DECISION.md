# Final C12S-01 Decision

## Primary result

`B_USEFUL_RECONSTRUCTION_OF_STANDARD_MATHEMATICS`

The strongest recoverable formal object is a conventional cyclic model on `Z/12Z`. It cleanly types the anchor and its adjacent residues and distinguishes integer labels from congruence classes. The historical source separately attests a twelve-sector clock motif, an `11–12–13` sequence, the expression `6+I+6`, a rounded golden-angle value, and several source-local pairings.

The exact `-1/0/+1` rule is not stated by the historical source. `6+I+6` is historically real as an expression but lacks the definitions needed for a geometry. No mapping from that expression to the clock model survives typing controls. No common mirror/prime operator, golden-angle derivation, new P6R geometry, new primitive, or schema gap was found.
