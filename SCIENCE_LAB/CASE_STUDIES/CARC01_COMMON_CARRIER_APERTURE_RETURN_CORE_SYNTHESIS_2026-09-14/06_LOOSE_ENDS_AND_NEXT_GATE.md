# Loose ends and next gate

## Resolved by CARC-01

- Primes are no longer treated as a missing universal research branch. They are an exact discrete carrier/address profile within a broader comparison method.
- Matrices, layers, slices and apertures receive distinct roles.
- Color is separated from ILAU semantic status.
- OLS semantics, CRIC execution records, NRRC rich trace envelopes and THE EYE display are assigned different ownership layers.
- Prime/CRT and NBV/Neon are shown to share a record spine without claiming a shared mechanism.

## Still open

1. **Prime/CRT Trace gap:** QRTp does not define meaningful `D`, `C` and `Phi` components for NRRC's `K_gamma`. A complete NRRC trace must not be fabricated from visual analogy.
2. **NBV Trace gap:** a deterministic trace extractor and phase rule are absent. Pixel masks and curvature-sensitive bands are not automatically an NRRC local descriptor.
3. **Profile registry gap:** neither domain binding is a registered NRRC extension or general CRIC conformance profile.
4. **Adapter gap:** no frozen Prime/CRT→CRIC or NBV→CRIC adapter exists.
5. **THE EYE gap:** no approved namespaced result projection consumes these records.
6. **`Q°` gap:** frame, unit, sign convention, periodicity and operator role are not consistently declared across the visual corpus.
7. **Visual corpus gap:** glyph/color/layer plates need source-record pointers; resemblance alone cannot bind them.
