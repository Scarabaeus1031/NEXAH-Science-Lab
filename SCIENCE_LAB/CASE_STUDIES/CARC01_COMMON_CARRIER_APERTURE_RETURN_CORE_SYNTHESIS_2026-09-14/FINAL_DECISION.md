# Final decision

## Decision

**A — COMMON_EXECUTION_SPINE_IDENTIFIED; TWO_DOMAIN_BINDINGS_SUPPORTED_DOCUMENTARILY; IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_ACTIVATED**

The apparent loose ends are real at the adapter and registry level, but not at the architectural level. A common core already exists in the corpus. It is best expressed by the CRIC-shaped carrier/view/address/Q-T-R/observation/reconstruction/residual/ILAU/receipt chain, governed semantically by OLS. NRRC remains the richer optional trace-return envelope and must not be forced onto profiles that do not define all five `K_gamma` components.

Prime/CRT fits the common core as an exact discrete address-and-return profile. NBV/Neon fits it as a deterministic visibility-layer and residual profile. Their shared structure is methodological, not ontological or causal. THE EYE may later display a bounded projection but must not own or silently flatten the source record.
