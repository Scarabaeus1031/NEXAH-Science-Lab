## Initial assessment

The corpus is relevant to one coherent subject: Rödelheim history, historical
geography, hydrology, settlement, boundaries, routes and cartographic
provenance. Its files do not all carry the same authority.

The 23 September 2026 Gesamtreport and its two Markdown test notes are the best
current orientation layer. They explicitly distinguish what is visible, what
is independently supported and what remains open. The separate historical
closing report and the RTF note contain stronger narrative claims without an
adequate source apparatus and remain working interpretations only.

The visual corpus is useful for comparison, annotation and candidate discovery.
It is not a set of 92 independent historical proofs. Overlays that lack
georeferencing, documented scale and held-out control points remain
illustrative.
