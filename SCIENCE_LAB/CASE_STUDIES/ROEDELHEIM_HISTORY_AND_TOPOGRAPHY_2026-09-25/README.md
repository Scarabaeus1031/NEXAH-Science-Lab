## Purpose

This case study is the Science Lab evidence home for the history, historical
topography and cartographic provenance of Frankfurt-Rödelheim. It preserves the
received maps, reports, image tests and working interpretations while keeping
historical claims separate from visual resemblance, symbolic interpretation
and unregistered overlays.

Mission Control may point to this package as the maintained History and
Historical Topography subject case. Mission Control does not own or promote the
evidence stored here.

## Evidence ladder

1. **Source-bound evidence** - archival maps or public records with an
   identifiable holding institution, reference, date, scale and applicable
   legend.
2. **Independent local support** - a claim supported by a named municipal,
   archival or scholarly source, such as the Solmspark island between Nidda and
   Mühlgraben.
3. **Reproducible comparison** - a registered map comparison with stable
   control points, held-out checks, documented error and source-identical
   features.
4. **Working interpretation** - a plausible historical or topographic reading
   that still lacks the necessary source or registration.
5. **Visual or symbolic hypothesis** - diagrams, alignments, Fourier images,
   glyph readings and synthetic plates used for orientation or candidate
   discovery only.

## Current claim ceiling

The corpus supports Rödelheim as a historically changing river, island,
boundary and passage landscape. It supports continued investigation of Nidda,
Mühlgraben, Solmspark, Biegwald, historic land divisions and transport
corridors.

It does not yet establish a continuous geometric survey figure, a hidden
military or hermetic code, a specific former watercourse beneath every modern
street, local clay extraction on every proposed parcel, or a shared physical
mechanism inferred only from similar shapes.
