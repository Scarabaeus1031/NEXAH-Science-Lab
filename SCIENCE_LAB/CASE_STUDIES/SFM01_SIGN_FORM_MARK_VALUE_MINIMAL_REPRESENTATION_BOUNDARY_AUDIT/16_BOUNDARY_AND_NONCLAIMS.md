# 16 — Boundary and Nonclaims

```text
MARK != FORM

MARKER_ROLE != FEATURE
ABSENCE_OF_MARKER != ABSENCE_OF_INFORMATION
CONTEXT_CONSTRAINS_MAPPING

```

## Nonclaims

SFM-01 does not create:

- a universal representation stack or semiotic ontology;
- a NEXAH-specific core or scientific gain;
- a physical or mathematical information theory;
- a new meaning theory;
- a `BINDER`, `CONTEXT`, `SIGN`, `FORM`, `!` or `?` operator;
- intrinsic punctuation semantics or adopted syntax;
- sacred geometry, hidden number codes or intrinsic meaning from Glagolitic glyph shape;
- ancient NEXAH, a universal Slavic alphabet or continuity from historical scripts to modern Croatian orthography;
- a fixed Croatian Glagolitic alphabet size detached from a specific source, period, variant and abecedarium;
- a new operator, architecture or scientific claim from the foreign control;
- changes to VOR-01, CUN-01 or GRB-01;
- a NEXAH architecture or ORION capability change;
- research or implementation authorization.

The four retained terms are ordinary documentary abstractions. Every actual mapping remains domain- and context-specific.
