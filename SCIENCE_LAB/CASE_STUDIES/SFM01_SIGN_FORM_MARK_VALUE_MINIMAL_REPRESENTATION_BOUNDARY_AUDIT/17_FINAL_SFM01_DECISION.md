# SFM-01 — Final Decision

## Primary result

`C_STANDARD_DOMAIN_TERMINOLOGY_SUFFICIENT_NO_NEXAH_SPECIFIC_CORE_NEEDED`

The reduction retains four common documentary types—`MARK`, `FORM`, `UNIT`, `VALUE`—because collapsing any adjacent distinction makes predecessor evidence unrepresentable. This does not establish a new core, ontology or theory. Standard writing-system, linguistic, grammatical and evidence terminology is sufficient.

Other useful candidates are better classified outside the minimal stack: `CARRIER`, `SIGN`, `FEATURE` and `MEANING` are domain extensions; `CONTEXT` is a required mapping field; `ATTESTATION` is an evidence-layer field; `INFORMATION` is too broad to increase precision. The Human shorthand remains provenance only.

The authorized Croatian Glagolitic / Croatian Church Slavonic control does not change the four-type result. It independently reinforces `SCRIPT != LANGUAGE`, `FORM != VALUE`, `VALUE_TYPE != VALUE` and the composite-unit boundary. `MISLITE` carries distinct phonetic, numeric, name and lexical relations; `JERY` is a sourced historical digraph. No fixed alphabet inventory is asserted.
