# 12 — Standard Terminology Control

The predecessor source ledgers already use established domain terms sufficient for all retained distinctions:

- writing/orthography: character, grapheme, digraph, trigraph, composite symbol, orthographic unit;
- cuneiform studies: mark/impression, sign form, sign identity/name, sign value, syllabic/logographic/determinative function;
- grammar: marker/exponent, grammatical feature, article, definiteness, agreement, zero/non-overt expression;
- evidence practice: source, corpus, attestation, reconstruction;
- general documentation: carrier, form, unit, value, context.

The reduction does not require a renamed NEXAH vocabulary. `BINDER_FORMAL_NEED=NO` is preserved.

`STANDARD_TERMINOLOGY_SUFFICIENT=YES`

Palaeoslavistics additionally supplies standard terms for script, grapheme/letter, letter name, phonetic value, numeric value, digraphic structure, script variant and abecedarium.
