# 10 — Context Role Control

Across all three predecessors, context selects or constrains valid mappings:

- VOR-01: language and orthographic/lexical context;
- CUN-01: language, period/corpus and inscription context;
- GRB-01: language, phonological, grammatical and discourse context.

Context is not another object between form and value. It is a field or set of constraints on the mapping relation.

```text
CONTEXT_STATUS=REQUIRED_MAPPING_FIELD
CONTEXT_IS_OPERATOR=NO
```

The field must name its domain-specific content. Bare `CONTEXT` without language/system and relevant conditions is under-specified.
