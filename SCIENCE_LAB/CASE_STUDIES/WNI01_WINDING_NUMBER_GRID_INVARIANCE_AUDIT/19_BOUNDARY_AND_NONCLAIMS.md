# Boundary and nonclaims

WNI-01 establishes only standard winding behavior in declared synthetic controls.

It does not establish:

- a new invariant, theorem, topology, or number theory;
- a physical mechanism, wind model, vortex law, or atmospheric result;
- that historical WIND or +11 WIND meant WINDING;
- that Q7/Q11/Q13/Q17 are topological or physical regimes;
- a 7×11 or 77 structure;
- universal grid preservation independent of encoding/sampling;
- winding from visual loops, spirals, figures, or gestalts;
- historical Q-Mirror physical/transport prose;
- equivalence of the prior signed-area proxy and full W(C,p);
- coordinate, path, event, history, or provenance equality from returned W;
- a new OLS primitive, RID field, operator, implementation, Rust source, or ORION capability.

No predecessor was changed. The bounded local audit is closed.
