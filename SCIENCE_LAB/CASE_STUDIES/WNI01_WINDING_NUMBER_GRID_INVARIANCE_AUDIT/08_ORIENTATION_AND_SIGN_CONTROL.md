# Orientation and sign control

Signed winding and absolute winding are separate observables.

| Case | Signed W | |W| |
|---|---|---|
| Translation/rotation/positive scale | Preserved | Preserved |
| Reflection | Sign reversed | Preserved |
| Q image measured about 0 | Sign reversed | Preserved |
| Q with finite transformed (p) | Difference rule; may change magnitude | Not generally preserved |
| Missing/unknown traversal order | Unresolved | Unresolved unless an adequate alternate contract exists |

Orientation is supplied by parametrization order; a separate label can document that convention. Removing only a redundant prose label does not erase orientation if ordered parametrization remains authoritative. Removing the meaning of sample order does.

Therefore winding is not orientation itself: sign depends on orientation, while magnitude records net enclosure multiplicity.
