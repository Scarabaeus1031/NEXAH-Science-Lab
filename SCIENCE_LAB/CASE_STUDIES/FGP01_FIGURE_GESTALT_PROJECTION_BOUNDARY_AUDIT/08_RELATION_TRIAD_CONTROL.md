# Relation Triad Control

Register two relata `A` and `B` and a relation record `R` with its own type, direction policy, criterion and provenance.

```text
(A, R, B)
A != B
A != R
B != R
```

The triple is a formal relational tuple: two relata plus one declared relation. The relation is not a third relatum of the same role and does not convert the number two into three.

`RELATA_DISTINCT_FROM_RELATION=YES`; `RELATIONAL_TRIAD_FORMALLY_EXPRESSIBLE=YES`; `TWONESS_EQUALS_TRINITY=NO`.

No Plato, Pythagoras, trinity, number, glyph or metaphysical source is needed. The result follows from independent record identity and typed relation roles already present in OLS/RID/ETRI/GAVP.
