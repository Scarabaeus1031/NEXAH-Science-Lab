# Multi-view / Multi-Gestalt Controls

## Same source/state, several figures

For one registered `X(t0)`, use `P1(x,y)=x` and `P2(x,y)=x+y`. Distinct rule/frame/loss provenance yields figures `F1` and `F2` while X remains unchanged. This is existing SWM/GAVP/NEXUS multi-view behavior.

## Same figure, several formal organizations

Let Figure `F` contain six unchanged marks `p0…p5` at regular-hexagon vertices.

- Criterion `C1`: partition adjacent pairs `{p0,p1}`, `{p2,p3}`, `{p4,p5}` → organization `G1`.
- Criterion `C2`: partition alternating vertices `{p0,p2,p4}`, `{p1,p3,p5}` → organization `G2`.

`F` and its bytes/marks remain unchanged; only criterion-relative grouping changes. This formally demonstrates multiple organizations, not an empirical claim about human perception. `OBJECT_CHANGED=NO`.

## Same figure/result, several states

The many-to-one projection `P(x,y)=x` maps `(0,1)` and `(0,-1)` to the same result. Equal figure/view content therefore need not identify source state.

## Final test ledger

- Object: registered `X` with identity/revision.
- State: `X(t)` with time/frame/provenance.
- Changed: projection rule/view and possibly represented content; not necessarily X.
- Projected: only declared fields/relations.
- Figure: source-typed represented configuration.
- Gestalt: criterion/model-relative grouping of figure elements.
- Observer-relative: criterion/model/context and grouping assertion.
- Preserved/lost: exactly the projection/render ledgers; unrepresented state is lost or unresolved.
- Inference: contract-declared relations only; never source identity, ontology, truth or inner essence.
- Q°: derived marker in each declared view.
- Mirror change: path/figure may change; object need not; organization/interpretation may or may not.
