# Boundary and Nonclaims

FGP-01 is documentary/formal classification only. It does not create a perception theory, Gestalt psychology, ontology, mathematical theory, physical theory, consciousness account, psychology claim, essence claim, number theory, historical reconstruction, Q° ontology, operator or implementation.

The candidate chain is not a mandatory universal pipeline. Its typed optional dependencies are:

```text
Structure --[declared realization/conformance]--> Instance
Instance --[time/revision-qualified state attribution]--> State(t)
State(t) --[declared projection/observation transformation]--> ProjectionResult
ProjectionResult --[render/representation derivation]--> Figure/View
Figure --[criterion/model-relative grouping]--> GestaltRecord
GestaltRecord --[downstream claim/evidence relation]--> Interpretation
```

A figure may be produced without a state record when its typed source is geometry; Gestalt and interpretation are optional. Every arrow requires its declared rule, inputs, losses and provenance.

OLS, RID-01, ORION V1, SWM-01, TITAN-00, GAVP-01, ETRI-01, AOSD-01, RPR-01, OTC-01, NEXUS-01 and PG-01 remain unchanged. No Rust or code was created.
