# Mirror Control

Schematic observation chain:

```text
Object/State
 --[signal generation]--> Light/Signal
 --[mirror/path transformation]--> Transformed signal path
 --[sensor observation]--> Measurement/Readout
 --[render]--> Figure
 --[criterion/model]--> Perceptual organization
 --[interpretive claim]--> Interpretation
```

Each arrow is a declared event, path transformation, observation, representation, grouping or interpretation—not identity. ETRI-01 supports that a reflection transform can change orientation while preserving selected geometry; OTC-01 supplies the distinct signal/path/instrument/readout chain. This audit does not claim an optical experiment or full mirror physics.

Changing mirror pose can change path, admitted signal and figure without changing object identity/state. It may also change an attributed organization or interpretation, but none is required. `OBJECT != MIRROR_IMAGE`; mirror image/readout/figure are not self; perception is not figure; interpretation is not perception or inner essence.

External appearance remains distinct from internal state. Interpreting appearance does not directly measure an inner essence.
