Status: `EXPLORATORY_LINEAGE / NOT_WP5_SCORED_INPUT`

## Why this belongs here

The WP5 Family Office fixture did not arise in isolation. It is the controlled
successor to External Test 01, in which the same normalized strategic
refinancing question received returns from several AI and comparison services.
Those returns exposed the concrete model differences that WP5 later converted
into a frozen ground-truth fixture.

They therefore belong to the case lineage and interpretation. They do not
belong retroactively to the already frozen WP5 scoring packet because the raw
outputs, exact model versions and timestamps were not archived before the
exploratory comparison.

## What carried into WP5

The exploratory returns motivated five controlled issue classes:

1. debt carried to the refinancing horizon;
2. scope of debt receiving the new rate;
3. unsupported treatment of reported liquidity as freely deployable;
4. confusion between a cost increase and an equal percentage reduction of
   operating surplus;
5. a reserve-asset recommendation made beyond the available tax and security
   evidence.

WP5 did not score the historical vendors. It created two synthetic confirmed
AnalysisRecords containing exactly those frozen issue classes and tested the
deterministic comparator against a separately hashed answer key.
