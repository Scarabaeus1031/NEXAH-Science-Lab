# Fixed Baseline Instructions

Role: independent qualified reviewer  
Timebox: 45 minutes  
Allowed tools: ordinary calculator or spreadsheet, plain-text notes  
Forbidden: NEXAH output, evaluator key, web research, LLM or additional source
material

Review the supplied CaseRecord, both AnalysisRecords, admitted evidence and
source projections. Identify decision-relevant contradictions, calculation or
time-axis differences, unsupported evidence use, incompatible assumptions or
operations, and recommendations that require abstention.

For every finding record:

1. a short finding ID and description;
2. severity: `CRITICAL`, `MATERIAL` or `CONTEXT`;
3. exact claim IDs;
4. exact evidence IDs and locator where applicable;
5. whether the issue requires `ABSTAIN`;
6. whether the issue is unresolved rather than false;
7. start and end timestamp.

Do not rank the two analyses as globally correct or better. Do not infer absent
tax, liquidity or security facts. Do not recommend a financial product or
execute a decision.
