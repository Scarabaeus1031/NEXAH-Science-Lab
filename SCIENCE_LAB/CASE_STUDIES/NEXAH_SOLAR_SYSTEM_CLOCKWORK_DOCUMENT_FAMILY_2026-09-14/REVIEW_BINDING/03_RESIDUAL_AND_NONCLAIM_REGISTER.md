8. **Time-sensitive astronomy not revalidated.** Values and labels concerning 3I/ATLAS and other astronomical objects were not subjected to a currentness audit in this custody pass.

## Bounded positive finding

The family is useful as a typed orientation dossier. Its value is strongest where it separates established mechanics, calculations, candidate mappings, and annotations, and where the Lorenz bundle tests a visual “switch corridor” against step-size refinement rather than promoting the rendered shape into a physical object.

Research activation should require separate tests with declared inputs, units, generators, current authoritative ephemerides where applicable, and explicit null or comparison conditions.
