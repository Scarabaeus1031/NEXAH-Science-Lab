# Boundary and Nonclaims

AREV-01 consolidates standard distinctions; it does not add scientific content.

It does not establish:

- new graph theory or geometry;
- quaternion structure;
- physical field dynamics, torsion, force or resonance;
- memory in a spiral or coil;
- prime-angle coupling;
- planetary, Venus, Uranus or biological semantics;
- “Rosenwinkel” or special-angle significance;
- a universal operator;
- canonical mathematical meaning for stick, V, Y, fork, wheel, coil, mirror, Q°, i, j or l;
- Rust production readiness or implementation authority.

Historical names may remain documentary aliases with explicit local glosses. They cannot determine type semantics. The proposed interface remains optional and future-facing.
