# NEXAH Science Lab — Subject Overview

Maintained through: `2026-10-12`

Status: `CURRENT_NAVIGATION_VIEW / NO_STATUS_OR_AUTHORITY_TRANSFER`

This is the canonical scientific entrance to the Science Lab. It answers four
questions without replacing the owning evidence packages:

1. which subject or established scientific neighborhood owns the question;
2. what NEXAH actually tested or established;
3. which result is negative, bounded, open or only a hypothesis; and
4. where the evidence, specialist route and Mission Control status live.

Start with the [Artifact and Significance Catalog](ARTIFACT_CATALOG/README.md)
when the question is why a family matters rather than only which subject owns
it. The Lab Register remains the status navigator; case-study packages retain
evidence authority; Mission Control retains the current portfolio and
activation view. This overview is therefore the **science index**, not a new
evidence owner.

## Presentation rule: three lenses before many subjects

The Science Research Desk now uses three stable **reading lenses** as its
primary human navigation:

| Lens | First question | Typical objects |
|---|---|---|
| **Science** | What was asked, tested and found, and where does the claim stop? | question, method, result class, residual, open question and evidence pointer |
| **Builder** | What exists as software, instrument, interface or reproducible component? | HTML tool, source, runner, GLB, replay contract and reusable module |
| **Human** | Why might this matter, and how can an interested reader understand it without losing the boundary? | explanation, guided view, consequence, decision and next question |

These are presentation lenses, not disciplines, evidence classes or new
Navigator identities. The subject shelves below remain the canonical metadata
for disciplinary ownership. A flagship may appear through all three lenses
while retaining one or more precise subject homes and one owning evidence
package.

## Milky Way · Pineal Aperture · Andromeda

Primary: scientific visualization and coordinate/frame geometry.

Secondary: astronomy interface, computer science and information design.

It is not measured astronomy, biology or pineal-gland research, and it does
not establish physical coupling between galaxies.

The two named galaxies are procedural carrier fields. The Pineal Aperture is
an observer-local selection and comparison operator. The instrument becomes an
astronomical research object only when a named catalog, coordinates,
uncertainties, epoch and projection contract are bound. Until then its highest
relevance is as a transparent demonstrator of frames, cuts, provenance and
residuals.
