# Science Lab Intake

Status: `CLOSED_DURING_STRUCTURE_FREEZE`

No new Lab or Study may enter while the Lab is under Structure Freeze.

After reopening, a proposed question begins as one copy of
[`LAB_ADMISSION_RECORD.md`](../TEMPLATES/LAB_ADMISSION_RECORD.md) inside this
directory. Creating the record does not admit the Study and does not authorize a
new top-level folder.

The Research Director checks completeness and overlap. The Human Owner then
records `ADMIT`, `REJECT`, `PARK`, or `RETURN_FOR_REVISION`. Only `ADMIT` may add
a row to `LAB_REGISTER.md` and consume an OPEN slot.

Ideas without a bounded question remain in Mission Control/Incoming. They do not
enter this directory automatically.
