## Four stages and twelve operators

| stage | operators | working function |
|---|---|---|
| `FOUNDATION` | `01 Observe`, `02 Reference`, `03 Select` | establish observation, context and relevance |
| `REDUCTION` | `04 Filter`, `05 Compare`, `06 Relate` | reduce, differentiate and bind |
| `TRANSLATION` | `07 Scale`, `08 Transform`, `09 Predict` | change level or representation and form an expectation |
| `AGENCY` | `10 Act`, `11 Trace`, `12 Reflect` | intervene, retain history and revise |

The sequence is read as a cycle: after `Reflect`, orientation may begin again
at a deeper or differently represented level. That recurrence is a reading
rule, not a claim that all systems implement one universal twelve-step
architecture.
