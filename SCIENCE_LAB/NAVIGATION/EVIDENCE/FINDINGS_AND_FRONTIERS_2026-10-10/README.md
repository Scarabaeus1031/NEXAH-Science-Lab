## Current synthesis

The strongest coherent NEXAH research object is not one preferred number,
grid, geometry or physical mechanism. It is the study of **identifiable and
invariant relations under declared changes of representation**.

The recurring inspection grammar is:

```text
Carrier → State → Operator → View → Fiber → Return → Residual → Evidence
```

The central bounded finding is:

> A record can retain its identity and target exactly while its apparent
> topology changes substantially under a harmless re-encoding.

The operational return is:

> Preserve identity. Declare the cut. Compare relations. Keep the residual.

## Evidence classes

### 1. Established exact and deterministic relations

These are valid only inside their declared finite objects and operators.

- exact CRT and finite representation returns;
- declared involutions, rotations, quotient counts and reconstruction keys;
- the bounded G0→G1→G2 relation-object crosswalk;
- aligned multi-view reconstruction of one finite relation state;
- exact carrier return where required side information is retained;
- the conditional signed reciprocal identity
  `(Q'_epsilon)^2 = epsilon I` under its explicit A0-BL-045 bridge contract.

These results do not establish a universal carrier, operator or ontology.

### 2. Bounded method findings

- **Carrier–View–Residual:** persistent identity can be separated from
  topology introduced by a cut on two distinct carriers.
- **Transition & Identifiability:** heterogeneous instruments can be compared
  through one typed inspection grammar without merging their authorities.
- **Translation Fidelity:** coarser certificates were generally more robust
  and less discriminative; richer certificates retained more distinctions but
  were more representation-sensitive in the frozen synthetic studies.
- **Multi-view reveal:** three aligned lossy views reconstructed the relation
  state while a non-trivial source fiber remained.
- **A0 local separation:** oriented seam and local signatures separate the
  finite A0-BL-035 population, but require 100 signatures for 100 histories
  and therefore do not provide compression.

These are method results, not new Prime theory, cellular-automaton law or
physical invariants.

### 3. Valid negative and boundary findings

- no Life/E8 identity or E8-specific Life mechanism was established;
- no prime-specific coding advantage survived the registered controls;
- Mod 17 did not retain unique status in the controlled comparison;
- several equal-number, shared-shape and endpoint-return correspondences did
  not identify a shared operator or mechanism;
- the original n+1 contribution claim was not supported against established
  comparison practices and a fair-information baseline;
- A0-BL-021 established path dependence with an explicit typed

- A0-BL-035 identified no compressed sufficient Splinter selector;
- A0-BL-045 showed that local `Q^5` data does not select the seam topology and
  that algebraic signed return does not imply Euclidean isometry.

These results constrain the programme and must remain visible.

### 5. Open frontiers

The next legitimate question is whether a declared path through several views
produces a stable, out-of-sample and return-capable relation that outperforms
alternative mappings.

This frontier is now an **Owner-selected research backlog item**. Its status is
`PREPARATION_ONLY / NOT_ACTIVE`: the decision authorizes literature review,
baseline selection and an admission-ready Intake, not scientific execution,
Lab Register admission, an OPEN or ACTIVE slot, claim promotion or publication.

The smallest current programme is:

1. fix one finite carrier and an admissible view family;
2. declare direct and composed representation paths;
3. separate carrier-retained, view-induced, coupling-induced and unresolved
   structure;
4. compare against relabeling, alternative-map, no-coupling and lossy-view
   controls;
5. require a return or reconstruction criterion;
6. preserve failure and residuals as results.

The programme fails if the signature depends on an arbitrary cut, disappears
under matched controls, cannot be reconstructed under its declared contract or
only restates visual similarity.
