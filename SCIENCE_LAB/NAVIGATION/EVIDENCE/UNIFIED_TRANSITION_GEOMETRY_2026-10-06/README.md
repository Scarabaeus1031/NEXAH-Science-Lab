## Mission statement

> NEXAH develops Unified Transition Geometry—an exploratory framework for
> orientation in complex systems. It connects local observations, global
> structures, recursive stability, and navigable transitions across
> mathematical, visual, computational, and experiential representations.

UTG is the orientation roof. Orientation is the purpose. NEXAH remains the
system, atlas and instrumentarium. This package does not declare UTG an
established physical theory.
