# THE EYE Lineage Lens

Status: `INTERNAL_READ_ONLY_SOURCE_BOUND_LENS`
Maintained: `2026-10-21`

adoption and contracts through the bounded comparator, fixed demonstrations,
Kernel adapter and NEXAHEDRON result boundary. ORION remains a separate pinned
gateway and is never presented as a later THE EYE stage.

hashes, currentness and Owner decisions remain in Mission Control. The route is
internal and the public Navigator allowlist remains empty.
