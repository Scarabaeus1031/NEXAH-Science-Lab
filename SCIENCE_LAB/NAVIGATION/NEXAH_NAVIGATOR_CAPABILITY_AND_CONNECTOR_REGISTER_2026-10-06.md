## Purpose and conclusion

The Science Navigator is the central orientation instrument for the current
NEXAH research landscape. It binds more governed sources, views and evidence
routes than any earlier single interface, but it does not replace Mission
Control, Science Lab records, OLS 1.0, ORION certification or source custody.

The current decision is **consolidation before menu growth**. Existing deep
links remain stable. New vocabulary, modules or connectors enter only through
the registered admission path below; no term becomes canonical merely because
it appears in a visual or in the Navigator.

## Information architecture decision

The current interface has grown through valid research additions. The next UI
change should group the existing routes instead of adding another top-level
item for every term:

1. **Orientation** — home, About, central question and role boundaries;
2. **Framework** — UTG, Dynamics and structural framework views;
3. **Families** — Family Connection Map, Sequence and Lineage;
4. **Language** — OLS-facing research grammar and Envelope;
5. **Atlas** — modules, instruments, catalog and operational comparisons;
6. **Evidence** — evidence records, relations, provenance and admission;
7. **About** remains persistently reachable as a utility route.

The six-domain grouping is implemented in the internal read-only pilot. Home
now begins with LIFE and E8 Mutatio as executable flagships, followed by the
Observatory, Transversum, Tessarec/LOKI and ROOT7. The earlier five-item
Current Focus remains a governed supporting projection; About is a separate
authority explanation; and Vocabulary exposes controlled first-use records.
Existing routes and deep links remain valid. This presentation change does not
alter the canonical family map, OLS authority or public admission.

The current composition and open gates are controlled by
`NEXAH_NAVIGATOR_V2_1_CURRENT_STATE_2026-10-07.md` and its machine-readable
JSON companion.

## Admission rule for anything new

A proposed term, module, view or connector must provide:

1. a stable ID and named source;
2. the owning record and provenance;
3. its relation to established vocabulary;
4. one declared mapping type: scoped equivalence, specialization/composition,
   analogy/reading aid, historical source language or unresolved relation;
5. evidence state and claim ceiling;
6. explicit nonclaims;
7. lifecycle and visibility;
8. Human Owner review where canonical language, publication or activation is
   affected.

Until those fields exist, the item may be visible as a candidate but may not
change OLS, the canonical family map, scientific status or public scope.

## Priority rule

Before adding another top-level menu item:

1. place the item in one of the six domains above;
2. prefer a typed connector or lens over a new standalone module;
3. preserve existing IDs and deep links;
4. show evidence and negative boundaries in the same view;
5. reject silent canonicalization and silent publication.
