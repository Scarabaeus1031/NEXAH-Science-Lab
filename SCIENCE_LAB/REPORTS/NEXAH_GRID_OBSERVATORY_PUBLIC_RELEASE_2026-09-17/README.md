## Scientific boundary

The release defines a typed representation and return architecture. The
astronomical views are coordinate-frame and observer-orientation diagrams, not
ephemerides or evidence of physical coupling. Generated figures, pulses,
overlaps and characteristic polynomials remain bounded to their declared
selection, projection and graph rules.

The zodiac/ecliptic ring and cylindrical projection remain documented design
candidates. They are not implemented by this release.

Integrity hashes are recorded in [`SHA256_MANIFEST.txt`](SHA256_MANIFEST.txt).
