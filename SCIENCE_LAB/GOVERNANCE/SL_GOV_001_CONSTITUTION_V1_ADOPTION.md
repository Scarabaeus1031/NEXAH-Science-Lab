# SL-GOV-001 — Science Lab Constitution V1 Adoption

Decision date: 2026-08-14

Decision owner: **Human Owner**

Decision: `ADOPT_WITH_IMMEDIATE_STRUCTURE_FREEZE`

## Problem

The repository preserved substantial research, audits and evidence but lacked a
single enforced admission, WIP, run, closure and external-onramp contract. The
result was an expanding top-level corpus that remained difficult for the Owner
and inaccessible to a third-party contributor.

## Adopted resolution

- Adopt `LAB_CONSTITUTION.md` as the Lab operating constitution.
- Adopt one combined `LAB_REGISTER.md`; do not create separate universal
  Question and Lab catalogs in V1.
- Retain existing historical report forms.
- Require the canonical Labreport schema for new closures and owner-selected
  retrospective closures.
- Set normal WIP to one ACTIVE plus two OPEN cycles.
- Set current scientific WIP to zero under `STRUCTURE_FREEZE`.
- Require a complete Run Contract before any runnable claim.
- Prohibit new top-level research packages without admission.
- Preserve distributed evidence and existing canonical homes.

## Non-effects

This decision does not change a scientific result, adopt a NEXAH/ORION claim,
authorize execution, reopen a closed line, publish data, merge to main or move
historical packages.

## Next gate

Complete and verify every Structure Freeze exit item. Reopening requires a
separate Human Owner decision.
