# Knowledge-promotion rules

```text
RAW RESULT
  -> provenance and contract verified
AUDITED RESULT
  -> claim/nonclaim and defect classification fixed
INTEGRATED RESULT
  -> architecture/capability/elimination ledgers updated
LAB KNOWLEDGE
  -> canonical Labreport indexed and cycle CLOSED
```

Promotion gates:

1. evidence identity and scope are explicit;
2. validity/audit status is known;
3. replay/replication is recorded when required;
4. competing explanations, ceilings, nulls and information boundaries are
   retained;
5. wording does not exceed the evidence class;
6. downstream non-implications are explicit;
7. closure artifacts are hash-bound.

Negative, failed, invalid, uninformative, non-identifiable, lossy, undefined,
and theoretically eliminated results are durable knowledge. They remain in
the Labreport and eliminated-questions ledger so future work cannot silently
repeat a closed branch.

No plot, metric, code path, passing unit test, prototype, or symbolic document
is promoted merely because it exists or executes.
