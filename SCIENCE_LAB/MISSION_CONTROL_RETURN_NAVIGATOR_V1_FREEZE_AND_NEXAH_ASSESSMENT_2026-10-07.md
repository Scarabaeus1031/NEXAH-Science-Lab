## 5. Freeze rule

While frozen, the following changes require a separate Human Owner reopen or
version decision:

- a new top-level domain or route;
- a new conceptual module or canonical Connection Family;
- promotion of a candidate, archive object or incoming artifact;
- a new normative OLS term or silent change to a controlled term;
- alteration of a package-local result or claim ceiling;
- public allowlisting or release;
- replacement of a current master surface;
- a new Current Focus item that changes the five-item baseline.

Permitted maintenance is limited to evidence-preserving repair:

- broken-link, syntax, accessibility, portability or security repair;
- correction of a demonstrable transcription error;
- deterministic regeneration from unchanged controlling data;
- documentation of a known limitation without changing the claim.

Every permitted repair must retain a recorded delta, rerun the relevant
builders and audit, and either preserve the freeze manifest or issue a new
versioned freeze receipt.

## 6. What today's work means for NEXAH

### 6.1 NEXAH has become self-describing

The research landscape is no longer accessible only through folder knowledge,
historical memory or isolated visual artifacts. A new reader can now move from
plain-language orientation to families, concrete instruments, typed relations,
evidence and claim boundaries through one governed surface.

This does not prove scientific utility, but it changes the evaluable object.
NEXAH can now be assessed as a coherent research-navigation architecture
rather than only as a large archive of ideas and demonstrations.

### 6.2 Representation is now an operational discipline

The repeated distinction between carrier, frame, cut, projection,
reconstruction, residual and return is no longer only philosophical prose. It
appears in registries, routes, instruments, evidence records, status labels and
negative controls.

The strongest common proposition is therefore not that all systems are one.
It is that several bounded views can be connected closely enough to compare
and reconstruct while their non-identities remain visible.

### 6.3 Negative results are part of the architecture

Block 03, source ambiguity, `ABSTAIN`, quarantined ACR-42 work, missing GLBs,
open bridges and unresolved terminology remain visible. The Navigator does not
hide them to create a smoother story.

For NEXAH this is important: the system is beginning to preserve the boundary
of knowledge as carefully as the positive relation.

### 6.4 The human/instrument boundary is implemented

Mission Control, Science Lab, Navigator, OLS, ORION and NEXAHEDRON now have
distinct stated responsibilities. The Navigator can orient and compare, but it
cannot activate research, transfer evidence, extend OLS, certify ORION or
publish itself.

The distinction between human meaning and machine structure is therefore part
of the interface contract rather than only a declared ideal.

### 6.5 Local language has become more scientifically connectable

Terms such as UTG, OIL/RIS, MRB, ACR, `ø`, `œ`, Connection Family and claim
ceiling now appear beside established or adjacent vocabulary, explicit
non-equivalences and controlling sources.

This improves Anschlussfähigkeit: a reader can recognize what is standard,
what is locally defined, what is historical, what is fixture-bound and what
remains unresolved.

### 6.6 The next risk is expansion without evaluation

The Navigator can continue absorbing artifacts indefinitely. That is no longer
the highest-value action. The present system is sufficiently coherent to test
whether it actually helps people orient, retrieve evidence, recognize limits
and formulate better next questions.

Freezing now protects the evaluable object from moving while it is being
assessed.

## 7. What today's work does not mean

The completed integration does not establish:

- a universal NEXAH theory or shared physical mechanism;
- new mathematics from glyphs, numerical recurrences or visual symmetry;
- a validated general reconstruction method;
- human utility or comprehension improvement;
- scientific novelty beyond the bounded package findings;
- a production-ready product;
- a public Navigator release;
- new ORION capability, OLS grammar or research activation.

## 8. Assessment gate

The next phase is a Human Owner and reader assessment, not additional intake.
It should answer:

1. Can a first reader explain what NEXAH, the Science Lab and the Navigator
   each do?
2. Can the reader locate one module, its instrument, its evidence and its claim
   ceiling without repository knowledge?
3. Can the reader distinguish relation from identity, framework from validated
   application and representation from source?
4. Does the vocabulary connect local NEXAH language to established fields
   without flattening either side?
5. Do Primegrid and OIL/RIS work as useful examples, or do they dominate the
   larger framework too strongly?
6. Which parts belong in a later public NEXAH entrance, and which must remain
   internal research infrastructure?
7. Does the Handbook provide enough shared context to evaluate NEXAH as a
   method rather than as a collection of artifacts?

No assessment result is presumed. A later decision may retain the baseline,
revise it as Navigator v1.1, define a bounded public pilot or keep the system
internal.
