# History and Historical Topography

Status: `MAINTAINED_SUBJECT_ENTRANCE / NAVIGATION_AND_EVIDENCE_RULES`

## Subject scope

This shelf covers historical claims tied to place, landscape, settlement,
boundaries, routes, water systems, land use, buildings and maps. It combines
historical source criticism with historical geography and cartographic
provenance.

The first maintained case is [Rödelheim History and Historical
Topography](../../CASE_STUDIES/ROEDELHEIM_HISTORY_AND_TOPOGRAPHY_2026-09-25/README.md).

## What counts as evidence

- archival identity, shelfmark or stable public source;
- date, creator, scale, legend and map purpose where available;
- separation of original scan from annotation or reconstruction;
- corroboration across independent records;
- georeferencing with documented control points and residual error when spatial
  persistence is claimed;
- explicit uncertainty when a name, sign, route or watercourse is ambiguous.

## Claim boundaries

Visual similarity is a research prompt, not proof of historical continuity or
intention. A modern street bend does not by itself establish an earlier river
arm. A line in a map does not become a road, boundary, watercourse or military
axis without the applicable legend and provenance. Symbolic and curatorial
readings remain separate from historical claims unless independently sourced.

## Review route

Historical claims should be reviewed by the relevant archival, local-history,
historical-geography, hydrology, archaeology or building-history expertise.
The owning case-study package retains evidence authority; Mission Control keeps
only the access pointer and portfolio status.
