## Research reading of the JANUS group

The pictures establish that the same repository workflow produced phase,
score, threshold, geometry, gate-candidate, density and multiscale views. They
do not establish that the score is a coordinate-invariant coherence measure or
that threshold crossings predict externally labelled transitions.

The current experimental archaeology already records the relevant limits:

- the main JANUS scalar uses a norm of componentwise forward/backward products,
  not a dot-product cosine, and changes under general coordinate rotation;
- EXP-25 selects exactly 60 candidates per smoothing scale by quantile
  construction, while cross-scale location persistence was not scored;
- the EXP-24 surrogate statistic is degenerate and cannot validate JANUS
  strength;
- path/navigation interpretations remain unresolved and are sensitive to
  perturbation.

Therefore the group is useful as `VIS / TEST` evidence for a representation
pipeline. It is not validation of a universal aperture or navigation operator.

## One-second display orbit: superseded placement

The first Tessarec demonstrator joined the two displayed time projections by
one closed comparison orbit:

```text
T^4 -> T^3(q) -> T^4.
```

For that preliminary interface clock only,

```text
f = 1/T
T = 1.00 s
f = 1.00 Hz.
```

The control executes exactly one cycle and stops. The equality is the exact
frequency/period relation for that declared display clock; it does not assign a
one-hertz physical frequency to the represented carrier, pearl or field.

Later owner review recovered the missing five-clock side. The canonical current
placement is therefore

```text
T_j -> H_j -> Q°(t0) -> Pi_Q -> trace -> mask -> T^3(q) -> return,
```

where each `H_j` may execute one normalized cycle per display second. The real
period `T_j` remains unchanged. `Q°` is the common observation cut and has no
independent frequency in Test 04C. The earlier `T^4 <-> T^3(q)` orbit remains a
carrier/record-return picture, not the complete clock linkage. See
`19_FIVE_H_Q_TRIPTYCHON_RAINBOW_LOOM_SYNTHESIS.md`.

The eight-address cylinder is a current visualization of the existing
`8 <-> 16` sign-state motif. A related historical source is present at:

`SCIENCE_LAB/CASE_STUDIES/CIKADA_3301_LATER_MARKER_PROVENANCE_INTAKE/CIKADA 3301 LATER MARKER CORPUS/🧭🧭🧭GITHUB-TODO_UPLOAD NAVIGATION/🜂🜂🜂 ANKH 2040 — QED Summary Index/visual Gallery III/ROOT-432 CYLINDER BLUEPRINT.png`

The intake classifies that source under weak filesystem provenance and an
expression/marker layer. Its historical physical claims are not inherited by
