## Important branch distinctions

1. **DIN/US format lenses are page/view frames.** They govern `CENTER`, `FIT`, `CROP` and `SCALE` and preserve or lose display information in declared ways.
2. **Gate-operator sheets are radial state bins.** The historical `sheet-aware` Lorenz script divides projected radius into six bins and constructs a state-transition matrix. It is not a paper-format model.
3. **CRT is exact arithmetic.** Polar drawings, matrices and glyphs are representations layered over it; they do not alter the theorem.
4. **A number can carry several records.** Numeric value, factorization, residue tuple, grid address, digit string, visual position and authorial association must remain separate fields.
5. **A relation can become a later carrier.** The observer diagrams and generation-corner material repeatedly move from points to relations and from relations to systems. This is a central sequencing idea, but each transition needs its own typed map.

## Transversal evolution line

The branching history does not mean that the materials are disconnected. Across
the branches, the Human Owner repeatedly developed the same transformation
problem through different carriers: Euclidean construction, discrete grid,
layered stair, folded/cylindrical or helical transport, shadow/color projection,
residual and return. This transversal line is recorded separately in
`08_EVOLUTIONARY_TRANSFORMATION_SPINE.md`; it supplements the provenance tree
without turning the historical branches into one retroactively unified theory.
