## Authority precedence and corrections

The corpus contains self-correction. The August 21 full audit already separates
exact arithmetic, established science, models and open claims. The later
`nexah_transition_bridges.pdf` is the controlling claim-boundary supplement for
cross-domain comparisons. It explicitly rejects or narrows several stronger
statements found in `NEXAH_ILAU_Chemistry_Framework_Report_v2.pdf` and older
working reports.

Current boundaries:

- Stern-Gerlach is an established example of axis-dependent binary measurement,
  not proof of Split Rest.
- Aharonov-Bohm is an established flux-dependent phase effect, not proof of a
  universal NEXAH grid or cosmic boundary.
- the cylindrical periodic-table fold changes representation and adjacency; it
  is not a physical law;
- the Pa-231 sevenfold facts coexist, but no nuclear-shell causal coupling or
  Pa-controlled platinum relay is established;
- protein folding is a legitimate representation-audit case, but AlphaFold did
  not prove a NEXAH grid and no four universal NEXAH folding forces are shown;
- the Potential Architecture plate is a broad authored synthesis. Claims such
  as biology being the universal slit between chemistry and physics remain
  analogy/hypothesis until independently defined and tested;
- the chemical boundary sequence is scientifically promising because it names
  measurable variables, but the located corpus supplies a protocol/visual, not
  a completed empirical result set.

## Intake thread contract

only list files. Each intake should contain:

1. origin question and initial observation;
2. ordered stages, including failed or superseded stages;
3. the transition between every pair of stages;
4. carrier/frame before and after each transition;
5. operator or translation rule, if defined;
6. residual/return at that step;
7. status per claim: exact, established reference, representation, analogy,
   empirical result, open or corrected;
8. source binding to document page, HTML, image, code and output data;
9. current working conclusion and the question that remains open;
10. explicit statement whether the intake activates research. Stage 0 default:
    `NO`.

An image may be an important state in the thread, but its visible text is not a
substitute for reading the surrounding report, code, data and revision history.
