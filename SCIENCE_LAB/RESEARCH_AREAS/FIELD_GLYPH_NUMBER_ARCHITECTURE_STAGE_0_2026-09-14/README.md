# Field Glyph and Number Architecture

Date: `2026-09-14`

Status: `STAGE_0_CURRENT_WORKING_TRUTH_NO_ACTIVE_RESEARCH`

## Purpose

This is the Stage 0 entry point for a distinct NEXAH research area: how discrete marks, numbers, positions, frames and transformations become readable relations over time.

The area is broader than Prime/CRT and narrower than a universal theory. It preserves the development from Numbermagick, CRT 11357 and the old UCRT material through Prime Genesis, the Visual Equation Atlas and the bounded CIKADA/Shadow studies. It treats mathematics both as a formal discipline and as an authored expressive language. Expression may generate questions and representations; it does not inherit scientific authority without a declared operator and test.

## Working question

> Given a carrier or field, a region and time, a cut or frame, and a declared decoder, what does a mark or number point to; which relations survive a transformation; which new relations appear; and what remains unresolved?

## Current research map

```text
event or field
  -> cut / frame / sampling
  -> discrete address or mark
  -> local relation
  -> transformation / reflection / fold
  -> network, family or flow
  -> observation / reconstruction / return
  -> retained, lost, added or unresolved record
```

The observer line is upstream of any physics claim. `A` and `B` produce situated views; their oriented difference can become a relation; a later `C` can observe that relation. Later operations may act on already formed relations rather than on isolated points. This is a research grammar for sequencing and emergence, not a claim that every use of `A/B/C` shares one ontology.

The seven research branches are cross-sections through the corpus. A second,
longitudinal organization is equally important: field/carrier -> cut -> relation
-> grid -> staircase -> fold/helix -> carrier change -> shadow/color projection
-> residual -> return. This owner-recognized evolutionary spine is documented
and bounded in `08_EVOLUTIONARY_TRANSFORMATION_SPINE.md`.

## What is already present

- exact arithmetic, CRT, factorization, indexing and format-view controls;
- polar, Cartesian, grid, strip, fold, spiral and return representations;
- candidate number/operator navigation and glyph-address grammars;
- executable transition-matrix and sheet-state experiments;
- bounded negative and positive results for reconstruction, projection and shadow/crown formation;
- a large visual and authored expression corpus.

## What is not established

- no universal prime generator, privileged-number law or single cross-family decoder;
- no demonstrated derivation of physics from the visual or numeric structures;
- no proof that repeated shapes or numbers across modules have one cause;
- no adopted canonical glyph language;
- no active experiment, backlog or product activation from this Stage 0 record.

A later experiment requires a single named question, frozen input, declared decoder/operator, comparator or null, output metric and stop rule.
