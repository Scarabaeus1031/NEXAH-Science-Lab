## Writing rule

When a historical acronym has no adopted expansion, keep the token and say that the expansion is unresolved. Do not invent a long form. When a source uses the same word for different objects, qualify it: `page sheet`, `radial state sheet`, `visual plate`, `graph layer` or `branch sheet`.
