The elemental symbols, alloy constituents and some color/material observations
are potential source anchors. The following older claims are not accepted as
formal or empirical results:

- `F = E = nu*h` is dimensionally wrong; the valid photon relation is
  `E = h*nu`, while `nu` and `E` are not equal quantities;
- `rho_c = c^2/(phi*hbar)` is not established as a mass or energy density by
  the document and is dimensionally unsupported for that label;
- `E = m*c*k_beta` is undefined in the supplied source;
- pH, oxidation and reduction are not interchangeable axes;
- color, temperature, conductivity and consciousness are not demonstrated to
  arise from one geometric cause;
- the neutrino/metal weaving and universal Gold-Mercury resonance statements
  are authored expression, not evidence-backed physical mechanisms.

The correct current disposition is therefore **source preserved, expression
recognized, empirical claims re-typed and not promoted**.

## 4. Fe as residual of stone

The owner statement `Fe is the residual of stone` can now be placed against the
existing metals corpus without adopting its universal-resonance claims:

```text
stone sample X
  -> preparation and assay A
  -> elemental/mineralogical observation O_A(X)
  -> Fe-bearing fraction R_Fe(X;A)
  + non-Fe matrix
  + detection-limit/uncertainty residual.
```

Three quantities must remain distinct:

1. total elemental Fe abundance;
2. abundance of iron-bearing mineral phases;
3. yield of recovered metallic Fe after a reduction/separation process.

Only a declared method can decide which one `R_Fe` denotes.
