# Research Area Map

## Area identity

Working name: **Field Glyph and Number Architecture**

This area studies the relation between discrete expression and structured fields. Its unit is not “the number alone,” but a typed occurrence of a number, mark or relation inside a carrier, frame, region, time and transformation history.

## Shared process grammar

```text
1. FIELD        declare carrier, event or source
2. FRAME        declare coordinates, region, time and scale
3. CUT          select what becomes visible or addressable
4. MARK         encode a number, glyph, cell or relation record
5. RELATE       compare at least two situated records
6. TRANSFORM    move, fold, reflect, project or reconstruct
7. EMERGE       register a new relation only after its prerequisites exist
8. RETURN       compare with the declared source or earlier frame
9. CLASSIFY     retain I/L/A/U and provenance
```

“Emergence” here means operational dependence on prior steps: a relation or network is unavailable until its source elements and maps have been constructed. It does not by itself mean ontological or physical emergence.

## Longitudinal process view

```text
carrier -> cut -> elementary distinction -> relation/unfolding -> grid/address
        -> layer/stair -> fold/cylinder/helix -> carrier change
        -> shadow/color projection -> record -> residual -> return
```

The shared grammar above is more specific than a claim of visual similarity but
less strong than a theorem: multiple source modules instantiate adjacent parts,
while no single historical source formally defines the whole composite map.

## Three authority lanes

| Lane | Admissible content | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| Formal | exact arithmetic, definitions, proofs, deterministic code and frozen tests | equation, algorithm, verified table, bounded result |
| Model | explicitly chosen representation, coordinate system, decoder, comparator and tolerance | map, simulation, reconstruction, residual |
| Expression | human-authored visual, musical, poetic or mnemonic organization | glyph, plate, score, visual analogy, question generator |

The lanes may be connected by a provenance record; they may not silently inherit one another's authority.
