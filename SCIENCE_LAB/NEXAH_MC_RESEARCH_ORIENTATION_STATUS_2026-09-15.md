```text
SOURCE / CARRIER
  -> FRAME + ADDRESS
  -> CUT / APERTURE
  -> OPERATOR Q/T/R

  -> REGISTRATION / BINDER
```

This is a methodological and documentary unification. Each profile keeps its
own carrier, metric, scale, baseline and stop rule. Similar diagrams do not
establish equal mechanisms, and arithmetic equality does not establish
physical coupling.
