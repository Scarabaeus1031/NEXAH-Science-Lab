## What this Lab is

The NEXAH Science Lab studies bounded questions about representations,
information loss, structural relations, deterministic operators and their
operational limits. It values negative, invalid, uninformative and null outcomes
as durable evidence when their protocols and boundaries are valid.

It does not claim universal NEXAH validity, new physical law, navigation or
control capability, human benefit or external-domain validity merely because a
synthetic experiment or replay passes.

## Safe first contribution

During Structure Freeze, an external contributor may only:

- review documentation and links;
- report a missing dependency, ambiguous instruction or broken path;
- independently inspect a manifest or claim boundary without executing science;
- propose a bounded correction without changing evidence.

Do not run or modify a Study until the Lab is reopened and that Study has an
explicit Run Contract.

## How to report a problem

Provide:

- repository revision;
- operating system and runtime;
- exact command or document path;
- expected and observed behavior;
- complete error output or hash divergence;
- whether tracked files changed;
- whether any scientific result was exposed before the problem.

Do not repair or overwrite a frozen artifact. A reproducible contradiction is a
valuable result and must remain inspectable.

## Contribution boundary

A contribution can improve documentation, reproducibility or implementation
without adopting a scientific interpretation. Scientific status changes require
the Study's audit path and Human Owner decision.
