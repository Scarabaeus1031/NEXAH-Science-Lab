# FIELD NOTES GOVERNANCE

Status: `CANONICAL GOVERNANCE REFERENCE`

Operational effect: `NONE`

## 1. Purpose

`FIELD_NOTES` is the protected space for exploratory thinking.

Field Notes record:

- observations;
- intuitions;
- visual language;
- metaphors;
- design ideas;
- historical reflections;
- questions;
- possible research candidates.

They are intentionally created before scientific review.

This document governs the use of `FIELD_NOTES`. It does not modify the
Constitution or Research and introduces no scientific claims.

## 2. Scientific Status

Field Notes are:

```text
UNREVIEWED
```

They are not:

- evidence;
- research;
- architecture;
- mathematical definitions;
- scientific objects;
- publications.

## 3. Operational Effect

```text
OPERATIONAL_EFFECT: NONE
```

Creating, editing or deleting a Field Note changes no scientific,
architectural or governance state.

## 4. Authority

Field Notes belong to Human observation.

They record possibilities.

They do not establish truth.

## 5. Role of ORION

ORION reviews Field Notes. Its task is classification.

It is not validation, execution or publication.

Possible review outcomes include:

- Archive;
- Library;
- Design;
- Historical reference;
- Existing literature;
- Research candidate;
- Duplicate;
- Out of scope.

Classification does not promote a Field Note into research automatically.

## 6. Promotion Path

```text
Observation

↓

FIELD NOTE

↓

ORION REVIEW

↓

Classification

↓

Optional Research Candidate

↓

Research Direction

↓

Mathematical Core

↓

Protocol

↓

Evidence

↓

Publication
```

Every promotion requires explicit Human authorization.

No automatic transition exists.

## 7. Relationship to Other Repository Layers

| Layer | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `LUCY` | records Human purpose |
| `FIELD_NOTES` | record exploratory observations |
| `RESEARCH` | records bounded scientific questions |
| `MATHEMATICAL_CORE` | records declared mathematical objects |
| `PROTOCOL` | records verification procedures |
| `EVIDENCE` | records executed observations |
| `PUBLICATION` | records externally communicated results |

Each layer has a different purpose.

## 8. Guiding Principle

Field Notes preserve curiosity.

ORION provides orientation.

Research requires evidence.

Mathematics requires declared objects.

Meaning remains a Human activity.

## Closing Statement

Field Notes are intentionally incomplete.

Their value lies in preserving observations until they can be reviewed,
classified, refined or respectfully discarded.

They are the laboratory notebook of the NEXAH ecosystem.
