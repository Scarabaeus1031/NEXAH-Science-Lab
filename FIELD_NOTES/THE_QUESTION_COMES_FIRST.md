# THE QUESTION COMES FIRST

Status: `REFLECTION`

Governed by: `FIELD_NOTES/FIELD_NOTES_GOVERNANCE.md`

Operational effect: `NONE`

## Purpose

This note records one methodological reflection.

It does not define science.

It does not define mathematics.

It records one possible attitude toward inquiry.

## Reflection

Questions often survive longer than answers.

Answers belong to a particular state of knowledge.

Questions may continue to guide observation even when answers change.

For this reason, preserving a good question may be as valuable as preserving a
current answer.

## The Handle

A question is not a conclusion.

It is a handle.

A handle allows a Human to approach a problem.

It does not solve the problem.

It makes investigation possible.

## Observation

Different disciplines may produce different answers.

A carefully formulated question may remain meaningful across those
disciplines.

Questions therefore help preserve orientation while knowledge evolves.

## Human

Curiosity begins with observation.

Research begins when observation becomes a declared question.

Evidence begins when the question becomes testable.

Publication begins after evidence has been reviewed.

Each step has its own place.

## Explicit Non-Claims

This note does not claim:

- a philosophy of science;
- a research method;
- a mathematical principle;
- superiority of questions over answers;
- universal validity.

It records one Human reflection only.

## Closing Reflection

A good answer explains.

A good question continues to open paths.

The laboratory preserves both.

ORION may help distinguish them.

The Human decides which path is worth following.
