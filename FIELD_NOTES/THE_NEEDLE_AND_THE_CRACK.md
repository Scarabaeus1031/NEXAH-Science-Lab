# THE NEEDLE AND THE CRACK

Status: `REFLECTION`

Operational effect: `NONE`

## Purpose

This note records the distinction between symbolic intuition and mathematical
objects.

> The following concepts are metaphorical orientation language.
> They are not mathematical definitions.

- A crack represents discontinuity, separation or transition.
- A needle represents connection, anchoring or traversal.
- A root is understood here as an anchoring relation rather than a fracture.
- These are visual and conceptual metaphors.
- They introduce no scientific claims.

> Mathematics studies declared objects.
> Metaphors help Humans see them.
