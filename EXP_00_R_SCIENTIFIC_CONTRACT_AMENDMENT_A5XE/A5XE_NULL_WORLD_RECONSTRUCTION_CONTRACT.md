# A5XE Null-World Reconstruction Contract

Each N1/N2/N3/N4_T/N4_F family contains exactly repetition IDs 0–199 and only population/RNG/frozen-rule evidence—not final statistics. Given canonical rows and the accepted A3 namespace, each derivation independently regenerates every permutation/donor draw and transformed rank world.

- N1 applies the forward action map separately by representation/training seed, recomputes null carrier actions from physical labels and refits baseline/augmented models.
- N2 permutes complete F weak-rank vectors without replacement within split and seed; recipient carrier outcomes remain observed.
- N3 recomputes phase/target bins, clockwise empty-bin map and different-seed donor pools, then makes one replacement draw per recipient.
- N4 recomputes full-training LOO cutpoints, right-insertion bins, carrier-specific strata and higher-first merges before within-stratum F-rank permutation.

For every world the derivation recomputes mean coherence, top-action agreement and T/F coefficients and held-out gains. Missing donor/refit/statistic/world invalidates with no retry. Monte Carlo uses exactly `p=(1+k)/201`, `k=count(null>=observed)`, adverse ties and `k<=4`; coefficient-null columns remain mandatory diagnostics only.
