## 6. The actual research subject

The Owner clarified that no completed scientific breakthrough was being
claimed. The significant realization was that the drift had identified the
research subject itself:

> How much structured diversity can be generated from small typed starting
> records and elementary operators, and how can genuine preservation,
> translation, compression and prediction be distinguished from numerical or
> visual resemblance?

This is not the claim that small numbers explain the universe. It is a
testable programme about typed generation and comparison:

```text
small carrier
  -> operator grammar
  -> expression, state and relation spaces
  -> representations
  -> domain-bound dynamics
  -> readout
  -> invariant / loss audit
  -> residual
  -> Return or stop
```

A mathematical finding would require a frozen grammar, controls and an outcome
such as canonical coverage, minimal description, a proved invariant, a useful
compression result, held-out predictive value or an explicit map intertwining
two operators.

## 8. Next domain architecture

The next comparison was described as:

```text
PHYSICS  |  BIOLOGY  |  CHEMISTRY
edge     |  binder   |  edge
```

This is a provisional research layout, not a claim that biology is literally
the zero of nature or that physics and chemistry are symmetric substances.

Biology occupies the candidate middle because living systems bind physical
flows and chemical transformations into maintained histories involving
boundary regulation, metabolism, repair, reproduction and adaptation.

The existing `1|0|1` record supplies the provisional visual grammar:

```text
1        |        0        |        1
edge A   | marked middle   | edge B
         | point / circle  |
         | cut / stitch    |
         | living binder   |
```

The middle is not empty. Depending on the declared view it may be the point,
circle, aperture, seam, channel or living binder through which the two edge
accounts can be compared.

The record must remain typed:

```text

101         decimal number and 26th prime
101*2^k     tested dyadic SNCE carrier
```

Their coexistence is an example of the research subject, not evidence that
their mechanisms are identical.
