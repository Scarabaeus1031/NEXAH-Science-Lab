# Corpus Discovery and Authority Map

## Discovery result

The ecosystem is distributed across four controlling surfaces:

| Surface | Authority in this audit | Material inspected |
|---|---|---:|
| NEXAH Core | Framework, released OLS, governance, evidence pointers, typed orientation models | 24 files |
| NEXAH-ORION | Certified V1 scope, source boundaries, historical/separate runtime and LYRA work | 18 files |
| Science Lab | Closed tests, decisions, RID-01/SWM-01, RUN01–04, scientific currentness | 26 files |
| Mission Control | Location/authority register, claim ladder, portfolio/currentness state | 6 files |
| **Total** | | **74 files** |

Twenty-six inspected records were terminal decisions, certifications, frozen baselines, or final gate records. Discovery excluded vendored dependencies, caches, generated workspace clones, and broad historical archives unless a controlling record pointed to them.

## Authority order

1. Human Authority and accepted governance decisions.
2. Released/frozen specifications and certified baselines within their named scope.
3. Terminal case decisions and immutable result records.
4. Implemented source plus tests for bounded software behavior.
5. Current evidence records and adopted claim matrices.
6. Informative architecture, demonstrators, and expression material.
7. Historical or superseded interpretation.

No arrow transfers authority. OLS owns semantics; ORION owns its certified deterministic responsibilities; Science Lab owns its recorded tests; Mission Control owns operational routing; Human Authority owns activation and adoption.

## Important discovery corrections

- OLS is not merely a minimal vocabulary. `OLS-RELEASE-1.0.0` contains seven normative documents, one informative companion, stable registries, operator contracts, profile composition, derivations, conformance, versioning, checksums, and release verification.
- ORION is not merely a proposal. Version 1 contains a certified deterministic implementation through Structural Representation, UNDERSTAND, Relations, Navigation, Orientation Map, and Expression, ending at `at_slice_iv_certified`.
- ORION Runtime, Gateway, provider/context, transformation-planning, and LYRA source also exist, but later authority records keep them historical, experimental, separately governed, or not adopted.
- The Evidence Atlas is an existing human-navigable evidence layer, but it is not a uniform machine-readable claim–code–data–output graph.
- RID-01 closes the schema gap identified by SWM-01 at schema level only; it does not supply an adapter, Rust source, ORION integration, or runtime.

## Coverage limits

This is a controlling-record inventory, not a byte-level inventory of every historical asset. `MISSING` is used only where multiple authoritative records positively require a role and the inspected corpus identifies no accepted implementation for that role.
