# Governance and Authority Map

| Authority | Owns | Does not acquire |
|---|---|---|
| Human Authority | adoption, activation, consequential decision, prototype gate | scientific truth by approval |
| NEXAH governance | ecosystem scope, provenance, evidence discipline, version/freeze rules | ORION execution authority |
| OLS release | semantic ownership and conformance language | runtime or empirical truth |
| ORION certified baseline | bounded deterministic structural responsibilities | Human meaning, science, application authority |
| Science Lab | recorded test inputs, controls, outputs, negative results | cross-repository adoption authority |
| Evidence Atlas | claim/evidence navigation and limitation visibility | automatic canonical admission |
| Mission Control | location, routing, portfolio/currentness pointers | technical or scientific ownership |

STOP, ABSTAIN, HOLD, REVISE, freeze, custody, and supersession are operational controls rather than scientific findings. Later terminal decisions govern status; historical material remains available without becoming current authority.

No authorization in TITAN-00 activates a successor, Runtime, ORION extension, Rust build, application, or research program.
