# TITAN-00 Final Decision

## 1. What exists?

A governed orientation ecosystem exists: released OLS semantics; a certified deterministic ORION structural core; extensive bounded audit/evidence records; typed observer/representation distinctions; repository-specific orientation models; expression/demonstrator layers; and RID-01's machine-readable interface contract.

## 2. What is solid?

OLS release integrity and documented semantics are solid within specification scope. ORION V1 is solid and integrated inside its certified structural scope. RUN01–04 and later boundary audits strongly establish non-collapse rules in their bounded formal/synthetic cases. RID's schema and 20 fixtures are solid at schema level.

## 3. What is partial?

Corpuswide provenance linkage, the Evidence Atlas machine layer, OLS-to-RID-to-ORION mapping, public Runtime compliance, and Human-facing contract binding are partial.

## 4. What is expression only?

Historical glyph systems, FIELD/FORM imagery, animal/mirror/rope/spiral/color/number mappings, and the HTML visual series remain creative, explanatory, mnemonic, or archival. They do not define machine semantics.

## 5. What was rejected?

Letter/glyph-derived semantics, recurrence as validation, a tested universal architecture, and chemistry as a proof point were rejected or bounded away. These are not implementation gaps.

## 6. What is actually missing?

Six bounded gaps remain: an adopted OLS–RID–ORION crosswalk; a RID validator/canonicalizer; one minimal pre-defined execution path; a RID-to-ORION adapter outside certified V1; end-to-end conformance/replay; and an RID-bound Human-inspectable result view.

## 7. Which gaps matter for machine execution?

G01–G05 matter. G02 and G03 block any minimal execution; G04 and G05 block integration. There is no T0 coherence failure and no research gap that must be solved to build a minimal contract checker.

## 8. Is OLS a vocabulary, grammar, or formal language?

OLS 1.0 is a formal implementation-independent semantic language specification with vocabulary, typed operator contracts, composition, derivations, and conformance. It is not an executable programming language and defines no mandatory concrete serialization.

## 9. What exactly is ORION at present?

ORION is a certified deterministic structural orientation core through Expression and STOP. Its repository also preserves broader executable candidate components that are outside the certified/adopted V1 boundary. It is not one general integrated semantic machine.

## 10. What exactly does RID-01 add?

RID-01 adds a current, versioned, machine-readable contract and 20 schema fixtures that bind the later type distinctions into one inspectable interface. It adds no adapter, runtime, Rust code, ORION capability, or scientific claim.

## 11. Is a minimal Rust core now justified?

Componentwise, yes; globally, no. A read-only validator/canonicalizer for the frozen RID contract could be justified after Human approval without inventing semantics. An operator engine or ORION integration is not yet justified.

## 12. What is the smallest next build, if any?

If Human Authority opens the Prototype Gate, build only a read-only RID validator/canonical serializer against the existing 20 fixtures. Do not connect it to ORION, add execution semantics, or create a UI in that first slice.
