# 03 — View 5+1 Map

## Observed overview structure

| Position | Overview | Primary question |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Human View | What happened to a bounded record through observation and interpretation? |
| 2 | Science View | What was retained, lost, added, or unresolved in the evidence process? |
| 3 | Builder View | What is implemented, coupled, missing, or blocked? |
| 4 | Outreach View | What object moves through which channel, track, resonance, and gate? |
| 5 | Owner View | What is active, blocked, prioritized, versioned, or awaiting a decision? |
| +1 | `CURRENT` Control View | What is operationally current across the distinct views? |

## Why this matters

Mission Control already practices a form of **Gewaltenteilung der Darstellung**:

- no one overview is treated as the whole event;
- each view has a bounded purpose and vocabulary;
- the control view relates them without erasing their differences;
- contradictions can remain visible until resolved;
- authority remains with the appropriate source and decision owner.

This is the operational form of the proposed Theory of Perspective label. The contribution is the disciplined arrangement and comparison of representations, not a claim that reality is arbitrary or that all views are equally adequate.

## What TOP adds—and does not add

TOP adds a common audit question across the existing views:

```text
From Q° at time t, through which cut and transformation was this record produced,
and what returns when it is compared with its declared source?
```

TOP does not add:

- a new repository;
- a new domain authority;
- an absolute meta-view outside all perspectives;
- permission to substitute visual analogy for evidence;
- proof that a recurring pattern is a physical law.

## Blind flex identified

The ecosystem is unusually strong at keeping multiple views open, but it has not consistently turned that capability back upon its own repository and overview architecture.

The blind flex is therefore:

> NEXAH already performs multi-perspective bounded comparison internally, but does not yet maintain a shared, typed account of what changes when evidence, semantics, processing, interaction, public presentation, and operational currentness pass between repositories.

This is a method visibility problem before it is a software problem.
