# EXP-T01 Scope and Boundaries

Status before execution: `PREREGISTERED / NOT YET EXECUTED`  
Authority: isolated Science-Lab experiment; `NOT_ADOPTED`

EXP-T01 tests only whether a small deterministic evaluator distinguishes
ordinary invertible recovery, perturbation-induced degradation, proven
information loss, and non-identifiability. A separate synthetic linear-system
subtest uses `STILLPOINT` only as a label for the preregistered equilibrium
`x_eq=(0,0)`.

The experiment does not test or establish NEXAH/ORION validity, a new theorem or
invariant, physical or universal stability, IEEE/PEGASE behavior, early warning,
prediction, risk, control, product readiness, cosmology, 137/137.5, 7.83 Hz,
Betasplit, quantization claims, or fluid/quantum mechanisms.

Evidence labels are restricted to `MATHEMATICAL_DEFINITIONAL`, `ENCODED`,
`SOFTWARE_CHECK`, `EMPIRICAL_SYNTHETIC`, and `HYPOTHESIS`. Representation
equality, structural preservation, recoverability, and dynamical stability are
reported separately.

The canonical NEXAH repository was inspected read-only. No general recovery API
or LEO inverse contract suitable for reuse was located; EXP-T01 therefore uses
only its frozen, local, standard-library implementation.
