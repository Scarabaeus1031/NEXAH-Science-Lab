## Why the negative results add value

The sequence converted an attractive invariance observation into a bounded,
testable methodological question. Counterfactual misses showed that stability
could be compression; the replication quantified that failure; the independent
decoder then rejected the exact hierarchy while preserving the broader
association. This narrows the hypothesis, identifies decoder and transformation
dependence, and supplies conditions an external replication can genuinely
falsify. Hiding these failures would turn a reproducible boundary into an
unsupported discovery claim.
