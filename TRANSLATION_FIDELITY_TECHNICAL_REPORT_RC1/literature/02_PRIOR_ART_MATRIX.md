## Matrix decision

No row supports a new mathematical principle. The strongest potentially
distinct item is the combined workflow: preregister admissible transformations,
evaluate a certificate ladder on positive and matched negative pairs, report
`R` and `D` separately, and retain explicit collisions and destroyed
distinctions. The search found the components and the Pareto idea, but not this
exact audit bundle as a standard named protocol. That is `POSSIBLY` distinct as
engineering methodology, not established as scientifically superior.
