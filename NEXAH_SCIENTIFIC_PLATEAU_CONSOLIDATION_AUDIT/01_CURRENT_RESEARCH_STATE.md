# Current Research State

## Evidence-backed plateau

NEXAH has reached a consolidation plateau. Its strongest reproducible content is a set of explicit transformations, provenance records, collision-aware representation checks, and adversarial gates. No established novel theorem or materially distinct algorithm survives the strongest comparisons.

| Line | Current state | Evidence level | Strongest valid claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Translation Fidelity | Internally complete; external replication remains justified | completed synthetic studies plus literature audit | Coarse certificates were often more robust and less discriminative in the frozen batteries; the broad trade-off is prior art |
| Translation Recovery T01 | Completed bounded software test | deterministic synthetic result | Registered invertibility/loss statuses and certificate collisions were correctly detected; no new recovery mathematics |
| Legacy field→graph | Partial executable chain | code/provenance audit | field→derived candidates→nearest-seed partition→adjacency graph exists; source-state and downstream spectrum links do not |
| T02 | Frozen | design audits only | A nontrivial finite-sample decision design may be possible, but task, population, power, and distinctness gates are unsatisfied |
| ORION | External review only | design/metrology/application audits | Standard optical-inertial orientation under dropout is measurable; `NEXAH_METHOD=UNDEFINED` |
| Dynamical/stability work | Historical and bounded | mixed code, benchmark, and reconciled claims | Some simulations, geometry, Kuramoto dynamics, and numerical analyses are real; general stability, prediction, and control claims are not supported |
| Navigation | Orientation/structural traversal only | architectural and bounded benchmark evidence | Representations can orient analysis; operational steering or safe control is unvalidated |
| Symbolic layer | Design/metaphor | no technical evidence | May aid communication if explicitly separated from evidence |

## Current restrictions

T02 remains `FROZEN`. ORION remains `EXTERNAL_REVIEW`; it becomes a standard demonstrator if no independent task owner supplies a signed task contract. Translation Fidelity must not be extended internally merely to reproduce a known trade-off. Canonical NEXAH remains unchanged by this audit.
