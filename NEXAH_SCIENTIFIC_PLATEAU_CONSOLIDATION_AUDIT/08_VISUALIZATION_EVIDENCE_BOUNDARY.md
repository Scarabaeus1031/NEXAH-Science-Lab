# Visualization Evidence Boundary

| Category | Definition | Evidence role | Required label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific plot | deterministic rendering of registered data/equations | displays evidence; not independent evidence | source, code, parameters, units, uncertainty |
| Structural diagram | software/mathematical architecture | documents asserted links | distinguish implemented, partial, conceptual, absent arrows |
| Explanatory illustration | communicates a model | explanatory only | `ILLUSTRATION` |
| Speculative visualization | proposes a pattern or hypothesis | hypothesis-generating only | `SPECULATIVE / NOT VALIDATED` |
| Symbolic/artistic plate | aesthetic or metaphorical composition | no technical role | `SYMBOLIC / NOT TECHNICAL EVIDENCE` |

Visual structure, symmetry, apparent alignment, and visual smoothness cannot establish invariance, stability, causality, correspondence, or physical meaning. A scientific plot may be a target node in a Representation Ledger when its rendering operator and source are reproducible. The evidence remains the registered data, equations, and analysis, not the visual impression.
