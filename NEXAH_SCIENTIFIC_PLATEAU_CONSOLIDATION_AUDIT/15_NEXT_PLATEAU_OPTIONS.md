### 1. Representation Ledger over existing evidence

Formalize a minimal, machine-checkable schema and populate it only for already supported transformations: Translation Fidelity/T01 and the verified legacy field→candidate→partition→graph edges. This has the highest defensibility, uses existing artifacts, exposes missing correspondences, and makes no external assumption.

### 2. Canonical-pipeline reproducibility assessment

Without rerunning an experiment or changing canonical NEXAH, specify the deterministic inputs, operator versions, correspondence indices, semantic labels, expected outputs, and failure conditions needed to reproduce the strongest legacy partial chain. This may falsify the belief that the chain is sufficiently complete for canonical status.

### 3. External-anchor outreach, then pause

Send one method-neutral task-contract packet to a genuinely independent owner. T02 needs task, costs, effect margin, and process population; ORION needs pointing tolerance, latency, reliability, outage population, and decision consequences. This is outreach, not experiment authorization. If no owner accepts the premise, stop the associated line.

## Priority rationale

The order follows scientific defensibility, independence from unsupported assumptions, reproducibility, falsifiability, reuse of existing code/data, external dependency, and expected information gain. Documentation-only consolidation is the fallback if the ledger cannot be made executable. No new experiment is the immediate next action.
