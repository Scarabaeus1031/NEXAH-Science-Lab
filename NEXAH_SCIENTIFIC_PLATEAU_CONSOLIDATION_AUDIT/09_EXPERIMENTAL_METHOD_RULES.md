# Experimental Method Rules

## Permanent sequence

`claim → strongest comparator → information parity → external task → preregistration → experiment`

1. State a falsifiable NEXAH-specific claim and its null.
2. Define the strongest conventional comparator, including modern pooling, calibration, abstention, and remeasurement where relevant.
3. Enforce equal observations, labels, compute, tuning, and decision opportunities.
4. Obtain a task, population, costs, tolerance, and meaningful effect from an owner independent of NEXAH.
5. Freeze estimands, power, multiplicity, missingness, stopping, and negative dispositions.
6. Execute only after all gates pass.

Every experiment must be allowed to eliminate the NEXAH-specific interpretation. A result that only distinguishes NEXAH from a weak baseline is not evidence of distinctness. Internally selected synthetic populations cannot replace the external task anchor when task utility is claimed.

T02 demonstrates why circular target construction, incomplete baselines, and decision freedom are fatal. ORION demonstrates why a plausible bench and measurable state remain insufficient without a defined method and signed external tolerance/cost contract.
