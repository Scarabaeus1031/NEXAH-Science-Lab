# Scope and Evidence Rules

## Mandate

This is a consolidation audit of completed NEXAH work. It executes no experiment, constructs no benchmark, changes no canonical NEXAH file, and treats ORION as a separate application candidate rather than evidence for NEXAH.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Frozen results, replay records, code-linked provenance, and completed independent audits.
2. Well-defined mathematics and primary prior art.
3. Preregistered but unexecuted designs, usable only as design evidence.
4. Architectural proposals, diagrams, and terminology, usable only as proposals.
5. Symbolic or aesthetic material, never technical evidence.

`SUPPORTED_INTERNAL_RESULT` requires a named completed result and its boundary. A design cannot support an empirical claim. A visualization cannot promote a hypothesis. Absence of a prior-art match cannot establish novelty.

## Fail-closed rules

- New notation is not new mathematics.
- A new combination is not a new theorem or algorithm.
- Robustness is not fidelity, completeness, truth, or decision value.
- Navigation means orientation or traversal unless controlled intervention is validated.
- Historical proxy labels do not inherit the meaning of Morse, Lyapunov, Koopman, or transition theory.
- Every future test must permit elimination of the NEXAH-specific interpretation.
