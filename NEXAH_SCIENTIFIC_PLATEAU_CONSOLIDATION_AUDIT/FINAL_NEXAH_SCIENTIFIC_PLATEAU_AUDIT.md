# Final NEXAH Scientific Plateau Audit

## Final disposition

`NEXAH_CENTER_IS_HYBRID_ARCHITECTURE`

After removing NEXAH terminology, symbolic language, visual aesthetics, historical naming, speculative physical interpretation, and unsupported novelty claims, the reproducible remainder is primarily a network of explicit transformations between mathematical, computational, geometric, graphical, visual, measurement, and decision representations. Provenance, information-loss accounting, uncertainty boundaries, negative-result retention, and adversarial experiment gates give that network its scientific discipline.

This is not a novel mathematical theory or a materially distinct algorithm. The relevant mathematics has high prior-art overlap. Translation Fidelity internally reproduced a known robustness–discrimination relationship with implementation qualification; its exact hierarchy failed independent replication. The legacy field→critical-candidate→partition→adjacency chain is real but partial and semantically narrower than its historical labels. T02 remains frozen because its external task, population, power, and method-distinctness gates are unresolved. ORION remains external review: the standard optical-inertial task is plausible, but `NEXAH_METHOD=UNDEFINED` and no independent task contract exists.

## What survives inspection

- A defensible experimental discipline requiring strongest comparators, information parity, external task ownership, preregistration, and permission for NEXAH to lose.
