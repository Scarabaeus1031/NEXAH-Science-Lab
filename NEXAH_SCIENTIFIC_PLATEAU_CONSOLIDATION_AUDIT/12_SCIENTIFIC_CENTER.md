# Scientific Center

## Decision

`NEXAH_CENTER_IS_HYBRID_ARCHITECTURE`

The present center is an explicitly bounded hybrid of:

1. **Representation-translation architecture:** named operators and correspondences among mathematical, computational, geometric, graphical, visual, measurement, and decision representations.
2. **Scientific knowledge architecture:** provenance, authority, uncertainty, information loss, negative-result retention, and current/historical claim status.
3. **Experimental methodology:** strongest comparator, information parity, external-task anchoring, preregistration, and genuine falsifiability.

The first component is primary. The other two make it scientifically usable and prevent representational convenience from becoming a truth claim.

This is not option A or B: novelty and algorithmic distinctness are not established. It is not primarily a dynamical-systems toolkit: the strongest legacy chain is partial and several important terms were proxies. It is not primarily visualization: visualizations have no independent evidentiary authority. It is coherent enough to reject “no center,” but requires explicit reclassification away from any implied unified new science.

The center is supported as architecture, not yet validated for incremental scientific utility.
