# Stop / Keep Rules

## Stop

- Stop searching for novelty after observing a result.
- Stop treating visual symmetry, smoothness, resonance, or alignment as evidence.
- Stop introducing special constants without a physical derivation and uncertainty model.
- Stop building benchmarks around NEXAH vocabulary or expected failure modes.
- Stop comparing against weak baselines when a stronger direct estimator exists.
- Stop using symbolic labels in technical claims.
- Stop presenting adjacent artifacts as a connected pipeline without an operator and provenance edge.
- Stop expanding frameworks before interfaces are implemented and tested.
- Stop internal experiments without an independently defined task, population, costs, and effect margin.
- Stop converting robustness into fidelity, truth, completeness, prediction, control, or utility.
- Stop replacing negative results with renamed hypotheses.

## Keep

- Reproducible, versioned transformation pipelines.
- Explicit source/operator/target correspondence and loss records.
- Hash-bound provenance and replay.
- Strong-comparator and information-parity audits.
- Fail-closed uncertainty, abstention, and non-identifiability statuses.
- Collision and counterfactual-discrimination checks alongside robustness.
- Negative/inconclusive-result preservation and explicit reopening conditions.
- Clear separation of current, historical, proposed, and symbolic content.
- Visual explanation when linked to data/code and labeled by evidentiary role.
- Adversarial preregistration gates that can terminate a NEXAH interpretation.
