# Final Recommendation

## Decision

`APPROVED AS LAB CANDIDATE`

## Basis

The repository contains enough evidence to define:

- one primary research question;
- one explicit null and one explicit test hypothesis;
- typed observed, estimated, derived, and unknown quantities;
- a bounded input/output contract;
- observable error and reacquisition measures;
- simple comparison conditions;
- falsification and blocked-result criteria;
- strict scientific and authority boundaries.

The proposal remains independent of Q°, Pattern of Orientation, and its visuals. Those materials explain provenance but do not carry the test.

## Qualification

Approval as a candidate does not authorize a Lab, assign an identity, approve the hypothesis, freeze a protocol, select a benchmark, create code, or permit publication. Exact model parameters, estimator, information-matching rule, thresholds, environment, repository, and owner remain absent.

## Main dependencies

- one frozen synthetic state/observation model;
- one fixed history-dependent weighted estimator;
- matched static and constant-velocity masks;
- preregistered metrics, thresholds, and paired seeds;
- source/record/unknown classification;
- simple baselines;
- named Research/Validation owner and independent review.

## Critical risks

- motion confounded with information amount;
- ground-truth leakage;
- result-driven estimator, weight, or threshold changes;
- multiple-hypothesis and successor-Lab expansion;
- symbolic Q° language treated as evidence;
- generalization from one synthetic case.

## Next permitted action

Owner review of this feasibility package. If accepted, Thomas may separately authorize one protocol-design mission naming the Research/Validation owner, repository, benchmark-selection rule, and review gate.

No implementation action is permitted.

Operational effect: `NONE`
