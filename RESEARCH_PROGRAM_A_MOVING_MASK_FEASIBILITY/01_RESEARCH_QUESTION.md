# Research Question

## Primary research question

> For one frozen finite-dimensional synthetic state model and one fixed history-dependent weighted estimator, does a constant-width observation mask moving at constant velocity change estimation error and reacquisition behavior relative to an information-matched static mask?

This is the one Lab question.

## Reduction from the handoff

The handoff contains several questions:

- width effect;
- velocity effect;
- weighting effect;
- smooth but unsupported output;
- reacquisition;
- existence, uniqueness, sensitivity, and observability.

They do not form one minimal first Lab without reduction. The primary question retains the handoff's H2 temporal-mask claim and H5 recovery measure. Error and reacquisition are response quantities for the same comparison, not separate research programmes.

## Comparison contract

The moving and static conditions must match on:

- state trajectory;
- observation operators;
- mask width;
- observation horizon;
- sampling frequency;
- estimator and history policy;
- weights;
- noise law and paired seeds;
- total or predeclared information-loss matching rule.

Without the last item, mask motion is confounded with the amount of information removed.

## Excluded questions

- whether Q° is a universal operator;
- whether the estimator is true or optimal;
- whether mask width alone increases error;
- whether adaptive weighting improves performance;
- whether a moving mask changes a physical source;
- whether the result transfers to nonlinear, biological, chemical, or real-world systems;
- whether the estimator should control action;
- whether observer change, Pattern, Binder, Whip, or successor Labs are valid.
