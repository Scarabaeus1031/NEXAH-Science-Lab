## Purpose

This register makes the recoverable Rödelheim extended-field material
discoverable inside the active Science Lab. It does not alter the Moving Mask
research question, create an operator or convert historical language into
evidence.

Shared orientation question:

> What relations remain invariant when motion, masking, projection or trace
> transforms a state into another representation?

This question is an organizing question. Each experiment must declare its own
objects, transformations, observables and falsification conditions.

## Smallest defensible extraction

### Existing operations, not new canonical operators

1. `project`: map a declared source into a receiving representation;
2. `mask`: withhold a declared part of a record;
3. `retain`: preserve a time-indexed or accumulated record;
4. `relate`: compare records using one declared reference;
5. `register`: align representations before comparison;
6. `segment`: divide a trace using a declared rule;
7. `transition`: mark a declared change in state or direction.

These names describe actions already present in the sources. They do not amend
OLS or establish NEXAH operators.
