# Future Research Interface — Finite Algebra to Power-Grid Models

## Verified algebraic object

The verified object is the finite modular conjugacy

`(Z_3885, T_1301) ≅ (Z_777 × Z_5, T_524 × id_Z5)`

and its consequence that every `T_524` cycle has five period-preserving CRT
fiber lifts.

## Requirements for any separate IEEE experiment

A future experiment would need an independently preregistered bridge contract
defining, at minimum:

- the objects in IEEE-9 and IEEE-14 corresponding to modular states, cycles,
  periods, residues, and operators;
- measurable observables and a scale-transfer rule fixed before outcomes are
  inspected;
- a strongest relevant comparator or null model that can reproduce apparent
  scaling without the proposed bridge;
- exact success criteria, multiplicity correction, and out-of-sample data;
- a falsification condition, such as failure of the preregistered mapping or
  no predictive improvement over the comparator.

No such bridge is defined or demonstrated by this package. The finite algebra
does not establish power-grid behavior, physical-network isomorphism, control,
navigation, domain transfer, or universality.
