# Gate 2 — Method-Independent Process Family

## Proposed substrate under attack

The prior audit proposed hidden finite-state Markov processes observed through a
sequence of many-to-one aggregation/observation maps. That family is compatible
with the question, but compatibility is not independent justification.

An auditable family specification would have to fix, without method trials:

- distribution of hidden-state counts and transition structures;
- stationary/nonstationary assumptions and mixing-time range;
- observation operators, noise and missingness;
- aggregation-map source and compression levels;
- prevalence of rare states/transitions;
- task-relevant and irrelevant distinctions generated by an external task law;
- trajectory counts, lengths and dependence structure;
- train/calibration/test population and any shift;
- correspondence availability and label permutation rules;
- held-out counterfactual mechanism;
- scorer truth and exclusions for non-identifiable cases.

## Strongest benchmark-engineering criticism

The current prose can generate nearly any desired difficulty. Collisions, rare
strata, unknown correspondence and multi-stage aggregation are also precisely the
phenomena named by the candidate NEXAH workflow. Choosing their prevalence after
thinking about NEXAH could create an artificial advantage. Conversely, smooth
well-sampled task risk could make B8 trivially dominant. The unspecified family
therefore contains enough degrees of freedom to manufacture any sign of
`Delta(n,b)`.

## Neutral alternatives

Prefer an established, independently published source of problems over a custom
NEXAH generator. Candidate families, subject to a separate selection audit, are:

- standard finite-state HMM/POMDP simulation suites with aggregation chosen by
  published state-abstraction rules;
- published Markov lumpability/aggregation models with task outcomes added by a
  prespecified external decision rule;
- established selective-prediction or state-abstraction benchmarks where
  remeasurement/abstention already has operational meaning.

No family is selected here. A standard family is not automatically adequate: it
must expose multiple representations under equal observations and have scorer-
identifiable task risk. Custom additions should be minimal and justified by the
external task contract, not by certificate vocabulary.

## Verdict

`GATE_2_CONDITIONAL`

The substrate is scientifically recognizable, but no independent family or
parameter distribution is fixed. The benchmark-engineering objection is not yet
neutralized.
