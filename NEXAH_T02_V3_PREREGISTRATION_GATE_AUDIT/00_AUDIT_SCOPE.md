# Audit Scope

## Purpose

This is an adversarial design audit of the three gates that must precede any
T02-v3 preregistration. It asks whether a finite-information comparison between
a candidate NEXAH estimator and the strongest fair direct estimator remains a
scientifically identifiable question.

This audit does not implement, tune, simulate, freeze or preregister anything.
It creates no data, benchmark cases, results, figures, executable files or hash
manifest. Original T02, T02-v2, the previous information-limited design audit,
canonical NEXAH and ORION are out of scope and immutable.

## Established boundary

The exact public first-loss question is already rejected as an incremental-value
test. Under equal complete information, a task-aware plug-in comparator computes
the same target. The only candidate question retained here is:

> Under identical finite observations, task information and resource limits,
> does a prespecified structured estimator change externally scored decisions
> relative to the strongest conventional direct task-risk estimator?

This is a method-comparison question inside established statistics and decision
theory. It is not evidence for new mathematics, extra information, universal
NEXAH superiority, physical validity or ORION.

## Burden of proof

Each gate must receive positive, NEXAH-independent support. `UNCLEAR` is not a
pass. A benchmark choice is contaminated when it would not be selected without
NEXAH, or when an otherwise reasonable alternative could reverse the ranking and
no external authority fixes the choice.

The current audit may identify a path to satisfying a gate, but a possible path
is classified `CONDITIONAL`, not `PASS`.
