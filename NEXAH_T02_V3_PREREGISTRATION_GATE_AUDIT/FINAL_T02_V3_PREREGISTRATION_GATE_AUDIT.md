# Final T02-v3 Preregistration Gate Audit

## Decision

T02-v3 is not ready for preregistration. All three gates remain conditional:

- Gate 1 has a method-neutral decision form, but no independent owner has fixed
  the task threshold, costs or minimum meaningful effect.
- Gate 2 uses a recognizable Markov/aggregation substrate, but no externally
  motivated family or parameter distribution prevents benchmark engineering.
- Gate 3 has an identifiable risk contrast in principle, but no quantitative
  power basis exists and method separation is unclear after strengthening B8.

This is not `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`: a completed external contract and population could
make `Delta(n,b)` estimable. It is not yet `REDUCES_TO_ESTABLISHED_METHODS` either:
reduction is a serious live possibility, but the candidate NEXAH mapping is too
underspecified to prove equivalence. Conditional status preserves neither a NEXAH
advantage nor authorization to proceed.

## Strongest adversarial findings

1. Cost ratios can legitimately reverse optimal decisions; without an owner-fixed
   primary vector, they can also reverse the reported method ranking.
2. Collision prevalence, rare strata, correspondence uncertainty, noise, mixing
   and sample size can manufacture either sign of `Delta` unless inherited from
   an independent population.
3. A strong B8 may use hierarchical pooling, state-space models, calibration,
   selective prediction and remeasurement. Those capabilities subsume most of
   the current verbal description of the NEXAH candidate.
4. All null, NEXAH-loss and B8-loss regimes are plausible, but their natural
   support and statistical precision are not established.
5. Equal-information consistent methods should converge to the same optimal
   decision. Any persistent unexplained NEXAH advantage would signal asymmetry,
   misspecification or unequal resources.

## Gate-resolution sequence

The only permitted next action is a single independent specification package:
an external task owner must fix the task, loss and effect margin and nominate an
established process population without reference to either method. Only after
that may a separate design-only review assess NEXAH/B8 operational distinctness
and calculate power. Failure to obtain that package should stop the T02 line; it
must not be replaced by internally tuned synthetic choices.

No preregistration, implementation, experiment, ORION inference or canonical
NEXAH change is authorized by this audit.
