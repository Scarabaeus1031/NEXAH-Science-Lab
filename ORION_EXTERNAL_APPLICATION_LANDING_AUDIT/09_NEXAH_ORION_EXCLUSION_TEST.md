## Reverse-causality warning

it contains optical and inertial sensors is inherently post hoc. The defense is
not to claim a landing now: an independent domain owner must reject, modify or
adopt the blank contract without seeing ORION/NEXAH method performance.

The device name must be absent from task requirements. If a requirement refers to
certificates, translation fidelity or ORION-specific dropout, it is internally
contaminated and cannot close a gate.
