# Scope and Boundaries

Status: `FROZEN / NOT EXECUTED`  
Authority: isolated Science-Lab preregistration; `NOT_ADOPTED`

declared distinction disappears in

```text
field Z -> numerical derivatives D -> critical candidates C
        -> nearest-seed partition P -> region-adjacency graph G
```

It is not a stability, Morse–Smale, Koopman, Lyapunov, physical, predictive,
early-warning, risk or control experiment. Historical PNGs are provenance only,
never observations. Canonical NEXAH is read-only and unchanged.

The legacy audit supports only a partial historical chain and recommends a new,
deterministic, correspondence-preserving test
(`../NEXAH_LEGACY_REPRESENTATION_PIPELINE_AUDIT/11_T02_CANDIDATE_PIPELINE.md`).

is known in substance; no new mathematical principle is under test
(`../NEXAH_TRANSLATION_FIDELITY_RECOVERY_LITERATURE_GAP_AUDIT/FINAL_LITERATURE_GAP_AUDIT.md`).

No result-dependent change is permitted. Failed fixture, detection, adjacency or
correspondence preconditions produce `PRECONDITION_FAILED`; they do not authorize
replacement, threshold tuning or a new counterfactual.
