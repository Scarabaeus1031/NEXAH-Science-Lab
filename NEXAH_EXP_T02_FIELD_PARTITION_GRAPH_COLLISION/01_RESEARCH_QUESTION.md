# Research Question

For each preregistered pair `(Z,Z')`, at which representation stage does the
declared task-relevant distinction cease to be detectable?

The experiment compares `(Z,Z')`, `(D,D')`, `(C,C')`, `(P,P')`, and `(G,G')`.
Visual similarity is never a metric. “Fidelity” means only equality or distance
under the certificate explicitly registered in `07_CERTIFICATE_REGISTRY.md`.

Primary estimand: for every pair and certificate, the binary relation
`PRESERVED`, `CHANGED`, `COLLISION`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, plus the first stage at
which a distinction required upstream becomes invisible downstream.

Secondary methodological estimand: whether that collision/correspondence ledger
changes a conclusion relative to the complete preregistered baseline set. A
zero-increment result is fully valid.

No population inference is planned: there are exactly three designed
counterfactual pairs. R and D are descriptive finite-panel rates only.
