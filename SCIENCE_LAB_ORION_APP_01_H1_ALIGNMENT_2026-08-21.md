## 7. Baselines, metrics, failure criteria and STOP

The fair first comparison is exactly the existing two-condition design:
`BASELINE_SUMMARY.md` versus `READER_MODE.md`, with identical source identity,
question, task form and 15-minute maximum. An ordinary LLM, a cautioned LLM and
an ORION-structured condition are not silently added; the current benchmark
explicitly does not test benefit against live search or an LLM and does not test
ORION integration.

The primary behavioral score is the 0–15 Bounded Orientation Score across
mechanism, wider relation, evidence awareness, uncertainty/boundary awareness
and continuation/STOP quality. The frozen planning acceptance rules additionally
require median improvement of at least 2 points with Cliff's delta at least
0.33, provenance completion of at least 80% and 20 percentage points over the
baseline, unknown recognition of at least 75% and not below baseline, no higher
unsupported-claim rate, no packet-attributable severe authority confusion,
median time no greater than 1.5 times baseline, and all validity gates.

Clarity and cognitive load remain subjective secondary ratings. They cannot
rescue a failed behavioral or safety gate. Valid outcomes remain `PASS`,
`PARTIAL`, `NO_BENEFIT`, `HARM` and `INVALID`; only a valid result produced under
a later authorization could select among them.

Even a measured method advantage is practically non-useful when it is unstable,
depends on post-hoc choices, requires excessive manual annotation or review,
causes unnecessary STOPs, worsens clarity materially, exceeds the time gate, or
has complexity cost greater than its bounded orientation gain. A valid
`NO_BENEFIT` or `HARM` result is sufficient evidence to return the application
to The EYE for `PARK` consideration; this report does not make that later
portfolio decision.
