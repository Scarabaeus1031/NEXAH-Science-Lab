## Frame boundaries fixed today

- In Earth Sky, the Milky Way is the background field, the Pineal Aperture is
  the observer window and Andromeda is a deep-sky object.
- The Pineal Aperture is not Saturn, the Oort Cloud, the Sun, Earth or Moon.
- The Oort Cloud belongs only to the heliocentric frame and is currently a
  schematic outer shell.
- Earth and Titan are not a two-body subsystem. Earth is Sun-bound; Titan is
  Saturn-bound inside a nested planetocentric frame.
- Solar and planetary body sizes, distances and animation rates are schematic.
  They are not ephemerides or scale models.
- Named or measured stellar positions require a declared catalog. The bundled
  template contains demonstration rows, not astronomical measurements.

## Importer correction

Instrument records and observer-state records are rejected as star maps.
Blank coordinates remain absent and are not coerced to the origin. Valid star
maps must provide horizontal and vertical coordinates or right ascension and
declination, plus a Milky Way or Andromeda carrier assignment or an explicit
fallback selection.
