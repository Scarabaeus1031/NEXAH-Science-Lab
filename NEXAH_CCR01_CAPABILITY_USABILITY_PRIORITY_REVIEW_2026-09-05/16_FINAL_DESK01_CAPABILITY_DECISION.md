# Final Desk 01 Capability Decision

## Primary decision

**B — STRONG_RESEARCH_AND_ORIENTATION_ENVIRONMENT_INTEGRATED_PRODUCT_INCOMPLETE**

NEXAH has more than concepts and visuals. It has published semantics, governed workflows, installable and tested components, certified structural processing, contract validation, sealed comparison demonstrations, public editorial navigation and a public bounded Human Workspace. These establish a real ecosystem and working research environment.

The integrated product remains incomplete. The public experience does not expose the strongest machine comparison chain; the certified ORION Core is narrower than historical Runtime/Gateway ambitions; NRRC is local candidate staging; durable user records and a common intake boundary are absent; and capability selection still depends heavily on the Owner–AI dialogue.

## Direct closeout answers

### Is NEXAH a thought model, framework, research environment, software system or product?

It is a Human orientation practice, a strong framework, a working research environment and a multi-component software system. It is also an early bounded product through its public surfaces. It is not yet one complete integrated product.

### Which capabilities actually work today?

Public Library/Living Atlas navigation, NEXAHEDRON's browser-local Human Workspace, curated reference cases, OLS 1.0 publication, the NEXAH Kernel and source adapters, Network Orientation, the IEEE benchmark path, the certified ORION structural chain, THE EYE's sealed internal comparison/projection chain, the local NRRC validator, provenance/manifests and Science Lab governance all have bounded operational evidence.

### Which are usable without the Owner and current chat history?

The public Library/Atlas, NEXAHEDRON Workspace and curated cases are directly accessible. Technical users can independently use OLS, Kernel/CLI and ORION repositories with their documentation. THE EYE, NRRC staging, most Science Lab tools, Builder Lab and advanced visual instruments still require expert local context or are not publicly exposed.

### Where should the HTML and visual corpus live?

Working instruments and bounded negative experiments belong in Observatory or Science Lab. Stable explanatory relationships belong in Atlas. Experiential public candidates belong in Arena. Screenshots, duplicates, superseded variants and unresolved analogies belong in Archive. Source masters and exports remain related but distinct.

### What should remain active?

The public NEXAH and NEXAHEDRON entrances, OLS, maintained Kernel applications, certified ORION Core, provenance/manifest checks, bounded Science Lab governance, sealed THE EYE work and archive/genealogy management.

### What should route to Archive, Arena, Atlas or Observatory?

Builder bulk, screenshots, duplicate HTML and the BTC orphan result route to Archive. Selected visual essays and operator plates may enter Arena. Curated genealogies and bounded reference maps belong in Atlas. HTML instruments, Rödelheim labs, GLB builders and candidate contract/comparison work belong in Observatory until stronger gates close.

### What is the strongest distinctive capability?

The evidence-bound comparison discipline combining ILAU, componentwise residuals, ReturnTest, provenance, claim ceilings and Human Authority.

### What is the largest usability gap?

The absence of a discoverable capability router and public comparison entry that explains required inputs, output status, limitations, residuals and next Human action.

### What is the single most valuable missing connection?

A bounded route from a declared user need to the correct existing capability and, for comparison tasks, from confirmed input to the sealed ILAU/residual result projection—without transferring record ownership or claim authority to NEXAHEDRON or THE EYE.

### What should Desk 01 prioritize next, and what should it stop?

Prioritize one current capability index with real entry points, maturity, user/input/output, claim ceiling and next action; then run a separately authorized external-user validation of the public Workspace and one comparison journey. Stop producing new general architecture maps, duplicate visual variants and unregistered tools until current routing and usability evidence are coherent.

## Compressed statement

NEXAH is a governed orientation ecosystem with public editorial spaces, a bounded Human Workspace, tested structural and comparison components, and a disciplined research archive. Its strongest value is preserving loss, additions, uncertainty, residuals, provenance and Human claim authority across representations. It is usable today in several narrow forms, but not yet one integrated externally validated product. The next need is truthful capability routing, not another architecture layer.

This review maps the machine that exists. It does not enlarge it.
