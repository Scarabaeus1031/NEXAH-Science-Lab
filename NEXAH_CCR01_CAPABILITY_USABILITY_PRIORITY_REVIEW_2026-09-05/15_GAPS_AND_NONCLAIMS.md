# Gaps and Nonclaims

## Largest usability gap

The largest gap is discoverable capability routing. The ecosystem contains public editorial surfaces, a public Human Workspace and several tested components, but a new user cannot reliably answer:

```text
Which tool fits my input?
What must I provide?
What will it return?
What does its status mean?
What remains unresolved?
What may I do next?
```

The most valuable missing connection is one bounded, non-autonomous route from a declared user need and input type to an existing capability record and its actual entry point, followed by a Human-readable result/limitation page. It must route; it must not infer truth or claim to be a general AI router.

- no public general ILAU comparator;
- no durable public Orientation Record, account or history;

- no current integrated ORION target-representation generator;
- no evidence of external user validation sufficient for M7;
- no general capability registry exposed across public and technical surfaces;
- inconsistent dependencies and entry points in Builder Lab;
- only an archived BTC output, not a recoverable dedicated BTC tool entry;
- many HTML instruments lack publication footers, self-contained dependencies or current navigation;
- strong internal test evidence is fragmented across historical revisions and scopes;
- NEXAHEDRON's current THE EYE working tree has uncommitted changes, so source presence and deployed behavior must remain distinct.

## Mandatory nonclaims

This review does not claim:

- a new mathematical theory or ontology;
- that known graph, geometry, dynamical-systems or contract techniques are NEXAH inventions;
- that visual recurrence proves physical, biological, cosmological or causal identity;
- that color, glyphs, 5+2, musical harmony, 432/440/444 Hz, golden-angle references or OEIS matches establish ontology;
- that attractive HTML is an instrument above M3;
- that every historical HTML belongs in active navigation;
- that the Builder Lab is one coherent product;
- that a schema test is product validation;
- that NRRC is production-ready;
- that THE EYE is complete or publicly available;
- that ORION's target role is an implemented general processor;
- that a residual is automatically relevant;
- that NEXAHEDRON public page availability proves full ORION operation;
- that Owner/Codex use is external validation;
- that the public Library is a scientific evidence engine;
- that the historical BTC result is an integrated NEXAHEDRON market capability;
- that FIELD ART is scientific evidence;
- that NEXAH is a general AI router;
- market value, improved decisions or superior user outcomes without external evidence.

## Freeze reminder

No recommendation in this package authorizes integration, source changes, deployment, publication, new tests or research. Recommendations identify the smallest next product/documentation decisions only.
