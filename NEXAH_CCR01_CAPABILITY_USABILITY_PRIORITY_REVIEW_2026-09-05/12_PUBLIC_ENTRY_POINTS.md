Observed on 2026-09-05. Reachability checks were non-mutating and do not constitute a hosted usability study.

## Publicly usable feature count

The review counts three M6 capability families:

1. NEXAH public Library/Living Atlas navigation;
2. NEXAHEDRON bounded Human Workspace;
3. NEXAHEDRON curated reference cases.

OLS and public repositories are publicly accessible documentation/source, but their primary maturity is M1 or M4 rather than M6. THE EYE is not counted because its public route is absent. No feature is M7 because no external evaluation evidence was found.
