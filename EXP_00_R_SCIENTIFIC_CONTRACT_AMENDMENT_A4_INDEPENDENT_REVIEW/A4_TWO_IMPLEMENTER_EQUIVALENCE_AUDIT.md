# A4 Two-Implementer Equivalence Audit

Teams A and B can independently reproduce the same N1–N5 populations, RNG draws, bins, null statistics, P1–P3, amplitude P5, encoded classification, and Lorenz ceiling.

They can still diverge scientifically:

1. **P4:** one team can enforce frozen V1's “every preregistered carrier/leave-one” clause using the single-representation attribution diagnostics; another can follow A4's report-only declaration. This can change P4 and the Rössler label.
2. **Validity:** the teams can implement different concrete parity/distinctness/provenance predicates behind the same machine gate token.
3. **Sensitivity completeness:** they can disagree about what outputs make a non-P5 sensitivity complete.
4. **Seed-dominance identity:** they can compare exact rational objects or a rational aggregate with a separately rounded float at zero tolerance.

At least one remaining choice can change validity, P4, and classification. Therefore `Review(A4) ≠ A4` and the two-implementer test fails.
