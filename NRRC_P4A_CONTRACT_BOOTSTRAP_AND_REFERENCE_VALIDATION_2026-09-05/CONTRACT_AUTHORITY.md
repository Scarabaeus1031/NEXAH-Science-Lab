# Contract Authority

The authorized mandate covers NRRC namespace governance, schema bytes and immutable releases, versions and compatibility, conformance, deprecation/migration, canonical serialization, content hashes, optional signature policy, extension/profile registration and acknowledgement records.

It excludes OLS redefinition, ILAU modification, ORION execution, THE EYE presentation control, producer-observation ownership, Human decisions and scientific claims.

The 14 contract types are frozen for P4A:

1. `RelationRecordEnvelope`
2. `CarrierStateRef`
3. `FrameRef`
4. `ApertureRef`
5. `RepresentationRef`
6. `TraceRecord`
7. `TraceLocalDescriptor`
8. `TraceSideStatus`
9. `ILAUAuditRecord`
10. `ResidualRecord`
11. `ReturnTestRecord`
12. `ProvenanceStage`
13. `ClaimCeilingRef`
14. `HumanAuthorityBoundary`
