# Privacy and data classification

P4A fixtures are synthetic and contain no personal, medical, financial or biological data. Production ingestion is unauthorized.

A future producer must declare an approved classification, purpose, minimization, retention/deletion, access, disclosure and redaction policy. Hashes do not anonymize content. Redacted derivatives require new identity and hash plus explicit omitted paths.

```text
P4A_DATA_CLASSIFICATION = SYNTHETIC_NONPERSONAL_TEST_DATA
REAL_PERSONAL_DATA_ALLOWED = NO
MEDICAL_DATA_ALLOWED = NO
FINANCIAL_DATA_ALLOWED = NO
PRODUCTION_RETENTION_POLICY = UNRESOLVED
```
