## Freeze semantics

- Exact filename match, normalized-Unicode match, and variant-without-(1) are distinguished.
- The RTFD directory SHA-256 is a tree digest over ordered relative paths and member SHA-256 values; it is not a byte hash of a nonexistent ZIP.
- Missing exact variants are not called absent globally.
- UNRESOLVED_NOT_ABSENT applies where whole-disk traversal or exact historical package identity is not proven.
- Source titles have no scientific authority by themselves.
