# Required Validation

## Priority order

This order follows Program C. It does not authorize execution.

### 1 — Local dynamical diagnostic comparison

Application: AP-11.

Required gate:

1. freeze one directional-coherence statistic and semantics;
2. align implementation and definition;
3. retain the degenerate surrogate result as failed evidence;
4. compare with direction change, curvature, density, rotation, and shuffled-order nulls;
5. use held-out labels and predeclared metrics;
6. report redundancy or failure without promotion.

### 2 — IEEE reproducibility and baseline boundary

Application: AP-01.

Required gate:

- preserve official G4 failure;
- expose G3 equivalence and G4 diagnostic evidence together;
- do not invent a tolerance;
- obtain bounded external replay review;
- compare against conventional indicators only under an approved protocol.

### 3 — Repository orientation outcome

Application: AP-06.

Required gate:

- freeze reader tasks and expected evidence boundaries;
- test claim-to-evidence traversal and reading-path answers;
- record errors, unsupported confidence, time, and comprehension;
- use independent readers;
- keep Library, evidence, and implementation authorities separate.

### 4 — Source-bounded cartography outcome

Application: AP-07.

Required gate:

- use the three frozen pilot artifacts;
- predeclare reader tasks and outcomes;
- compare Reader/Baseline/Explain forms without revising them during the test;
- record analyst and source limitations;
- do not infer scientific correctness from reader results.

### 5 — Moving-mask benchmark

Application: AP-08.

Required gate:

- freeze one synthetic model, observation family, estimator, weights, translations, mask schedules, paired seeds, and information-matching rule;
- score error, information state, false confidence, and reacquisition under a predeclared decision rule;
- compare no-mask, static-mask, and moving-mask conditions;
- stop after one terminal result.
