# Blocked Applications

## AP-12 — Governed scientific-record interchange

### Blocker

OLS publication integrity is established, but interoperability is not. The repository lacks:

- normative machine syntax or schema;
- parser, serializer, and validator;
- executable conformance cases;
- governed mapping to an existing provenance or research-object representation;
- two independent implementations performing a round trip.

### Unblock condition

A separately authorized minimal exchange must preserve selected claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and reference-space records across two implementations. Documentation examples are insufficient.

### Current disposition

Keep blocked. Do not call OLS an interoperability standard.

## AP-13 — Operational power-system decision support

### Blocker

The maintained IEEE line is an ordered benchmark computation. It has no observed operational trajectory, independently observed outcome, classical stability/security baseline comparison, calibrated warning criterion, control guarantee, or safety case.

### Unblock condition

Requires all of:

- externally governed data and license;
- observed measurements and outcomes;
- domain-standard comparisons;
- independent power-systems review;
- uncertainty, failure, and decision boundaries;
- separate control and safety authorization for any intervention claim.

### Current disposition

Keep blocked. Preserve historical experiments as research history, not current capability.

## Excluded rather than blocked

Historical scenarios, books, visuals, legacy navigation prototypes, personal-orientation concept records, and generalized recovery/control narratives are not promoted into this blocked list. They are `NOT AN APPLICATION` under the current Programs A–C evidence gate.
