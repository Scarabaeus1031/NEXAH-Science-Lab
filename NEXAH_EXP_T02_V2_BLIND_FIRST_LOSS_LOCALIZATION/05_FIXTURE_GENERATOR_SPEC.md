# Fixture Generator Specification

- Primary held-out cases: 84, stratified 12 each across `T1`–`T6`,`NO_LOSS`.
- Instrumentation controls: 14, two per truth label; fixed and separately marked.
- Source observables per case: one target plus 5 nuisance observables.
- Exact values: signed integers in `[-10^6,10^6]` generated by Python's frozen
  `random.Random` from a 256-bit seed.
- Target distinction: unequal before `k*`, equal from `k*` onward; unequal at all
  stages for `NO_LOSS`.
- Each nuisance receives an independently generated valid monotone loss schedule.
- Stage aliases are random bijective strings; correspondence maps source IDs to
  aliases without values or truth labels.
- Graph payloads are deterministic nuisance channels with their own monotone
  schedules. They do not determine target truth.
- Case IDs are SHA-256-derived opaque identifiers and contain no label.

The generator validates monotonicity, stratification, one-to-one aliases,
finite/integer payloads and absence of truth fields in public cases. Failure is
`PRECONDITION_FAILED` and cannot be excluded or replaced.

The suite seed is created once at authorized execution, durably sealed before
diagnostics, and revealed in final results for replay. Retries use the same sealed
seed; no seed shopping is allowed.
