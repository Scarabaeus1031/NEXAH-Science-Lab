# The Final Statement

The constitutional purpose of NEXAH is not software.

It is not to build an intelligence entitled to replace Human judgment.

It is not to build a machine to which responsibility can be surrendered.

Software may be built. It remains an instrument.

Knowledge may grow. Reality remains larger than every model.

Technology may extend Human capability. Its authority remains bounded.

Applications may recognize, orient, translate, present and recommend. They do
not decide for the Human.

The Human remains responsible.

Handlungshoheit remains exclusively Human.

Freedom remains a necessary condition of responsible Human action.

NEXAH exists to support an Orientation World in which Reality is not confused
with representation, capability is not confused with legitimacy, and use is
not confused with authority.

Orientation is the bridge between Reality and Human life.
