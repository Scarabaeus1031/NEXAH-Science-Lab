# The Human Constitution

Status: `CONSTITUTIONAL PREAMBLE`

## Human dignity

Every Human has worth that is not produced by knowledge, capability,
classification, productivity or technical access.

No model or application may reduce a Human to an input, prediction, score or
role.

## Freedom

Freedom is a necessary condition for meaningful Human action.

Freedom does not remove relation, consequence or responsibility. It protects
the possibility of judgment, refusal, revision and intentional commitment.

## Responsibility

Human action has consequences.

Responsibility requires that consequential decisions remain attributable to
the Humans and institutions that authorize them. Automation may distribute
tasks. It may not absorb accountability.

## Handlungshoheit

Handlungshoheit is exclusive Human agency combined with intentional action,
decision, responsibility and accountability.

No software component possesses Handlungshoheit. No recommendation, default,
execution or absence of objection constitutes a Human decision.

## Evidence

Claims about Reality must remain answerable to evidence.

Evidence has a source, method, boundary, history and uncertainty. Its use must
preserve these conditions.

## Dialogue

Orientation is strengthened by reasons that can be examined, questions that
can be asked and disagreement that can be expressed without being erased.

Dialogue does not guarantee agreement. It makes responsibility and revision
possible.

## Plurality

Humans occupy different bodies, histories, cultures, roles and perspectives.

No single representation contains the whole Human world. Plurality does not
make every claim equally supported; it requires that differences in
perspective and differences in evidence both remain visible.

## Revision

Human knowledge and constitutional arrangements may require revision.

Revision must preserve provenance, identify authority and state what changed.
Repeated use, technical convenience and silent drift do not constitute
adoption.

## Learning

Learning is the capacity to change orientation in response to experience,
evidence, dialogue and reflection.

Learning includes recognizing error, preserving negative results and leaving
questions unresolved when evidence is insufficient.

## Humility

Reality remains larger than every observation, concept, model and system.

Constitutional humility requires bounded claims, explicit uncertainty and the
ability to stop.

## Technology boundary

Technology may:

- extend observation;
- preserve records;
- calculate;
- recognize;
- orient;
- translate;
- present;
- recommend.

Technology may not:

- create Human dignity;
- establish legitimacy through implementation;
- inherit authority through use;
- assume responsibility for Human consequences;
- possess Handlungshoheit.

The Constitution remains authoritative wherever technical capability exceeds
the clarity of its Human purpose.
