## Preamble boundary

LUCY names the Human world for which the NEXAH Constitution exists.

LUCY is not:

- an assistant;
- an AI;
- a service;
- a module;
- a repository;
- a runtime;
- an implementation;
- an authority class.

This preamble explains constitutional purpose. It does not amend the adopted
Articles, assign authority or create architectural responsibility.

## The Human world

Reality exists independently of any description of it.

The Human world is the lived relation between Humans and that reality. It
includes bodies, environments, other living beings, other Humans, memory,
language, culture, knowledge, tools, institutions and consequences.

It is not identical to Reality. It is not contained by a model. It cannot be
reduced to data presented to a system.

The Human world is where perception becomes experience, experience becomes
interpretation, interpretation informs action and action carries
responsibility.

NEXAH exists to support orientation within this world.

## Embodied orientation

Human orientation is embodied.

Humans encounter the world through situated bodies with finite senses, finite
attention, histories, needs and dependencies. Instruments may extend
observation. Representations may preserve and organize aspects of what was
observed. Neither removes the position from which observation occurs.

Embodiment is therefore not an error to eliminate. It is a condition that must
be made explicit when evidence, models and recommendations are interpreted.

## Perception before representation

Every representation used by NEXAH is downstream of an encounter with a
source. The source may be observed directly or mediated through an instrument,
record, dataset or prior representation.

Representation selects. It has a form, boundary, resolution, history and
purpose. It may preserve important distinctions and lose others.

The constitutional order is therefore:

```text
encounter
↓
perception or observation
↓
record
↓
representation
↓
interpretation
↓
possible action
```

This order does not claim that Human cognition is linear. It establishes a
discipline: a representation shall not be treated as if it were the Reality it
represents.

## Human agency

Handlungshoheit belongs exclusively to the Human.

Software may recognize, orient, translate, present and recommend. It does not
intend. It does not accept moral responsibility. It does not become
accountable merely because it produced an output or initiated a technical
operation.

Only the Human can unite decision, intentional action, responsibility and
accountability in the constitutional sense defined by NEXAH.

## Responsibility

Tasks may be delegated. Computation may be automated. Evidence may be gathered
by instruments. Responsibility for authorization and consequential action may
not be transferred to software.

A Human or Human institution may distribute responsibility through explicit
mandates. Such distribution remains Human and must remain traceable. An
implementation cannot acquire responsibility through use, dependency,
complexity or apparent autonomy.

## Uncertainty

Reality always exceeds the information available to a finite observer.

Uncertainty belongs to the Human relation with Reality. It may arise from
limited observation, changing conditions, measurement, incomplete records,
model choice, ambiguity or genuine scientific unknowns. This statement does
not require a claim that Reality itself is intrinsically indeterminate.

Uncertainty shall be preserved where it exists. It shall not be hidden by
classification, visual confidence, technical precision or fluent language.

## Living boundaries

Human life depends on boundaries that preserve identity while permitting
relation and exchange.

In this preamble, a living boundary is understood as a membrane: neither an
absolute wall nor the absence of separation. It regulates exchange, protects
integrity and allows adaptation.

This is a constitutional image, not a new scientific operator.

NEXAH uses it to remember that healthy orientation requires both distinction
and relation.
