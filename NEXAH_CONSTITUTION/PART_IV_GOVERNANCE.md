# Part IV — Governance

Status: `ADOPTED`

## Purpose

Governance preserves the Constitution, records authority assignments and
controls constitutional change.

Governance does not create scientific truth, semantic meaning, structural
validity, recognition results or Human decisions.

## Constitutional custody

Governance shall preserve:

- the adopted Constitution;
- the Authority Table;
- the Authority Map;
- the canonical distinction between Human Authority and Handlungshoheit;
- explicit authority owners and scopes;
- decision provenance;
- freeze status;
- supersession history.

## Governance decision requirements

Every constitutional governance decision shall identify:

- the Human exercising Handlungshoheit;
- the authority affected;
- the current owner;
- the proposed owner, if changed;
- the current and proposed scope;
- exclusions;
- dependencies;
- affected artifact class;
- duplication and leak analysis;
- adoption status;
- effective boundary.

## Prohibited governance implications

Governance approval does not by itself establish:

- semantic correctness;
- structural conformance;
- recognition accuracy;
- translation fidelity;
- domain validity;
- application correctness;
- permission outside the recorded decision.
