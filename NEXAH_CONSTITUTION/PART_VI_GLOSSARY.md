# Part VI — Glossary

Status: `CANONICAL`

## Authority

The bounded constitutional right to govern or issue one declared artifact
class within an explicit scope.

## Authority owner

The identified responsibility or Human mandate holder constitutionally assigned
to an authority.

## Authority scope

The closed boundary within which an authority may issue valid claims or
artifacts.

## Authority leak

An explicit or implicit exercise of authority outside its assigned scope.

## Authority transfer

A change in constitutional ownership. Consumption, copying, translation,
rendering, execution order and technical integration are not authority
transfers.

## Artifact class

The declared category of records or outputs governed by one bounded authority.

## Constitution

The governing laws that define, bound and preserve authorities throughout
NEXAH.

## Constitutional actor

The Human exercising Handlungshoheit. Software is not a constitutional actor.

## Domain Authority

Authority to determine validity within one named domain, method and evidence
boundary.

## Governance Authority

Authority to govern adoption, custody, versioning, freezes, exceptions and
constitutional change procedures.

## Handlungshoheit

The highest NEXAH constitutional principle: exclusive Human agency combined
with intentional action, decision, responsibility and accountability.

Handlungshoheit cannot belong to software and cannot be transferred through
automation or use.

## Human Authority

A bounded mandate held by an identified Human. Human Authority describes
jurisdiction; Handlungshoheit describes the exclusive Human capacity through
which that jurisdiction is exercised.

## Recognition Record

An IRIS artifact recording what was detected, selected, framed, masked or left
uncertain. It is not truth or certification.

## Semantic Authority

OLS authority over canonical vocabulary, definitions, semantic contracts and
permitted semantic mappings.

## Structural Authority

ORION authority over declared structural representation, relations, navigation,
orientation maps and structural expression within an explicit scope.

## Translation Authority

Authority to assert the fidelity of a declared transformation. It governs
fidelity, not source meaning.

## Presentation Authority

SIRIUS authority over the shared application-facing presentation boundary.

## Application Authority

Authority of an independent application owner over its local interface,
workflow, state and bounded recommendations.

## STOP

The mandatory result when a proposal violates the Constitution or when its
authority owner, scope, exclusions, dependencies or artifact class cannot be
established explicitly.
