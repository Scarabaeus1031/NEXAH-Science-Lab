# Final Review Scope

This is a read-only, evidence-first, adversarial review of Representation Ledger V3. V3's own readiness statement is not treated as evidence.

Inspected material comprises the V3 schema and Samples A–D, the V2 blind re-review, the V2 schema revision, and the original code/data/audit artifacts referenced by the samples. Evidence hashes for the frozen Case C and D artifacts were independently recalculated and matched the V3 values. The two legacy source files for A and B were inspected directly.

The review asks whether an independent researcher can recover substantially the same scientific account and whether the ledger design is precise enough to authorize a minimal structural validator specification. It does not assess novelty, establish scientific truth, or authorize implementation.

No V3 file, source artifact, schema, experiment, T02, ORION, or canonical NEXAH material was modified. No experiment or validator was run or created; schema checks and read-only evidence inspection are review operations.

## Decision rule

A pass requires every conjunct in the supplied final rule. One blocking failure in append-preserving cross-revision claim identity therefore precludes PASS even if the scientific reconstructions and other structural tests succeed. There is no V4 path.
