# History-Completeness Final Test

## Result

The three states have bounded and intelligible semantics:

- `HISTORY_COMPLETE`: no unresolved prior references, local to the declared series and located evidence;
- `HISTORY_PARTIAL`: at least one named unresolved prior reference;
- `HISTORY_UNKNOWN`: no defensible completeness assessment.

Revision greater than one requires a previous-record reference and at least one known prior-record reference. A schema-valid complete record cannot simultaneously list unresolved prior references. Samples A and B honestly declare partial history because their historical data instances are unavailable. Case C declares local completeness for the reviewed claim set. Case D identifies its known prior record and has no asserted unknown predecessor.

These states do not claim global completeness and cannot discover hidden external history. That limitation is explicitly documented. The Case D claim-identity break is assessed separately; it does not make the completeness vocabulary itself dishonest.
