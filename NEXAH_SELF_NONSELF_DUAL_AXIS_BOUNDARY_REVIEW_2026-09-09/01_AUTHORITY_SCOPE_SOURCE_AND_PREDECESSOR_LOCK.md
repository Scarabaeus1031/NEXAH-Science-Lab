# Authority, Scope, Source and Predecessor Lock

## Authority order

1. current mission and its explicit stop conditions;
2. repository authority/constitution and Human Authority artifacts;
3. closed predecessor reviews, treated as immutable inputs;
4. primary scientific sources;
5. repository visual and semantic artifacts, at their declared authority level;
6. this additive synthesis.

Repository terms and visuals do not outrank mechanics, control theory, cell biology, or empirical evidence. Human approval does not make a scientific claim true; it governs authorization and accountability.

## Scope

In scope: scientific currentness, terminology, non-identity constraints, minimal equations, energy/work/impulse closure, four-condition comparison, falsifiers, repository capability status, and an optional future test specification.

Out of scope: code, apparatus, simulations, participant studies, parameter fitting, empirical claims, publication, outreach, changes to ORION/NEXAH core, and reopening closed workstreams.
