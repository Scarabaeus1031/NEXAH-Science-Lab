# Claim and Non-Claim Ledger

| ID | bounded claim | status |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | A time-dependent controller-side assignment/exchange boundary can be formalized. | justified specification |
| C2 | Dual-axis coordination can be represented with declared axes, frames, relative angle, coupling, contact and events. | standard + justified integration |
| C3 | Variable coupling has an explicit stiffness-programming power port. | standard mechanics |
| C4 | Sequential braking/release can redistribute energy/timing subject to external/contact and actuator ledgers. | plausible standard mechanism; effect untested here |
| C5 | A body/task/contact-conditioned angle corridor is more defensible than one universal optimal angle. | justified bounded inference |
| C6 | Selective exchange is a legitimate architectural comparison between an interface model and cells. | comparison only |
| C7 | `LIFE/LIVE/EVIL` can mark representation/reversal/viewpoint operations. | linguistic/visual only |

Explicit non-claims:

- no new physics, biology, neuroscience, or immunology;
- no cross-domain identity and no claim that a controller has biological selfhood;
- no universal optimal swing or privileged `X°`;
- no physical meaning from `T`, `BP`, split glyphs, words, colors, or resemblance;
- no energy from nothing, and no momentum change without the required external impulse;
- no claim that 90° has a new physical effect;
- no claim that roundness entails return, or that return entails success/persistence;
- no empirical validation, implementation, clinical/athletic recommendation, publication readiness, or core-system change.

Falsifiers are operational: ledger nonclosure, coordinate dependence, failure of the 4−3 contrast, unstable event detection, inability to bind pressure to a measured quantity, or a visual/source interpretation that cannot be traced to a primary artifact.
