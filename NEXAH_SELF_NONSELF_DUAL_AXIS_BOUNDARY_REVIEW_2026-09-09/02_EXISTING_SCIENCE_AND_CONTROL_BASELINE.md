# Existing Science and Control Baseline

## Mechanics and motor control

- A multibody plant may have several moving segment axes. Axis direction, reference frame, origin, and whether an axis is directed or unsigned must be declared.
- Internal joint torques redistribute momentum within a closed system; total momentum changes require external impulse. Ground-reaction force is an external contact force measured/estimated at the support interface.
- Counterrotation is ordinary relative rotation until a defined plant, frame, coupling, and contact model gives it a specific dynamical role.
- Sequential braking can redirect energy/momentum among degrees of freedom and alter endpoint timing; it does not generate energy.
- Internal models and feedback control are established motor-control constructs, but a controller's estimate is not an ontological self. Representative primary references: [Wolpert et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7569931/), [Todorov & Jordan](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn963), [Crevecoeur et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23825396/), and [Saunders & Knill](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12904935/).
- Force-plate observables and their interpretation require a measurement model; see the biomechanics treatment by [Robertson et al.](https://people.umass.edu/~gc/protected/Robertson_Force.pdf).

## Pressure and contact

Physical pressure is normal force per area, not a synonym for load, torque, effort, intention, or stored energy; see the [IUPAC Gold Book definition](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04819). A spatial pressure field may produce a resultant force and moment, but these must be integrated with geometry.

## Cell biology

Cell membranes support selective exchange through passive and active transport, channels, carriers, gradients, and regulated internal state. Sources: [Principles of Membrane Transport](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26815/), [Cell Membranes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9928/), and [Membrane Structure](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26864/).

Immunological self/non-self is a specialized biological concept, not a generic controller partition. Even within immunology, a simple self/non-self account is not exhaustive; compare the textbook framing in [Immunobiology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27090/) with the [danger-model critique](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11951032/).

## Currentness conclusion

The proposed work needs no new physics, biology, or neuroscience. Its scientifically legitimate contribution is a typed control-and-interface formalization plus explicit non-identity rules. Claims of a universal angle, new energy source, literal cell equivalence, or natural law are unsupported.
