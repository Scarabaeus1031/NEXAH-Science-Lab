# Terminology correction

The fixed-width solution set has 81 ordered entries: 40 non-fixed two-element orbits plus fixed point 4444, hence 41 unordered orbits.

Exactly 18 ordered entries belong to nine leading-zero-sensitive orbits: one member is written 0abc and its reverse is cba0. Canonical four-digit integer enumeration excludes the low member because its canonical spelling has fewer than four digits, but retains the high member. Therefore:

- 63 fixed-width entries are not leading-zero-dependent: 31 complete non-fixed orbits plus 4444.
- canonical enumeration contains those 63 entries plus nine orphan high members, totaling 72.
- the 72-member canonical subset is not closed under four-character reversal.
- it must not be called a complete canonical orbit set.

Classification: TERMINOLOGY_CORRECTED, LEADING_ZERO_DEPENDENT, DOMAIN_RESTRICTED.
