# Position Statement

## Scientific position

The surviving research program is a small collection of established scientific questions concerning:

- identifiability under incomplete or transformed observation;
- state estimation under time-varying observation loss;
- correct semantics for missingness, censoring, numerical failure, and provenance;
- existence and stability of weighted estimators on declared spaces;
- invariant statistics and property-preserving reduction of graphs;
- incremental value of trajectory-derived diagnostics against standard baselines.

These questions belong primarily to inverse problems, state estimation, statistics, formal methods, applied mathematics, network science, and dynamical systems.

## Repository contribution currently visible

The repository supplies:

- bounded synthetic counterexamples for reconstruction and projection collapse;
- one feasible intermittent-observation experiment design;
- deterministic graph and benchmark analysis implementations;
- one bounded local-instability result;
- preserved negative and failed results;
- provenance and claim-boundary practices.

It does not currently supply:

- a new mathematical field;
- a new general theory of representation or orientation;
- a novel estimator class;
- a general information-loss measure;
- a validated graph invariant;
- a validated transition detector;
- operational power-system decision support;
- evidence of scientific priority.
