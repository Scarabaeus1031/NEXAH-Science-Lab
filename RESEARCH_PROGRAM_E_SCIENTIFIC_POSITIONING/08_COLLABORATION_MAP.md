## Best external starting point

`Independent review of the existing Lab 0.4 projection case as a finite inverse problem.`

Reasons:

- the source records and four projections already exist;
- the problem can be stated without project terminology;
- the expected output is a finite set of indistinguishability classes and preserved identities;
- standard rank, null-space, and multi-view language applies;
- no new experiment, operator, or universal claim is required;
- a negative result is useful.

## Domain-specific specialist routes

These routes are retained because the questions may be useful to specialists,
not because a general NEXAH contribution has been established. Detailed
source and packet requirements are maintained once in the
[ARCHY partner register](../00_INCOMING/ARCHY_ARCHITECTURE_ARCHAEOLOGY_DOMAIN_PARKING_2026-10-01/03_TODO_AND_PARTNER_REGISTER.md).

| Case | Smallest legitimate collaboration object | Entry condition | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Göbekli / Ancient Architecture | correction review of one source-bound Enclosure D orientation comparison | exact source ledger, chronology, axis/horizon uncertainty, alternatives and repaired scholarly letter | `PROMISING_SPECIALIST_DIALOGUE / NOT_SEND_READY` |
| Roman Dodecahedron | two-stage falsification review: ideal geometric/optical feasibility, then historical artifact/function assessment | selected measured artifact corpus, compact metric/test table and strict separation of modern usability from ancient use | `PROMISING_RESEARCH_QUESTION / SPECIALIST_PACKET_PREPARABLE` |

These are not added to the numbered Program E priority list: that list ranks
general method collaboration. The table above preserves domain opportunities
without conflating them with broad scientific novelty.

## Collaboration boundary

External collaboration should ask one conventional question and expose one bounded package. It should not ask collaborators to evaluate NEXAH as a whole.
