# External Reviewers

## Reviewer selection rule

Select reviewers by methods and benchmark expertise. Do not ask one reviewer to assess the complete repository or its terminology. No individual is contacted or nominated by this report.

| Review package | Required reviewer profile | Relevant community | Independence requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-Q01/E-Q05/E-Q07 representation and inversion | Applied inverse-problems researcher with identifiability and regularization expertise | SIAM inverse problems / imaging science; numerical analysis | No participation in repository definitions or Labs 0.3–0.4 |
| E-Q06 time-varying observations | State-estimation or stochastic-control researcher familiar with intermittent observations | [IEEE Control Systems Society](https://ieeecss.org/); IFAC system identification/estimation | Must accept ordinary state-space formulation and preregistered comparison |
| E-Q04 aggregation | Geometric statistician or multisensor-fusion researcher | Applied mathematics; [IEEE Signal Processing Society](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/index.php/community-involvement/technical-committees) | No commitment to `Q°` or repository ontology |
| E-Q02 transition records | Formal-methods researcher or scientific-provenance standards engineer | Model checking; [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/); COMBINE/SBGN | Must test interoperability and semantic separation, not visual similarity |
| E-Q03 incomplete/failure records | Missing-data statistician plus numerical-analysis reviewer | Statistical methodology; scientific computing | Two reviewers preferred because statistical and solver failure are distinct |
| E-Q08/E-Q09 graphs | Network scientist or graph-algorithms researcher | [Network Science Society](https://www.netscisociety.net/about); spectral graph / sparse linear algebra communities | Must compare with standard invariants and reduction methods |
| E-Q10 diagnostics | Nonlinear-dynamics researcher plus statistical signal-processing reviewer | SIAM dynamical systems; IEEE signal processing; fluid-transport/LCS community | Event labels and baselines frozen before review |
| IEEE Geometry application | Power-systems researcher or research software engineer with numerical power-flow expertise | IEEE Power & Energy / power-systems research software | Preserve G3 result and G4 failure; no operational claim request |

## Review evidence required

Every reviewer receives:

- one ordinary-language question;
- exact inputs, outputs, assumptions, and non-claims;
- repository provenance as an appendix;
- standard methods and benchmark list;
- frozen code/data/environment where applicable;
- positive, negative, failed, and blocked records;
- questions the reviewer may answer and questions outside scope.

## Reviewer stop conditions

Do not initiate review when:

- the statistic or observable is undefined;
- repository terminology is required to understand the question;
- the reviewer would need to reconstruct missing source evidence;
- benchmarks or comparison metrics can still change;
- an external opinion would be presented as validation or endorsement.

## Possible external reviewers

The permitted output is a reviewer profile or community, not a named endorsement:

- inverse-problems and computational-imaging methodologists;
- state-estimation and intermittent-observation researchers;
- missing-data statisticians;
- formal-methods and provenance-standards engineers;
- geometric statistics and multisensor-fusion researchers;
- network-science and graph-reduction researchers;
- nonlinear-dynamics, coherent-structure, and change-point researchers;
- power-systems reproducibility reviewers.
