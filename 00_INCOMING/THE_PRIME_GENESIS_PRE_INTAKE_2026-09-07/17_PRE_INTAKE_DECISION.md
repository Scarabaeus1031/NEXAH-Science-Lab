# Pre-intake decision

## Decision

`B — VALUABLE_STRUCTURE_WITH_OPEN_PARAMETER_OR_NOTATION_DEPENDENCIES`

The package contains valuable exact mathematics and a coherent orientation vocabulary, but it is not mathematically reconciled as a whole. The decisive blockers to A are:

- contradictory Fibonacci and prime indexing;
- an inaccurate/incomplete Riemann explicit-formula presentation;
- QRT operators without executable definitions;
- lambda, grid, lens and embedding parameters without a predeclared selection rule;
- generated visuals without source generators or exact reproduction records;
- physical, geographic and symbolic language that exceeds the mathematical evidence.

## What survives the representation change

The strongest retained structures are exact arithmetic, CRT reconstruction within a declared residue domain, Cassini's ±1 identity, the bilinear difference identity, explicit carrier/view separation and residual-preserving return logic.

## What improves NEXAH

Prime Lens can become a versioned multi-view inspection protocol; Root Lens can preserve generating relations; Mirror Thread can separate reversal operators; CRT can model loss and reconstruction precisely; Residual Literacy keeps open U from being erased by numerical zero; the machine-as-instrument framing correctly leaves claim authority with humans.

These are proposals, not adoptions. No Canon, ORION, NEXAHEDRON, GLB or closed test was changed.
