## Classification decision

The reviewed material is not treated as one new project. It indicates two
different NEXAH subject areas — comparable to separate school subjects — with
different methods and evidence rules:

1. **Architecture** — buildings, envelopes, materials, spatial legibility,
   environmental stabilization, passive and active systems, adaptation and
   settlement form.
2. **Archaeology** — sites, objects, layers, historical sources,
   reconstruction, provenance and interpretations of past orientation and
   building practices.

`SPECULATIVE` is not a third subject area. It is a status label that may occur
inside either subject and must remain visible.
