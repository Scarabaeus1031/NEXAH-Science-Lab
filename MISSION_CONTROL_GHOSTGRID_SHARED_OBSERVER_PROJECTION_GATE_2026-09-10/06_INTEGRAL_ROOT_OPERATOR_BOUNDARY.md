# Integral/root operator boundary

For a square grid containing N equal cells, linear_scale=sqrt(N), subject to the declared square-cell interpretation.

For scalar samples f on a finite grid, field aggregation may be sum over G of f. A continuous model may instead define an integral over G of f dA, but that requires a domain, measure, units, and field interpretation.

SQRT=SCALE_EXTRACTION
SUM_OR_INTEGRAL=FIELD_AGGREGATION

Square root and integration are not general inverse operators. The exponent relation is linear scale versus two-dimensional area/cell count: doubling linear resolution quadruples the cell count. No physical integral field is inferred from a rendered visual.
