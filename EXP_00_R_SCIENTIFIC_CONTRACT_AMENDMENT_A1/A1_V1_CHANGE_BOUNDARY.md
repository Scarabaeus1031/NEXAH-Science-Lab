A1 is not byte-for-byte or fully semantic equivalence with an already executable V1 rule. V1 did not uniquely determine A–C.

## Frozen components unchanged

A1 does not change:

- Rössler equations or parameters;
- RK4, `dt=0.005`, decision interval, or `H=1.0`;
- `B=e_x` in the experiment, scalar action set, or amplitudes;
- target construction, objective, or success threshold;
- TRAJECTORY or LEARNED_FIELD scientific definitions/hyperparameters;
- information parity or analytic-field prohibition;
- primary all-jointly-supported population including zero selections;
- separate nonzero-proposal support-validity gate;
- support thresholds/abstention;
- carriers, ranking, ties, or Kendall-tau-b coherence;
- primary model/covariates/endpoints;
- 500 seed-cluster bootstrap repetitions;
- 200 repetitions for each stochastic N1–N4 family;
- sensitivity registry;
- Lorenz-v2 P4 interpretation or maximum partial cross-system label.

## Prohibited interpretation

A1 must not be cited as evidence that EXP-00-R will replicate or that coordinate invariance holds. It only makes failure/pass rules executable.
