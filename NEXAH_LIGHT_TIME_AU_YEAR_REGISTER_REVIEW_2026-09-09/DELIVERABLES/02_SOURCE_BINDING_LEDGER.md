## Primary question

A complete physical chain requires all of: a physical emission event; a defined propagation medium and geometry; an independently established distance; an independently timestamped reception event; a calibrated delay model; uncertainty propagation; and reproducible data. Such a chain is **not demonstrated by the currently bound sources**. The visual V1 alone has an evidential ceiling of metaphor/schema.
