# Authority, Scope, and Stop Boundary

## Authorized

Read-only scientific-currentness review; source binding; dimensional and epistemic audit; bounded comparison of five experiment families; formal specification of one minimum experiment; additive creation of this review bundle.

## Not authorized

Modification or reactivation of existing workstreams; implementation; procurement; apparatus operation; data collection; test execution; interpretation of uncollected data; commit; push; or alteration of pre-existing files.

## Authority hierarchy

1. BIPM/CGPM for SI definitions.
2. IAU resolutions for the astronomical-unit and Julian-year conventions.
3. NIST metrology references for refractive-index and uncertainty considerations.
4. Repository artifacts only for claims demonstrably present in exact files.
5. Visual/metaphorical material only as non-authoritative explanatory context.

A lower tier cannot override a higher-tier definition. A visual cannot establish a physical source, timestamp chain, calibration, or measured result.

## Fail-closed rule

Unlocatable, unenumerated, ambiguous, or uncalibrated evidence is recorded as NOT DEMONSTRATED. It is never promoted to absence, existence, implementation, validation, or measurement.
