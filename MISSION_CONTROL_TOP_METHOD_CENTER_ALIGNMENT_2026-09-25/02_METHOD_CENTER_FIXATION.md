Status: `FIXED_AS_CURRENT_SYNTHESIS / NON-NORMATIVE_NAME / NO_ACTIVATION`

The controlling documents retain their authority. TOP is fixed as the short
name for their common perspective instrument.

## Fixed statement

> NEXAH TOP is a Human-governed perspective instrument for comparing bounded
> representations of a source while preserving their construction, relations,
> differences, provenance, residuals and limits. It does not create a new
> science and does not replace domain knowledge.

## Objectivity statement

TOP does not reject objectivity. It rejects unexamined representational
authority. Its working aim is:

> Objectivity through auditable relations among declared views, not through an
> undeclared view from nowhere.

This is a methodological orientation statement. Particular objective claims
still require domain evidence, a valid comparison and independent review where
appropriate.

## Crownpiece interpretation

TOP may be described as the NEXAH methodological crownpiece because it makes
the shared purpose of many modules visible. `Crownpiece` is a navigation and
communication designation. It does not outrank the Constitution or create
scientific, organizational or runtime authority.

## Failure controls

Stop or reduce the claim when:

- a source and its representation are treated as identical without proof;
- the selected perspective is not declared;
- unlike domain quantities are merged by a binder;
- an ILAU class is used without a comparison universe and criterion;
- return similarity is reported as global identity;
- a residual is interpreted as new physics merely because it remains;
- several perspectives are treated as equally supported despite unequal
  evidence;
- TOP is presented as a replacement for specialist science.
