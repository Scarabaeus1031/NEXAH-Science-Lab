# TOP alignment and non-duplication audit

Status: `REVIEW_COMPLETE`

## Audit question

Does `TOP — Theory of Perspective` define a new scientific center, or does it
name and explain the already established NEXAH methodological center?

## Finding

The existing center is already explicit in the NEXAH Scientific Constitution
and current Science Lab synthesis. The constitution begins from the fact that
knowledge is encountered through representation and defines NEXAH as an
orientation, translation, representation, comparison and evidence
architecture. The current overview already calls Orientation the NEXAH
umbrella and the present middle of the system.

TOP is therefore justified only as an additive name and explanatory lens.

## Non-duplication result

TOP does not require:

- a new OLS primitive;
- a fifth ILAU class;
- promotion of `Q°` into an operator;
- a new scientific ontology;
- renaming the Sealed Comparison Method;
- replacing domain methods;
- reclassifying visual work as scientific evidence;
- activating a Study, experiment, runtime or publication.

## Distinct contribution of the name

The name adds one useful public and cross-subject function: it makes the
existing architecture understandable as a separation of representational
roles. Its value is pedagogical, navigational and methodological. Any claim
that TOP improves scientific outcomes remains a separate empirical question.
