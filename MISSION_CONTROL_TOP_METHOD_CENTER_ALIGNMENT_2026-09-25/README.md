## Decision

`TOP — Theory of Perspective` is the concise cross-disciplinary name for the
already existing NEXAH orientation center. It does not replace the Scientific
Constitution, Orientation, OLS, ILAU, NRRC, the Sealed Comparison Method or any
domain method.

TOP names their common human-readable purpose:

> Compare bounded representations without confusing any representation with
> the whole source; keep cuts, transformations, retained relations, losses,
> introduced structures, unresolved questions, provenance, residuals and
> Human responsibility visible.

In this record, `Theory` means an organized perspective and review framework.
It does not mean a new predictive theory of nature.

## Gewaltenteilung der Darstellung

TOP uses **Gewaltenteilung der Darstellung** as its explanatory principle:

1. source or event is not the record;
2. selection and aperture do not own the source;
3. representation does not own interpretation;
4. interpretation does not create evidence;
5. a binder permits comparison without merging boundaries;
6. a decision remains reviewable and revisable;
7. Human authority retains `ABSTAIN` and `STOP`.

The principle is not that all views are equally valid. Views may be rejected
when their source, mapping, evidence, comparison frame or claim boundary is
insufficient.

## Compact instrument

```text
SOURCE / EVENT
  -> DECLARED PERSPECTIVE OR Q-CUT
  -> APERTURE / SELECTION
  -> REPRESENTATION / RECORD
  -> BINDER / DECLARED COMPARISON
  -> ILAU: RETAINED / LOST / ADDED / UNRESOLVED
  -> TYPED RESIDUAL
  -> BOUNDED RETURN
  -> HUMAN INTERPRETATION / REVISION / ABSTAIN / STOP
```

`Q°` remains a declared local observation state or cut. It is not promoted
to an OLS primitive and is not itself the invariant. `ILAU.I` denotes what is
retained under the declared comparison; it does not denote truth in general.

## Cross-disciplinary role

TOP may be applied across school subjects because it regulates the passage
between representations rather than replacing the subject matter:

- physics keeps physical models, units and experimental evidence;
- chemistry keeps substances, reactions, instruments and chemical evidence;
- biology keeps biological objects, protocols and domain review;
- history keeps sources, provenance, perspective and source criticism;
- mathematics keeps definitions, assumptions and proof;
- design and visualization keep construction choices and reader effects.

The same TOP questions can recur across these fields, but no mechanism,
invariant or result transfers between fields without a declared mapping and
domain-appropriate evidence.
