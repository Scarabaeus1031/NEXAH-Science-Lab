# PAD assessment

## P — Prior art

The package resembles established preregistration, executable specifications, schema validation, provenance manifests, deterministic reproducible pipelines, fail-closed workflow gates, adversarial mutation testing and independent-implementation verification.

Classification: **KNOWN**.

## A — Actual delta

After subtracting those known components, the specific contribution is their unusually strict integration into a transitive scientific-authority chain in which raw evidence is intended to reconstruct validity and classification mechanically. The review finds no basis for claiming that the individual methods, or their combination, are novel merely because the construction is elaborate.

Classification: **INTEGRATION**.

## D — Demonstration

An advantage over a simpler reproducible-science pipeline would require prospective comparison across multiple preregistered experiments: fewer undetected validity errors, less implementer divergence, reproducible classifications by independent teams, acceptable implementation cost, and successful handling of authorized registered evidence. A5XEF supplies synthetic conformance evidence, not that comparative empirical demonstration.

Classification: **UNKNOWN**.
