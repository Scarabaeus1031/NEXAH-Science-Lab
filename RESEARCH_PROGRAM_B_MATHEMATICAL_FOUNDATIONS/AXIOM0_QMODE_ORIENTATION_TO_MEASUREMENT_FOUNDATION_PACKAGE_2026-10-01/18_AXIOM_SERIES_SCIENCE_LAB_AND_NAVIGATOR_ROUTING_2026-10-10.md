as scientific content:

- the frozen Master Axiom Chain as a universal system;
- chromatic orientation treated as physical mechanism;
- matter as fixation of orientation;
- Ghostgrid or an orientation network treated as physical spacetime;
- universal energy, field, consciousness or cosmological interpretations;
- privileged numerical and angular constants without a declared derivation or
  preregistered test;

  evidence of one universal mechanism;
- visual similarity, recurrence or symbolic resonance treated as proof;
- self-declared `canonical`, `stable`, `complete` or `frozen` source labels
  treated as Science Lab validation.

These exclusions concern scientific admission, not cultural, visual,
historical or creative value.
