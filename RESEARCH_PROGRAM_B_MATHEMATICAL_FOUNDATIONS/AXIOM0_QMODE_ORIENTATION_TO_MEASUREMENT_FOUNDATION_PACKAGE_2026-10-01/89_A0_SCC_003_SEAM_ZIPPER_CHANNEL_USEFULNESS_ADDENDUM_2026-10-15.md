# SCC-003 Addendum Notes — Seam, Zipper, Channel and Usefulness

Date: `2026-10-15`

## 1. Owner direction and purpose

The Human Owner selected one continuation question for development:

> Does the declared Pascal seam become useful when it is treated as a typed
> zipper and channel rather than only as a quotient identification?

The purpose is not to enlarge the novelty claim around a decorative pentagon.
The purpose is to determine whether the construction provides a measurable
advantage for NEXAH: joining bounded carriers while retaining recurrence,
source position, orientation, inspectability and an explicit Return path.

This note records and sharpens the question. It does not activate a Study,
execute a test, change the SCC-003 theorem, create `PENT-08`, or authorize a
publication claim.

## 2. Existing exact substrate

The continuation starts from facts already fixed by SCC-003:

- five depth-six Pascal carriers contain `5 * 28 = 140` raw face positions;
- their five apices form one quotient class;
- thirty non-apex side pairs form thirty seam classes;
- seventy-five interior positions remain singleton classes;
- the closed quotient therefore has `1 + 30 + 75 = 106` vertices;
- the quotient retains `180` directed recurrence edges and face-incidence
  provenance;
- every glued side value is `1`, so the quotient weight is well-defined;
- the typed object carries the exact tested `D5` action.

These facts establish a seam quotient. They do not yet establish a channel,
transport law, usefulness advantage or Riemann relation.

## 3. Recovered design intention

The Owner recollection supplies a design hypothesis that was not compactly
preserved in the narrow paper:

```text
Pascal side chains of 1
  -> compatible seams
  -> folded or adjacent carrier faces
  -> zipper-like local passage
  -> typed channel across a boundary
  -> Return with retained provenance
```

The innovation candidate is therefore not merely the pentagonal layout. It is
the possibility that a boundary can simultaneously:

1. separate two local descriptions;
2. identify a compatible interface;
3. admit a controlled transition;
4. retain enough orientation and provenance for Return.

The remembered `takeaway box` is treated as an unresolved source object. No
named or hash-bound record for that box has yet been recovered. Its intended
role appears to have been the transition from *seam as boundary* to *seam as
usable channel*, but this remains a provenance target rather than evidence.

## 4. Working distinctions

The research must keep four layers separate.

| Layer | Current meaning | Evidence state |
|---|---|---|
| seam | the declared equivalence class `S_(n,s)` joining two Pascal side positions | exact in SCC-003 |
| fold | a representation or embedding that places carrier faces against one another | exact only for the separately declared representation |
| zipper | an ordered, oriented rule for traversing or pairing interface states | candidate; exact historical rule not yet recovered |
| channel | a transition contract carrying a declared state, record or update across an interface | candidate; requires ports, state, update and Return semantics |

A seam is not automatically a channel. It becomes a channel only when the
carried object, admissible transition and Return record are declared.

## 5. Zipper coordinate — preserved but not invented

The historical corpus contains signed left/right or forward/reverse language,
and later bounded work contains exact `+1/-1` actions. The Owner also recalls
the local offsets `+/-1, +/-2, +/-3` as an in-between or zipper description.

The present SCC-003 specification does **not** derive one canonical
`+/-1,+/-2,+/-3` seam coordinate. This recollection is therefore recorded as
an explicit recovery question:

> Can the historical zipper offsets be reconstructed from a dated source and
> mapped unambiguously to level, face, side or branch coordinates of the
> Pascal carrier?

Until that map is recovered, only the minimal orientation label
`epsilon in {-1,+1}` may be used as a formal-design comparator. The values
`+/-2` and `+/-3` must not be generated from visual proximity or numerology.

## 6. The boundary as the orientation object

The candidate operational reading reuses the existing A0 boundary grammar:

```text
state + frame + boundary + side
  -> admissible crossing
  -> translated state
  -> Return record + residual
```

For SCC-003, the boundary is useful precisely because it is not erased by the
gluing. The quotient node may be shared while its two face incidences remain
distinguishable. This suggests a two-level record:

```text
shared quotient address
+ face-side provenance
+ optional orientation / residual coordinate
```

The research question is whether this representation is smaller, clearer or
more reliable than keeping five disjoint carriers or joining them through an
untyped hub.

## 7. Critical-line provenance and strict boundary

An April 2026 exploratory Core note described a NEXAH `Critical Line` as an
`Elastic Axis`, `basin boundary`, `0-line` and `backflow channel`. This is real
historical provenance for the language remembered by the Owner.

It is **not** currently a mathematical identification with the Riemann
critical line. In established notation the Riemann critical line is

```text
Re(s) = 1/2.
```

It is vertical in the conventional complex-plane coordinates; a `45 degree`
appearance requires a separately declared coordinate transformation. No map
from the SCC-003 carrier or its seams to `s`, the zeta function or its zeros
has been supplied. Later Lab records explicitly classify comparable half-line
connections as `STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY_ONLY / IDENTITY_NOT_TESTABLE`.

The permitted continuation language is therefore:

> **critical seam** or **operational critical line** — a declared local
> boundary across which side, transition and Return are measured.

`Riemann / Critical Line` remains a historical mnemonic and source-search
label. A Riemann claim would require a separate source-bound map, theorem or
test and is outside this addendum.

## 8. Base 20 / Base 40 usefulness hypothesis

The equatorial midpoint of a depth-six Pascal face carries
`binom(6,3)=20`. In the separately declared double-face reading:

```text
unfolded multiplicity ledger: 20 + 20 = 40
glued quotient ledger:        shared weight = 20
```

This provides a concrete utility hypothesis: one shared address may retain
two source incidences without summing away their provenance. NEXAH may be able
to move between a quotient ledger and a multiplicity-aware ledger depending
on the question being asked.

This does not establish positional base-20 or base-40 arithmetic. The testable
advantage is typed multi-view accounting, not radix symbolism.

## 9. Bounded usefulness question

The candidate research question for Admission is:

> Compared with disjoint faces, an apex-only hub and an equal-size arbitrary
> gluing, does the typed Pascal seam reduce interface duplication or routing
> cost while preserving facewise recurrence, source provenance and exact
> Return with a smaller residual record?

The scientific object is the finite SCC-003 carrier plus explicitly declared
interface records. The proposed method class is theoretical / design
feasibility.

### Candidate comparators

| ID | Comparator | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `C0` | five disjoint depth-six Pascal faces | no shared interface baseline |
| `C1` | common apex only | weak joining without side-chain channel |
| `C2` | SCC-003 seam quotient without side/orientation memory | tests whether quotient alone is sufficient |
| `C3` | matched arbitrary boundary pairing | tests whether the Pascal `1`-chain compatibility matters |
| `C4` | typed SCC-003 seam plus minimal Return metadata | candidate zipper/channel condition |

### Candidate measures

1. duplicate interface positions removed;
2. facewise recurrence violations introduced;
3. source-incidence reconstruction accuracy;
4. minimal residual bits or symbols required for exact Return;
5. number of ambiguous transitions at a seam;
6. locality of an update before and after crossing;
7. preservation under the declared `D5` action;
8. human inspection cost in a later, separately authorized usability study.

Mathematical utility and Human usability are separate stages. A shorter data
record does not by itself prove that a person understands or operates the
system more effectively.

## 10. Candidate hypotheses and negative outcomes

### `H-U1` — compatible-boundary advantage

The full side-chain gluing preserves facewise Pascal recurrence with fewer
duplicate interface positions than `C0` or `C1`.

### `H-U2` — typed Return advantage

Adding the minimal face-side/orientation residual to the quotient address
permits exact source-incidence reconstruction with less metadata than storing
the entire raw face position independently.

### `H-U3` — zipper transition advantage

A recovered or explicitly designed zipper rule gives a deterministic local
transition and inverse/Return rule across every declared seam.

### `H-U4` — multi-ledger advantage

The same interface can expose shared quotient state and unfolded multiplicity
without conflating them, as illustrated by the exact `20` versus `40` ledger.

### Null and failure outcomes

- `NO_ADVANTAGE_OVER_MATCHED_GLUE`;
- `RETURN_METADATA_EQUALS_OR_EXCEEDS_RAW_ADDRESS`;
- `CHANNEL_RULE_UNDERDETERMINED`;
- `RECURRENCE_OR_PROVENANCE_LOST_AT_INTERFACE`;
- `HISTORICAL_ZIPPER_SOURCE_UNRECOVERED`;
- `RIEMANN_MAPPING_UNDEFINED`;
- `HUMAN_USEFULNESS_NOT_TESTED`.

These outcomes are valid results. They do not damage the existing SCC-003
finite quotient theorem.

## 11. Admission requirements and STOP

Before execution, one intake must freeze:

1. the carried state and its units/types;
2. seam ports and side semantics;
3. the zipper transition and inverse or Return rule;
4. the residual-address vocabulary;
5. the matched arbitrary-gluing control;
6. exact utility measures and tolerances;
7. fixtures and deterministic replay;
8. failure semantics and claim ceiling.

STOP if the zipper rule can only be chosen after observing a preferred result,
if `+/-1,+/-2,+/-3` cannot be source-bound or independently defined, or if the
candidate carries no less information than the raw disjoint representation.
