### Orthogonal-function and phase correction

For the displayed functions

```text

```

phase cycle is


with four 90-degree transitions and 360-degree closure. Three independent
carrier axes may still be declared for vector-valued channels, but that is not
the same claim as scalar-function orthogonality.

A zero crossing is retained as an event detector, not an automatic state
cause. Operational use requires a sampling rule, tolerance, crossing
direction, transition map and failure behavior.
