## Why this guide exists

NEXAH A0 contains exact finite mathematics, formal-method arguments,
interactive instruments, historical source work and a broader orientation
practice. These layers are related, but they do not have the same evidentiary
status.

This guide gives an external reader a short route through the paper family
without asking them to review the whole NEXAH ecosystem. It separates:

1. what is mathematically exact;
2. what is a documented method;
3. what is an instrument or demonstrator;
4. what is metaphorical orientation; and
5. what still requires external review.

This is a routing document, not a review result, submission, publication,
Study, new Paper G or new scientific claim. Distribution or reviewer contact
requires a separate Human Owner decision.

## The shortest honest description

NEXAH A0 studies how a held source can pass through declared frames,
representations and cuts while keeping identity, information loss, residuals
and possible Return inspectable.

Its working orientation key is:

```text
NOTICE
-> ORIENT
-> DECLARE THE CUT
-> COMPARE
-> RETURN WITH THE RESIDUAL
```

`TOI = Theory of Oriented Interfaces` is an internal reading key.
`TOI != TOE` is the explicit boundary: the programme does not claim a Theory
of Everything, a universal physical interface law or a finished theory.

## Five layers that must remain distinct

| Layer | What exists | What may honestly be said | What may not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Exact mathematics** | finite constructions, proofs, enumerations and executable checks | the declared bounded object has the stated property, subject to proof and implementation review | worldwide novelty, physical mechanism or universal applicability |
| **Formal method** | typed Source/View/Operator/Residual/Return distinctions | the method makes representation loss and recovery conditions explicit | that every representation is recoverable or that Return establishes identity |
| **Instrument** | HTML readers, comparators, ledgers and visual demonstrations | a reader can inspect a declared transformation or compare views | that animation, visual similarity or interaction is scientific evidence |
| **Metaphorical orientation** | seam, aperture, wound, mask, lens, echo, bridge, inside/outside | these terms can organize attention and disclose a question | that the metaphor names a physical, chemical, biological or psychological mechanism |
| **Human practice** | `LOOK AGAIN / LISTEN AGAIN`, declared perspective and residual-aware comparison | this is the pedagogical purpose of the programme | demonstrated effectiveness, diagnosis, norm or validated Human utility |

## What is currently strongest

### 1. Exact, finite and inspectable objects

- the SCC-003 cyclic five-face Pascal quotient;
- the all-`N` injective, `C3`-equivariant Pascal–Quaternion representation
  with an exact inverse under its declared conditions;
- finite information-loss and residual-accounting cases;
- executable ledgers and negative controls that retain failed identities and
  non-recoverable projections rather than hiding them.

These are bounded internal results. Exactness is not the same as novelty, and
local independent implementation is not external replication.

### 2. A coherent method architecture

The reusable contribution candidate is not a world formula but a disciplined
record structure:

```text
SOURCE ADDRESS
-> TYPED TRANSFORMATION
-> REPRESENTATION
-> RETURN OR RESIDUAL
```

The method asks a reader to keep at least six things distinct:

- source or carrier;
- selected frame and aperture;
- operator or transformation;
- displayed representation;
- information lost or retained;
- conditions under which a Return is possible.

### 3. Inspectable instruments

The readers and demonstrators make cuts and comparisons visible. Their value
is methodological and editorial unless a separate protocol supplies a
question, comparator, outcome vocabulary, falsifier and evidence route.

## What remains metaphor

The terms `seam`, `wound`, `scar`, `aperture`, `mask`, `lens`, `echo`,
`bridge`, `inside`, `outside`, `flow` and `return` often describe how a reader
is invited to orient attention. They are productive only while their type is
declared.

For example:

- a seam may be a graph quotient, an interface boundary, a visual mark or a
  narrative metaphor;
- an echo may be an acoustic measurement, a repeated signal, a delayed visual
  trace or an editorial return;
- a surface may be a physical boundary, a rendered envelope, a record view or
  a local representation.

No cross-domain identity follows from the shared word or visual form.
Physics, chemistry, biology, psychology and pedagogy each require their own
qualified sources, operational definitions and review.

## Five bounded review tasks

An external reader should choose one task rather than assess “all of NEXAH.”

### Task 1 — mathematical correctness and prior art

Use the frozen SCC-003 packet. Check the quotient definition, counts,
recurrence preservation, typed `D5` action, proof completeness, verifier
independence and closest mathematical predecessors.

### Task 2 — formal Return analysis

Review Paper C. For each case, check whether source, map, codomain, fibres,
residual and inverse conditions are typed correctly. Identify any place where
representation recovery is confused with source identity.

### Task 3 — method comparison

Review Paper E against established work in provenance, measurement theory,
information loss, model/view separation, interface design and scientific
visualization. Determine which elements are established practice, useful
synthesis, terminological variation or genuinely distinctive procedure.

### Task 4 — observer and envelope critique

Review Paper F as a conceptual model. Test whether intrinsic and observed
envelopes are defined sufficiently to support falsifiable comparisons. Reject
any passage that silently moves from a display model to a physical mechanism.

### Task 5 — Human readability and utility

Review one instrument with a separate Human protocol. Ask whether declaring
source, frame, cut and residual helps a reader detect a representation error.
Do not infer effectiveness from the author's own use or from visual appeal.

## Questions on which disagreement is useful

1. Which exact statement is false, underspecified or already known?
2. Which type distinction is necessary, redundant or inconsistently applied?
3. Where does the corpus move from proof to interpretation without marking
   the transition?
4. Which instrument reveals a real comparison, and which merely stages one?
5. Which metaphor clarifies the object, and which creates a false mechanism?
6. What is the smallest independently testable method claim?
7. What evidence would change the present disposition?

## Reviewer return minimum

A useful review return should state:

- reviewer identity, field and relevant qualification;
- conflict-of-interest statement;
- exact files or manifest reviewed;
- whether any executable checks were run and in what environment;
- accepted, rejected and unresolved statements separately;
- citations and structural mappings for prior art;
- one recommended disposition;
- limitations of the review.

Suggested dispositions:

- `BOUNDED_STATEMENT_ACCEPTED`
- `REVISION_REQUIRED`
- `FORMAL_CLAIM_REJECTED`
- `PRIOR_ART_EQUIVALENCE_FOUND`
- `METHOD_SYNTHESIS_USEFUL_BUT_NOT_NOVEL`
- `INSTRUMENT_REQUIRES_PROTOCOL`
- `REVIEW_INCOMPLETE`

No reviewer disposition automatically changes a Study, Finding, Paper,
publication or Navigator state. Results return through the existing Science
Lab and Human Owner gates.
