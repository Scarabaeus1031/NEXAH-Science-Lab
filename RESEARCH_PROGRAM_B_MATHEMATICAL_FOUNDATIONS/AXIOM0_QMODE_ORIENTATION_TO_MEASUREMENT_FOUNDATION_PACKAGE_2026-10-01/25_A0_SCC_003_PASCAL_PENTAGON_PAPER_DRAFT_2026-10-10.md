## Abstract

We define a finite weighted complex by cyclically gluing five copies of the
depth-six Pascal carrier along complete radial side chains. The successive
open quotients have `28, 49, 70, 91, 112` vertices. Closing the remaining
terminal seam identifies six pairs of non-apex vertices and produces a
connected quotient with `106` vertices and `180` directed recurrence edges.
The binomial weights and Pascal recurrence are retained facewise. Rotation of
the five faces and reflection across one face axis descend to typed
automorphisms, and the resulting typed automorphism group is exactly the
dihedral group `D_5` of order `10`. We give a deterministic reference object,
an independent union-find verifier and a bounded comparison with nearby
Pascal-triangle graph constructions. The note claims neither worldwide
novelty nor a physical interpretation; independent mathematical and
literature review remain open.

## 2. The carrier

Let

```text
T_6 = {(n,k) in Z^2 : 0 <= k <= n <= 6}.
```

The depth-six Pascal carrier is the directed weighted graph

```text
P_6 = (T_6, E_6, w_6),

E_6 = {
  ((n,k),(n+1,k)),
  ((n,k),(n+1,k+1))
  : 0 <= k <= n < 6
},

w_6(n,k) = binom(n,k).
```

It has `28` vertices and `42` directed edge incidences. Its two radial sides
are

```text
L = {(n,0):0<=n<=6},
R = {(n,n):0<=n<=6}.
```

We retain two typed layers: the triangular macro-face used for gluing and the
directed recurrence graph inside that face.

## 3. Five copies and the seam relation

Index five copies by `C_5 = Z/5Z`. A raw vertex is `(f,n,k)` with
`f in C_5` and `(n,k) in T_6`. Identify all five apices, and for every face
index `f` and level `1<=n<=6` impose

```text
(f,n,n) ~ (f+1,n,0).
```

The exclusion of `n=0` avoids recounting the already common apex. The
equivalence relation generated by these pairs has:

- one apex class of size five;
- thirty non-apex seam classes of size two; and
- seventy-five singleton interior classes.

The closed object is not an anonymous graph. It is the quotient together with
its weights, directed adjacency and five retained face-incidence maps.

## 4. Open construction and cyclic closure

For `1<=j<=5`, let `K_open(j)` contain faces `0,...,j-1`, with common apex and
only the consecutive seam identifications. The two terminal sides remain
unpaired. Each new face shares exactly the apex and six non-apex side
vertices. It therefore contributes

```text
28 - 7 = 21
```

new vertices. Hence

```text
|V(K_open(j))| = 28 + 21(j-1) = 21j + 7,
```

which yields

```text
28, 49, 70, 91, 112.
```

Closing the terminal seam of `K_open(5)` pairs its twelve terminal non-apex
vertices level by level. The six pairs are disjoint and generate no further
identifications, so

```text
|V(K_closed)| = 112 - 6 = 106.
```

The corresponding six duplicate side edges reduce the directed quotient-edge
count from `186` to `180`.

## 5. Weights, recurrence and connectivity

Define the quotient weight by

```text
w([f,n,k]) = binom(n,k).
```

This is well-defined because every nontrivial non-apex class identifies two
side vertices of weight one; the apex class also has constant weight one.

For every retained face incidence, Pascal's identity remains

```text
binom(n,k) = binom(n-1,k-1) + binom(n-1,k),
```

with zero extension outside `0<=k<=n`. The assertion is deliberately
facewise. Summing every incoming edge of the simple quotient graph would erase
face incidence and can double-count a glued seam.

Every `P_6` is connected when directions are ignored, and all face apices are
identified. Thus every open complex is connected. A quotient of a connected
graph is connected, so `K_closed` is connected.

## 6. Dihedral action

On raw positions define

```text
r(f,n,k) = (f+1,n,k),
m(f,n,k) = (-f,n,n-k),
```

with face indices modulo five. Both maps preserve the seam relation and
descend to the quotient. They preserve level, weight, directed recurrence
edges and macro-face incidence, and satisfy

```text
r^5 = e,
m^2 = e,
m r m = r^-1.
```

The ten maps `{r^a, m r^a : 0<=a<5}` therefore act as typed automorphisms.
Conversely, every typed automorphism induces an automorphism of the retained
five-face adjacency cycle. This action is injective because fixing a face and
its two seams fixes every internal Pascal position. Since
`Aut(C_5)=D_5`, the typed automorphism group is exactly `D_5`.

This statement concerns the weighted, directed, face-incidence object. It is
not an assertion that a graph6 string alone certifies the full automorphism
group of every untyped relabeling.

## 8. Nearby constructions

The bounded prior-art search located two especially relevant neighbors:

1. The Hilton–Pedersen Pascal-Hexagon line extends ordinary binomial values
   through six triangular sextants and studies cross-sector patterns. It is
   the strongest multi-sector Pascal baseline found, but it does not define
   five finite copies, complete side-chain gluing or the `112 -> 106` quotient.
2. Quint's infinite Pascal graph is the closest literal graph-joining
   baseline found: two parity-derived triangular graphs are joined at their
   apex vertices. It is infinite, unweighted in the present sense, uses two
   copies and has no complete side-chain quotient.
