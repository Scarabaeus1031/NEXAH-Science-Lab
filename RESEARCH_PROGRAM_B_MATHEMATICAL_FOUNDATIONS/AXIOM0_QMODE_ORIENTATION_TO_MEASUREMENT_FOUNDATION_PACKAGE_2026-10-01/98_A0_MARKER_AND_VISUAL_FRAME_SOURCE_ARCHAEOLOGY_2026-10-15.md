The poster therefore remains a supplied hypothesis graphic.

The following arithmetic is exact:

- `1729 = 7 × 13 × 19`.
- `1836 = 2² × 3³ × 17`.
- `23³ + 24³ + 25³ = 41,616 = 204²` and `204 = 12 × 17`.
- `φ(1836)/1836 = φ(41616)/41616`, because both numbers have prime support `{2,3,17}`.

The printed decimal `0.449187…` is incorrect. The common ratio is

`(1−1/2)(1−1/3)(1−1/17) = 16/51 ≈ 0.31372549`,

with `φ(1836)=576` and `φ(41616)=13056`. The poster's compression/filling roles and module semantics have no located executable definition and remain documentary hypotheses.
