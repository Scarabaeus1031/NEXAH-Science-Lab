# Excluded physics and numerology

Status: `EXPLICIT FOUNDATION BOUNDARY`

## Excluded from the adopted foundation

The following historical families are not part of the foundation accepted by
this package:

- planetary, solar, astronomical or cosmological interpretations;
- matter, force, spacetime or field claims inferred from AXIOM-0 diagrams;
- HM as an established physical carrier field;
- universal emergence of time, dynamics, matter or observation;
- physical conclusions attributed to Wheeler, Bohr, Penrose, Rovelli, Jung or
  Goethe without a precise sourced argument;
- universal significance assigned to `-1/12`, `13`, `1729`, `sqrt(2)`, `pi`,
  `phi` or other number markers;
- similarity of form across physics, biology, chemistry, planets or culture as
  evidence of one shared mechanism.

## Preservation rule

Exclusion from the current foundation does not require deletion. Such material
may remain in historical custody as:

- intellectual genealogy;
- artistic or philosophical exploration;
- visual hypothesis;
- future bounded research question;
- record of an abandoned or superseded interpretation.

It may return to scientific consideration only through a separate domain-bound
question with definitions, prior art, evidence, controls, uncertainty and a
failure condition.

## Citation boundary

Historical thinkers may motivate questions. Their names or quotations do not
validate NEXAH claims, and interpretive affinity must not be presented as their
endorsement of AXIOM-0 or Q-MODE.
