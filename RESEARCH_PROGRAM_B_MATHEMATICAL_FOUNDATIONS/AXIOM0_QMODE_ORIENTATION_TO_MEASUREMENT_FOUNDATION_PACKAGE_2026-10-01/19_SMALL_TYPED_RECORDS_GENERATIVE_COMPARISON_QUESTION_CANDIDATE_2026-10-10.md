## Candidate question

how much structured diversity can be generated, and which observed relations
represent genuine preservation, translation, compression or predictive value
rather than numerical or visual resemblance?

## Candidate object

A finite typed generative system:

```text
G = (C, O, T, cost, V, R)
```

where:

- `C` is a declared finite carrier or seed-record family;
- `O` is a frozen typed operator set;
- `T` contains the domains, codomains and composition rules;
- `cost` assigns a declared description or operation cost;
- `V` is a finite set of representation and readout maps;
- `R` is a result vocabulary containing exact return, approximate return,
