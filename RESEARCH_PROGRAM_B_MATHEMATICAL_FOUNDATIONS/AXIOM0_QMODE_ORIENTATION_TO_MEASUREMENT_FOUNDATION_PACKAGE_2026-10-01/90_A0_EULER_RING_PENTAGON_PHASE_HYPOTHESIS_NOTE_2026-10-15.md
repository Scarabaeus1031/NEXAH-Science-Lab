## Question

Can the recurring visual relation between a central Euler/phase ring, a
regular pentagon, the golden ratio and Fibonacci sampling be written as one
typed mathematical chain without identifying its distinct carriers?

This note records the question and the already established mathematical
links. It does not promote the combined chain to a theorem about NEXAH, a
physical mechanism or a privileged sampling law.

## Typed hypothesis

Let the complex unit circle be the continuous phase carrier

```text
S^1 = { exp(i theta) : theta in R }.
```

Select five equally spaced phase states

```text
z_k = exp(2 pi i k / 5),  k = 0,1,2,3,4.
```

The selected points form a regular pentagon. In this construction:

- `0` is the centre of the complex coordinate plane;
- `1` is the chosen unit-radius seed;
- `^` is the exponentiation operation in `exp(i theta)`;
- `degrees` are a readout of angular phase, with a step of `72 degrees`;
- multiplication by `i` is a quarter-turn operator;
- `pi` supplies the circular phase scale;
- `phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2` is recovered from the ratio of pentagon diagonal to
  side;
- Fibonacci ratios approach `phi`, but the Fibonacci sequence is not the
  phase ring or the pentagon.

The exact geometric relation is

```text
|z_k - z_(k+2)| / |z_k - z_(k+1)| = phi.
```

The proposed NEXAH reading is therefore:

```text
coordinate seed 0 / 1
  -> continuous Euler phase exp(i theta)
  -> five-state sampling S^1 -> C5
  -> pentagon metric readout phi
  -> Fibonacci as a discrete approximation or sampling schedule
  -> declared comparison / Return
```

The arrows are typed maps. They are not identity signs.

## What is established

1. Fifth roots of unity form a regular pentagon on the complex unit circle.
2. Adjacent selected phases differ by `2 pi / 5 = 72 degrees`.
3. The diagonal-to-side ratio of a regular pentagon is `phi`.
4. Consecutive Fibonacci ratios converge to `phi`.
5. A cycle graph `C5` has `V=5`, `E=5`, `F=0` and Euler characteristic
   `chi=0` when treated as a one-dimensional cell complex.
6. A filled pentagon has an attached two-cell and therefore `chi=1`.

The Euler phase formula and the Euler characteristic are different uses of
Euler's name. Neither may silently substitute for the other.

## Working hypothesis

The phase ring can serve as a common comparison carrier from which the
pentagon is obtained by a declared five-sample operator. The pentagon then
admits an exact metric readout in `phi`, while Fibonacci can supply a separate
discrete schedule or approximation family. This chain may become useful for
module-to-module transport only when each transition preserves an explicit
address, parameter set, residual and Return key.

Potential usefulness is therefore not "the symbols belong together". It is:

```text
one declared carrier
  + typed sampling map
  + measurable metric readout
  + controlled discrete comparator
  + reconstructible Return
```
