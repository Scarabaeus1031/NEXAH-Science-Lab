## Purpose

This record separates the common A0 research protocol from the protocols of
its reader works. It does not create one Study per HTML page. A Work Protocol
states what a maintained work is for, which sources it may read, which outputs
it may produce and where its claim boundary lies.

The common A0 research question remains:

> What remains identifiable and comparable when a carrier is transformed,
> observed or represented differently, and what is introduced by view, cut,
> operator or coupling?

## A0 Master Protocol

- **Object:** relations between carrier, frame, view, operator, record,
  information loss, residual and Return.
- **Method:** declare types; preserve provenance; compare representations;
  test exact inverse, bounded reconstruction or explicit abstention.
- **Common record:** source → carrier → transformation/view → readout →
  retained/lost/added/unavailable → comparison → bounded Return or STOP.
- **Authority:** the Science Lab package and source records remain
  authoritative; A0 is the reader and synthesis interface.
- **Boundary:** no universal ontology, physical mechanism or publication
  authority follows from a shared visual or mathematical grammar.

## A0 Book Protocol

- **Role:** eight-chapter guided introduction from distinction to accountable
  readout and Human Return.
- **Allowed inputs:** established mathematics, bounded NEXAH records and
  source-bound cultural orientation material.
- **Output:** readable explanations, minimal exact instruments and explicit
  claim boundaries.
- **Underwindows:** Record / Play, chapter instruments, Lab Orientation
  Machine and chapter source returns.
- **State:** frozen local reader proof with a post-milestone editorial frame;
  not a second edition and not public.

## Mathfield Protocol

- **Role:** mathematical workshop for definitions, finite carriers,
  operators, joints, exact examples and negative type controls.
- **Allowed inputs:** Field Notes, executed finite records, declared standard
  mathematics and source-bound demonstrators.
- **Output:** inspectable definitions, local calculations and typed joints.
- **Underwindows:** Fields & Joints Atlas, Boundary Graph, Q4 Joint Audit and
  related exact calculators.
- **Boundary:** Mathfield does not own the global A0 research log, lifecycle
  ledger or portfolio currentness.
