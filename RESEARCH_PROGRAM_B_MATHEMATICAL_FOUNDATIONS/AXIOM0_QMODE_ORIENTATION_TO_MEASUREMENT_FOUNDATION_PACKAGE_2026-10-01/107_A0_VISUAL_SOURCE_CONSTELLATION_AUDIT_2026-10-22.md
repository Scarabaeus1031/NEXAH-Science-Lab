# A0 visual source constellation audit

Date: 2026-10-22
Scope: documentary reconciliation of the supplied visual set with existing A0, Science Lab and Navigator holdings
Authority effect: none; no Study, Finding, relation, module, publication or evidence state is created or promoted

## Verdict

The supplied visuals are predominantly already present as source-bound instruments, result reports, controls or historical markers. The remaining gap is a reader-facing route across those holdings, not a missing common mathematical model.

The visuals must remain separated by measurement type. In particular:

- `49%` is a side-share / boundary-position parameter.
- `18%` is a fixed mask occupancy.
- NRMSE values are normalized errors and become percentages only after an explicitly declared `×100` conversion.
- projected radii and golden-ratio pairings are geometric coordinates or ratios, not occupancy percentages.
- page FIT dimensions and pixel coordinates are representation diagnostics, not carrier distances.

No common percentage axis is admitted by this audit.
