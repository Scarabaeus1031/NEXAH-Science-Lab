A cyclic five-face quotient of depth-six Pascal carriers

A finite binomially weighted face complex with 106 vertices, 180 directed recurrence edges and exact typed D5 symmetry.

Abstract

We define a finite weighted complex by cyclically gluing five copies of the depth-six Pascal carrier along complete radial side chains. The successive open quotients have 28, 49, 70, 91 and 112 vertices. Closing the terminal seam identifies six pairs of non-apex vertices and produces a connected quotient with 106 vertices. Binomial weights and Pascal recurrence remain valid facewise. The five-face rotation and reflection generate the exact typed automorphism group D5. A deterministic data object and independent verifier make the construction reconstructible without this page.

2 · Carrier

The depth-six Pascal face.

Let T₆={(n,k)∈ℤ²:0≤k≤n≤6}. Directed edges connect each position to its two Pascal children, and the weight is w(n,k)=C(n,k).

P₆ = (T₆,E₆,w₆)
|T₆| = 1+2+…+7 = 28
|E₆| = 2(1+2+…+6) = 42

The triangular macro-face used for gluing and the directed recurrence graph remain two typed layers.

3 · Construction

Five copies, one cyclic seam rule.

4 · Main results

Growth by 21; closure by six.

Preservation

The quotient weight is well-defined because glued side weights are all one. Pascal's identity remains true within every retained face incidence. The complex is connected through the common apex.

The recurrence is facewise. Summing all incoming edges after forgetting face incidence can double-count a seam and is not the asserted operation.

5 · Symmetry

The typed group is exactly D5.

r(f,n,k) = (f+1,n,k)
m(f,n,k) = (-f,n,n-k)
r⁵=e,  m²=e,  mrm=r⁻¹

Rotation and reflection preserve seams, levels, weights, recurrence edges and face incidence. Every typed automorphism also induces an automorphism of the five-face adjacency cycle; fixing a face and its seams fixes all its Pascal positions. Thus the induced action is injective and the typed group has exactly the ten elements of D5.

8 · Contextual representations

Useful views, outside the theorem.

A regular metric pentagon, a pentagonal bipyramid, H2⊕A1 roots and a common C₃₀ phase cover are separately bounded representations or controls. They do not generate the quotient and do not establish physical resonance, a universal solid, a crystallographic Lie system or an identity between pentagonal and hexagonal carriers.
