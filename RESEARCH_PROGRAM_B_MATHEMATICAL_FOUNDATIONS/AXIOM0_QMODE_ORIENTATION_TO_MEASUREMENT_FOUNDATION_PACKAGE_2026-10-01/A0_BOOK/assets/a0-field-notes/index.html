2026-10-15 coupling note — Euler product, five-joint cycles and the Rootroom boundary

Notiz lesen

The exact Riemann/Ihara bridge is an Euler-product analogy, not an identity of the two zeta functions:

Riemann: zeta(s) = product over primes p of (1-p^(-s))^(-1)
Ihara:   Z_G(u)  = product over primitive non-backtracking cycles [P]
                   of (1-u^length(P))^(-1).

Primitive graph cycles occupy the structural position of primes in the factorization. They are not prime numbers. Euler therefore names the product organization here; it does not by itself insert Euler's number e, Euler characteristic or an Euler spiral.

For one adjacency eigenvalue lambda of a connected (q+1)-regular graph, the nontrivial Bass factor is

1 - lambda*u + q*u^2.

Its two roots are

u_plus/minus(lambda) = (lambda +/- sqrt(lambda^2-4*q))/(2*q).

If |lambda| <= 2*sqrt(q), write

lambda = 2*sqrt(q)*cos(theta).

Then the roots form a conjugate pair

u_plus/minus = q^(-1/2)*exp(+/- i*theta).

This is the rigorous Rootroom for the present graph-zeta question: one spectral value opens into two oriented phase roots on the critical circle. For the cubic Blue Body, q=2, so the radius is 1/sqrt(2).

Five-joint result and negative control

The declared Blue Body shell contains sixteen pentagonal faces, eight hexagonal faces and two octagonal faces. Each oriented pentagonal face gives a primitive length-five non-backtracking circuit and therefore a length-five Euler factor. This makes the local five-joint reading mathematically relevant.

It does not imply that the complete graph has five equally spaced nontrivial Ihara poles. Equal pentagonal pole phases would require adjacency eigenvalues

lambda_k = 2*sqrt(2)*cos(2*pi*k/5),  k=0,...,4.

The corresponding distinct targets are approximately

 2.8284271247,  0.8740320489,  -2.2882456113.

The frozen Blue Body spectrum does not contain these values. Its nearest recorded eigenvalues are respectively

 2.7313013160,  0.7834755828,  -2.1700864866.

Therefore:

ALL_NONTRIVIAL_POLES_LIE_ON_RADIUS_1_OVER_SQRT2  NUMERICAL_PASS
FIVE_EQUAL_GLOBAL_POLE_PHASES                    REJECTED_FOR_FROZEN_SHELL

The current shell is globally an O2-P16-H8 graph with eight-position rings; it is not a single C5 carrier. Local pentagonal joints and the global pole distribution must remain separate.

cos(2*pi/5) = (sqrt(5)-1)/4,
cos(4*pi/5) = -(sqrt(5)+1)/4.

2*sqrt(2)*cos(2*pi/5) =  sqrt(2)*(sqrt(5)-1)/2,
