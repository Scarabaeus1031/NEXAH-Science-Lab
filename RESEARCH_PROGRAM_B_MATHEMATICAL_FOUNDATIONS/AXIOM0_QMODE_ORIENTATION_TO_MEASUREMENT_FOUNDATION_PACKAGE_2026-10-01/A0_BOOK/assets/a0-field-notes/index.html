Here I means only marked/occupied position. It does not yet mean the identity operator, Roman numeral one, electrical current, the complex unit i, quaternion unit i or the ILAU class I. Any later use of one of those meanings needs a separate typed map.

Let

Sigma = {0,1}

Then the proposed side change is exact:

kappa(101) = 010
kappa(010) = 101

This is a complement involution. It is not produced by reflection:

rho(101)=101
rho(010)=010.

Both words are palindromes. A diagram that changes 101 into 010 is therefore performing a state complement, even if it also looks like a mirror or a fold. Nonsymmetric controls such as 100 and 001 are required to tell reflection, complement and complement-after-reflection apart.

The middle coordinate C is a fixed position under the reflection rho, but its state changes under kappa. Therefore the proposed black/red pillar, obelisk or stick can consistently denote:

It cannot simultaneously mean an invariant middle value, because 0 becomes 1 in the proposed transition. Black/red remains a display state, not a mathematical type. If colour later carries sign, direction or evidence state, that map must be declared independently.
