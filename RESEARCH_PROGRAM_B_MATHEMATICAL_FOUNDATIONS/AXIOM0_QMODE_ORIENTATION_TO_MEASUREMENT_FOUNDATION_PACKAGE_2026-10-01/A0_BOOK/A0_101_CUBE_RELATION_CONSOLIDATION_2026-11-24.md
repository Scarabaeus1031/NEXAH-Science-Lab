## Cleanup decision

Nothing is deleted or merged in this pass. The next cleanup should operate in
four lanes:

1. **Carrier:** exactly one declared source record and address contract.
2. **View:** every cube-like display names what it adds, loses or leaves
   unresolved.
3. **Operator / controller:** motion, fold, tilt, phase, topology and control
   remain separately typed.
4. **Envelope / shadow:** projection boundaries never inherit carrier identity
   merely because they share an outline.

This is the lowest-risk way to consolidate the work: reuse the existing
carrier identity, route A0/101 to it, and turn duplicate-looking Cubes into
named views instead of silently merging them.
