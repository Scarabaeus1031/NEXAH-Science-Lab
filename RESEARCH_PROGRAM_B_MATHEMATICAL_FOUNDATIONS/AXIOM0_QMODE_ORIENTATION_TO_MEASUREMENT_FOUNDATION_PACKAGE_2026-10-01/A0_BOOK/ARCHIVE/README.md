# A0 Edition Archive

This directory is the only destination for superseded A0 site editions after
an explicit Owner disposition. Archived editions are provenance records, not
active entrances or source authorities.

Current inventory at `2026-10-29`:

- archived deployment snapshots remain in
  `40 EXPERIENCE PRODUCTION/NEXAH deployment backups/` and are classified as
  `ARCHIVED_DEPLOYMENT_SNAPSHOT / NONAUTHORITATIVE`;
- no second maintained full A0 edition was found or moved;
- Mission Control staging/release files are generated projections and therefore
  are not copied here;
- the redirect-only `../A0/index.html` remains in place as a stable alias to
  the canonical `../A0_BOOK/index.html#top`.

Any future archived edition must receive a dated subdirectory and a receipt
containing its former path, disposition date, reason, source revision and
SHA-256 inventory. It must not be linked as the canonical A0 home.
