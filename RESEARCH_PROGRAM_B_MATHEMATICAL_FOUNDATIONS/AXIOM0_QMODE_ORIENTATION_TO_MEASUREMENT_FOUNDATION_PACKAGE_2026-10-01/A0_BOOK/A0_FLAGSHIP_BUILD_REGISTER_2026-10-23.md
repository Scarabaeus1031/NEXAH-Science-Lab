does not mean product certification, publication approval, scientific evidence

## Rating method

Each build receives `0–2` points in five presentation criteria:

1. `SOURCE` — source ownership and return are visible;
2. `PLAY` — a visitor can perform a bounded action;
3. `VERIFY` — deterministic checks or a replay receipt exist;
4. `BOUNDARY` — claim and non-identity limits are visible;
5. `SHOW` — the route is concise enough for a guided exhibition.

The total is a curation score, not an evidence score.
