## What this package is

This package preserves one bounded reading of the current A0 story:

> Different source systems can meet on a common observation and control plane
> while retaining their own operators, clocks, state and return records.

The Arkade is interpreted as the switchboard for that comparison. It does not
turn the participating systems into one carrier or one physical mechanism.

## The story in five steps

1. **Separate sources.** The decimal ladder `12.6 → 13.7 → 14.8`, the visual
   pulse address `7.83`, the Mandelbrot mask and the Arkade worlds belong to
   different contracts.
2. **A declared view.** A view can expose, suppress, colour or synchronize a
   finite part of a source without mutating its source identity.
3. **A boundary event.** Near a declared decision boundary, a small parameter,
   aperture or iteration change can alter the visible classification. This is
   where elaborate forms become inspectable; a finite render is not the full
   mathematical boundary.
4. **A retained witness.** A collapsed or masked display is not automatically
   information-free. Source, operator, parameters, residual and return record
   determine what can still be reconstructed.
5. **A typed return.** The comparison closes only when each source returns by
   its own contract. Shared timing or choreography is not source identity.

## Exact interpretation boundaries

- The canonical decimal markers are **12.6 and 13.7**. `13.6` belongs only to
  the separate transversal reading `12,7 ↔ 13,6`.
- `7.83` is a **visual time/address marker**, not a calibrated physical
  frequency in the current A0 sources.
- The black shadow is read as a finite mask/hold or non-visible region in a
  declared view. It is not evidence for a new substance, force or ontology.
- Visual similarity and co-animation do not establish an operator relation.
- The Arkade is an interface/orchestration layer over separate worlds.
