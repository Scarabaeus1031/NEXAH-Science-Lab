## Result

The A0 Book now exists as one coherent eight-chapter guided-reading surface.
It begins with distinction, moves through point, relation, coordinate,
discrete carrier, dynamics, projection and evidence, and returns to one Human
comparison through seven questions.

The proof adds three bounded cultural transitions:

1. **Record / Play** at the entrance: a voice becomes a record through a
   declared cut and can be replayed without becoming the whole source.
2. **Crystal View** in Chapter 06: one locally hosted 3D artifact can be viewed
   from several positions; the change of appearance is illustrative and does
   not replace the chapter's mathematical controls.
3. **Rooms of the Machine** in Chapter 08: a closing atlas relates the book to
   the wider Science Lab without merging its instruments into one mechanism.

The entry surface also makes the Human premise explicit: people reasonably
seek simple answers to complex questions, while a convincing representation
can still be incomplete. A single cultural reference to a *Theory of
Everything* names the largest form of that hope without turning the book into
a TOE proposal, refutation or physical theory.

The entry order now treats `00 · Introduction` as the explicit beginning. The
cover routes first to `00`; the eight-chapter map follows immediately, before
the large Record / Play scene. Book Thesis, orientation route and repeatable
method remain available as deeper reading after the chapter choice instead of
creating additional competing beginnings.

Record / Play remains the bounded cultural demonstration of voice, cut,
record and replay, but no longer delays access to the chapter route. Both local
GLB viewers expose camera controls, Safari-compatible pointer gestures and a
slow auto-rotation that makes their interactive status visible; user drag or
zoom remains a view change only and does not alter the artifact. The
public-facing header points to `nexah.de`; a separate reciprocal Science
Navigator link is retained as a source-landscape route. Local HTTP review
rewrites only those Navigator routes from the Book server on port `8876` to
the Navigator server on port `8878`, including the LIFE lineage.
Repository-relative `file://` targets remain unchanged.

Human View explanations remain available where abstraction increases. The
book follows the editorial rule `LOOK -> NAME -> TEST -> RETURN`: show the
object first, name the distinction, preserve the source and claim boundary,
then return judgment to the Human.

## Claim ceiling

This milestone establishes that a bounded internal book proof exists and is
locally readable. It does not establish publication readiness, independent
reader comprehension, Human utility, scientific novelty, a physical mechanism
or a Theory of Everything.

The mathematical statements retain their chapter-level sources and claim
boundaries. Cultural visuals and 3D objects are orientation instruments, not
scientific evidence.

## Successor boundary
