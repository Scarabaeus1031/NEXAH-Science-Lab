paper. One source record can return through its Base-11 address, a CRT address,
a Z-slice, a mask-extrusion position or a full-envelope location without those

position, twist and deliberate absence can jointly encode a reading. It does

Paper E · Preparation note — invariant selection in the S/Z–Khipu instrument

35_NEXAH_ORIENTATION_METHOD_POSITION_PAPER_DRAFT_2026-10-12.md

This bundle gives the orientation method a deliberately small worked grammar:

```text
Source -> Operator -> View -> Return
1..1111 -> standard beta or declared S/Z operator -> slice/envelope/knot -> n
```

The method requires the reader to name what is actually retained. In the polar
demonstrator the verified finite invariant is the winding closure
`102 degrees * 60 = 17 * 360 degrees`, reported as signed `W=+17/-17` after
orientation is chosen. By contrast, `P=alpha+beta` and
`P_chi=alpha+abs(beta)` are coordinates on parameter space. Fixing either does
not by itself conserve energy, bound an orbit or prove stability.

The Khipu lens contributes a disciplined negative control: a visible gap can be
information without being the number zero, and a twist direction can select a
reading without changing the source carrier. Historical analogy, dynamical
operator and arithmetic address remain separate evidence types.

Paper F · Preparation note — Primecube shadow envelope and observer cuts

36_A0_TESSAREC_FRAME_ENVELOPE_OBSERVER_PAPER_DRAFT_2026-10-12.md

Primecube now supplies three equivalent pack views over a single declared
address body: Z-slices, `101/20` mask extrusion and the full `11^3` envelope.
Their finite balance is

```text
1331 = 11 * 121
1111 = 11 * 101
 220 = 11 * 20
```

Thus `101/121` of the address mask carries the retained 1111 records and
`20/121` denotes shadow positions. The near match
`101/121 = 0.834710...` to the separate trigonometric input `-0.834` is shown
only as a comparison; equality and mechanism are explicitly rejected.

Z-slice, mask-extrusion, full-envelope, root-slice and the 25-address window
centred at 13 are observer cuts. They may change adjacency, occlusion and
apparent boundary while source membership and exact address Return stay fixed.
The shadow fibre is therefore an address-level complement, not an unseen
physical body or an additional set of Primecube records.

diagram. It retains `1111` carrier records and `220` record-absent shadow
addresses as different types; the equality of the volume accounts adds no
