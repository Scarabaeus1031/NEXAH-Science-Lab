## Editorial vocabulary

The following lanes are editorial routes, not scientific lifecycle changes:

- `CORE_I`, `CORE_II`, `CORE_III` — material to be rewritten into the public
  essay body;
- `SUPPORT` — a bounded callout, source note or compact example;
- `INSTRUMENT_APPENDIX` — implementation detail linked from an essay, not part
  of its main argument;
- `OPEN_QUESTIONS` — a short public question may be retained, but not the raw
  Owner note;
- `INTERNAL_ARCHIVE` — provenance or analogy retained only in the private
  Fieldbook.

`APPLIED` below means operationalized in a declared A0 instrument. It never
means theorem, physical validation or scientific adoption.
