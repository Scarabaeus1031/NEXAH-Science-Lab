## 3. Scientific-neighborhood reading

THE MACHINA currently sits most plausibly at the intersection of:

1. **research instruments and interactive scientific visualization** — a
   Human-readable interface to bounded objects and transformations;
2. **bidirectional transformations, lenses and provenance** — explicit
   separation of source, view, update, residual and Return;
3. **human-computer interaction and explanation systems** — guided access,
   staged disclosure and inspectable state transitions;
4. **reproducible computational research** — frozen source custody, test
   receipts and declared failure/claim boundaries;
5. **design research and exhibition media** — a carrier that makes a method
   experiential without turning the experience itself into evidence.

The components of these neighborhoods are established. The candidate
contribution is their unusually explicit, source-bound integration into one
finite orientation-and-Return environment. A fresh specialist prior-art and
baseline comparison has not been performed for that conjunction. The current
assessment therefore uses `PLAUSIBLY DISTINCTIVE`, not `NOVEL`.

## 5. What the machine contributes now

The strongest current contribution is not a universal machine claim. It is a
disciplined **Human carrier for source-bound relations**:

- different sources remain different;
- views can be synchronized without merging their authority;
- a route can be replayed while retained, lost, introduced and unresolved
  information remains visible;
- Exact Return is claimed only inside the declared local contract;
- the interface can stop rather than invent a missing witness.

That is a credible methods and instrument contribution. It is currently
stronger as an inspectable research architecture than as a new scientific
result.

## 6. Next evidence that could change the assessment

No further connector or room is required for this assessment. A
status-changing step would be a separately authorized, frozen evaluation such

This proposal is not an activated Study or outreach instruction. The separate
