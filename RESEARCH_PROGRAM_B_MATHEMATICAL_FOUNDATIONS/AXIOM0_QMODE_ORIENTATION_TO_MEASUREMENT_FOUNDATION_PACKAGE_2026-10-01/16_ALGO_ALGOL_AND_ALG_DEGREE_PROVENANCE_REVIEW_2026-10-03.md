## Purpose

This note separates three visually and phonetically similar labels that occur
in different historical NEXAH source families. It prevents a later synthesis
from silently treating `ALGO`, `Algol` and `ALG°` as one operator.

The explicit `Ramanujan Escape` construction is present by v5. It is described
through a pentagonal base, golden-ratio geometry, a claimed Rogers-Ramanujan or
modulo-five connection, an asymmetric hinge near `0.31/0.325`, an outward

The visual `algol_ramanujan_escape_net.png` labels the escape as leaving a
discrete stable core through a hinge gate. This establishes visual and textual
genealogy inside the package. It does not establish the claimed mathematical
correspondence to Rogers-Ramanujan identities.

The relevant version history is not cumulative evidence:

  overrides and deterministic smoothing;
- v8 states that the proposed universal hinge is a detector-geometry artifact
  that moves when the detector window changes;
- v9 calls the predictive result a clear failure but retains the escape-net
  narrative;
- v10 develops Boundary, Escape Channel and Return language while continuing
  unsupported number and physics interpretations;
- v16-v19 reintroduce expansive physical, astronomical and universal claims
  without the missing code, data or independent validation.

## Durable residue

The strongest recoverable contribution is not the proposed modulus or a
physical Ramanujan mechanism. It is the following bounded methodological
pattern:

```text
representation has an aperture
  -> the aperture induces a measurement boundary
  -> the boundary conditions admissible observations and transitions
  -> a crossing may leave the current model or regime
  -> a return comparison records nonidentity and residual
```

This pattern is compatible with the BoundaryRelation synthesis because both
keep object, representation, detector, boundary, crossing and return record
distinct. Compatibility does not prove historical identity.

## Required evidence for a future bridge

A future proposal connecting any two of the rows must provide:

1. the exact source object and version;
2. the exact spelling or glyph;
3. a declared carrier and domain;
4. input and output types;
5. the transformation or transition rule;
6. invariants, losses and residuals;
7. a negative control distinguishing spelling resemblance from functional
   equivalence;

Boundary/Escape grammar remain separate.
