## What a slice means here

A slice is a declared view or fiber of a carrier, not a new tile and not a
new state identity. In particular:

- the eight Tessarec spectrum lanes are sorted-index display slices;
- the six spectral fields are value intervals in `[-3,3]`;
- an Eleven-Space residue is a many-to-one arithmetic view;
- a Pascal Hamming-weight cell is a quotient fiber;
- the SCC-003 side chain is a seam domain in a different Pascal carrier;
- the CON–DAO breath changes display separation and orientation only.

This distinction permits the views to be coordinated while preventing a
lane, residue, fiber, seam and physical face from becoming synonyms.
