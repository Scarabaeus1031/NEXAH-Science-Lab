# Cartography and visual models

Status: `VISUAL_INTUITION / EXPLANATORY_ASSET / NOT_EVIDENCE_BY_ITSELF`

## Role of the visuals

The visual corpus is retained because it records the development of the
conceptual framework and can expose questions that prose alone may hide. Its
primary present roles are:

- conceptual cartography;
- historical genealogy;
- communication and teaching;
- discovery of candidate objects, transformations and boundaries;
- comparison of alternative representations.

## Main visual families

| Family | Current interpretation | Prohibited automatic inference |
|---|---|---|
| line, loop and figure-eight | path, return and orientation intuition | a universal transition law |
| torus, Möbius strip and Klein bottle | orientability and identification questions | that the depicted system has that topology |
| folds, shells and domes | projection and layered-access intuition | physical geometry or cosmology |
| root space and base plate | proposed coordinate or reference organization | necessity of `sqrt(2)`, `pi`, `phi` or `-1/12` |
| proof temples, trees and cascades | proof-architecture explanation | existence of the represented proof |
| observer maps and cartographies | frame-dependent access and projection | observer-created physical reality |
| dual AXIOM-0 and HM carrier field | historical model proposal | established field, particle or mechanism |

## Promotion rule

A visual becomes part of a mathematical or empirical evidence package only
after the following are declared independently of the image:

1. objects and carriers;
2. mappings or operations;
3. constraints and domains;
4. comparison or evaluation rule;
5. failure and uncertainty behavior;
6. a proposition, hypothesis or endpoint that can fail.

Until then the correct status is `VISUAL_INTUITION` or `VISUAL_MODEL`.
