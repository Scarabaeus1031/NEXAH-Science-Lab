## ACR-38 versus ACR-39

The distinction matters for reuse:

- ACR-38 is a retained static result record. Its finite checks say that the
  residual side is necessary for invertibility.
- ACR-39 has a retained runner and a larger exact audit with 5,460 records and
  zero full-record collisions. It formalizes
  `H=(m,t mod 7,t mod m,CA,CB)` and the projections `P_C(H)=CA`, `P_D(H)=CB`.
- ACR-39 confirms that the complete record reconstructs both the phase address
  and `x=CA+CB`; either view alone is lossy. The C/D swap preserves address and
  sum, but not the semantic role assigned to a side.

Thus ACR-39 is the stronger reusable engineering precedent. It supports a
**carrier-record requirement**, not a geometric elevator.

## What is already tested, so should not be repeated

1. Standard CRT reconstruction for coprime products (`5x7`, `7x11`, and the
   HMA-01 Mod-77 product space).
2. Exact finite address/remainder decomposition for the `11x111=1221` carrier.
3. The distinction between 111 sample points and 110 intervals.
4. Direct 11-to-111 cell nesting: already contradicted as a canonical identity.
5. A/B or C/D single-view loss versus paired exact reconstruction.
6. Standard winding invariance and the failure of the historical `+11 WIND`
   identification.
7. The numeric identities `112x110=12320` and `111x111=12321`; the difference
   of one is exact but is not yet an operator.

## The remaining testable question

The unresolved object is not another CRT table. It is a declared, reproducible
typed map with return data:

```text
L : G11 x K -> G111
R : image(L) -> G11 x K
R(L(a,k)) = (a,k)
```

Here `a` is the source address and `K` must be declared rather than inferred.
The archaeology suggests a minimal candidate key with four separately typed
parts:

```text
K = (scale/register, side-or-residual, orientation, provenance)
```

This is a design constraint, not a recovered historical formula. A valid next
test must compare explicit candidate lifts under collision count, roundtrip,
boundary behavior and destruction controls. It must reject any candidate that
works only because the source address was silently copied through.
