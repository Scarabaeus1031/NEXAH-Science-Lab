## Purpose

This package preserves and reconciles a historical body of NEXAH work on
orientation, persistence, comparison, projection, measurement and
cartography. It records that the corpus is a genuine conceptual predecessor of
the current NEXAH comparison/orientation method while preventing historical
visual, mathematical and physical language from being promoted as one unified
theory.

The package is not a new Lab, Study, experiment, theorem set, physical model or
Core adoption. It was created inside the existing documentation-only
Mathematical Foundations research program because `SCIENCE_LAB/INTAKE` is
closed during Structure Freeze.

## Package thesis

The most durable methodological chain isolated from the source corpus is:

```text
orientation
  -> distinction and relation
  -> persistence
  -> comparability
  -> projection
  -> measurement
  -> cartography and representation
```

This chain is a research and reconciliation object. The arrows are not, by
their presence here, logical implications or established physical laws.
