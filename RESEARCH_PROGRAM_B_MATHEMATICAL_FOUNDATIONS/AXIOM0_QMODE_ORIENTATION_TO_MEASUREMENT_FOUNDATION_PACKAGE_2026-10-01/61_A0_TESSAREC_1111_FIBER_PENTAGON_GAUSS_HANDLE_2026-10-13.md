## 2. Pentagon ratio audit

For a regular pentagon with side `s`, its diagonal is `phi*s`. A diagonal cut
produces a golden isosceles triangle and a four-sided remainder. The remainder
is **not** itself a rhombus. Their exact area relation is:

```text
A_remainder / A_triangle = phi^2
A_triangle / A_pentagon = (5-sqrt(5))/10
```

Two mirrored golden triangles glued along their `phi*s` bases do form a
rhombus with side `s`, angles `72°/108°`, and long diagonal `phi*s`.

## 3. Apex cut to a six-sided boundary

Truncating one pentagon apex at fractional distance `t` along the two adjacent
sides removes a triangle and leaves a six-sided boundary. The cut edge is:

```text
cut_edge = phi * t * s.
```

At `t=1/phi`, the new edge equals the original side `s`. The remaining figure
is a symmetric hexagon, but not a regular hexagon.
