## 1. What has actually changed

The earlier Series-I assessment correctly found no admitted theorem. That
statement is now historically superseded for the later A0 corpus. Since then,
A0 has created at least three different kinds of genuine result:

1. **Object-level mathematics:** the 106-vertex cyclic five-face Pascal
   quotient with 180 directed recurrence edges and exact typed `D5` symmetry.
2. **Exact representation mathematics:** the all-`N` injective,
   `C3`-equivariant Pascal–Quaternion map with exact inverse Return and the
   required `N=0` faithfulness qualifier.
3. **Finite information-loss results:** path dependence, non-compressing
   collision removal, coordinate-fold fibre counts and minimal residual-bit
   requirements.

The corpus also contains many exact arithmetic relations. Exactness alone is
not novelty. `37+51=88`, `47+41=88`, `404+707=1111`, `1087+7801=8888`,
`4774+4114=8888`, Euler-totient profiles, prime indices and palindrome closure
are valid observations or test fixtures. They become scientific findings only
when a predeclared carrier, selection rule, comparator and falsifier make them
discriminative rather than retrospectively selectable.

Pascal graphs, generalized Pascal pyramids, multinomial path graphs and
Pascal-related dihedral symmetries are established subjects. Quint's Pascal
graph, Hilton–Pedersen–Rosenthal's Pascalian hexagon construction, work on

```text
five finite depth-six faces
+ complete radial side-chain quotient
+ 112 -> 106 cyclic closure
+ retained facewise Pascal recurrence
+ exact typed D5 automorphism group.
```

That non-match is evidence for a reviewable candidate, not proof of priority.
The result is narrow enough to submit for expert assessment and concrete

**Novelty confidence:** `MODERATE CANDIDATE / UNESTABLISHED EXTERNALLY`.  
**Publication readiness:** closest of the A0 results, after external proof and
literature review.

### 3.2 Pascal–Quaternion all-`N` connector

**Internal result.** For

```text
P_N = {(a,b,c) in Z^3_>=0 : a+b+c <= N},
Phi(a,b,c) = (a+b+c)1 + ai + bj + ck,
u = (1+i+j+k)/2,
```

`Phi` is injective and `Ad_u` cycles the imaginary coordinates; inverse
conjugation gives exact Return. Proper coordinate permutations form
`A3=C3`; faithful restricted action requires `N>=1` because `P_0` is a
singleton.

**Assessment:** `CORRECT AND ELEGANT; NOVEL PACKAGING MORE LIKELY THAN DEEP NEW THEOREM`.

The proof is sound and the qualifier is important. But its ingredients are
standard: barycentric/simplex coordinates are injective coordinate systems;
vertex permutations act on the coordinates; unit-quaternion conjugation
represents `SO(3)` rotations; a 120-degree rotation about `(1,1,1)` cyclically
permutes the three coordinate axes. The potentially distinctive part is the
specific source-bound pairing with the finite Pascal carrier and the explicit
Return contract—not the underlying quaternion fact by itself.

**Novelty confidence:** `LOW–MODERATE AS A STANDALONE THEOREM`; stronger as a
worked connector inside Paper C.  
**Publication readiness:** suitable as a proposition/example in a broader
methods paper after specialist review; probably too elementary alone.

### 3.4 Coordinate-fold and assembled-interface theorems

**Internal results.** For the coordinatewise `2m -> 2m-1` fold, a target with
`k` central coordinates has fibre size `2^k`; exactly
`C(d,k)(2m-2)^(d-k)` targets have that size. Exact pointwise Return requires
and is achieved by `k` conditional bits; a fixed-width worst-case address
requires `d` bits. A labelled two-carrier assembly adds one branch bit at a
glued interface.

**Assessment:** `EXACT GENERAL PROPOSITIONS; MATHEMATICALLY ELEMENTARY`.

The statements are useful because they make the Return cost explicit and
prevent a false inverse. The underlying reasoning is direct fibre counting
and zero-error side information. Information theory has a much broader theory
of source coding with decoder side information and graph entropy. Therefore
these propositions are best presented as transparent lemmas supporting a
formal interface contract, not as a new information theory.

**Novelty confidence:** `LOW AS PURE MATHEMATICS / MODERATE AS ENGINEERING CONTRACT`.  
**Publication readiness:** include as methods lemmas, with a proof and explicit
comparison to zero-error side-information literature.

## 6. What is genuinely valuable even if novelty shrinks

Scientific value does not require every component to be unprecedented. The
A0 corpus has already learned several durable things:

- **Return is typed.** Endpoint, structure, representation, operator, history
  and identity are different claims.
- **A residual is information, not decoration.** When a quotient folds a
  fibre, exact Return needs enough side information to select a preimage.
- **Negative results improve the architecture.** Path dependence,
  non-compression, missing intertwiners and failed identity collapses are
  informative outcomes.
- **A number is not an object identity.** Equal totals and shared residues can
  be deliberate tests without becoming mechanisms.
- **The visual layer is strongest when it exposes a formal decision.** It is
  weakest when labels such as resonance or quantum stand in for measurement.
- **The governance is part of the research quality.** Source authority,
  replay, claim ceilings and STOP rules have prevented multiple false
  promotions.

## 7. Main weaknesses

1. **External review is missing.** Local clean-room replay is not independent
   academic validation.
2. **The literature search is bounded.** MathSciNet/zbMATH-style specialist
   coverage and expert citation chasing remain incomplete.
3. **The corpus is selection-rich.** Many numbers, transforms and visual
   views create a large post-hoc search space. Confirmatory claims need frozen
   selectors and fresh data or untouched instances.
4. **General significance is not yet shown.** Exact finite constructions can
   be correct but too special to matter outside their own definition.
5. **No strong external baseline has been beaten.** JARP and the visual tools
   are not yet compared to a simpler ledger, provenance graph or lens-based
   workflow.
6. **Physical vocabulary outruns evidence.** The current mathematical corpus
   does not support quantum, elemental, astronomical or energetic mechanisms.
7. **The public story risks overcoupling.** The more every number appears to
   connect to every instrument, the harder it becomes to see the few results
   that can survive independent review.
