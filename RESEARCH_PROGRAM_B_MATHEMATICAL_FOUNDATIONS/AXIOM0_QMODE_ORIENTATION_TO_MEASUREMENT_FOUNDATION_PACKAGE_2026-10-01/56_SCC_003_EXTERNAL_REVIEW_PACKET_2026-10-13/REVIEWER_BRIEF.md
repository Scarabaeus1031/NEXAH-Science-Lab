# Reviewer Brief — SCC-003

## Requested expertise

Primary reviewer: finite graph theory, algebraic combinatorics, enumerative
combinatorics or simplicial/quotient constructions.  
Literature reviewer: mathematical information specialist or mathematician able
to search MathSciNet, zbMATH, Crossref, arXiv and citation networks.

## Object

Let `P_6={(r,k):0<=k<=r<=6}` with weight `C(r,k)` and directed Pascal
recurrence edges. Take five labelled copies. Consecutive copies share a
complete seven-node side chain under the declared seam equivalence, all apices
are identified, and the last side chain is closed to the first. The quotient is
typed by weights, directed recurrence, face incidence and seam provenance.

The submitted propositions establish:

1. open counts `28,49,70,91,112`;
2. cyclic closure identifies six additional non-apex pairs and gives 106
   quotient vertices;
3. the quotient has 180 unique directed recurrence edges;
4. it is connected and preserves the facewise Pascal recurrence;
5. its declared rotations and reflections form a typed `D5` action;
6. the full typed automorphism group is exactly the ten declared elements.

## Why review is requested

The authors have a formal specification, compact proofs, a deterministic
reference export and a separately implemented local verifier. A bounded
prior-art audit located nearby Pascal graphs, multi-sector Pascal extensions,
binary Pascal symmetries, Pascal networks and generalized Pascal pyramids, but
no exact predecessor. Database coverage and specialist review remain
incomplete.

## What is not claimed

- no universal Pascal algebra or general `m,d` theorem;
- no physical, quantum, harmonic, elemental or astronomical mechanism;
- no novelty inference from the number 106;
- no claim that metric, 3D, root-system, D30 or quaternion representations are
  part of the narrow theorem;
- no worldwide priority claim.

## Requested mode

Please review the formal object before inspecting visual or contextual
materials. A prior-art objection should identify an explicit mapping from a
published construction to the five controlling features in the claim table;
terminological resemblance alone is insufficient.

If a proof fails, report the earliest failing definition or inference. If a
predecessor is found, cite the exact theorem/construction and classify it as
equivalent, immediately specializing to SCC-003, or merely analogous.
