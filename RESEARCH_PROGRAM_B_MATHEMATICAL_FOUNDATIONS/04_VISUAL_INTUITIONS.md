# Visual Intuitions

These families can motivate a question but do not define mathematical objects by themselves.

| ID | Visual family | Mathematical question it may suggest | Why it remains visual intuition |
|---|---|---|---|
| VI-01 | stones, joints, maps, and masonry | compatibility and composition of representation maps | no common domain, map class, or compatibility law is supplied by the imagery |
| VI-02 | river, path, whip, rope, and weaving | ordered paths, alignment, and transport | the depicted path has no fixed parameter, carrier, or identity contract |
| VI-03 | switchyard, joystick, rails, belt, and five-leaf center | bounded coordinates and weighted aggregation | interval normalization is established, but the visual arrangement does not define a general operator |
| VI-04 | sheet, fold, torus, cylinder, nautilus, and mirror | projection, topology, and representation change | visual transformation does not establish homeomorphism, manifold structure, or invariant |
| VI-05 | figure-eight, paired loops, and Geometry of Balance | bounded oscillation, counter-direction, or compensation | the evidence review finds multiple non-equivalent balance models and no shared mathematical definition |
| VI-06 | compass, anchor, crown, pentagon, and relational center | reference frames, symmetry, or barycentric position | symbol recurrence does not specify a group action, symmetry class, metric, or estimator |

## Routing rule

A visual family becomes mathematical evidence only after its objects, mappings, constraints, and comparison rule are declared independently of the image. Until then it remains `VISUAL INTUITION`.
