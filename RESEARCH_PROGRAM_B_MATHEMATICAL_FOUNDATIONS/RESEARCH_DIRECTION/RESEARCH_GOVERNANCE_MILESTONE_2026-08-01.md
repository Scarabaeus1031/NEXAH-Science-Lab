# Research Governance Milestone

Date: `2026-08-01`

Status: `ADOPTED MILESTONE`

Authority: `Thomas`

Operational effect: `NONE`

## Milestone

On 1 August 2026, the NEXAH research process established explicit separation
among:

- architecture;
- governance;
- research;
- evidence;
- scientific objects;
- execution;
- publication.

This marks the transition from exploratory architecture to governed research.

## Lifecycle

Every future scientific object follows the same controlled lifecycle:

```text
Architecture boundary
↓
Governance authority
↓
Bounded research question
↓
Evidence consolidation
↓
Source-freeze preparation
↓
Canonical source freeze
↓
Canonical scientific object
↓
Replay preparation
↓
Independent review gate
↓
Explicit execution authorization
↓
Evidence and validation
↓
Publication decision
↓
Maintenance or archive
```

Each transition requires the authority, evidence and approval defined by its
governing documents.

## Governance rule

No experiment may bypass the governance pipeline.

In particular:

- architecture does not authorize research execution;
- research questions do not create evidence;
- evidence does not create a canonical scientific object;
- source preparation does not perform a source freeze;
- a source freeze does not authorize execution;
- execution does not establish validation automatically;
- validation does not authorize publication automatically;
- publication does not transfer scientific or Human authority.

## Human authority

Every gate requiring judgment must identify the Human exercising
Handlungshoheit.

Software, documentation completeness, repository state, technical capability
and prior execution cannot substitute for explicit Human authorization.
