## Purpose

This package is the current umbrella reference for mathematical research inside
NEXAH.

Research is intentionally smaller than the Architecture.

NEXAH is an orientation architecture. Its mathematical research concerns only
bounded questions that can be stated using declared objects, maps, equivalence
relations, invariants, perturbations and failure conditions.

No mathematical novelty, scientific priority or new mathematical field is
claimed.

## Governing distinction

```text
LUCY
Human world and purpose

↓

NEXAH Constitution
Authority and governance

↓

NEXAH Architecture
Orientation system

↓

Research
Bounded mathematical questions
```

Architecture, philosophy, governance and mathematics are distinct layers.
Mathematical work does not acquire architectural or constitutional authority by
being included in NEXAH.

```text

```

mathematics/non-mathematics boundary. Programs E, F and G supply literature

program.
