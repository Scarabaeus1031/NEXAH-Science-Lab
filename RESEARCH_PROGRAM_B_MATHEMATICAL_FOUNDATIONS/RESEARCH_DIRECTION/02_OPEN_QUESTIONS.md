# Open Questions

Status: `OPEN — NO RESULT IMPLIED`

## Classification rule

Each question is assigned one primary research-action class. The classification
does not imply novelty or membership in one new geometry.

| ID | Question | Class | Minimum prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | What is the minimum selected observation family that separates every admitted source pair? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | finite source class, observation family, equality rule |
| Q-02 | How can unresolved equivalence classes be characterized for a declared family of non-injective maps? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | fibers or equivalence partitions, domain and map family |
| Q-03 | What separation margin survives a bounded perturbation of sources, observations or operators? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | metric, perturbation set and stability criterion |
| Q-04 | What is the computational complexity of selecting a minimum or near-minimum separating view family? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | finite decision problem and input-size model |
| Q-05 | When do two representation-change paths commute exactly or within a declared defect bound? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | composable maps, comparison space and defect measure |
| Q-06 | How should identifiability be evaluated when observation is partial, censored or missing under a declared mechanism? | `PROTOCOL QUESTION` | distinct missingness, censoring and observation records |
| Q-07 | How does a declared multi-view reconstruction baseline behave when source provenance remains external to observed coordinates? | `COMPUTATIONAL EXPERIMENT` | frozen reconstruction method, data, metrics and nulls |
| Q-08 | Under what conditions does a named invariant descend to the quotient induced by observational equivalence? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | invariant, equivalence relation and quotient structure |
| Q-09 | Which parts of the finite view-selection problem are already covered by sensor selection, combinatorial test cover and inverse-problem formulations? | `LITERATURE-REVIEW QUESTION` | frozen scientific wording and comparison criteria |
| Q-10 | Which finite definitions extend consistently to continuous, manifold-valued, graph-structured or otherwise structured source spaces? | `THEOREM CANDIDATE` | one selected carrier and declared regularity assumptions |

## Priority order

The smallest responsible order is:

1. Q-09 — identify established formulations and prevent terminology drift;
2. Q-01 and Q-02 — formalize finite separation and failure;
3. Q-03 — add perturbation stability;
4. Q-04 — study selection complexity only after the decision problem is fixed;
5. Q-05 and Q-08 — admit representation composition or quotient claims only
   when the required structure exists;
6. Q-06 and Q-07 — retain as separate protocol and computational work;
7. Q-10 — extend beyond finite sets only after one finite result survives review.

## STOP conditions

Stop formalization when:

- the source class or map family is undefined;
- an equality or tolerance rule is missing;
- visual language is used in place of a mathematical object;
- a question changes after results are inspected;
- an established formulation has not been compared;
- provenance is used as evidence of identifiability;
- architectural terminology is treated as mathematical evidence.
