## Position

The NEXAH mathematical research direction is coherent only after reduction to
ordinary mathematical language and bounded objects.

NEXAH is not currently a new mathematical field. Its viable mathematical core
concerns representation and observation maps, identifiability, named
invariants and stability under declared changes of representation.

The wider NEXAH architecture is not mathematical evidence. Architectural
coherence cannot establish a theorem, scientific validity or novelty.

## Terminology boundary

NEXAH may retain `transversal` internally as a working metaphor for crossing
representations.

It shall not present `Transversal Geometry` as a public mathematical field or
as established mathematical transversality unless future work genuinely uses
that formal meaning and passes independent literature review.

Scientific documents should prefer:

```text
invariant and identifiable structure under changes of representation
```

or the exact established discipline applicable to the object, such as inverse
problems, observability, invariant theory or multi-view geometry.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest current object is the finite deterministic protocol selected by
Programs F and G. It classifies observational equivalence for fixed source
identities under four declared orthographic observation maps.

That object supports a bounded protocol and replay question. It does not by
itself support:

- a general theorem;
- a new geometry;
- a novelty claim;
- continuous-source conclusions;
- empirical conclusions;
- generalization beyond the frozen object.
