# Minimum Mathematical Research Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Freeze vocabulary boundaries

Purpose: prevent visual, editorial, semantic, and mathematical uses of the same names from being merged.

Required output:

- one source/target representation declaration template;
- one typed loss/failure statement;
- one explicit distinction between relation, mathematical operator, estimator, metric, and visual motif.

Exit: each later question can state its objects and authority without project-specific metaphor.

## Stage 1 — FQ-01 representation maps

Start with one existing finite synthetic projection case. Declare source, target, domain, map, identity rule, lost distinctions, and failure set.

Exit: one property is either preserved, lost, or undecidable under the declared map. No general theory is required.

## Stage 2 — FQ-03 unavailable-information semantics

Use existing Lab 0.2 classes and the Lab 0.3 negative result to determine whether the classifications remain complete and non-conflating for one reconstruction case.

Exit: masked, reconstructed, underdetermined, unknown, boundary, and computational failure cannot be silently substituted.

## Stage 3 — Choose one inverse/estimation question

Preferred first choice: RQ-01, because existing Labs 0.3 and 0.4 already contain deterministic counterexamples and no new implementation is required for initial mathematical analysis.

Alternative after separate authorization: RQ-02 Moving Mask, using the completed Program A feasibility review.

Exit: one scoped identifiability statement or one preregistered numerical protocol; not both at once.

## Stage 4 — FQ-02 transition/path records

Only after representation identity is stable, compare the existing graph, parameter-sweep, cluster, and censored-path cases.

Exit: common composable fields are separated from domain-specific non-composable fields.

## Stage 5 — RQ-05 transport reduction

Restate the existing graph-compression sequence using declared preserved properties and tolerances. Test the current evidence before proposing a general reduction theorem.

Exit: preservation or failure is shown for named graph quantities; H-06 spectral alignment remains a separate hypothesis test.

## Stage 6 — RQ-06 diagnostic distinctness

Freeze Gate and directional-coherence definitions independently. Run their already proposed baseline and null comparisons. Demote either measure if a simpler diagnostic explains it.

Exit: bounded retained value or reduction.

## Parked until definitions exist

- RQ-04 relation-derived observable and encoding invariance;
- general FQ-04 aggregation outside the Euclidean weighted mean;
- general proof claims for cross-domain geometry or universal operators.

## Stop rule

Stop a branch when its object cannot be defined independently of a visual, its result reduces to a simpler existing quantity, its identity changes across representations without a declared rule, or its evidence cannot distinguish the claim from a counterexample.
