# Validation Roadmap

This roadmap orders existing validation gates. It does not authorize or design new experiments.

## 1 — Preserve exact and negative baselines

- retain C-01 through C-10 unchanged;
- retain G4 failure C-42 unchanged;
- retain the Lab 0.3 negative result;
- bind each result to its exact protocol and hashes.

Exit: no positive or negative result can be restated beyond its source scope.

## 2 — Repair claim-to-evidence navigation

- assign the 49 reduced claim units stable documentation references;
- link primary files, independent routes, failures, and missing evidence;
- avoid copying payloads into a new authority.

Exit: a reviewer can traverse every claim to evidence or an explicit absence record.

## 3 — Resolve the JANUS/DCO evidence break

Use the already recorded Mission 01 sequence:

1. freeze one directional-coherence definition;
2. align implementation and semantics;
3. invalidate or rerun the degenerate surrogate route only under separate authority;
4. compare against direction change, curvature, and shuffled-order nulls;
5. report reduction if the measure adds no information.

Exit: C-30 through C-33 receive bounded results or remain unvalidated/unsupported.

## 4 — Complete IEEE evidence review without changing G4

- preserve `G4_clean_replay_failed`;
- expose G3 equivalence and instrumented G4 diagnostics together;
- do not infer a tolerance;
- proceed to any claim-boundary or external-review gate only through separate approval.

Exit: reviewers can distinguish byte failure, low-order numeric divergence, structural agreement, and unresolved scientific equivalence.

## 5 — Validate one research candidate at a time

Order:

1. Program B representation-map review;
2. Program A Moving Mask protocol only if separately authorized;
3. reader outcome test;
4. OLS interoperability experiment.

Exit: one bounded result per authorized question. No cross-program maturity transfer.

## Stop conditions

- no primary source;
- formula or outcome changes after results;
- failed result is reinterpreted as support;
- visual or publication state is treated as evidence;
- independence is claimed for a shared-author/shared-code route;
- scope expands to operational, causal, or universal language.
