# NEXAH Removal and Baseline Test

`NEXAH_REMOVAL_TEST = YES`

Removing the NEXAH name leaves a comprehensible generic system: typed source/target boundaries, explicit operators/components, preservation/loss criteria, uncertainty, provenance, epistemic dimensions, and claim history. The samples use ordinary code/data concepts and decontaminate historical labels within their contracts.

Incremental utility over competent conventional documentation is `CLEAR`, though not novel. The synthesis makes four concerns jointly queryable:

1. a translation edge and its component boundary;
2. operational preservation/loss and uncertainty;
3. source traceability and independent epistemic dimensions;
4. retained negative scientific claims.

A provenance/knowledge graph, FAIR manifest, and disciplined repository could implement the same ideas. The value is the constrained combined profile and fail-closed vocabulary, not a new scientific or mathematical principle.
