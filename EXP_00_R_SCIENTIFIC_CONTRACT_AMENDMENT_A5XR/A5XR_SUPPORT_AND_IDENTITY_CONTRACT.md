# A5XR Support and Identity Contract

Registered identities are authority-bound without outcome access; this task's fixture uses a structurally identical, explicitly separate 30-seed synthetic registry. Support blocks require exact unique registry membership, unique expanded row IDs, 50 rows/seed, nested nonzero≤joint≤total counts, total 1,500, fractions in `[0,1]` equal raw counts, T/F OOS≤.10, joint≥.80 and at least 20 authoritative seeds with ≥20 both-nonzero rows. Unknown/duplicate seeds, duplicate rows and impossible counts fail.
